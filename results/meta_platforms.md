## Aktuelle Meldungen
- Meta's board declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.525 per share of Class A and Class B common stock on September 10, 2026, payable September 28, 2026 to holders of record as of September 21, 2026.
- Meta stock closed at USD 648.03 on September 11, 2026; some analysts see further upside toward USD 820–860 on AI growth and monetization progress, though the position's current price reflects a pullback from that level.
- Meta acquired Swedish AI startup Stilla.ai, folding its team and technology into the Meta Business Agent product, which automates business messaging across WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram for more than 1 million businesses.
- Meta launched its own AI-content detection system based on a "Content Seal" watermark, initially working only for images generated with Meta AI.
- Production of Meta's in-house "Iris" AI chip (co-developed with Broadcom on design, fabricated by TSMC) is set to begin in September 2026, with a roadmap for new chip generations roughly every six months through 2027.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- Continued build-out of proprietary AI infrastructure, including the in-house "Iris" chip entering production this month, aimed at reducing dependence on third-party GPU suppliers (Nvidia/AMD).
- Bolt-on AI acquisitions continue (Stilla.ai) to strengthen Meta's automated business-messaging and AI-agent offerings across WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram.
- New AI content-provenance tooling ("Content Seal" watermark/detection) signals growing focus on trust and safety around AI-generated media, an area of ongoing regulatory attention.
- Meta Connect 2026 (September 23–24) remains the next major catalyst, expected to showcase further AI, AI-glasses and VR developments.
- Central open question for investors remains unchanged: whether record AI capex will translate into durable returns, given continued operating-margin compression despite strong revenue growth.
