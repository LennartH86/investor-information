## Recent News
- Q3 Fiscal 2027 results (reported November 17, 2026): revenue of $115.8 billion, up 103% year-over-year and up 20% from Q2; Data Center revenue of $107.6 billion, up 110% YoY.
- Company began shipping the NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72 platform, described as the fastest product ramp in company history; also introduced the DSX POD reference design and a Vera CPU rack-scale platform for HPC.
- Q4 FY2027 guidance set at $130.0 billion (±3%), assuming no Data Center compute revenue from China.
- Confirmed CEO transition and reversal: Nvidia's board accepted Jensen Huang's resignation as President, CEO and board member on November 11, 2026 (SEC 8-K), with CFO Colette Kress named interim CEO; reporting tied the exit to a personal relationship (Washington Post, Variety, Al Jazeera). On November 23, 2026, the board reinstated Huang as President, CEO and board member effective immediately, saying it had consulted senior U.S. government officials and found no national-security concern (Nvidia press release).
- NVIDIA introduced GR00T, Isaac Lab 3.0 and new open benchmarks for robotics/physical AI at the CORL conference.

## Management
- Jensen Huang was reinstated as President and CEO (and board member) on November 23, 2026, after a roughly two-week resignation triggered by reports of a personal relationship with a Chinese tech executive; Nvidia's own statements have not detailed the reasons for either the resignation or the reinstatement beyond citing a government security review with no adverse findings.
- Colette Kress (EVP and CFO since 2013) served as interim CEO during the transition and has returned to her CFO role; Harvey C. Jones was named the company's first-ever Chair of the Board as part of the transition filings.
- In earlier-2026 public appearances, Jensen Huang emphasized physical AI, world models and robotics as the next major growth frontier, and repeatedly stressed the need for "new social norms" in an AI-driven society.

## Financials
- Q3 FY2027 (ended October 25, 2026): revenue $115.8B (+103% YoY, +20% QoQ); Data Center segment $107.6B (+110% YoY).
- Full fiscal year 2026 revenue: $215.9 billion, up 65% YoY; Q4 FY2026 GAAP EPS $1.76, non-GAAP EPS $1.62.
- Quarterly dividend of $0.25/share, payable December 23, 2026 to shareholders of record as of December 2, 2026.
- Board increased the share repurchase authorization by $150 billion (announced September 2026), leaving roughly $227.3 billion still available for buybacks.

## Strategy & Outlook
- Continued aggressive buildout of AI/data-center compute capacity, headlined by the ramp of the Vera Rubin NVL72 platform and new rack-scale HPC designs (DSX POD, Vera CPU platform).
- Active M&A and strategic-investment strategy to consolidate the AI compute/networking stack, including a reported ~$20 billion acquisition of AI chip startup Groq, plus multi-billion-dollar strategic stakes in Marvell ($2B), Lumentum ($2B), Coherent ($2B), Synopsys ($2B), CoreWeave ($2B), and a reported $30 billion investment in OpenAI; a reported Hugging Face acquisition is unverified.
- Guidance assumes no Data Center compute revenue from China for the near term, highlighting ongoing geopolitical/export-control risk to the China market.
- Expansion into physical AI and robotics (GR00T, Isaac Lab 3.0) positions NVIDIA for growth beyond data-center GPUs, per Jensen Huang's public comments on the next decade of AI.
- The November CEO resignation/reinstatement episode is resolved for now with Huang back in charge, but residual governance and reputational questions (and any congressional interest reported at the time) are worth monitoring going forward.
