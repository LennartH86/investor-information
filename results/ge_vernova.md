## Management
- CEO Scott Strazik continues to describe a global electricity "Supercycle" driven by AI/data-center demand and electrification, and refers to GE Vernova as a "75,000-person startup" following the 2024 spin-off from GE.
- At the BofA Global Industrials Conference (March 2026), Strazik noted that services revenue made up about 45% of last year's revenue, backed by an $85 billion services backlog.
- Strazik advocates an "all-of-the-above" energy mix (gas, renewables, nuclear, grid) and calls gas a "force multiplier" for economic growth; he regularly appears at investor conferences (BofA, Bernstein, Goldman Sachs in December 2026).
- Eric Gray was named CEO of GE Vernova's Power segment and discussed the company's strategy in a separate interview; this is distinct from the group CEO role held by Strazik.

## Strategy & Outlook
- Trench Group: In October 2026, GE Vernova signed an agreement to acquire Trench Group (high-voltage grid equipment) from Triton Partners for approximately $9.0bn; expected to add roughly $5bn in backlog. Closing date still not confirmed in available sources.
- Prolec GE: Completed the acquisition of the remaining 50% stake in the former joint venture with Xignux on 02.02.2026 for $5.275bn, funded equally by cash and debt (including a first-ever $2.6bn senior notes issuance). Strengthens the Electrification segment.
- Robotech Automation: Acquisition of the Montreal-area robotics integrator announced 21.05.2026; closing was expected in early Q3 2026, with no update found confirming completion (financial terms undisclosed).
- Strategic focus remains on the "Electricity Supercycle" (AI/data-center demand), grid modernization (e.g., the large HVDC order in India), continued onshore wind order intake in Europe, and electrification, complemented by further bolt-on M&A in gas, grid and electrification capacity.
- Management has raised full-year 2026 guidance repeatedly across quarters (revenue, margin, free cash flow), signaling continued confidence given order momentum and a record backlog heading into 2027.
