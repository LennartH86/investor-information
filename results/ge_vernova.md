## Management
- CEO Scott Strazik has repeatedly described a global electricity "supercycle," citing accelerating demand from AI/data centers and broader electrification as the key growth driver.
- Strazik has characterized GE Vernova as effectively a "75,000-person startup" following its 2024 spin-off from GE, emphasizing a sharpened strategic focus that he credits for the market's re-rating of the stock (market cap approaching ~$200B as of early 2026 coverage).
- Management has publicly stated that roughly 25% of global electrons are powered using GE Vernova equipment, and that hyperscale data centers' share of the order book is projected to rise substantially from ~10% in 2024.
- Strazik has emphasized an "all of the above" energy mix view (gas, renewables, nuclear, grid), calling gas a "force multiplier" for economic growth, and has addressed state-level pushback on new energy projects at investor conferences (BofA, March 2026; Bernstein, May 2026).

## Strategie & Ausblick
- Trench Group acquisition: GE Vernova signed an agreement (announced October 2026) to acquire Trench Group, a global leader in high-voltage grid equipment, from Triton Partners for approximately $9.0 billion (USD equivalent), adding ~$5 billion in backlog and framework agreements. Closing status/date not confirmed in available sources.
- Prolec GE: Completed the acquisition of the remaining 50% stake in its former Prolec GE joint venture with Xignux on February 2, 2026, for $5.275 billion, funded with an equal mix of cash and debt (including a $2.6 billion senior notes offering — the company's first debt issuance as a public entity).
