## Aktuelle Meldungen
- GEV stock is up roughly 44% year-to-date and about 54% over the trailing twelve months, though it pulled back around 6% over the past month on broader concerns about the durability of the AI/data-center power-demand trade.
- Analyst coverage remains heavily positive but split on near-term targets: roughly 24 Buy, 6 Outperform, 7 Hold and 2 no-opinion ratings, with a mean price target near $1,236 — about 36% above a recent $912 close.
- CEO Scott Strazik reiterated at the September 16, 2026 Morgan Stanley Laguna Conference that the backlog will reach $200 billion "very early in 2027," up from the record $176 billion at the end of Q2, and noted that some 2032 gas-turbine delivery slots are already being sold.
- On September 23, 2026, GE Vernova announced a Rotor Life Extension (RLE) services agreement covering five 9F gas turbines (~1,250 MW total) at three Egyptian power plants (Kureimat, Nubaria, Cairo North), extending their operating life by 13–15 years, with services running 2028–2035.
- A Fortune profile (published August 2026) detailed Strazik's turnaround of the roughly two-year-old spin-off, whose stock has risen about 600% since separation from GE, built around an "electrify the world" strategy.

## Management
- CEO Scott Strazik continues to frame strategy around a multi-year electricity-demand "supercycle," publicly stating that 2030–2040 could be an "even better decade" than 2020–2030 for the company.
- Strazik maintained a heavy investor-conference and media schedule through 2026 (Bank of America Global Industrials in March, Bernstein Strategic Decisions in May, Morgan Stanley Laguna in September), consistently reinforcing the long-cycle power growth narrative.
- Leadership has emphasized running the roughly 75,000-employee company with a "startup" mentality despite its scale, per a Semafor profile.
- Analyst sentiment remains strongly positive overall, with the majority of coverage at Buy/Outperform.

## Finanzielles
- Q1 2026: backlog grew to $163 billion (including the newly consolidated Prolec GE); adjusted EBITDA of ~$0.9 billion nearly doubled year-over-year (margin +390 bps to 9.6%); Electrification segment booked $2.4 billion in equipment orders for data centers, more than all of 2025.
- Q2 2026: revenue of $11.1 billion (+22% YoY, +12% organic); record orders of $24.2 billion (+88% organic); backlog reached a record $176 billion; adjusted EBITDA margin of 11.3% (+340 bps organically); free cash flow of $5.1 billion; cash balance of $13.1 billion (+$4.3 billion year-to-date); adjusted EPS of $2.47 missed the Street's $3.04 estimate.
- Full-year 2026 guidance was raised again to revenue of $45.5–46.5 billion, free cash flow of $11.5–12.5 billion, and adjusted EBITDA margin of 12–14%; the company now targets at least 125 GW of gas equipment under contract by year-end 2026.
- Completed the $5.275 billion acquisition of the remaining 50% of the Prolec GE joint venture on February 2, 2026, funded via a mix of cash and debt (including $2.6 billion of new senior notes), consolidating a North American transformer manufacturer with ~10,000 employees.
- Committed to $6 billion of capex from 2025–2028 (including $1 billion from Prolec GE) and $5 billion of R&D from 2025–2028 to support the energy-transition build-out.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- Continued ramp of gas turbine and electrification manufacturing capacity (gas capacity additions up roughly 50% year-over-year) to meet AI/data-center-driven electricity demand, alongside international service contracts such as the Egypt rotor life extension deal.
- Backlog trajectory (targeting $200 billion by early 2027) and capacity expansion plans (20 GW annualized gas run-rate in H2 2026, rising to 24 GW by 2028 and 30 GW by 2030) point to sustained multi-year growth, though the Q2 EPS miss shows margin delivery and cost execution remain areas to watch.
- Management's public framing of 2030–2040 as an "even better decade" underscores confidence in a long-duration growth story; the position remains best viewed as a structural bet on the power/electrification buildout rather than a near-term value or income play.
