## Aktuelle Meldungen
- Studsvik AB, GE Vernova Hitachi Nuclear Energy, GE Vernova Financial Services, DS Investment Partners and Samsung C&T agreed to advance the "ReFirm" nuclear program in Sweden — an initial four-unit BWRX-300 small modular reactor project totaling 1.2 GW, with the first unit targeted to enter operation in the mid-2030s.
- GE Vernova was selected by Laing O'Rourke to deliver a new 400 kV Gas-Insulated Switchgear (GIS) substation at Chesterfield as part of National Grid's transmission expansion, supporting increased network capacity and renewable energy connections in the UK.
- GE Vernova signed an agreement to form a joint venture with LS Electric to strengthen its position in the Voltage-Sourced Converter (VSC)-HVDC business for Korea's next-generation power grid.
- The company launched a new 420 kV, 63 kA CO2-O2 Live Tank Circuit Breaker (GL316c) at the CIGRE Paris 2026 conference, extending its high-voltage grid equipment portfolio.
- No major new financial disclosures were identified in the 30.08–06.09.2026 window; the next scheduled investor relations event/filing is expected around September 16, 2026, potentially including Q3 commentary.

## Management
- CEO Scott Strazik continues to frame GE Vernova as a "75,000-person startup," reiterating targets to grow revenue by roughly 50% by 2028 versus the 2025 base while more than doubling profit margins.
- CFO transition remains on track: Claire McDonough (former Rivian CFO) joins GE Vernova in November 2026 and fully succeeds Carolina Dybeck Happe as CFO effective January 1, 2027.
- Recent management commentary (Fortune, Semafor, CNBC profiles through 2026) continues to credit Strazik's operational turnaround and capital allocation discipline for the stock's roughly six-fold rise since the 2024 spin-off.

## Finanzielles
- Q2 2026: Revenue 11,1 Mrd. USD (+22% YoY); orders 24,2 Mrd. USD (+88% organic); backlog reached a record 176 Mrd. USD (up from 129 Mrd. USD a year earlier); cash balance 13,1 Mrd. USD (+4,3 Mrd. USD YTD).
- Q1 2026: Orders 18,3 Mrd. USD (+71% organic); revenue 9,3 Mrd. USD (+16%); net income 4,7 Mrd. USD (50,9% margin); backlog 163 Mrd. USD including Prolec GE.
- Full-year 2026 guidance was raised twice, most recently to revenue of 45,5–46,5 Mrd. USD and free cash flow of 11,5–12,5 Mrd. USD.
- Prolec GE joint venture (transformers, ~10.000 Mitarbeiter, 7 Werke) fully acquired for 5,275 Mrd. USD, abgeschlossen am 2. Februar 2026, hälftig aus Cash und Fremdkapital finanziert.
- Capex-Zusage von 6 Mrd. USD (2025–2028, davon 1 Mrd. USD Prolec GE-bezogen) und F&E-Zusage von 5 Mrd. USD (2025–2028); Dividende wurde verdoppelt, Aktienrückkaufprogramm aufgestockt.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- GE Vernova continues to position itself as a key beneficiary of AI/data-center-driven electricity demand, grid electrification, and the global need for new transformer, switchgear and HVDC capacity.
- Recent wins (Sweden nuclear SMR program, UK substation contract, Korea HVDC joint venture) illustrate geographic and technology diversification across nuclear, grid equipment and electrification.
- Gas turbine manufacturing capacity continues to scale (targeting 20 GW output in 2026, rising toward 30 GW by 2030) against an order book that already stretches toward 2028.
- Wind remains the comparative soft spot within an otherwise strengthening order and backlog picture across Power and Electrification.
