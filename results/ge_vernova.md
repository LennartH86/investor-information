## Management
- CEO Scott Strazik continues to frame global electricity demand as a "supercycle," driven by accelerating AI/data-center load growth and broader electrification.
- Strazik has described GE Vernova as effectively a "75,000-person startup" since its 2024 spin-off from GE, crediting a sharpened strategic focus for the market's re-rating of the stock (market cap approaching ~$200B in earlier 2026 coverage).
- Management states that roughly 25% of global electrons are powered using GE Vernova equipment, and that hyperscale data centers' share of the order book is projected to rise to around 25% of 2026 orders, up from ~10% in 2024.
- Strazik maintains an "all of the above" energy mix view (gas, renewables, nuclear, grid), calling gas a "force multiplier" for economic growth, and has framed 2026 to staff as a year where "execution will matter more than ambition."

- Trench Group acquisition: GE Vernova signed an agreement (announced October 2026) to acquire Trench Group, a global leader in high-voltage grid equipment, from Triton Partners for approximately $9.0 billion (USD equivalent), adding ~$5 billion in backlog and framework agreements.
