# Rio Tinto (RIO.L)
_KW22 / 2026 | Zeitraum: 26.05.2026 – 31.05.2026_

## Aktuelle Meldungen

- **29 May 2026 – Aluminium Shipments to US Recover:** Rio Tinto reported that its aluminium exports to the United States have bounced back to approximately 80% of pre-tariff levels. After the Trump administration raised levies on aluminium to 50%, Rio Tinto had redirected Canadian-produced aluminium to European markets. The company's aluminium chief confirmed the recovery in flows to the US as of late May 2026.
- **19 May 2026 – 8 Billion Tonne Iron Ore Milestone (continued coverage):** Rio Tinto's milestone shipment of the 8 billionth tonne of iron ore from the Pilbara – departing Cape Lambert aboard the *Juno Horizon* bound for Nippon Steel Corporation – continued to generate coverage in the week of KW22, marking 60 years since the company's first iron ore export from Western Australia.
- **11 May 2026 – Jinbi Solar Project Financial Close:** Yindjibarndi Energy Corporation reached Financial Close and signed a 30-year Power Purchase Agreement with Rio Tinto for the Jinbi Solar Project in the Pilbara, supporting Rio Tinto's renewable energy transition at its Western Australian operations.
- **6 May 2026 – AGM Results:** The annual general meetings of Rio Tinto plc (London) and Rio Tinto Limited (Perth) were held on 6 May 2026 as contemporaneous meetings. All resolutions were carried.

## Management

- **CEO Simon Trott** (in office since 25 August 2025) continues to lead the company's strategic agenda around safety, productivity, and copper-led growth. Trott previously served as Iron Ore Chief Executive and brings 25+ years of experience across a wide range of commodities and geographies.
- **Former CEO Jakob Stausholm** led Rio Tinto from January 2021 through August 2025. During his tenure he reset the company's strategy toward energy-transition opportunities. Stausholm has publicly addressed his departure; he is now a fellow at the University of Oxford.
- **New Chief Legal Officer – Trudi Charles:** Rio Tinto appointed Trudi Charles to succeed Isabelle Deschamps as Chief Legal Officer, Governance & Corporate Affairs. Charles currently serves as Deputy General Counsel and Senior Vice President Legal at BP plc and will join Rio Tinto on **1 August 2026**.

## Finanzielles

- **Q1 2026 Production Results (released 21 April 2026):**
  - Copper-equivalent (CuEq) production grew **9% year-over-year** across the portfolio, driven by the continued ramp-up of the Oyu Tolgoi underground copper mine in Mongolia and strong performance from the integrated aluminium business.
  - Pilbara iron ore mines delivered strong operational output; shipments were constrained by two cyclones during the quarter.
  - Resolution Copper (Arizona) achieved a historic federal land exchange, unlocking the next development phase.
- **Full-year 2025 Final Dividend:** Declared on 19 February 2026 at **254.00 US cents per share** for the financial year ended 31 December 2025.
- **Boyne Smelter (Queensland) – Government Partnership (March 2026):** The Queensland and Commonwealth Governments committed a combined **A$2 billion over 10 years to 2040** in partnership with Rio Tinto to support continued operation of the Boyne aluminium smelter.
- **Capex Guidance 2026:** Approximately **USD 11 billion**, with the majority directed at copper and lithium expansion projects.
- **Upcoming Dates:** Half-year results 29 July 2026; Q2/half-year production report 5 August 2026.

## Strategie & Ausblick

- **CBA Aluminium Acquisition (January 2026):** Rio Tinto and Aluminum Corporation of China (Chalco) agreed to jointly acquire Votorantim's **68.596% controlling stake in Companhia Brasileira de Alumínio (CBA)** via a joint venture (Rio Tinto 33% / Chalco 67%), priced at a ~21.2% premium to the 20-day volume-weighted average price. The deal extends Rio Tinto's low-carbon, renewable-powered aluminium footprint in Brazil and the Atlantic region.
- **Nemaska Lithium – Continued Investment (February 2026):** Rio Tinto holds a **53.9% stake** in Nemaska Lithium (Government of Québec: 46.1%) and committed to invest **more than USD 300 million in 2026** to advance lithium production capacity in Québec.
- **Copper Growth – Oyu Tolgoi:** The Mongolian underground copper mine continues its planned ramp-up and is the primary driver of the company's CuEq production growth for 2026 and beyond.
- **Resolution Copper (Arizona):** Following the federal land exchange completed in Q1 2026, the project team is focused on advancing permitting for one of the world's largest untapped copper deposits.
- **Rhodes Ridge Iron Ore Development:** Rio Tinto (50% interest) is advancing pre-feasibility studies at Rhodes Ridge in Western Australia, targeting approximately **100 million tonnes per annum** of high-quality ore to sustain Pilbara mid-term production of **345–360 Mtpa**.
- **Aluminium US Re-engagement:** The recovery of US aluminium shipment flows (back to ~80% of pre-tariff levels) signals improved trade conditions and may support aluminium business margins in H2 2026.
- **Simandou (Guinea):** The Simandou iron ore project continues to advance as one of the world's largest mining infrastructure undertakings.
