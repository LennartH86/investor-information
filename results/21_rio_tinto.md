# Rio Tinto (RIO.L)
_KW25 / 2026 | Zeitraum: 13.06.2026 – 20.06.2026_

## Aktuelle Meldungen

- **18. Juni 2026 – Oyu Tolgoi Copper Exports Resume After Protest Disruption:** Rio Tinto restarted copper concentrate exports from its Oyu Tolgoi mine in Mongolia on June 18, 2026, following a brief one-day disruption caused by a protest. The mine continues its underground ramp-up as planned and remains a critical growth driver for the company's copper production targets.
- **June 2026 – Employee Share Plan Admission:** Rio Tinto plc applied to the London Stock Exchange for admission of 23,000 ordinary shares of 10 pence each to the Main Market. The shares are to be issued to satisfy awards made under the company's Global Employee Share Plan, with admission expected to take effect on Monday, June 23, 2026.
- **June 2026 – Sodexo Pilbara Facilities Management Contract Commences:** The previously announced seven-year contract with Sodexo for facilities management of Rio Tinto's fly-in fly-out (FIFO) accommodation villages, residential houses, and operational sites across Western Australia's Pilbara region officially commenced in June 2026.
- **Ongoing – 8 Billionth Tonne Milestone:** Rio Tinto continued to highlight the milestone of shipping its 8 billionth tonne of Pilbara iron ore, achieved in May 2026 aboard the vessel Juno Horizon, 60 years after the company's first iron ore export from Western Australia to Japan.
- **9. Juni 2026 – Rio Tinto Canada Fund Expands to C$13 Million:** Rio Tinto increased its annual community investment in Canada by 30%, bringing the total to C$13 million annually through the Rio Tinto Canada Fund, supporting Indigenous reconciliation, education, healthcare, wellbeing, and environmental protection.

## Management

- **CEO Simon Trott** (in office since 25 August 2025) has articulated a transformation strategy that reshapes Rio Tinto's operating model, investment priorities, and asset mix. The organisation is structured into three business units — Iron Ore, Copper, and Aluminium & Lithium — reflecting a tighter focus on the company's most competitive growth assets.
- **Cost Cuts and Restructuring:** CEO Trott has been pushing significant cost reductions with workforce implications. The restructuring delivered US$650 million of annualised productivity benefits within its first three months, targeting operational simplification and leaner central functions.
- **EBITDA Growth Target:** Under Trott's leadership, Rio Tinto projects EBITDA could increase by 40–50% by 2030, underpinned by 20% copper-equivalent production growth, improved cost structures, and a more focused asset portfolio.
- **Executive Team Change:** Trudi Charles, formerly Deputy General Counsel at BP plc, is set to join Rio Tinto as Chief Legal Officer on 1 August 2026, succeeding Isabelle Deschamps.

## Finanzielles

- **Q1 2026 Production Results (published 21 April 2026):**
  - Copper-equivalent (CuEq) production grew **9% year-on-year**, driven by the Oyu Tolgoi underground mine ramp-up and strong performance from the integrated aluminium business.
  - Pilbara iron ore output rose **13% to 78.8 million tonnes**, though shipments were partially impacted by two cyclones during the quarter.
  - Copper production reached **229 kt**, up 9% year-on-year.
  - Alumina production reached **2.0 Mt**, up 6% year-on-year.
  - Bauxite production was **13.3 Mt**, down 11% year-on-year.
  - Both the **Fenix 1B** and **Sal de Vida** lithium projects achieved mechanical completion on schedule, with first production targeted for the second half of 2026.
- **2026 Full-Year Guidance Confirmed:** Rio Tinto reaffirmed all production guidance for key commodities following the Q1 update.
- **Capital Expenditure Guidance 2026:** Approximately **US$11 billion**, weighted towards copper and lithium expansion projects.
- **Annualised Productivity Benefits:** US$650 million achieved from operational improvements, streamlining of central functions, and cost optimisations.
- **Upcoming Dates:** Half-year results 29 July 2026; Q2/half-year production report 5 August 2026.

## Strategie & Ausblick

- **Rio Tinto & BHP – Pilbara Iron Ore Collaboration:** Rio Tinto and BHP signed non-binding Memoranda of Understanding (MOUs) to explore collaborative mining of up to 200 million tonnes of iron ore at their neighbouring operations — Yandicoogina (Rio Tinto) and Yandi (BHP) — in the Pilbara region of Western Australia. The collaboration would involve development of Rio Tinto's Wunbye deposit and processing of BHP's Yandi Lower Channel Iron Deposit ore through Rio Tinto's existing wet processing facilities. First ore is expected in the early 2030s, subject to investment decisions and regulatory approvals. The collaboration builds on a 2023 mining agreement for the Mungadoo Pillar.
- **CBA Aluminium Acquisition (January 2026):** Rio Tinto and Chalco (Aluminum Corporation of China) entered into a binding agreement to jointly acquire Votorantim's 68.596% controlling stake in Companhia Brasileira de Alumínio (CBA). Rio Tinto holds 33% and Chalco 67% of the joint venture. The offer price is R$10.50 per CBA share, representing a premium of approximately 21.2% to the prior trading price.
- **Nemaska Lithium Investment:** Rio Tinto holds a **53.9% stake** in Nemaska Lithium (Government of Québec: 46.1%) and is investing **more than US$300 million in 2026** to expand lithium production in the province of Québec.
- **Asset Divestiture Programme (~US$10 Billion):** CEO Simon Trott is pursuing a comprehensive portfolio reset targeting disposal of non-strategic assets totalling approximately US$10 billion.
- **Boyne Aluminium Smelter (Queensland):** The Queensland and Australian federal governments committed a combined A$2 billion over ten years (to 2040) to secure the long-term future of the Boyne smelter, in which Rio Tinto has a significant interest.
- **Oyu Tolgoi – Copper Growth Engine:** The Mongolian underground mine continues its planned ramp-up and remains the primary driver of group-wide CuEq production growth, despite the brief export disruption in mid-June 2026.
- **Resolution Copper (Arizona, USA):** Resolution Copper secured a historic land exchange with the US federal government, unlocking the next development phase of one of the world's largest undeveloped copper deposits.
- **Three Business Unit Structure:** Rio Tinto operates as three business units — Iron Ore, Copper, and Aluminium & Lithium — reflecting a strategic pivot towards metals critical for the energy transition.
