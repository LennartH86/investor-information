# Rio Tinto (RIO.L)
_KW17 / 2026 | Zeitraum: 19.04.2026 – 26.04.2026_

## Aktuelle Meldungen
- **Q1 2026 Production Results (20.04.2026):** Rio Tinto reported 9% year-over-year copper equivalent production growth for Q1 2026, driven by the Oyu Tolgoi copper mine ramp-up and strong performance from the integrated aluminium business.
- **Pilbara Iron Ore:** Second highest Q1 Pilbara production since 2018, up 13% YoY; shipments were impacted by two cyclones during the quarter, with sales up 2%.
- **Simandou:** The first full SimFer shipment of high-grade Simandou iron ore product was successfully delivered to China, with first sales realised in April 2026.
- **Kitimat Conveyor Commissioning (16.04.2026):** Rio Tinto commissioned a new alumina conveyor at its BC Works smelter in Kitimat as part of a CAN$135 million investment to strengthen operational reliability.
- **Safety Incidents:** Tragic fatalities occurred at the Simandou and Kennecott operations during Q1 2026; both operations were shut down and progressively restarted. A staged restart at the Kennecott underground project commenced from 16 April 2026.
- **Share Price:** Rio Tinto shares reached record highs in April 2026, driven by strong copper demand and operational improvements.

## Management
- **CEO Simon Trott** has been leading the company since late 2025; his strategic programme focuses on making Rio Tinto a "Stronger, Sharper and Simpler" organisation, targeting a 40–50% earnings increase by 2030.
- Trott emphasised safety as the foundation of the business following the fatal incidents at Simandou and Kennecott.
- Rio Tinto announced an executive team change during the period (details on riotinto.com/en/news/releases/2026/rio-tinto-executive-team-change).
- **Q4 2025 Earnings Call:** Trott stated 2026 cash delivery will be materially above the $650 million run rate announced at Capital Markets Day.

## Finanzielles
- **Full-Year 2026 Analyst Estimates:** Average revenue forecast £47.3 billion (+11.0% vs LTM); EBITDA forecast £21.3 billion (+32.1% vs LTM); EPS forecast £6.24 (+38.7% vs FY 2025).
- **Net Debt:** Stood at $14.6 billion as of H1 2025 following the Arcadium Lithium acquisition; asset sales programme targeting ~$10 billion in proceeds to reduce debt (borates and titanium divisions under review).
- **CBA Acquisition Cost:** Rio Tinto's pro-rata share of the Votorantim/CBA acquisition is $297.8 million.
- **Next Earnings Date:** Half-year results scheduled for 29 July 2026.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- **Lithium Expansion:** Fenix 1B and Sal de Vida lithium projects achieved mechanical completion; first production on track for H2 2026. Rio Tinto plans to increase lithium capacity to 200,000 t/yr by 2028 (from ~57,000 t in 2025).
- **Aluminium Footprint Expansion:** Joint acquisition with Chalco of Votorantim's 68.596% stake in Companhia Brasileira de Alumínio (CBA) — 33% Rio Tinto / 67% Chalco JV; deal represents a ~21.2% premium; closing expected Q2 2026.
- **Resolution Copper:** Historic land exchange achieved; project team focused on unlocking next development phase of one of the world's largest untapped copper deposits.
- **Chile Lithium:** Agreements with Codelco and Enami expected to close H1 2026, granting access to two major untapped lithium resources.
- **Asset Sales:** Divestiture of borates and titanium units under consideration to raise ~$10 billion to reduce leverage and sharpen portfolio focus.
- **Simandou:** Ramp-up continuing; first commercial shipment delivered to China in April 2026 — a major milestone for the West African iron ore project.
