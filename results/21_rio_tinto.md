# Rio Tinto (RIO.L)
_KW26 / 2026 | Zeitraum: 20.06.2026 – 27.06.2026_

## Aktuelle Meldungen

- **23. Juni 2026 – Battery-Electric Haul Truck Trial Launched (BHP/Rio Tinto/Caterpillar):** BHP, Rio Tinto and Caterpillar officially launched a trial of two Cat® 793 XE Early Learner battery-electric haul trucks at BHP's Jimblebar iron ore mine in Western Australia's Pilbara region. The industry-first collaboration follows three months of on-site preparation. The current trial phase centres on static charging at designated bays during shift changes. A subsequent phase will evaluate dynamic (in-motion) charging via a specialised energy transfer system engineered to replenish battery capacity while trucks travel along the haul road. The trial directly supports Rio Tinto's commitment to reducing greenhouse gas emissions across large-scale mining operations.
- **12. Juni 2026 – China Baowu / Rio Tinto Steelmaking Emissions Trials:** China Baowu and Rio Tinto successfully completed industrial-scale pelletisation and shaft furnace trials in China using Rio Tinto's Pilbara Blend iron ore. The trials are part of a broader strategic partnership studying ways to decarbonise steelmaking. Results were assessed positively and pave the way for further collaboration on low-emission iron ore processing.
- **9. Juni 2026 – Rio Tinto Canada Fund Expanded to C$13 Million:** Rio Tinto increased its annual community investment in Canada by 30%, bringing total annual funding under the Rio Tinto Canada Fund to C$13 million. The fund supports Indigenous reconciliation, education, healthcare, wellbeing, and environmental protection initiatives across the country.
- **Ongoing – 8 Billionth Tonne Milestone:** Rio Tinto continues to highlight the historic milestone of shipping its 8 billionth tonne of Pilbara iron ore, achieved in May 2026 aboard the vessel Juno Horizon — 60 years after the company's first iron ore export from Western Australia to Japan.
- **Stock Performance (KW26):** Rio Tinto's London-listed shares (RIO.L) touched an all-time high of 83.08 GBP on 2 June 2026, retreating approximately 10% to around 74.58 GBP by late June 2026. The forward dividend yield as of 22 June 2026 stands at approximately 5.08%.

## Management

- **CEO Simon Trott** (in office since 25 August 2025, previously Iron Ore Chief Executive) is executing a strategic reset of Rio Tinto under the motto "stronger, sharper and simpler." He has reorganised the company around three core product groups — Iron Ore, Copper, and Aluminium & Lithium — and is applying stricter capital discipline across the portfolio.
- **Predecessor Jakob Stausholm** stepped down as CEO on 25 August 2025 after announcing his resignation in May 2025. Stausholm led the company through a period of cultural recovery following the 2020 Juukan Gorge scandal and had pursued a diversification strategy. He is currently a fellow at the University of Oxford.
- **Productivity Programme:** Within the first months of Trott's tenure, Rio Tinto delivered US$650 million in annualised productivity benefits and cost savings. Of these, US$370 million had already been realised, with the remainder delivered in Q1 2026.
- **Asset Divestiture Target:** CEO Trott is targeting US$5–10 billion from non-core asset disposals, including titanium dioxide and borates businesses, as well as commercial, partnership, or ownership changes across land, infrastructure, and processing assets.
- **EBITDA Growth Outlook:** Under the refreshed strategy, Rio Tinto projects a potential 40–50% increase in EBITDA by 2030, driven by 20% copper-equivalent production growth, a leaner cost base, and a more focused portfolio.
- **Upcoming Executive Change:** Trudi Charles (former Deputy General Counsel, BP plc) is set to join as Chief Legal Officer on 1 August 2026, succeeding Isabelle Deschamps.

## Finanzielles

- **Q1 2026 Production Results (published 21 April 2026):**
  - Copper-equivalent (CuEq) production grew **9% year-on-year**, driven by the Oyu Tolgoi underground ramp-up and strong aluminium performance.
  - Pilbara iron ore output rose **13% to 78.8 million tonnes** (shipments partially impacted by two cyclones in the quarter).
  - Copper production reached **229,000 tonnes**, up 9% year-on-year.
  - Aluminium production was **835,000 tonnes**, up 1% year-on-year.
  - Alumina production reached **2.0 million tonnes**, up 6% year-on-year.
  - Bauxite production was **13.3 million tonnes**, down 11% year-on-year due to weather disruptions (Cyclone Narelle) at Weipa and Gove.
  - Both **Fenix 1B** (Argentina) and **Sal de Vida** (Argentina) lithium projects achieved mechanical completion on schedule; first production targeted for H2 2026.
- **Full-Year Guidance 2026:** Reaffirmed across all key commodities following the Q1 update.
- **Capital Expenditure 2026:** Approximately **US$11 billion**, weighted towards copper and lithium growth projects.
- **Dividend – Final 2025:** US$2.54 per share (ex-date: 6 March 2026; payment: 16 April 2026).
- **Upcoming Financial Events:** Interim dividend announcement 29 July 2026 (anticipated US$1.48 per share, ex-date 17 August 2026, payment 25 September 2026); Half-year 2026 results scheduled for 13 August 2026; Q2 production report expected 5 August 2026.
- **Forward Dividend Yield:** ~5.08% as of 22 June 2026.

## Strategie & Ausblick

- **Rio Tinto & BHP – Pilbara Iron Ore Collaboration (January 2026):** Rio Tinto and BHP signed non-binding Memoranda of Understanding (MOUs) to explore collaborative mining of up to 200 million tonnes of iron ore at their neighbouring Yandicoogina (Rio Tinto) and Yandi (BHP) operations in Western Australia. The structure covers: (1) development of Rio Tinto's Wunbye deposit; and (2) BHP supplying ore from its Yandi Lower Channel Deposit to Rio Tinto for processing at existing wet plants under agreed commercial terms. First ore subject to a final investment decision is anticipated in the early 2030s. The collaboration leverages existing transport infrastructure, minimising additional capital requirements. It builds on the 2023 Mungadoo Pillar mining agreement between the two companies.
- **CBA Aluminium Acquisition (January 2026):** Rio Tinto and Chalco (Aluminum Corporation of China) agreed to jointly acquire Votorantim's 68.596% controlling stake in Companhia Brasileira de Alumínio (CBA) through a joint venture (Rio Tinto: 33%, Chalco: 67%). The all-cash offer is R$10.50 per CBA share — a 21.2% premium to the 20-day weighted average trading price. The transaction values Votorantim's stake at approximately US$902.6 million; Rio Tinto's share is approximately US$297.8 million. CBA operates a vertically integrated, low-carbon aluminium business in Brazil powered by 100% renewable electricity (1.6 GW portfolio including 21 hydropower plants and wind complexes). Following closing, the joint venture will launch a mandatory tender offer for remaining CBA shares under Brazilian law.
- **Nemaska Lithium (Québec, Canada):** Rio Tinto holds a 53.9% stake (Government of Québec: 46.1%) and is investing more than US$300 million in 2026 to continue developing the lithium hydroxide plant in Bécancour, Québec. Construction reached 60% completion at end of 2025.
- **Oyu Tolgoi (Mongolia):** The underground copper-gold mine continues its planned production ramp-up and is the primary driver of group-wide copper-equivalent production growth.
- **Resolution Copper (Arizona, USA):** Secured a historic federal land exchange, unlocking the development pathway to one of the world's largest undeveloped copper deposits.
- **Boyne Aluminium Smelter (Queensland, Australia):** The Queensland and Australian federal governments committed a combined A$2 billion over ten years (to 2040) to secure the long-term future of the Boyne smelter, in which Rio Tinto holds a significant interest.
- **Decarbonisation Focus:** The battery-electric truck trial with BHP and Caterpillar (Cat® 793 XE) and the Baowu pelletisation/shaft furnace trials in China mark concrete progress on Rio Tinto's twin goals of reducing Scope 1 emissions in operations and enabling lower-emission steelmaking for customers.
