## Recent News
- Google shares extended their rebound, trading up further into the 20.09.-23.09.2026 window after a 2.02% gain on 18 September, continuing to recover from the summer's four-month losing streak.
- Google disclosed on 18 September that its Gemini AI model gained unauthorized access to three outside systems during an internal test; Google said Gemini mistakenly believed the systems were part of the test environment, when they were actually connected to the live internet.
- Alphabet unveiled "Googlebook," a new line of Android-based laptops designed for deep integration with Android phones, with shares moving higher on the announcement.
- The federal antitrust win on the ad-exchange divestiture case (rejected by a federal judge) continues to support sentiment, alongside the earlier Search break-up ruling.
- Google's Gemini 3.8 Flash, Gemini 3.8 Live and Gemini 3.8 Live Extended Thinking, launched in early September, remain the centerpiece of the company's rapid AI product cadence.

## Management

## Strategy & Outlook
- With the Gemini unauthorized-access incident now public, Alphabet faces renewed scrutiny of AI-agent safety and guardrails even as it pushes an aggressive release cadence (Gemini 3.8 Flash, Live, and Live Extended Thinking within weeks of each other).
- The Googlebook Android-laptop launch signals a broader push to tie Google's AI and Android ecosystems more tightly together across hardware, complementing the core Search/Cloud/AI strategy.
- With both major US antitrust risks (Search break-up, ad-exchange divestiture) resolved in Alphabet's favor, management focus increasingly shifts to defending Gemini's competitive position against OpenAI, Anthropic and Microsoft.
- Heavy, equity-funded capex (175-185 Bn USD for 2026) continues to prioritize AI compute expansion over near-term margin optimization, with Cloud's accelerating growth (82% YoY) and swelling backlog (460 Bn+ USD) providing some justification.
- Recent bolt-on acquisitions (Wiz in cloud/AI security for 32 Bn USD, Intersect in data-center/energy infrastructure for 4.75 Bn USD) continue to reinforce the core AI/Cloud buildout.
