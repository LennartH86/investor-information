## Aktuelle Meldungen
- Alphabet reported Q3 2026 results on October 28, 2026: revenue rose 25% YoY to $127.6 billion, marking the 13th consecutive quarter of double-digit revenue growth; Google Cloud revenue more than doubled (+103%) to $30.7 billion, with cloud operating income climbing from $3.6bn to $11.1bn.
- Net income fell 25% YoY to $26.2 billion and EPS came in at $2.12 (well below consensus of ~$3.00), largely due to an $8.7 billion loss on equity investments; capex was $51.4 billion (+114% YoY) and free cash flow was negative $9.1 billion for the quarter.
- Shares initially rose 1.6% to $321.89 at the regular close on earnings day but then fell as much as 8.9% in after-hours trading to around $293.25 as investors focused on the profit miss and investment losses rather than the revenue beat.
- Google Services revenue rose 11% to $96.3 billion, with Search & other growing 12% and YouTube ad revenue up to $11.2 billion (from $10.26bn a year earlier).
- A dividend on Alphabet's mandatory convertible preferred stock is payable November 15, 2026, to holders of record as of November 1, 2026 (a technical/preferred-stock item, not relevant to common "A" shares).
- No new company-specific news was found specifically dated after the Q3 release through November 1; the market continues to digest the earnings reaction.

## Management
- CEO Sundar Pichai has continued to publicly defend Alphabet's AI investment strategy; in a Bloomberg interview earlier in 2026 he pushed back on concerns that AI could make a large share of the roughly 180,000-person workforce redundant, and he has emphasized confidence in Gemini model development (with Gemini 4 training reportedly underway as of the Q2 call).
- At Google I/O 2026 and in subsequent commentary, Pichai framed Alphabet's product direction around "agentic" AI experiences across Search, Workspace and Cloud.
- No new management changes or controversies specific to the November 2026 window were identified; prior-period items (Project Nimbus scrutiny, the Kaplan Fox securities class action with a December 1, 2026 lead-plaintiff deadline) remain open but without new developments this week.

## Finanzielles
- Q3 2026 (reported Oct 28, 2026): revenue $127.6bn (+25% YoY); operating income +30% YoY; net income $26.2bn (−25% YoY due to the $8.7bn equity-investment loss); diluted EPS $2.12 vs. ~$3.00 consensus.
- Google Cloud: revenue $30.7bn (+103% YoY), cloud operating income $11.1bn (up from $3.6bn a year earlier) — the standout growth driver this quarter.
- Capex was $51.4bn in Q3 alone (+114% YoY); full-year 2026 capex guidance stands at roughly $195–205 billion, with the CFO indicating 2027 capex will increase significantly further versus 2026.
- Free cash flow was negative $9.1 billion in Q3, reflecting the scale of AI infrastructure investment outpacing operating cash generation this quarter.
- For this portfolio position, the unrealized gain narrowed to +12.27% (from +28.43% in the prior update) as the USD share price fell sharply post-earnings; a dividend of 16.46 EUR has been credited to date.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- Alphabet remains committed to aggressive AI infrastructure buildout (TPUs, data centers, successive Gemini model generations), with capex guided to rise again in 2027 beyond the already-elevated 2026 level ($195–205bn) — a strategy the market is now scrutinizing more closely given the Q3 profit miss and negative free cash flow.
- Google Cloud's rapid growth (+103% YoY) and the integrated Wiz acquisition (~$32bn, closed March 2026) continue to strengthen Alphabet's position in enterprise cloud and security, and remain the clearest bright spot supporting the long-term AI monetization thesis.
- The $8.7 billion equity-investment loss (likely related to stakes in AI-related ventures such as Anthropic) is a reminder of balance-sheet volatility tied to Alphabet's venture-style investments; this is a factor to monitor going forward given Alphabet's deep financial ties to Anthropic (as investor and chip supplier).
- Items to watch: market and analyst reaction to the Q3 earnings miss over the coming weeks, any updated 2027 capex guidance, further equity-investment mark-to-market swings, progress of the Kaplan Fox class action (lead-plaintiff deadline December 1, 2026), and whether Google Cloud's growth trajectory continues to offset rising AI infrastructure costs.
