- CEO Sundar Pichai continued to make public appearances defending Alphabet's AI investment strategy and vision; a widely covered interview marking his 10-year tenure as Google CEO (with John Collison and Elad Gil) discussed managing the roughly $180bn+ capex budget, the industry's "supply crunch," and why Google was historically cautious about releasing transformer-based products.
- Pichai has also framed Alphabet's product roadmap around "agentic" AI across Search, Workspace and Cloud, including the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform unveiled at a recent developer event, positioning Alphabet to compete more directly for enterprise AI-agent workloads.
- No new management changes or controversies specific to the 29.10.–05.11.2026 window were identified; the Kaplan Fox securities class action (lead-plaintiff deadline December 1, 2026) remains open but without new developments this week.

## Finanzielles
- Q3 2026 (reported Oct 28, 2026) remains the latest reported quarter: revenue $127.6bn (+25% YoY), operating income $40.6bn (+30% YoY, 31.8% operating margin), net income $26.2bn (−25% YoY due to the $8.7bn equity-investment loss), diluted EPS $2.12 vs. ~$3.00 consensus.
- Google Cloud revenue reached $30.7bn (+103% YoY), with cloud operating income rising to $11.1bn from $3.6bn a year earlier — the standout growth driver.
- Q3 capex was $51.4bn (+114% YoY); full-year 2026 capex guidance stands at roughly $195–205bn, with 2027 capex guided to rise "significantly" further, a point markets continue to scrutinize given the negative $9.1bn free cash flow in Q3.
- Berkshire Hathaway reportedly increased its Alphabet stake to roughly $37 billion, a notable vote of confidence from a well-known value-oriented investor.
- For this portfolio position, the unrealized gain improved to +15.19% (from +12.27% in the prior update) as the share price recovered part of the post-earnings decline; cumulative dividends received remain unchanged at 16.46 EUR.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- Alphabet continues its aggressive AI infrastructure buildout (TPUs, data centers, successive Gemini model generations) and is positioning the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform to capture enterprise "agentic AI" demand alongside Google Cloud's rapid growth.
- The ~$32bn Wiz acquisition (closed around March 2026) and the pending ~$4.75bn Intersect acquisition (data center and energy infrastructure, announced December 2025, expected to close in H1 2026) both continue to build out Alphabet's cloud, security and data-center capacity.
- A federal judge declined the Department of Justice's request to force a sale of Google's AdX ad exchange, removing one near-term regulatory overhang, though broader antitrust scrutiny of Alphabet's ad-tech and search businesses persists.
- Items to watch going forward: the trajectory of 2027 capex guidance and its impact on free cash flow, further equity-investment mark-to-market volatility (notably Alphabet's financial ties to Anthropic), progress of the Kaplan Fox class action (lead-plaintiff deadline December 1, 2026), and whether Google Cloud's growth continues to offset elevated AI infrastructure spending.
