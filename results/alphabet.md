## Aktuelle Meldungen
- Google launched Gemini 3.8 Flash in early September, its third Flash-tier model in six weeks, alongside a new cybersecurity-focused model aimed at government and enterprise customers; the release is described as a code-focused evolution of the Gemini family.
- A federal judge rejected the Department of Justice's push to force Google to sell its ad exchange, a second major antitrust win in 2026 after the earlier ruling that spared the company from a Search-business break-up.
- Berkshire Hathaway has expanded its Alphabet stake to roughly 37 Bn USD, following an initial 10 Bn USD private placement about 15 months ago and a further 17 Bn USD purchase in Q2 2026.
- Waymo, Alphabet's autonomous-driving unit, announced plans to launch its first fully autonomous robotaxi service in Europe, targeting commercial ride-hailing in Munich by the end of 2027.
- Alphabet shares entered September still recovering from a four-month losing streak (the longest since 2015), with the antitrust rulings and continued AI momentum supporting sentiment.

## Management
- CEO Sundar Pichai continues to frame Alphabet's AI investment and full-stack approach as redefining "every part of the business," a message reiterated since the Q2 2026 earnings call.
- Pichai was featured in TIME's 2026 "Most Influential Companies" collection and profiled by Fast Company for his strategy to make Gemini "the only AI that matters," reflecting an aggressive push to embed Gemini across Google's products.
- In a 2026 interview with John and Elad Gil, Pichai discussed managing a roughly 180 Bn USD investment program and described 2026 as "the year of the supply crunch," citing memory and power constraints as key bottlenecks to AI scaling.
- At Google I/O 2026, Pichai characterized the year as one of "relentless shipping," noting the industry has entered a phase where customers expect to see tangible value from AI products in daily use.
- Management continues to defend the scale of AI capex publicly even as free cash flow remains under pressure from the infrastructure build-out.

## Finanzielles
- Q2 2026 revenue: 119,8 Mrd. USD, +24% year over year (23% constant currency), the 12th consecutive quarter of double-digit growth; Google Cloud revenue reached 24,8 Mrd. USD (+82%).
- Q2 2026 net income available to common stockholders rose 298% and EPS increased 294% to 9,11 USD, reflecting both operating leverage and one-off items versus the prior-year quarter.
- Q1 2026 revenue was 109,9 Mrd. USD (+22%), with net income up 81% and EPS of 5,11 USD; Google Cloud revenue grew 63% to 20,0 Mrd. USD.
- Google Services revenue in Q2 was 94,5 Mrd. USD (+15%), with Search & other +17%, subscriptions/platforms/devices +15% and YouTube ads +13%.
- Google Cloud's backlog reached over 460 Mrd. USD as of Q1 2026, underscoring strong forward demand for AI infrastructure and enterprise services.
- Alphabet remains among the world's largest listed companies by market capitalisation, having briefly touched 4 Trillion USD in January 2026 on the Apple-Siri/Gemini deal news.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- 2026 capital expenditure guidance stands at up to 205 Mrd. USD, reflecting the ongoing AI infrastructure build-out across data centers and compute.
- Alphabet raised roughly 85 Mrd. USD in equity offerings in mid-2026 to fund AI compute infrastructure, including Berkshire Hathaway's initial 10 Bn USD private placement (since expanded to ~37 Bn USD in total holdings) and a 30 Bn USD underwritten offering.
- M&A activity remains elevated in 2026, including the completed Wiz acquisition (cloud/AI security, closed March 2026 at 29,5 Bn USD), the Intersect data-center and energy infrastructure deal (completed at roughly 5,9 Bn USD), and the smaller Producer acquisition (generative AI music, February 2026).
- Two consecutive favourable antitrust rulings (no Search break-up; no forced ad-exchange divestiture) materially reduce regulatory tail risk for the core advertising business going forward.
- Waymo's planned expansion into Europe (Munich robotaxi service targeted for end of 2027) signals continued diversification of Alphabet's long-term growth bets beyond core Search and Cloud.
- Rapid, closely spaced model releases (Gemini 3.8 Flash following prior Flash iterations within six weeks) signal an aggressive cadence intended to defend Google's position against Anthropic, OpenAI and Microsoft in enterprise and consumer AI.
