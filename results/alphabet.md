- In earlier 2026 remarks that remain the most recent substantive CEO commentary, Pichai discussed managing a roughly 180 Bn USD AI investment program and described 2026 as "the year of the supply crunch," citing memory and power constraints as key bottlenecks.
- Pichai has also noted that the broader AI shift has opened new opportunities for Alphabet to deploy capital into startups, reflecting a broader capital-allocation stance beyond pure infrastructure spend.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- The newly announced ~15 Bn USD German AI-infrastructure investment extends Alphabet's European data-center buildout, adding to prior multi-billion-dollar commitments across the region and reinforcing the long-term AI compute strategy.
- Continued heavy capex and equity-funded infrastructure investment (including Berkshire Hathaway's expanded ~37 Bn USD stake) signal that Alphabet is prioritizing AI compute capacity expansion over near-term margin optimization.
- With both major antitrust risks (Search break-up, ad-exchange divestiture) now resolved in Alphabet's favor, management attention can increasingly shift toward competitive defense of Gemini against OpenAI, Anthropic and Microsoft.
- Waymo's planned autonomous robotaxi launch in Munich, targeted for end of 2027, remains on track as a longer-term diversification bet beyond core Search and Cloud.
- Rapid, closely spaced Gemini model releases (3.8 Flash following prior iterations within weeks) continue to signal an aggressive product cadence intended to defend Google's enterprise and consumer AI position.
