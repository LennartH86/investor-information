## Recent News
- Google launched Gemini 3.8 Flash on 2 September 2026 (its third Flash model in six weeks), alongside a new cybersecurity-focused model aimed at government and enterprise customers, plus Gemini 3.8 Live and Gemini 3.8 Live Extended Thinking for real-time voice and complex reasoning use cases.
- Alphabet stock continued its rebound from the summer's AI-race setbacks (talent departures, a DeepMind restructuring) and rose 2.02% on 18 September 2026, with Wall Street analysts hiking price targets on renewed AI momentum.
- A federal judge rejected the Justice Department's push to force Google to divest its ad exchange, a second major antitrust victory after the earlier Search break-up ruling.
- Alphabet announced a planned ~13 Bn EUR (~15.1 Bn USD) AI infrastructure investment in Finland for 2027-2028, its largest in Europe, tied to roughly 7,000 jobs.
- Berkshire Hathaway CEO Greg Abel reiterated confidence in Alphabet's AI position following Berkshire's earlier multi-billion-dollar stake increase (including a $10 Bn private placement).

- At Google I/O 2026, Pichai said the company is "now in the part of the AI cycle where people want to see the value in the products they use every day," emphasizing product delivery over pure model races.

## Strategy & Outlook
- The newly announced ~13 Bn EUR Finnish AI-infrastructure investment (2027-2028) extends Alphabet's European data-center buildout and signals continued long-term commitment to AI compute capacity.
- With both major antitrust risks (Search break-up, ad-exchange divestiture) now resolved in Alphabet's favor, management attention can increasingly shift toward competitive defense of Gemini against OpenAI, Anthropic and Microsoft.
- The rapid Gemini release cadence (3.8 Flash, 3.8 Live, 3.8 Live Extended Thinking within weeks) underscores an aggressive product strategy aimed at rebuilding momentum after a difficult summer that included talent losses and a DeepMind restructuring.
- Heavy, equity-funded capex (175-185 Bn USD for 2026) continues to prioritize AI compute expansion over near-term margin optimization, with Berkshire Hathaway's stake increase seen as a vote of confidence in this strategy.
- Recent bolt-on acquisitions (Wiz in cloud/AI security for 32 Bn USD, Intersect in data-center/energy infrastructure for 4.75 Bn USD) continue to reinforce the core AI/Cloud buildout.
