## Aktuelle Meldungen
- Google launched Gemini 3.8 Flash on 01.09.2026, its third Flash-tier model release in six weeks, alongside a new cybersecurity-focused model aimed at government and enterprise customers.
- Alphabet shares snapped a four-month losing streak (the longest since 2015) and moved higher in early September on the Gemini 3.8 Flash news and broader AI momentum.
- The presiding judge in Alphabet's search antitrust case found Google acted as an illegal monopoly in online search, but declined to order a break-up of the company, removing a major structural overhang for the stock.
- Alphabet confirmed a quarterly cash dividend of 0,22 USD per share with an ex-date of 04.09.2026.
- No dedicated Alphabet corporate press release was issued in the reporting window 28.08.2026 – 04.09.2026 beyond the Gemini 3.8 Flash announcement; the most recent full earnings release remains the July Q2 2026 report.

## Management
- CEO Sundar Pichai used the Q2 2026 earnings call to frame AI investment and Alphabet's full-stack approach as redefining "every part of the business."
- Pichai was featured in TIME's 2026 "Most Influential Companies" collection, crediting him with pushing Google to the front of the AI race; the accompanying interview was conducted at Google's Mountain View headquarters.
- Fast Company profiled Pichai's strategy to make Gemini "the only AI that matters," describing an aggressive push to embed Gemini across Google's product surface.
- In an earlier 2026 interview, Pichai described 2026 as "the year of the supply crunch," citing memory and power constraints as key bottlenecks to AI scaling.
- Pichai has continued to defend the scale of AI capex publicly even as free cash flow has come under pressure.

## Finanzielles
- Q2 2026 revenue: 119,8 Mrd. USD, +24% year over year (23% constant currency), the 12th consecutive quarter of double-digit growth.
- Google Services revenue: 94,5 Mrd. USD (+15%), with Search & other +17%, subscriptions/platforms/devices +15% and YouTube ads +13%.
- Google Cloud revenue: 24,8 Mrd. USD, +82%, continuing a meaningful acceleration versus Q1's +63%.
- Q1 2026 net income grew 81% and EPS rose 82% to 5,11 USD; Google Cloud's backlog nearly doubled quarter over quarter to over 460 Mrd. USD.
- Operating income in Q2 was up 30% year over year with operating margin up roughly 2 percentage points.
- Alphabet remains among the world's largest listed companies by market capitalisation, having briefly touched a 4 Trillion USD valuation in January 2026 on the Apple-Siri/Gemini deal news.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- 2026 capital expenditure guidance stands at up to 205 Mrd. USD, reflecting the ongoing AI infrastructure build-out across data centers and compute.
- Alphabet raised 80 Mrd. USD in equity offerings in June 2026 to fund AI compute infrastructure, including a 10 Mrd. USD private placement with Berkshire Hathaway.
- M&A activity remains elevated in 2026, including the completed Wiz acquisition (cloud/AI security, closed March 2026), the pending Intersect data-center and energy infrastructure deal (~4,75 Mrd. USD), and the smaller Producer acquisition (generative AI music, February 2026).
- The favourable antitrust ruling (no forced break-up) reduces regulatory tail risk for the core Search and advertising business going forward.
- Rapid, closely spaced model releases (Gemini 3.8 Flash following prior Flash iterations within six weeks) signal an aggressive cadence intended to defend Google's position against Anthropic, OpenAI and Microsoft in enterprise and consumer AI.
