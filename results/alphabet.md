## Strategie & Ausblick
- The newly announced ~15 Bn USD European AI-infrastructure investment extends Alphabet's data-center buildout, reinforcing the long-term AI compute strategy.
- Continued heavy capex and equity-funded infrastructure investment (including Berkshire Hathaway's stake and public endorsement) signal that Alphabet is prioritizing AI compute capacity expansion over near-term margin optimization.
- With both major antitrust risks (Search break-up, ad-exchange divestiture) now resolved in Alphabet's favor, management attention can increasingly shift toward competitive defense of Gemini against OpenAI, Anthropic and Microsoft.
- Recent acquisitions in 2026 (Wiz in cloud/AI security, Intersect in data-center/energy infrastructure, Producer in generative AI music) illustrate a strategy of bolt-on deals supporting the core AI/Cloud buildout.
- Rapid, closely spaced Gemini model releases (3.8 Flash following prior iterations within weeks) continue to signal an aggressive product cadence intended to defend Google's enterprise and consumer AI position.
