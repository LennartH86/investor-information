## Strategy & Outlook
- Continued heavy investment in AI infrastructure (TPUs, data centers, Gemini models) remains Alphabet's core 2026 strategic focus, funded by record capex plus new debt/equity issuance (including an $84.75bn capital raise and a multi-currency bond with a rare 100-year "century bond" tranche).
- The ~$32bn acquisition of cybersecurity firm Wiz has closed, strengthening Google Cloud's enterprise security offering.
- Alphabet is supplying AI chips (reportedly up to a million specialized units) to Anthropic, and has backed a large Waymo funding round (~$16bn) to expand autonomous-driving investment.
- Google signed two 15-year agreements with TotalEnergies for roughly 1 gigawatt of combined solar capacity in Texas to help power data centers.
- Key risk flagged by analysts: sustaining premium Search growth against tougher comparisons while elevated capex intensity weighs on free cash flow; Q3 earnings (~October 27) and 2027 capex guidance are the next major catalysts.
