## Finanzielles
- Q3 2026 (reported 28 October 2026) remains the most recently reported quarter: revenue of $127.6 bn (+25% YoY, 13th consecutive quarter of double-digit growth); operating income +30% YoY at a 31.8% operating margin; net income fell to $26.17 bn (vs. $34.98 bn a year earlier), partly due to an $8.7 bn valuation loss on equity investments; diluted EPS of $2.12 vs. $2.87 a year earlier, below analyst expectations of roughly $3.01.
- Google Cloud revenue more than doubled to $30.71 bn (vs. $15.16 bn a year earlier), with operating income rising to $11.12 bn (vs. $3.59 bn); the Cloud backlog stands at roughly $561 bn. Advertising's share of total revenue fell to 64.3% from 72.5% a year earlier as Cloud and AI-driven revenue grew faster.
- Google Services grew 11% to $96.30 bn; Search & other grew 12% to $63.35 bn and YouTube ads grew 9% to $11.21 bn, though both reportedly came in below some analyst expectations.
- Q3 capex was $51.36 bn (vs. $23.95 bn a year earlier); free cash flow was negative at approximately -$9.07 bn (vs. +$24.55 bn a year earlier). Full-year 2026 capex guidance is cited as roughly $175–185 bn, with management confirming a further significant increase for 2027.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- Alphabet continues to push AI product rollout across its stack, including the new "Gemini for desktop" app, continued iteration on Gemini model generations, and the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform for enterprise "agentic AI," alongside its proprietary TPU chip lineup (TPU 8i Max and 8tx systems announced 9 November 2026).
- The roughly $32 bn Wiz acquisition (unconditional EU antitrust clearance confirmed, called the largest acquisition in Google's 28-year history by one source) and the roughly $4.8 bn Intersect acquisition (data center and energy infrastructure) remain the dominant 2026 M&A items; H1 2026 acquisition spending was reported at roughly $33.7 bn, though the breakdown by deal is not fully disclosed.
- Management has flagged that 2027 capital expenditures will "increase significantly" beyond the already elevated 2026 level (~$175–185 bn guided), to be detailed further on the Q4 2026 earnings call.
- Points to watch: the trajectory of Search and advertising revenue growth; the impact of further-rising 2027 capex on free cash flow; adoption and competitiveness of new TPU systems and newer Gemini models (including unverified "Gemini 4 Hydrogen" claims); progress of ongoing antitrust and privacy proceedings; and possible further valuation swings on Alphabet's equity holdings (e.g., Anthropic).
