## Management
- CEO Sundar Pichai continued his string of 2026 public appearances (Cheeky Pint podcast with John Collison and Elad Gil marking his 10-year CEO anniversary, plus prior interviews including The Verge's "Decoder" and 60 Minutes), in which he has repeatedly addressed the roughly 180 bn USD capex budget and a compute "supply crunch."
- Pichai continues to defend heavy AI investment against "bubble" criticism from analysts and media, positioning Google around agentic AI across Search, Workspace and Cloud, while also acknowledging declining referral traffic for publishers as AI-generated Search answers expand ("Google Zero").
- The departure of long-time AI researcher Jeff Dean after 27 years, along with other reported personnel exits, continues to be read by the market as a possible signal of intensifying competition for AI talent.

## Finanzielles
- Q3 2026 (reported 28 October 2026) remains the most recently reported quarter: revenue of $127.6 bn (+25% YoY, the 13th consecutive quarter of double-digit growth); operating income +30% YoY at a 31.8% operating margin; net income declined to $26.17 bn (vs. $34.98 bn a year earlier), partly due to an $8.7 bn valuation loss on equity investments; diluted EPS of $2.12 versus $2.87 a year earlier and well below analyst expectations of roughly $3.01.
- Google Cloud revenue reached $30.71 bn (more than doubling from $15.16 bn a year earlier); the Cloud backlog stands at roughly $561 bn.
- Google Services grew 11% to $96.30 bn; "Search & other" grew 12% to $63.35 bn but reportedly missed expectations across several sources, as did YouTube advertising revenue.
- Q3 capex was $51.36 bn (vs. $23.95 bn a year earlier); full-year 2026 capex guidance is cited inconsistently across sources as roughly $175–185 bn to $195–205 bn, with a further significant increase flagged for 2027. Free cash flow was negative in Q3 at approximately -$9.07 bn (vs. +$24.55 bn a year earlier).

## Strategie & Ausblick
- Alphabet continues to expand its proprietary AI chip lineup (new TPU systems 8i Max and 8tx, including "confidential inference" capability), alongside the continued rollout of successive Gemini model generations, and is positioning Google Cloud and the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform for growth in enterprise "agentic AI."
- The roughly $32 bn Wiz acquisition (reported as closed in 2026, with unconditional EU antitrust clearance) is regarded as one of the largest acquisitions in the company's history; the roughly $4.8 bn Intersect acquisition (data center and energy infrastructure) further expands Alphabet's cloud/data-center capacity. H1 2026 acquisition spending (net cash outflow) was reported at roughly $33.7 bn. Separately, a reported Anthropic investment of up to $40 bn (an initial $10 bn tranche at a $350 bn valuation, with further tranches tied to milestones) was cited by secondary sources, though its final status could not be confirmed.
- Google sold a minority stake in its Fiber business and now holds only a minority interest in the newly formed entity; the company has also placed a multi-currency bond offering (including a 100-year "century bond") to help fund its investment push.
- Points to watch: the trajectory of Search and advertising revenue following recent misses versus expectations; the impact of elevated and inconsistently guided 2026/2027 capex on free cash flow; competitiveness of the new TPU systems and newer Gemini models; progress of ongoing antitrust and privacy proceedings; the final status and structure of the reported Anthropic investment; and possible further valuation swings on equity holdings.
