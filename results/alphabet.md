## Management
- CEO Sundar Pichai used the Q2 2026 earnings call to frame AI investment and Alphabet's full-stack approach as redefining "every part of the business".
- Pichai described 2026 as "the year of the supply crunch" in an interview with John and Elad Gil (April 2026), while defending a capex budget he put at roughly 180 Mrd. USD.
- Pichai has repeatedly said Alphabet will keep expanding its engineering headcount even as AI capability grows, stressing that human talent remains central.
- TIME named Alphabet to its TIME100 Most Influential Companies 2026 list, crediting Pichai with pushing Google to the front of the AI race.
- Pichai presented the headline announcements at Google Cloud Next 2026.

## Finanzielles
- Q2 2026 revenue: 119,8 Mrd. USD, +24% year over year, the 12th consecutive quarter of double-digit growth and ahead of the ~116,93 Mrd. USD consensus.
- Google Services revenue: 94,5 Mrd. USD (+15%), with Search & other +17%, subscriptions/platforms/devices +15% and YouTube ads +13%.
- Google Cloud revenue: 24,8 Mrd. USD, +82%, driven by enterprise AI infrastructure and AI solutions.
- Operating income +30%; operating margin up 2 percentage points to 34%. EPS +294% to 9,11 USD, net income to common stockholders +298%.
- Q2 free cash flow turned negative at –5,86 Mrd. USD on the capex ramp; long-term debt rose from 46,5 Mrd. USD to 98,2 Mrd. USD and the share buyback programme was suspended.
- Alphabet is the third-largest listed company in the world by market capitalisation (as of July 2026); the shares were up about 16% year to date in 2026.
- GOOGL stock fell on the Q2 print despite the revenue beat, as investors reacted to the raised 2026 capex outlook.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- 2026 capital expenditure guidance was lifted at the Q2 call to as much as 205 Mrd. USD, above the 175–185 Mrd. USD range previously indicated.
- Alphabet announced equity offerings totalling 80 Mrd. USD in June 2026 to fund AI compute infrastructure, including a 10 Mrd. USD investment agreement with Berkshire Hathaway.
- M&A spending accelerated dramatically: roughly 33,7 Mrd. USD on acquisitions net of cash in H1 2026 versus about 350 Mio. USD in H1 2025 – close to a hundredfold increase.
- Key deals closed in March 2026: Wiz for 29,5 Mrd. USD (folded into Google Cloud) and Intersect for 5,9 Mrd. USD.
- AI adoption metrics cited for Q2: nearly 90% of the Fortune 100 use Gemini Enterprise, Gemini models process 22 Mrd. API tokens per minute, and the Gemini app has 950 Mio. monthly active users.
- Aggressive AI pricing suggests a market-share-first strategy in enterprise AI, which may weigh on cloud margins even as revenue accelerates.
