## Aktuelle Meldungen
- Iberdrola shares traded around EUR 20.25–20.35 in the 21–28 September window, gaining roughly 0.65% on 24 September as the IBEX 35 also advanced; the stock remains up about 9% year-to-date ("sube un 9% en el año", elEconomista, 13 September 2026).
- On 24 September 2026, the Spanish government published the new framework ("marco regulatorio") for centralized capacity auctions ("subastas de capacidad") in the Boletín Oficial del Estado (BOE), a tailwind for Spanish utilities including Iberdrola.
- Iberdrola confirmed roughly €30m in new contracts ("nuevos contratos") with Ingeteam and Ormazabal for battery-storage and grid equipment (announced 9 September 2026).
- The company opened a new customer service and sales point ("punto de atención y venta") in Granada, Spain, on 20 September 2026, part of its ongoing retail-network expansion.
- 8 September 2026 marked the 125th anniversary ("125 años") of Iberdrola's founding, prominently featured across its press room and corporate site.
- MarketScreener records an "Outperform" consensus from 23 analysts, though the average price target sits only about 1.18% above the current share price, signaling limited near-term upside after the 2026 rally.

## Management
- Executive Chairman Ignacio Sánchez Galán remains the public face of group strategy; no new interviews specifically fell within the 21–28 September window, but his earlier 2026 appearances (CERAWeek, Davos, ElEconomista) continue to frame coverage of the company.
- Galán argues Iberdrola's business model is "part of the solution to energy crises" ("parte de la solución") and reiterates that Europe remains on track to meet its 2030 decarbonization targets.
- Day-to-day CEO responsibilities rest with Pedro Azagra (in role since 2025, previously head of US subsidiary Avangrid), while Galán holds the Executive Chairman title and continues to frame energy security as inseparable from strategic autonomy.
- Management continues to describe Iberdrola as entering "a period of unprecedented growth," aiming to become the world's leading electricity company by market capitalization while maintaining a rising-dividend policy ("política de dividendo creciente").

## Finanzielles
- H1 2026: reported net profit +22% YoY to €4.34bn (~US$4.9bn); adjusted net profit +8% to €3.57bn; adjusted EBITDA above €8bn (+7%); electricity distributed +6.3% YoY.
- Q1 2026: adjusted net profit +11.4% YoY to €1,865m; FY2026 guidance raised to >8% adjusted net profit growth, led by the Networks division.
- A capital increase ("ampliación de capital") was carried out in July 2026 to help fund growth investments, including the Caruna acquisition.
- Adjusted net debt stands at approximately €50.3bn following completion of the Mexico thermal-generation divestment (net capital gain of €953m) and the Neoenergia (Brazil) minority buy-out (100% ownership since mid-May 2026, ~€980m / BRL 5,826m).
- FY2026 guidance confirmed at >8% adjusted net profit growth; consensus points to full-year net profit near €6.8bn, versus roughly €6.3bn for FY2025.
- Next reporting date: nine-month 2026 results ("resultados de los nueve meses") on 21 October 2026.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- Iberdrola agreed to acquire an 80% stake in Caruna Networks, Finland's largest electricity distributor (~1.5 million customers), for roughly €2bn equity value (~€5bn enterprise value); completion is expected in Q1 2027 and the deal is seen as accretive from year one, with ~7% annual net income growth expected from the asset.
- The strategic plan channels about 60% of planned investments into networks (roughly two-thirds distribution, one-third transmission), with more than 70% of grid capex directed to UK, US and Brazil.
- Iberdrola targets 15.5 GW of new renewable capacity additions between 2025 and 2030, having invested €2.2bn in renewables in H1 2026 alone.
- On 24 September 2026, Spain published its new centralized capacity-auction framework in the BOE, expected to support longer-term remuneration visibility for Spanish generation assets.
- The Neoenergia (Brazil) buy-out, completed mid-May 2026, gives Iberdrola 100% ownership and long-dated visibility from concessions extending more than 30 years.
- Key upcoming catalyst: nine-month 2026 results on 21 October 2026, which should give further visibility on FY2026 guidance and the Caruna integration timeline.
