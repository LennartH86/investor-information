## Management
- Executive Chairman **Ignacio Sánchez Galán** continues to act as the group's public face on strategy; recent 2026 public appearances include interviews at Davos (January, with Expansión, CNBC and the Wall Street Journal), CERAWeek (March, with elEconomista), and Abu Dhabi Sustainability Week, consistently emphasizing grid expansion, electrification demand, and ambitions to become the world's largest electricity company.
- **Pedro Azagra**, CEO since 2025 (previously head of US subsidiary Avangrid), continues to run day-to-day operations; no new governance or leadership changes were identified for this reporting period.
- Iberdrola's own materials are not fully consistent on titles: some pages describe Galán as "Executive Chairman and CEO," while others clearly assign the operational CEO role to Azagra.

## Financials
- Nine-month 2026 results (confirmed, published 21.10.2026): net profit of EUR 5.816 billion (+9.6–10% year-on-year), supported by record investments of EUR 10.087 billion (+13% y/y), mainly in Networks across all geographies; full-year 2026 guidance was raised, with management targeting a record year.
- Interim dividend of 0.275 EUR gross per share confirmed alongside the nine-month results.
- Second 2026 tranche of the optional scrip-dividend program ("Iberdrola Flexible Remuneration") confirmed, with an exercise window running from 15.12.2026 to 03.02.2027.
- Prior 2026 quarters for context: Q1 adjusted net profit +11% to EUR 1.865 billion (guidance raised to >8% growth); H1 net profit +22% to EUR 4.34 billion, driven by network investments in the UK, US and Brazil.
- No reliable, current P/E or P/B multiples were found in this research round.

## Strategy & Outlook
- Continued capital rotation toward regulated grid assets in stable jurisdictions, reinforced by the plan to double Spanish grid investment to EUR 6 billion by 2030, on top of the roughly 75% of nine-month 2026 capex already directed to the UK, US and Brazil.
- Pending acquisition of **Caruna**, Finland's largest electricity distributor, for approximately EUR 5 billion including debt (~80% stake), expected to close in Q1 2027 subject to regulatory approval; projected to add roughly 7% p.a. to earnings/asset growth.
- Acquisition of Spanish retailer **Contigo Energía**'s business (announced 28.09.2026) added around 100,000 electricity customers in Spain.
- Structural growth drivers remain electrification (data centers/AI demand, EVs, heat pumps) and continued grid build-out, themes Galán has repeatedly stressed at international forums (Davos, CERAWeek, Abu Dhabi Sustainability Week).
- With the share price near multi-year highs, much of the near-term re-rating already appears to have played out; the medium- to long-term investment case remains intact, supporting a **Hold** stance for now.
