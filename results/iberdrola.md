## Management
- CEO Pedro Azagra (in office since 2025) runs day-to-day operations and execution of the grid and growth strategy in the UK, US and Brazil.
- Executive Chairman Ignacio Galán, in a CERAWeek 2026 interview with ElEconomista, said the current geopolitical and energy crisis has had only a limited impact on Iberdrola, highlighted the company's ability to offer competitive long-term fixed prices, and stressed the need for grid infrastructure investment to advance Europe's electrification and strategic autonomy; he also underlined Spain's role in European competitiveness.
- Galán described Iberdrola as entering "a period of unprecedented growth," reiterating the ambition to become the world's leading electricity company by market capitalization while maintaining a rising-dividend policy and a solid balance sheet.
- In a January 2026 CNBC interview, Galán linked energy security directly to national security.

## Financials
- Q1 2026: adjusted net profit +11.4% YoY to 1,865 m EUR; adjusted EBITDA +2.4% to 4.1 bn EUR; networks EBITDA +9%, driven by the UK and US; renewable capacity +3,300 MW over the past year.
- H1 2026: reported net profit +22% to 4.34 bn EUR; adjusted net profit +8% to 3.57 bn EUR; adjusted EBITDA +7% to over 8 bn EUR.
- Growth drivers: a larger regulated asset base (RAB) across all core markets, strong performance in the UK, US and Brazil, and high hydro output in Spain.
- H1 2026 capex rose 25% to 7 bn EUR, with over 70% allocated to the UK, US and Brazil; grid investment alone grew 42% to nearly 4.4 bn EUR.
- Per MarketBeat (10 September 2026): trailing EPS ~4.72 USD, net income ~7.11 bn USD, net margin 15.59%, revenue ~51.52 bn USD, return on equity 9.88%.
- FY2026 guidance confirmed: adjusted net profit growth of more than 8%.

## Strategy & Outlook
- Acquisition of Caruna Group (Finland): Iberdrola is buying an 80% stake in Finland's largest electricity distribution operator for ~2 bn EUR equity value (~5 bn EUR enterprise value), adding ~2.5 bn EUR to the regulated asset base and 21% of the Finnish distribution market; closing expected Q1 2027, financed partly via proceeds from the divestment of thermal generation assets in Mexico.
- Full acquisition of Neoenergia (Brazil) completed in mid-May 2026, after buying the remaining 16.2% stake for roughly 980 m EUR (BRL 5,826 m).
- Iberdrola raised its 2025–2030 new-capacity target to 15.5 GW (+63% vs. the original 2025–2028 plan), with continued focus on regulated networks and renewables.
- 2.2 bn EUR was invested in renewable projects in H1 2026; strategic focus remains on expanding grid businesses in the US, UK, Brazil and, going forward, Finland.
- Next key date: nine-month 2026 results on 21 October 2026.
