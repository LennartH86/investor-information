## Strategie & Ausblick
- The record 10.087 bn EUR in 9M investment, skewed ~75% to the UK, US and Brazil, confirms the continued rotation toward regulated grid assets in stable jurisdictions; the multi-year investment program (2026–2031) remains above ~55–58 bn EUR.
- Acquisition of **Caruna Oy** (Finland's largest electricity distributor, ~2 bn EUR for an 80% stake, whole business valued at ~5 bn EUR including debt) remains on track for closing around end-2026/Q1 2027, pending foreign-investment, foreign-subsidy and EU merger approvals; it is Iberdrola's first Finnish investment and is expected to grow earnings/asset base by ~7% a year.
- UK network expansion (Electricity North West/ScottishPower integration) continues to be a key growth driver, with UK distribution volumes up over 20% YTD.
- Structural growth drivers remain electrification (data centers/AI demand, electric mobility, heat pumps), a theme Galán has repeatedly emphasized at international forums.
- A **Capital Markets Day in March 2027** is the next scheduled catalyst and is expected to detail updated multi-year targets following the raised FY2026 guidance.
- With the stock at a fresh high (22.14 EUR) following the guidance upgrade, the near-term re-rating has largely played out; the medium- to long-term case (grid expansion, electrification, geographic diversification via Caruna) remains intact and continues to support a **Hold** rather than an aggressive add at current levels.
