## Aktuelle Meldungen
- Iberdrola shares closed at 20.53 EUR on 11 September 2026, up 0.69% on the day, with a market capitalization of roughly 134-135 bn EUR; the stock is up about 9% year-to-date as of mid-September.
- On 14 September 2026, Bernstein reiterated a Neutral rating on Iberdrola with a 20 EUR price target, and coverage noted that several analysts see only limited further upside after the 2026 rally.
- Iberdrola is taking part in a sustainable-mobility event (16–22 September 2026) and Climate Week NYC (20–27 September 2026), reaffirming its commitment to transport electrification as a decarbonization driver.
- Nine-month 2026 results are scheduled for publication on 21 October 2026.
- Analysts cited by elEconomista expect Iberdrola's full-year 2026 net profit to reach close to 6.8 bn EUR, roughly 8% above the approximately 6.3 bn EUR estimated for 2025.

## Management
- CEO Pedro Azagra (in office since 2025, previously head of US subsidiary Avangrid) told The CEO Magazine that rising energy demand is driving the global energy transition and reshaping industries, economies and geopolitics; he pointed to nearly 15 bn EUR invested last year and 3,300 MW of added renewable capacity (wind, solar and storage).
- Executive Chairman Ignacio Galán reiterated that Iberdrola is prioritizing investment in the United States and the United Kingdom, with plans to invest around 40 bn EUR between 2025 and 2028 to modernize and expand transmission and distribution networks.
- Galán has repeatedly linked energy security to national and strategic autonomy in recent interviews (CNBC, CERAWeek, ElEconomista), stressing the resilience of Iberdrola's regulated business model amid geopolitical uncertainty.

## Finanzielles
- Q1 2026: adjusted net profit +11.4% YoY to 1,865 m EUR; adjusted EBITDA +2.4% to 4.1 bn EUR, driven by a 9% increase in the Networks segment.
- H1 2026: reported net profit +22% YoY to 4.34 bn EUR (roughly 4.9 bn USD); adjusted net profit +8% to 3.57 bn EUR; adjusted EBITDA +7% to over 8 bn EUR; R&D investment of 1.6 bn EUR.
- H1 2026 capex rose 25% to 7 bn EUR, with over 70% allocated to the UK, US and Brazil; grid investment alone grew 42% to nearly 4.4 bn EUR.
- Trailing metrics (per MarketBeat, early September): EPS ~4.72 USD, net income ~7.11 bn USD, net margin 15.59%, revenue ~51.52 bn USD, return on equity 9.88%.
- FY2026 guidance confirmed at adjusted net profit growth of more than 8%; consensus now points to full-year net profit near 6.8 bn EUR.
- Next reporting date: nine-month 2026 results on 21 October 2026.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- Iberdrola agreed to acquire an 80% stake in Caruna Group, Finland's largest electricity distribution operator, for roughly 2 bn EUR equity value (about 5 bn EUR enterprise value), adding ~2.5 bn EUR to the regulated asset base and 21% of the Finnish distribution market; the deal is expected to close in Q1 2027 and to be accretive from year one, with annual net income growth of around 7% plus potential upside from electrification and transmission investment.
- The Caruna deal is being financed partly through proceeds from the completed divestment of thermal generation assets in Mexico.
- Full acquisition of Neoenergia (Brazil) was completed in mid-May 2026 after buying the remaining 16.2% stake for roughly 980 m EUR (BRL 5,826 m), giving 100% ownership and visibility from concession renewals extending more than 30 years.
- Iberdrola raised its 2025–2030 new-capacity target to 15.5 GW (+63% vs. the original 2025–2028 plan of 9.5 GW), with continued focus on regulated networks and renewables in the UK, US, Brazil and, going forward, Finland.
- Key upcoming catalyst: nine-month 2026 results on 21 October 2026, which should give further visibility on FY2026 guidance and the Caruna integration timeline.
