## Management
- Executive Chairman **Ignacio Sánchez Galán** continues to act as the group's public face on strategy; his 2026 public appearances (Davos, CERAWeek, Abu Dhabi Sustainability Week) consistently emphasize grid expansion, electrification demand, and ambitions to become the world's largest electricity company.
- **Pedro Azagra**, CEO since 2025 (previously head of US subsidiary Avangrid), continues to run day-to-day operations; no new governance or leadership changes were identified for this reporting period.
- Iberdrola's own materials remain inconsistent on titles: some pages describe Galán as "Executive Chairman and CEO," while others clearly assign the operational CEO role to Azagra.

## Financials
- Nine-month 2026 results (confirmed, published 21.10.2026): net profit of EUR 5.816 billion (+9.6–10% year-on-year), supported by record investments of EUR 10.087 billion (+13% y/y), mainly in Networks across all geographies; full-year 2026 guidance was raised, with management targeting a record year.
- Interim dividend of 0.275 EUR gross per share confirmed alongside the nine-month results; second 2026 tranche of the optional scrip-dividend program ("Iberdrola Flexible Remuneration") has an exercise window running from 15.12.2026 to 03.02.2027.
- Prior 2026 quarters for context: Q1 adjusted net profit +11% to EUR 1.865 billion; H1 net profit +22% to EUR 4.34 billion, driven by network investments in the UK, US and Brazil.
- No reliable, current P/E or P/B multiples were found in this research round.

## Strategy & Outlook
- Continued capital rotation toward regulated grid assets in stable jurisdictions, reinforced by the plan to double Spanish grid investment to EUR 6 billion by 2030.
- Pending acquisition of **Caruna**, Finland's largest electricity distributor, for approximately EUR 5 billion including debt (~80% stake), expected to close in Q1 2027 subject to regulatory approval; projected to add roughly 7% p.a. to earnings/asset growth.
- Acquisition of Spanish retailer **Contigo Energía**'s business added around 100,000 electricity customers in Spain.
- Structural growth drivers remain electrification (data centers/AI demand, EVs, heat pumps) and continued grid build-out, themes Galán has repeatedly stressed at international forums.
- With the share price near multi-year/all-time highs, much of the near-term re-rating already appears to have played out; the medium- to long-term investment case remains intact, supporting a **Hold** stance for now.
