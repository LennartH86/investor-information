## Recent News
- The nine-month 2026 results ("resultados a nueve meses") were filed with the CNMV on Wednesday, 21 October 2026, before market open, with an analyst presentation at 9:30 Madrid time; detailed figures from that release were not yet available in search results as of this update (22.10.), so the headline numbers should be confirmed in the next update.
- Operating update from early October: electricity distribution reached nearly 207,000 GWh over the first nine months (+5.8%), driven mainly by the United Kingdom, where distribution rose 22.8% year-to-date, helped by the integration of Electricity North West.
- Nine-month production reached 90,560 GWh (+3%), and installed generation capacity now stands at 57,205 MW, 2,352 MW more than a year ago.
- A DailyForex preview ("Iberdrola afronta octubre con una valoración exigente") flagged the Q3/nine-month results as the key catalyst for the stock in October, with shares trading around 20.60 EUR and a market cap near 136.6 billion EUR at the time of writing.
- Iberdrola said in August it is advancing the rollout of agentic artificial intelligence to improve productivity across the group.

## Management
- Executive Chairman **Ignacio Sánchez Galán** remains the public face of group strategy; recurring themes from his 2026 appearances (Davos, Abu Dhabi Sustainability Week) continue to dominate: electrification, grid investment, and reducing regulatory bottlenecks.
- In a company interview, Galán stressed the urgency of expanding the electricity grid in the face of booming digital and industrial demand ("It is urgent to expand the electricity grid in the face of booming digital and industrial demand").
- At Davos (January 2026), Galán told Spanish newspaper Expansión that Iberdrola is planning to surpass its strategic plan and could become the world's largest electricity company by market capitalization ("We can become the world's largest electricity company").
- Group CEO **Pedro Azagra** continues to run day-to-day operations, while Galán oversees overall group strategy across the sub-holdings (ScottishPower in the UK, Avangrid in the US, Neoenergia in Brazil).
- No new leadership or governance changes identified in this period.

## Financials
- FY2026 guidance reaffirmed: adjusted net profit growth of more than 8%, already raised during the year following a strong Q1.
- H1 2026 (last fully reported period): reported net profit up 22% to approximately 4.34 billion EUR; adjusted net profit up 8% to approximately 3.565 billion EUR; adjusted EBITDA up 7.4% to 8.05 billion EUR, led by the Networks division.
- H1 2026 investment rose 25% to around 7 billion EUR, with more than 70% directed to the UK, US and Brazil, and close to two-thirds into the Networks business.
- Net debt stood at around 54 billion EUR at mid-year, with roughly 56 billion EUR expected by year-end 2026.
- Nine-month 2026 results were presented to the CNMV on 21 October 2026; specific figures were not yet reflected in available sources at the time of this update.
- Iberdrola sold its Mexican thermal plants for 4.2 billion USD during the year, part of the strategic rotation toward regulated network assets.

## Strategy & Outlook
- The agreed acquisition of **Caruna Oy**, Finland's largest electricity distributor, is on track: Iberdrola is paying around 2 billion EUR for an 80% stake, valuing Caruna at roughly 5 billion EUR including debt; Nordic pension funds retain the remaining 20%. Closing is expected in Q1 2027, subject to regulatory approval.
- The parallel acquisition of an 88% stake in **Electricity North West** (UK) reinforces the shift toward regulated network assets in the UK, US and Brazil.
- Structural growth drivers remain electrification via data centers/AI, electric mobility and heat pumps, themes Galán has repeatedly emphasized at international forums. The investment program through 2028 exceeds 58 billion EUR, concentrated in transmission and distribution networks in the UK and US.
- The nine-month results released on 21.10. are the near-term catalyst and should bring more clarity on FY2026 guidance, Caruna/Electricity North West integration progress, and capex execution; these details were not yet available in search results as of this update.
- Given the already demanding valuation near 52-week highs, near-term upside before clearer post-results commentary looks limited, while the medium- to long-term investment case (grid expansion, electrification, geographic diversification) remains intact.
