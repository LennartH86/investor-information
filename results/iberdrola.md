## Strategie & Ausblick
- Record 9M investment of 10.087 bn EUR, skewed ~75% to the UK, US and Brazil, confirms the continued rotation toward regulated grid assets in stable jurisdictions; the multi-year investment program (2026–2031) remains above ~55–58 bn EUR.
- Acquisition of **Caruna Oy** (Finland's largest electricity distributor; ~2.01 bn EUR for an 80% stake, whole-business valuation ~5 bn EUR including debt) remains on track for closing around end-2026/Q1 2027, pending foreign-investment, foreign-subsidy and EU merger approvals; Iberdrola's first Finnish investment, expected to grow earnings/asset base by ~7% a year.
- Further portfolio moves in 2026 include the **Contigo Energía** retail acquisition in Spain (adding ~100,000 electricity customers, announced 28.09.2026) and the earlier increase of the stake in **Neoenergia** (Brazil, ~30.3% stake from PREVI, pushing Iberdrola's holding toward ~84%+); a reported **PNM/Avangrid** US transaction (~$8.3bn enterprise value) was also mentioned in search results but could not be independently confirmed this round and should be treated with caution.
- UK network expansion (Electricity North West/ScottishPower integration) continues to be a key growth driver, with UK distribution volumes up over 20% YTD.
- Structural growth drivers remain electrification (data centers/AI demand, electric mobility, heat pumps), a theme Galán has repeatedly emphasized at international forums (Davos, CERAWeek, Abu Dhabi Sustainability Week).
- A **Capital Markets Day in March 2027** is the next scheduled catalyst and is expected to detail updated multi-year targets following the raised FY2026 guidance.
- With the stock at a fresh high (22.27 EUR) following the guidance upgrade, the near-term re-rating has largely played out; the medium- to long-term case (grid expansion, electrification, geographic diversification via Caruna) remains intact and continues to support a **Hold** rather than an aggressive add at current levels.
