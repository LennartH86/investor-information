## Strategie & Ausblick
- Record 9M investment of 10.087 bn EUR, skewed ~75% to the UK, US and Brazil, confirms the continued rotation toward regulated grid assets in stable jurisdictions; the multi-year investment program (2026–2028/2031) remains in the ~43–58 bn EUR range depending on the horizon cited.
- Acquisition of **Caruna Oy** (Finland's largest electricity distributor; ~2.0 bn EUR for an 80% stake, whole-business valuation ~5 bn EUR including debt) remains on track for closing around end-2026/Q1 2027, pending foreign-investment, foreign-subsidy and EU merger approvals; this would be Iberdrola's first Finnish investment, expected to grow earnings/asset base by ~7% a year.
- UK network expansion continues to be a key growth driver: the acquisition of **Electricity North West** (~5 bn EUR deal, announced January 2026) creates the UK's second-largest electricity network operator, alongside ScottishPower integration; UK distribution volumes are up strongly year-to-date.
- Portfolio rotation also includes the earlier divestment of the Mexican business (sold to Cox for ~US$4 bn, including a 12 GW renewable pipeline), the **Contigo Energía** retail acquisition in Spain (+~100,000 electricity customers), and the increased stake in **Neoenergia** (Brazil, ~30.3% from PREVI, pushing Iberdrola's holding materially higher); a reported PNM/Avangrid US transaction (~US$8.3 bn enterprise value) could not be independently reconfirmed this round and should be treated with caution.
- Structural growth drivers remain electrification (data centers/AI demand, electric mobility, heat pumps), a theme Galán has repeatedly emphasized at international forums (Davos, CERAWeek, Abu Dhabi Sustainability Week).
- A **Capital Markets Day in March 2027** remains the next scheduled catalyst, expected to detail updated multi-year targets following the raised FY2026 guidance.
- With the stock at a fresh high (22.79 EUR) following the guidance upgrade, the near-term re-rating has largely played out; the medium- to long-term case (grid expansion, electrification, geographic diversification via Caruna) remains intact and continues to support a **Hold** rather than an aggressive add at current levels.
