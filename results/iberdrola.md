## Management
- Executive Chairman Ignacio Sánchez Galán continues to be the public face of Iberdrola's strategy in 2026, giving interviews at CERAWeek and to ElEconomista (March 2026) and speaking at Davos (January 2026), where he stressed that Europe is on track to meet its 2030 decarbonization targets and highlighted the resilience of Iberdrola's regulated business model amid the geopolitical and energy crisis.
- Galán has repeatedly framed energy security as inseparable from national and strategic autonomy (CNBC, January 2026), and argues for the removal of regulatory and fiscal barriers to accelerate European electrification.
- Day-to-day CEO duties sit with Pedro Azagra (in role since 2025, previously head of US subsidiary Avangrid); note that this differs from the "Ignacio Galán as CEO" framing sometimes used in older coverage — Galán currently holds the Executive Chairman title.
- Management continues to emphasize that Iberdrola is entering "a period of unprecedented growth," with the ambition of becoming the world's leading electricity company by market capitalization.

## Finanzielles
- Q1 2026: adjusted net profit +11.4% YoY to 1,865 m EUR; guidance raised to 8%+ adjusted net profit growth for the year, led by the Networks division.
- H1 2026: reported net profit +22% YoY to 4.34 bn EUR (~4.9 bn USD); adjusted net profit +8% to 3.57 bn EUR; adjusted EBITDA +7% to over 8 bn EUR; electricity distributed up 6.3% YoY.
- H1 2026 capex rose 25% to 7 bn EUR, with over 70% directed to the UK, US and Brazil networks; grid investment alone grew 42% to nearly 4.4 bn EUR.
- Twelve-month rolling investment (to ~May 2026) reached 14.5 bn EUR, the majority directed to UK and US networks.
- Adjusted net debt stands at approximately 50.3 bn EUR following completion of the Mexico thermal-generation divestment and the buy-out of Neoenergia minority shareholders in Brazil.
- FY2026 guidance confirmed at adjusted net profit growth of more than 8%; consensus points to full-year net profit near 6.8 bn EUR, versus roughly 6.3 bn EUR for FY2025.
- Next reporting date: nine-month 2026 results on 21 October 2026.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- Iberdrola agreed to acquire an 80% stake in Caruna Networks, Finland's largest electricity distributor, for roughly 2 bn EUR equity value (about 5 bn EUR enterprise value); the deal, expected to close in Q1 2027, adds a new regulated-networks market and is expected to be accretive from year one.
- The Neoenergia (Brazil) buy-out was completed in mid-May 2026 (remaining 16.2% stake for ~980 m EUR / BRL 5,826 m), giving Iberdrola 100% ownership and long-dated visibility from concessions extending more than 30 years.
- Between 2025 and 2028, around 70% of networks-related investment continues to be directed toward the UK and US markets, reinforcing the shift away from thermal generation toward regulated networks and renewables.
- Iberdrola completed the sale of its Hungarian business for 171 m EUR, continuing the portfolio rotation out of non-core markets.
- Key upcoming catalyst: nine-month 2026 results on 21 October 2026, which should provide further visibility on FY2026 guidance and the Caruna integration timeline.
