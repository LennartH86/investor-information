## Recent News
- **21.10.2026 – 9M 2026 results:** Reported net profit rose 10% to 5.816 bn EUR; adjusted net profit rose 9% to ~5.1 bn EUR. Investment hit a record 10.087 bn EUR (+13%), with ~75% directed to the UK, US and Brazil; network capex grew 28% to 6.3 bn EUR. Adjusted net debt stands at 3.4x adjusted EBITDA. The board proposed an interim dividend of 0.275 EUR/share (+8.7%), with a supplementary dividend to be announced in 2027. A Capital Markets Day is scheduled for March 2027. Iberdrola also reaffirmed it keeps its BBB+ credit rating.
- **09.10.2026 – Operating update:** Electricity distributed in the first nine months reached nearly 207,000 GWh (+5.8%), driven mainly by the United Kingdom (+22.8% YTD, helped by the Electricity North West integration). Nine-month generation reached 90,560 GWh, and installed capacity stands at 57,205 MW (+2,352 MW y/y).
- A DailyForex preview ("Iberdrola afronta octubre con una valoración exigente" / "Iberdrola faces October with a demanding valuation") flagged the 9M results as October's key catalyst, with the stock trading around 20.60 EUR ahead of the print — since surpassed, with shares now at 21.83 EUR.

## Management
- Executive Chairman **Ignacio Sánchez Galán** remains the public face of group strategy; no new 2026 interview surfaced in this period beyond earlier CERAWeek and Davos/Abu Dhabi Sustainability Week appearances, where recurring themes are electrification, grid investment and reducing regulatory bottlenecks.
- At CERAWeek (earlier in 2026), Galán told Spanish newspaper ElEconomista that the current geopolitical/energy crisis has limited impact on Iberdrola, citing competitive, fixed long-term prices as part of the solution; he also backed extending the life of Spain's nuclear plants for supply security.
- Iberdrola's stated ambition under Galán remains becoming the world's leading electric utility by market capitalisation, alongside a growing dividend policy and solid financial structure.
- Group CEO **Pedro Azagra** continues to run day-to-day operations, while Galán oversees overall group strategy across sub-holdings (ScottishPower in the UK, Avangrid in the US, Neoenergia in Brazil).
- No new leadership or governance changes identified in this period.

## Financials
- **9M 2026 (new this period):** Reported net profit 5.816 bn EUR (+10%); adjusted net profit ~5.1 bn EUR (+9%); investment 10.087 bn EUR (+13%), record for the period; network investment 6.3 bn EUR (+28%); adjusted net debt 3.4x adjusted EBITDA.
- FY2026 guidance raised to **at least 10%** adjusted net profit growth (previously >8%), with an ambition to sustain ~10% annual adjusted growth over the next five years.
- Interim dividend raised 8.7% to 0.275 EUR/share; supplementary dividend to be announced in 2027.
- H1 2026 (prior period, for reference): reported net profit up 22% to ~4.34 bn EUR; adjusted net profit up 8% to ~3.565 bn EUR; adjusted EBITDA up 7.4% to 8.05 bn EUR, led by Networks.
- Credit rating reaffirmed at BBB+.
- Iberdrola sold its Mexican thermal plants for 4.2 bn USD during the year, part of the strategic rotation toward regulated network assets, partly funding the Caruna acquisition.

## Strategy & Outlook
- The 10.087 bn EUR in 9M investment, skewed ~75% to the UK, US and Brazil, confirms the continued rotation toward regulated grid assets in stable jurisdictions; the investment program through 2028 remains above 58 bn EUR.
- The agreed acquisition of **Caruna Oy**, Finland's largest electricity distributor, is on track: Iberdrola is paying ~2 bn EUR for an 80% stake (whole business valued at ~5 bn EUR including debt), with closing expected end of 2026 / Q1 2027, subject to foreign-investment, foreign-subsidy and EU merger approvals. Caruna is expected to grow earnings and asset base by ~7% a year.
- The smaller acquisition of **Contigo Energía**'s Spanish retail business (100,000+ customers, announced 28.09.2026) adds scale domestically; it will keep operating independently within the group, similar to the Niba brand.
- Structural growth drivers remain electrification (data centers/AI, electric mobility, heat pumps), themes Galán has repeatedly emphasized at international forums.
- A **Capital Markets Day in March 2027** is the next scheduled catalyst, expected to detail updated targets following the raised FY2026 guidance.
- With guidance now raised to at least 10% growth and the stock near 52-week highs, the near-term re-rating has largely played out; medium- to long-term case (grid expansion, electrification, geographic diversification via Caruna) remains intact and supports Hold.
