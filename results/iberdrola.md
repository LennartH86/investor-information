## Management
- Executive Chairman **Ignacio Sánchez Galán** remains the primary public voice of strategy; no new interviews were identified within the 22–29 September window, but his prior 2026 messaging (CERAWeek, Davos, ElEconomista) continues to frame coverage — emphasizing that *"el modelo de negocio de Iberdrola es parte de la solución a las crisis energéticas"* ("Iberdrola's business model is part of the solution to energy crises").
- Galán continues to press for the removal of regulatory and fiscal barriers to accelerate grid investment and electrification across Europe, and reiterates the ambition for Iberdrola to become the world's leading electricity company by market capitalization.
- Day-to-day CEO duties remain with **Pedro Azagra** (former head of US subsidiary Avangrid); Galán holds the Executive Chairman title.
- Iberdrola's Digital Summit ("EnergIA that Transforms") highlighted management's continued focus on AI as a driver of change across the group, though this predates the current reporting window.
- No governance or leadership changes were reported in the 22–29 September period.

## Financials
- H1 2026: reported net profit +22% YoY to 4,336–4,340 million EUR; adjusted net profit +8% to ~3,565 million EUR; adjusted EBITDA reached 8.05 billion EUR.
- H1 2026 total investment amounted to 7.00 billion EUR, with more than 70% directed to UK, US and Brazil networks.
- FY2026 guidance confirmed at >8% adjusted net profit growth; consensus points to full-year net profit near 6.8 billion EUR, versus ~6.3 billion EUR for FY2025.
- Rolling twelve-month investment (to ~May 2026) reached 14.5 billion EUR, the majority directed to UK and US networks.
- Adjusted net debt stands at roughly 50.3 billion EUR following the Mexico thermal-generation divestment (net capital gain of 953 million EUR) and the Neoenergia (Brazil) minority buy-out.
- Next reporting date: nine-month 2026 results on 21 October 2026 (presentation available in Spanish and English via investor relations).

## Strategy & Outlook
- The pending acquisition of an 80% stake in **Caruna Networks** (Finland's largest electricity distributor, ~2 billion EUR equity value / ~5 billion EUR enterprise value) remains on track to close in Q1 2027, expected to be accretive from year one with ~7% annual net income growth from the asset.
- The newly announced **Contigo Energía** retail acquisition (28 September 2026) extends Iberdrola's Spanish retail footprint by more than 100,000 customers, reinforcing the domestic supply business alongside the international networks push.
- Iberdrola targets 15.5 GW of new renewable capacity additions between 2025 and 2030, having invested 2.2 billion EUR in renewables in H1 2026 (over 70% in wind technologies).
- The company plans roughly US$42.2 billion of grid infrastructure investment, with more than 70% directed to UK and US networks, continuing the strategic shift from thermal generation toward regulated networks.
- The Neoenergia (Brazil) buy-out (completed mid-May 2026) gives Iberdrola 100% ownership with long-dated visibility from concessions extending more than 30 years.
- Key upcoming catalyst: nine-month 2026 results on 21 October 2026, which should provide further detail on FY2026 guidance, the Caruna integration timeline and the newly announced Contigo Energía deal.
