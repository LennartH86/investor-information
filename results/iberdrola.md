## Latest News
- The stock kept climbing through the period, moving from roughly 20.60 EUR in early October toward 21.72 EUR, close to its 52-week high (~22.08 EUR).
- Iberdrola confirmed its nine-month 2026 results will be filed with the CNMV on **Wednesday, 21 October 2026** before market open, with an analyst presentation at 9:30 Madrid time — just after this reporting window.
- September operating data (published 9 October): distributed electricity rose 5.8% year-on-year to about 206,947 GWh, driven largely by the UK (distribution there up 22.8% over nine months, helped by the Electricity North West integration); nine-month production reached 90,560 GWh (+3%); installed capacity now stands at 57,205 MW, up 2,352 MW year-on-year.
- Spanish financial outlet DailyForex flagged the upcoming Q3 report as the key share-price catalyst for October, alongside the "demanding valuation" narrative.
- No new M&A announcements emerged within the narrow 08–15 October window; the pending Caruna (Finland) and Electricity North West (UK) deals remain in their respective approval/integration processes.

## Management
- Executive Chairman **Ignacio Sánchez Galán** remains the public face of group strategy; no new interview specifically from the 08–15 October window was found. His recurring themes from earlier 2026 appearances (Davos, Abu Dhabi Sustainability Week, CERAWeek/ElEconomista in March) continue to frame market expectations: electrification, grid investment, and reducing regulatory barriers.
- At CERAWeek (March 2026) Galán argued the current geopolitical/energy crisis has had limited impact on Iberdrola, citing the company's ability to offer competitive fixed long-term prices, the strategic importance of grid investment for European electrification, and support for extending the operating life of Spain's nuclear plants.
- Group CEO **Pedro Azagra** (in office since June 2025) continues to run day-to-day operations, while Galán, as Executive Chairman, oversees overall group strategy across Iberdrola's subholdings (ScottishPower in the UK, Avangrid in the US, Neoenergia in Brazil).
- No new leadership or governance changes identified during the period.

## Financials
- FY2026 guidance reaffirmed: adjusted net profit to grow more than 8%, a target already raised earlier in the year after a strong Q1.
- H1 2026 (latest fully reported period, published 22 July): reported net profit +22% to about €4,336-4,340 million; adjusted net profit +8% to about €3,565 million; adjusted EBITDA +7.4% to €8,050 million (Networks +13%, Power & Customers +1%).
- H1 2026 investment rose 25% to about €7 billion, with over 70% directed to the UK, US and Brazil and nearly two-thirds into the networks business.
- Net debt stood at roughly €54 billion at H1, expected to reach about €56 billion by year-end 2026, driven mainly by capex and currency effects.
- Next official reporting date: nine-month 2026 results on 21 October 2026 (CNMV filing before market open, presentation at 9:30 Madrid time) — just outside this review window.
- Dividend policy: Iberdrola traditionally runs a scrip/cash dividend with a historical yield around 4%; 0.77 EUR in dividends has been recorded for this position in Parqet to date.

## Strategy & Outlook
- The agreed 80% acquisition of **Caruna Oy**, Finland's largest electricity distributor (equity value ~€2 billion, enterprise value including debt ~€5 billion), remains on track, with closing expected in Q1 2027 subject to regulatory approval; Finnish pension funds AMF and Elo retain the remaining 20%.
- The parallel acquisition of an 88% stake in **Electricity North West** (UK) for about £2.13 billion continues as part of Iberdrola's strategic shift toward regulated network assets in the UK, US and Brazil.
- Structural growth drivers remain data center/AI-driven electrification, electric mobility and heat pumps — themes Galán has repeatedly emphasized at international forums (Abu Dhabi Sustainability Week, Davos, CERAWeek). The investment program through 2028 is targeted at over €58 billion, concentrated in transmission and distribution networks in the UK and US.
- The key upcoming catalyst is the nine-month 2026 results on 21 October 2026, expected to provide further detail on FY2026 guidance, integration progress at Caruna/Electricity North West, and capex execution.
- Given the already "demanding" valuation near 52-week highs, near-term upside ahead of the earnings print looks limited, though the medium- to long-term investment case (network expansion, electrification, geographic diversification) remains intact.
