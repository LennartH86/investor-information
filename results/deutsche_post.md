The fundamental picture remains unchanged and positive: a twice-raised FY2026 guidance (operating EBIT above EUR 7.0 billion), a strong Q3 with marked profit growth at DHL Express, and an ongoing dividend policy support the valuation; holding the position continues to look sensible, while a selective top-up could be considered on a pullback toward EUR 52–55.)_

- Fortsetzung der "Strategy 2030" mit Fokus auf Wachstum in DHL Express und DHL Supply Chain sowie Investitionen in Robotik, KI und Infrastruktur.
  _(Continuation of "Strategy 2030", focused on growth at DHL Express and DHL Supply Chain as well as investments in robotics, AI and infrastructure.)_
- Mehrere kleinere Akquisitionen zur regionalen Expansion: Venipak (Baltikum, vereinbart 27.07.2026, Integration in DHL eCommerce), Open Market (Kolumbien, vereinbart 15.09.2026, ca. 4.100 Mitarbeitende für DHL Supply Chain), Aero Cargas S.A.   _(Several smaller acquisitions supporting regional expansion: Venipak (Baltics, agreed Jul 27, 2026, to be integrated into DHL eCommerce), Open Market (Colombia, agreed Sep 15, 2026, around 4,100 employees joining DHL Supply Chain), Aero Cargas S.A.
