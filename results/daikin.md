## Strategie & Ausblick
- Daikin continues to execute its long-term "FUSION30" strategy, as detailed in the newly released Integrated Report 2026, emphasizing sustainability and integrated air-conditioning/heating solutions.
- The company has reportedly earmarked an M&A budget of around ¥600 billion and has indicated willingness to exceed it if needed, targeting European heat-pump sales/service companies, refrigerated display-case manufacturers, and a leading position in North American commercial air conditioning. No new acquisitions were confirmed as closed in October 2026 specifically.
- Elliott's activist involvement adds a catalyst for potential capital-allocation changes (buybacks, portfolio optimization) that could support the share price going forward.
- Regional growth initiatives continue, including R&D investment in India and market expansion plans in Vietnam, reflecting Daikin's ongoing diversification beyond its core Japan/U.S./Europe markets.
