- **Bewertung:** Der Kurs hat sich gegenüber der Vorwoche leicht erholt (116,93 EUR vs. 114,60 EUR), liegt aber weiterhin rund 18% unter dem durchschnittlichen Einstandspreis, während die erste Dividendengutschrift (43,40 EUR) eingegangen ist. Angesichts der Rekordzahlen im FY2025, des laufenden Ausbaus im strukturell wachsenden Rechenzentrums-Kühlungssegment und der neuen FUSION30-Strategie erscheint die fundamentale Lage weiterhin solide, sodass ein Hold mit der Möglichkeit eines kleinen opportunistischen Nachkaufs bei anhaltender Kursschwäche sinnvoll bleibt.

## Aktuelle Meldungen
- Daikin Applied announced it is powering next-generation hyperscale data centers with comprehensive, end-to-end cooling solutions, reinforcing its positioning in the fast-growing AI/data-center infrastructure market.
- Daikin Applied continued its bolt-on growth strategy by acquiring a New York-based modular comfort systems provider, expanding its modular HVAC product and service offering in North America.
- Daikin leaders participated in multiple events during Climate Week NYC 2026 (week of Sept 21), as a Climate Group Gold Partner, including The Hub Live flagship sessions; David Calabrese (EVP, Daikin U.S. Corporation) spoke on a panel about keeping the lights on amid an uncertain global energy landscape.
- On September 7, 2026, Daikin issued a corporate/financial notice on the completion of the ex post facto adjustment for its Fully Committed Share Repurchase (FCSR) program.

## Management
- Masanori Togawa remains Representative Director, Chairman of the Board and CEO of Daikin Industries; Naofumi Takenaka serves as Representative Director, President and COO.
- Effective July 1, 2026, Yu Nishiwaki was named President and CEO of Daikin Applied (the North American subsidiary), with James Moe as Chief Operating Officer and Hirokazu Hirao as Chief Operating Officer of Manufacturing.
- Katsuyuki Sawai (Senior Executive Officer, Sustainability Promotion) gave a Climate Week interview on advanced technologies and Daikin's vision for supporting decarbonization goals.

## Finanzielles
- FY2025 (fiscal year ended March 31, 2026): record net sales of ¥5,015.0 billion (+5.5% YoY) and operating profit of ¥414.99 billion (+3.3% YoY); profit attributable to owners of the parent grew 4.0% YoY to ¥275.23 billion.
- Q3 FY2026 (nine months to Dec 31, 2025): revenue up 2.0% YoY to ¥3.666 trillion, though operating profit declined 3.4% to ¥307.9 billion; net profit attributable to owners rose 4.6% to ¥195.4 billion. Daikin raised its full-year sales forecast to ¥4.920 trillion (+1.7%) while lowering its operating profit forecast to ¥413.0 billion (-5.1%).
- H1 FY2026 (six months to Sept 30, 2025): net sales of ¥2,478.8 billion (-0.6% YoY), operating profit flat at ¥246.6 billion, ordinary profit +7.9% to ¥241.9 billion, and net profit +6.1% to ¥160.9 billion; gross margin improved to 35.6% from 34.5% YoY.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- Daikin's "FUSION30" strategic management plan (announced May 2026) continues to set the medium-term direction for growth, electrification and decarbonization initiatives.
- Continued expansion in data-center cooling: recent moves include end-to-end cooling solutions for hyperscale data centers, the acquisition of a New York-based modular comfort systems provider, and earlier acquisitions of DDC Solutions and Chilldyne, alongside a $163M investment in a new R&D test lab at Plymouth, Minnesota (completion planned for 2027).
- Ongoing bolt-on M&A activity and venture investments continue, including a stake in Asuene's $42M Series D round (July 22, 2026), signaling continued portfolio expansion into adjacent technology and sustainability-related businesses.
- Daikin continues to position itself around climate/energy-transition themes via its Climate Week NYC 2026 presence and Climate Group Gold Partner status, reinforcing its long-term electrification and decarbonization growth narrative.
