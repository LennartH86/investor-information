## Strategie & Ausblick
- Daikin continues to pursue an aggressive, well-funded M&A strategy, holding an estimated ¥600,0 billion (~USD 5,2 billion) acquisition war chest earmarked for targets including European heat-pump sales/service companies and refrigerated display-case manufacturers, as part of a push toward a No. 1 position in the North American residential and commercial air-conditioning market.
- The "FUSION30" strategic management plan (announced 12.05.2026) remains the guiding medium-term framework, emphasizing electrification, decarbonization and an AI-first R&D ecosystem.
- Data-center cooling remains a core growth pillar following the DDC Solutions and Chilldyne bolt-on acquisitions and the ongoing USD 163 million R&D test-lab investment at Daikin Applied's Plymouth, Minnesota facility (completion targeted for 2027).
- Record Q1 FY2027 top-line growth (+17,5% YoY) signals continued demand strength, though margin compression from cost inflation and tariff-related headwinds bears watching heading into the Q2 FY2027 report due 03.11.2026.
