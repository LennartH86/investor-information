## Strategie & Ausblick
- Daikin continues pursuing its long-term "Fusion 30" strategy, targeting a 10% operating margin and 12% ROE by FY2028.
- Continued expansion in AI data-center cooling (new Coolant Distribution Unit line, prior DDC Solutions acquisition) remains the key growth narrative beyond core HVAC; the new large-space Vertical Water / Space Cooler & Heater range announced this period extends Daikin's commercial/industrial HVAC portfolio further.
- Regional and service-network expansion continues: the planned Jeddah manufacturing facility, the agreed Anh Nguyen (Vietnam) acquisition, the closed Ganter acquisition (Germany, effective October 2, 2026), and the newly disclosed Saltire Facilities Management acquisition (Scotland) all extend Daikin's heating/cooling service capabilities internationally.
- Elliott's activist involvement remains a potential catalyst for capital-allocation changes that could support valuation.
