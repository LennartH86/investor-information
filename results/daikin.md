## Management
- Masanori Togawa remains Representative Director, Chairman of the Board and CEO of Daikin Industries; Naofumi Takenaka serves as Representative Director, President and COO.
- Effective July 1, 2026, Yu Nishiwaki was named President and CEO of Daikin Applied (the North American subsidiary), with James Moe as Chief Operating Officer and Hirokazu Hirao as Chief Operating Officer of Manufacturing.
- No further management changes were identified during the current reporting period; the most recent strategic guidance remains the FUSION30 plan presented at the May 2026 FY2025 results briefing.

## Financials
- FY2025 (fiscal year ended March 31, 2026): record net sales of ¥5,015.0 billion (+5.5% YoY) and operating profit of ¥414.99 billion (+3.3% YoY); profit attributable to owners of the parent grew 4.0% YoY to ¥275.23 billion; ordinary profit rose 11.4% YoY to ¥408.17 billion.
- FY2026 forecast (issued with FY2025 results): net sales of ¥5,150.0 billion (+2.7% YoY) and operating profit of ¥436.0 billion (+5.1% YoY).
- Q3 FY2026 (nine months to Dec 31, 2025): net sales up 2.0% YoY to ¥3,666.3 billion; operating profit down 3.4% YoY to ¥307.9 billion (impacted by U.S. tariffs, partially offset by pricing and cost measures); net profit attributable to owners rose 4.6% YoY to ¥195.4 billion; EPS of ¥667.22 vs. ¥637.72 a year earlier.

## Strategy & Outlook
- Daikin's "FUSION30" strategic management plan (announced May 2026) continues to guide medium-term growth, electrification and decarbonization priorities.
- Continued expansion in data-center cooling remains a core growth pillar: recent bolt-on acquisitions (DDC Solutions, Chilldyne) and a $163M R&D test lab investment at Daikin Applied's Plymouth, Minnesota headquarters (completion planned for 2027) underpin this strategy.
- Bolt-on M&A and venture investment activity continues, including the July 2026 participation in Asuene's $42M Series D round, reflecting ongoing portfolio expansion into adjacent sustainability/technology businesses.
- Daikin continues to reinforce its climate/energy-transition positioning through its Climate Week NYC 2026 presence and Climate Group Gold Partner status.
