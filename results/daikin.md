## Management
- Masanori Togawa remains Representative Director, Chairman of the Board and CEO of Daikin Industries; Naofumi Takenaka serves as Representative Director, President and COO.
- Effective July 1, 2026, Yu Nishiwaki was named President and CEO of Daikin Applied (the North American subsidiary), with James Moe as Chief Operating Officer and Hirokazu Hirao as Chief Operating Officer of Manufacturing, aimed at strengthening the operating model.
- Management presented FY2025 full-year results and the new "FUSION30" strategic management plan at a briefing in May 2026, outlining what the company will protect, change and continue to win with amid current uncertainty.

## Finanzielles
- FY2025 (fiscal year ended March 31, 2026): record net sales of ¥5,015.0 billion (+5.5% YoY) and operating profit of ¥414.99 billion (+3.3% YoY); profit attributable to owners of the parent grew 4.0% YoY to ¥275.23 billion.
- Q3 FY2026 (nine months to Dec 31, 2025): revenue up 2.0% YoY to ¥3.666 trillion, though operating profit declined 3.4% to ¥307.9 billion; net profit attributable to owners rose 4.6% to ¥195.4 billion. Daikin raised its full-year sales forecast to ¥4.920 trillion (+1.7%) while lowering its operating profit forecast to ¥413.0 billion (-5.1%).
- H1 FY2026 (six months to Sept 30, 2025): net sales of ¥2,478.8 billion (-0.6% YoY), operating profit flat at ¥246.6 billion, ordinary profit +7.9% to ¥241.9 billion, and net profit +6.1% to ¥160.9 billion.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- Daikin's new "FUSION30" strategic management plan (announced May 2026) sets the medium-term direction for growth, electrification and decarbonization initiatives.
- Continued expansion in data-center cooling: Daikin Applied has been acquiring companies such as DDC Solutions (ultra-high-density cooling cabinets and management software) and Chilldyne (negative-pressure liquid cooling for AI/high-performance data centers), alongside a $163M investment in a new R&D test lab at its Plymouth, Minnesota headquarters (completion planned for 2027) to bolster HVAC innovation for data centers and beyond.
- Ongoing bolt-on M&A activity and venture investments continue, including a stake in Asuene's $42M Series D round (July 22, 2026), signaling continued portfolio expansion into adjacent technology and sustainability-related businesses.
- Daikin continues to position itself around climate/energy-transition themes via its Climate Week NYC 2026 presence and Climate Group Gold Partner status, reinforcing its long-term electrification and decarbonization growth narrative amid an evolving global energy landscape.
