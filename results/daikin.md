## Strategy & Outlook
- Daikin continues to pursue its long-term "Fusion 30" strategy, targeting a 10% operating margin and 12% ROE by FY2028, alongside its sustainability agenda outlined in the Integrated Report 2026 and Sustainability Report 2026.
