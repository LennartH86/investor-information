- **Bewertung:** The stock is down roughly 19.5% from the average purchase price despite Daikin posting record FY2025 (ended March 2026) net sales (+5.5% YoY) and operating profit (+3.3% YoY), and Q3 FY2026 revenue growth of 2.0% with net profit up 4.6%, suggesting the share price weakness reflects broader market/currency (JPY) dynamics rather than deteriorating fundamentals. Given continued profit growth, the new FUSION30 strategic plan, and an active M&A/capex program in the structurally growing data-center cooling segment, a Hold appears reasonable for existing holders; a small opportunistic add could be considered by investors comfortable with further short-term volatility, rather than reducing the position at current depressed levels.

## Aktuelle Meldungen
- Daikin U.S. Corporation leaders participated in Climate Week NYC 2026 (Sept 15–21), addressing electrification, HVAC efficiency, refrigerant management and resilient buildings; David Calabrese (EVP, Daikin U.S.) spoke on the "Strait Talk, Reality Check" flagship panel on Sept 21.
- On September 7, 2026, Daikin announced completion of the ex post facto adjustment for its Fully Committed Share Repurchase (FCSR) program.
- Daikin was also active at other industry events in 2026 (e.g., WELL Flagship Summit) promoting its indoor air quality and decarbonization agenda.

## Management
- Masanori Togawa remains Representative Director, Chairman of the Board and CEO of Daikin Industries; Naofumi Takenaka serves as President and COO.
- In July 2026, Yu Nishiwaki was named President and CEO of Daikin Applied (North American subsidiary), with James Moe as COO and Hirokazu Hirao as COO of Manufacturing.
- Management presented the FY2025 full-year results and the new "FUSION30" strategic management plan in a May 2026 briefing, with Togawa and Takenaka answering analyst questions.

## Finanzielles
- FY2025 (fiscal year ended March 31, 2026): record net sales of ¥5,015.0 billion (+5.5% YoY) and operating profit of ¥414.99 billion (+3.3% YoY); profit attributable to owners of parent grew 4.0% YoY to ¥275.23 billion.
- Q3 FY2026 (nine months to Dec 31, 2025): revenue up 2.0% YoY to ¥3.666 trillion, but operating profit declined 3.4% to ¥307.9 billion; net profit attributable to owners rose 4.6% to ¥195.4 billion; full-year sales forecast was raised.
- H1 FY2026 (six months to Sept 30, 2025): net sales of ¥2,478.8 billion (-0.6% YoY), operating profit flat at ¥246.6 billion, ordinary profit +7.9% to ¥241.9 billion, and net profit +6.1% to ¥160.9 billion; gross margin improved to 35.6% from 34.5%.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- Daikin formulated its new "FUSION30" strategic management plan (announced May 2026), setting medium-term direction for growth, electrification and decarbonization initiatives.
- Continued expansion in data-center cooling: Daikin Applied acquired Chilldyne (liquid cooling for AI/high-performance data centers) in late 2025 and committed $163M to a new R&D test lab in Plymouth, MN to support HVAC innovation for data centers.
- Ongoing bolt-on M&A activity (e.g., Anh Nguyen Trading Technical Service, Landi, Duplomatic MS) and venture investments (e.g., a stake in Asuene's $42M Series D round in July 2026) signal continued portfolio expansion into adjacent technology and sustainability-related businesses.
- Daikin continues to position itself around climate/energy-transition themes (Climate Week NYC participation, Climate Group Gold Partner status), reinforcing its long-term electrification and decarbonization growth narrative.
