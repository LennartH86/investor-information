## Strategy & Outlook
- Daikin continues pursuing its long-term "Fusion 30" strategy, targeting a 10% operating margin and 12% ROE by FY2028.
- AI data-center cooling remains a key growth narrative beyond core HVAC: the DDC Solutions acquisition (AI data-center cooling technology) was reaffirmed this period, alongside the previously announced Coolant Distribution Unit line (400 kW to 2.5 MW per unit).
- Large-space and commercial/industrial HVAC expansion continues via the recently launched Vertical Water / Space Cooler & Heater range, Mini VRV 5 S-series retrofit technology, and the new-generation Intelligent Chiller Manager.
- U.S. growth strategy was a specific focus this period, presented at the J.P. Morgan Industrials Conference, in the context of tariff management and continued U.S. market investment.
- Regional and service-network expansion continues: the MOU with Senegal signals new workforce-development/market activity in Africa, alongside the previously reported planned Jeddah manufacturing facility, the Anh Nguyen (Vietnam) acquisition, the Ganter acquisition (Germany), and the Saltire Facilities Management acquisition (Scotland).
- The reported relocation of Daikin's Japanese HQ to Osaka Umeda Twin Towers South (if confirmed) would be an organizational rather than strategic change, but remains unverified from a primary source.
- Elliott's activist involvement remains a potential catalyst for capital-allocation changes that could support valuation.
