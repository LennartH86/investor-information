## Strategie & Ausblick
- Daikin continues pursuing its long-term "Fusion 30" strategy, targeting a 10% operating margin and 12% ROE by FY2028.
- Expansion in AI data-center cooling remains a key growth narrative beyond core HVAC, underscored by the new CDU product line and the earlier DDC Solutions acquisition of server rack-level cooling technology for AI data centers.
- Regional expansion continues: Daikin Applied Germany's acquisition of Ganter took effect October 2, 2026, extending heating and cooling service capabilities; the agreed acquisition of Vietnamese BMS integrator Anh Nguyen remained subject to regulatory approval per the most recent confirmed reporting; a planned manufacturing facility in Jeddah and a EUR 300 million investment in Daikin's Polish heat pump production site (recognized with an fDi Intelligence award, dated March 2026) further support growth outside Japan.
