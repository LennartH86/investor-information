## Strategy & Outlook
- Daikin continues pursuing its long-term "Fusion 30" strategy, targeting a 10% operating margin and 12% ROE by FY2028.
- Continued expansion in AI data-center cooling (Coolant Distribution Unit line spanning 400 kW to 2.5 MW per unit, announced November 2, 2026; prior DDC Solutions acquisition) remains the key growth narrative beyond core HVAC; the new large-space Vertical Water / Space Cooler & Heater range and Mini VRV 5 S-series retrofit technology announced this period further extend Daikin's commercial/industrial HVAC portfolio.
- Regional and service-network expansion continues: the planned Jeddah manufacturing facility, the agreed Anh Nguyen (Vietnam) acquisition (announced December 2025, closing status still unconfirmed), the closed Ganter acquisition (Germany, effective October 2, 2026), and the Saltire Facilities Management acquisition (Scotland) all extend Daikin's heating/cooling service capabilities internationally.
- Daikin's EUR 300 million residential heat pump factory in Poland received an fDi Intelligence award (March 2026), underscoring continued European manufacturing investment.
- Elliott's activist involvement remains a potential catalyst for capital-allocation changes that could support valuation.
