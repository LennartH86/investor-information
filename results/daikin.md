## Strategie & Ausblick
- Daikin continues to pursue its long-term "Fusion 30" strategy, targeting a 10% operating margin and 12% ROE by FY2028, alongside its sustainability agenda outlined in the Integrated Report 2026 and Sustainability Report 2026.
- Continued expansion in AI data-center cooling technology (new CDU product line, prior acquisitions of DDC Solutions and reportedly a direct-to-chip cooling company) positions Daikin in a high-growth adjacent market beyond its core HVAC business.
- Regional and service-network expansion continues: a planned new manufacturing facility in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia, an agreed acquisition of Vietnamese building-systems integrator Anh Nguyen Trading Technical Service (expected to close in Q1 FY2026, pending regulatory approval), and earlier European service-network acquisitions (Saltire Facilities Management in Scotland, Kylslaget in Sweden).
- An unverified March 2026 report (Kessan Insights) claimed a $3.2 billion acquisition of an unnamed US HVAC company; this could not be confirmed elsewhere and should be treated with caution pending official confirmation.
- Elliott's activist involvement remains a potential catalyst for capital-allocation changes (buybacks, portfolio optimization) that could support valuation over time.
