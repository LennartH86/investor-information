  _EN: The stock fell further to EUR 26.13 over the reporting week, putting the position in clearly negative territory versus the purchase price for the first time (-3.52%), well below Bernstein's recently lowered price target of EUR 28.10 and further still below the broader analyst consensus of around EUR 35.84. ## Management
- CEO Tim Höttges hob zuletzt hervor, dass er täglich 20 bis 30 Prozent seiner Zeit dem Thema Künstliche Intelligenz widmet und die Telekom hier eine Führungsrolle einnehmen müsse; dank KI-generiertem Code (teils 95%) sei die Entwicklungszeit für neue Software von früher drei Monaten auf teils nur 24 Stunden gesunken. \
  _EN: CEO Tim Höttges recently stressed that he dedicates 20 to 30 percent of his time each day to AI and that Deutsche Telekom must take a leading role here; thanks to AI-generated code (up to 95% in some cases), development time for new software has fallen from as much as three months previously to as little as 24 hours in some cases._
- Höttges und das Management präsentieren die KI-Strategie und Monetarisierungspläne beim AI Investor Day am 05.10.2026 ausführlich den Investoren. \
  _EN: Höttges and management will present the AI strategy and monetisation plans to investors in detail at the AI Investor Day on 05.10.2026._
