  _EN: The stock trades at EUR 26.80, almost unchanged versus the purchase price (-1.05%), after briefly dipping to EUR 26.67 (-1.22%) on 28.09.; Bernstein cut its rating to "Market-Perform" on 25.09.   _EN: CEO Tim Höttges is reportedly still pursuing the idea of a new holding company that could bring together Deutsche Telekom and T-Mobile US under common ownership via share purchases; talks remain at an early stage and are subject to regulatory approvals in Germany and the US._
