## Strategy & Outlook
- The new 20-year, up-to-207 MW power purchase agreement for a Texas data center (power available Q3 2027) extends Vistra's pattern of long-term contracted deals tied to AI/data-center demand growth.
- The Cogentrix Energy acquisition (definitive agreements executed 31.12.2025/announced 05.01.2026) covers roughly 5,500 MW of gas generation across PJM, ISO New England and ERCOT; aggregate consideration is approximately USD 2.3bn in cash (net of assuming ~USD 1.5bn of Cogentrix debt) plus 5,000,000 Vistra shares valued at USD 185/share. The deal remains on track to close in mid-to-late 2026, subject to remaining FERC, DOJ/Hart-Scott-Rodino and state regulatory approvals; management expects mid-single-digit per-share accretion in 2027 and high-single-digit accretion on average over 2027–2029.
- The USD 1.5bn subordinated notes offering signals continued active balance-sheet management to support growth capex and upcoming preferred-stock resets without diluting equity.
- Earlier agreements with Meta Platforms for 20-year power purchase agreements covering 2,609 MW of nuclear capacity from the Perry, Davis-Besse and Beaver Valley plants remain part of the company's data-center/AI-demand growth strategy.
- Key items to watch: closing and integration progress of Cogentrix, further analyst rating/price-target updates (Morgan Stanley's raised USD 227 target), and execution on the new Texas data-center PPA.
