## Strategie & Ausblick
- The approximately USD 4bn Cogentrix Energy acquisition (announced January 2026, ~5,500 MW of gas generation across PJM, ISO New England and ERCOT) has cleared FERC review and remains on track to close in mid-to-late 2026, still subject to remaining regulatory approvals (DOJ Hart-Scott-Rodino and certain state approvals).
- Helix Digital Infrastructure — the roughly USD 10bn venture with KKR, KIA and NVIDIA for integrated data-center power, connectivity and infrastructure serving AI hyperscalers — continues to develop, backed by an initial Vistra commitment of up to USD 1.0bn, with Vistra positioned as preferred power provider.
- PJM Interconnection's proposals to cap wholesale electricity prices are a key emerging risk to watch, as they contributed directly to the recent share-price decline and could pressure merchant power economics if adopted.
- Long-duration contracted cash flows remain underpinned by nuclear power purchase agreements, including 20-year deals with Meta covering 2,609 MW of capacity (Perry, Davis-Besse and Beaver Valley plants) plus associated uprates.
- Key items to watch: the outcome of the PJM price-cap proposals, final regulatory clearance and closing of Cogentrix, integration progress at Helix, and whether retail/generation margins hold up as prior hedges roll off through the remainder of 2026.
