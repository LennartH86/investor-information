## Strategy & Outlook
- The Cogentrix acquisition (~$4.0 billion, ~5,500 MW of gas-fired capacity across PJM, ISO-NE and ERCOT) closed in October 2026 and is expected to be mid-single-digit accretive to Adjusted FCFbG per share starting in 2027, and high-single-digit accretive on average over 2027–2029.
- This follows the 2025 acquisition of seven gas plants (2,600 MW) from Lotus Infrastructure Partners for $1.9 billion, continuing a pattern of growing dispatchable generation capacity.
- Vistra continues to position itself around data-center- and AI-driven power demand, including long-term PPAs (e.g., a 2.6 GW, 20-year nuclear PPA with Meta announced in January 2026) and management's expectation of 5–6% annual ERCOT load growth through 2030.
- Capital allocation remains focused on ongoing share buybacks (newly authorized $1.5 billion through 2028) alongside disciplined leverage management (target: net debt below 3x EBITDA).
- Management continues to emphasize nuclear and natural gas as strategic long-term bets to meet reliability and demand needs, complemented by solar and storage expansion.
