## Strategie & Ausblick
- The approximately USD 4bn Cogentrix Energy acquisition (announced January 2026, ~5,500 MW of gas generation across PJM, ISO New England and ERCOT) has cleared FERC review and remains on track to close in mid-to-late 2026; management expects mid-single-digit per-share accretion in 2027 and high-single-digit accretion on average over 2027–2029.
- Combined with the Lotus acquisition (closed October 2025, USD 1.9bn, ~2,600 MW), the two deals could add more than 8.1 GW of modern gas generation to Vistra's fleet.
- Helix Digital Infrastructure — the roughly USD 10bn venture with KKR, the Kuwait Investment Authority and NVIDIA for integrated data-center power, connectivity and infrastructure serving AI hyperscalers — continues to develop, with Vistra positioned as preferred power provider.
- The new USD 1.5bn subordinated notes offering signals continued active balance-sheet management to support growth capex and upcoming preferred-stock resets without diluting equity.
- Key items to watch: closing and integration progress of Cogentrix, continued build-out of Helix, whether the wide gap between share price and analyst targets narrows, and any further insider transaction activity following Burke's Intent to Sell filing.
