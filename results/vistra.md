## Strategie & Ausblick
- The approximately USD 4bn Cogentrix Energy acquisition (announced January 2026, ~5,500 MW of gas generation across PJM, ISO New England and ERCOT) has cleared FERC review and remains on track to close in mid-to-late 2026, still subject to remaining approvals.
- Management expects Cogentrix to deliver mid-single-digit per-share accretion in 2027 and high-single-digit accretion on average over 2027–2029.
- Helix Digital Infrastructure, formed with KKR, KIA and NVIDIA, continues as Vistra's platform for integrated data-center power solutions, backed by an initial Vistra commitment of up to USD 1.0bn.
- Long-duration contracted cash flows are underpinned by nuclear power purchase agreements, including 20-year deals with Meta covering 2,609 MW of capacity (Perry, Davis-Besse and Beaver Valley plants) plus associated uprates.
- Key items to watch: final regulatory clearance and closing of Cogentrix, integration progress at Helix, and whether retail/generation margins hold up as prior hedges roll off through the remainder of 2026.
