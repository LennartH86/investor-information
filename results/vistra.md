## Strategie & Ausblick / Strategy & Outlook
- The approximately $4.7 billion acquisition of Cogentrix Energy (ten gas-fired plants, roughly 5,500 MW across PJM, ISO New England and ERCOT) remains pending following FERC approval; closing continues to be targeted for mid-to-late 2026. Vistra closed a $2.25 billion private note offering in late January to help fund the deal.
- Helix Digital Infrastructure, a venture formed with NVIDIA, KKR and the Kuwait Investment Authority, positions Vistra as a preferred power supplier; Vistra's initial investment commitment is up to $1.0 billion.
- Reports of a potential ~$4 billion federal loan to upgrade Vistra's nuclear fleet signal continued policy tailwinds for the company's nuclear generation assets.
- Together with the Lotus Infrastructure acquisition closed in October 2025 (~$1.9 billion, 2,600 MW), Vistra continues building a larger, modern generation fleet to serve growing AI/data-center electricity demand, including new long-term power purchase agreements such as the 207 MW deal with New Era Technology in Texas.
- Extensive share buybacks and growth investments (gas plants, data-center power deals, nuclear upgrades) remain central pillars of capital allocation heading into 2027.
