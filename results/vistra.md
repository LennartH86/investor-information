## Recent News
- Shares rallied in early October on reports the U.S. government may offer Vistra up to roughly $4 billion (some reports cite $4.2 billion) in conditional financing to upgrade its nuclear fleet.
- Vistra will report Q3 2026 financial and operating results on Friday, Nov. 6, 2026, with a conference call/webcast starting 10:00 a.m. ET.
- On Sep 25, 2026, New Era reportedly secured 207 MW of capacity with Vistra for a Texas data center.
- Texas Governor Greg Abbott ordered an audit (announced in August) of planned data centers seeking ERCOT grid connections, and ERCOT postponed its "Batch Zero" review of large new loads; Vistra says its Comanche Peak data-center project still targets power delivery by the end of 2027 despite this regulatory friction.
- Stock is down roughly 28% over the trailing year per one analysis, closing around $152.02 on Oct 16, 2026.

## Management
- CEO Jim Burke described a "structurally improved demand environment" on the Q2 2026 earnings call, projecting annual load growth of at least 4-6% in ERCOT and 2-3% in PJM through 2030.
- Burke reportedly bought 2,000 shares in the open market at $135 (~$270,000) on Aug 24, 2026, disclosed via an SEC Form 4 — an insider purchase signaling management confidence.

## Financials
- Q2 2026 (reported Aug 7, 2026): Net income of $305 million; Ongoing Operations Adjusted EBITDA of $1,767 million. Net income fell year-over-year mainly due to a $488 million increase in unrealized mark-to-market losses on derivatives; Adjusted EBITDA growth was driven by higher energy/capacity prices and contributions from the Lotus-acquired plants.
- Q1 2026 (reported May 7, 2026): Net income of $1,029 million; Ongoing Operations Adjusted EBITDA of $1,494 million; Vistra's corporate credit rating was upgraded to investment grade by a second major rating agency.
- Full-year 2026 guidance reaffirmed: Ongoing Operations Adjusted EBITDA of $6.80-7.60 billion and Adjusted Free Cash Flow before growth of $3.925-4.725 billion; guidance excludes any benefit from the pending Cogentrix acquisition and recently signed Meta power purchase agreements (expected to contribute from 2027).
- Completed a $2.25 billion private notes offering in late January 2026 ($1.0 billion of 4.700% senior secured notes due 2031 and $1.25 billion of 5.350% notes due 2036) to help fund the Cogentrix acquisition.

## Strategy & Outlook
- Pending acquisition of Cogentrix Energy's gas-fired generation portfolio (~5,500 MW across 10 plants) from Quantum Capital Group for ~$4.7 billion (cash, Vistra stock, and assumed debt); FERC approval has been received, with closing anticipated mid-to-late 2026.
- Formed "Helix Digital Infrastructure" with KKR, the Kuwait Investment Authority, and NVIDIA, with an initial Vistra commitment of up to $1.0 billion; Vistra will serve as Helix's preferred power provider, tying the company further into AI/data-center power demand.
- Continued build-out of power supply for large data-center loads (e.g., the New Era deal and the Comanche Peak nuclear-adjacent data center project), positioning Vistra to benefit from structural electricity-demand growth, though subject to Texas regulatory/ERCOT interconnection timing risk.
- Nuclear fleet upgrades could be partly financed by a prospective multi-billion-dollar federal loan/financing package, which would support long-term baseload nuclear generation strategy if finalized.
