## Aktuelle Meldungen
- Vistra reported Q2 2026 results on 7 August 2026 (Form 8-K and 10-Q filed the same day): revenue of USD 4.017bn, down 5.5% from USD 4.250bn a year earlier and clearly below the ~USD 5.73bn consensus, while Ongoing Operations Adjusted EBITDA rose 31% to USD 1.767bn.
- Shares initially slipped on the revenue miss but recovered; the stock was trading above USD 139 in late August 2026 as investors digested the stronger EBITDA and the unchanged full-year outlook.
- Vistra received Federal Energy Regulatory Commission approval for its pending USD 4bn acquisition of Cogentrix Energy (10 modern gas plants, ~5,500 MW), a key step toward closing in mid-to-late 2026.
- The company announced Helix Digital Infrastructure, a data-center power venture with KKR, KIA and NVIDIA, to which Vistra is committing up to USD 1.0bn.
- Management publicly backed the proposed pause on new Texas data-center development pending an audit of the ERCOT interconnection queue.
- Around 24 August 2026 the CEO purchased roughly USD 270,000 of Vistra common stock, alongside several analyst price-target revisions.

## Management
- President and CEO Jim Burke led the Q2 2026 earnings call on 7 August 2026 and said the company remains on track for record full-year results, citing favourable hedging and strong plant performance through peak summer load in PJM and ERCOT.
- Burke described the Helix platform as a "rack-to-grid one-stop shop solution" for data-center customers, framing it as Vistra's answer to digital-economy power demand.
- Executives argued that power demand growth extends beyond AI data centers, pointing to broader electrification as a structural driver.
- Vistra executives supported the Texas data-center pause and the ERCOT queue audit, positioning the company as constructive on grid reliability rather than purely growth-maximising.
- The reported CEO open-market share purchase of about USD 270,000 in late August 2026 signals insider confidence at current price levels.

## Finanzielles
- Q2 2026: operating revenues USD 4,017m, operating income USD 553m, net income USD 305m, diluted EPS USD 0.76 (Q2 2025: USD 0.81).
- Q2 2026 Ongoing Operations Adjusted EBITDA of USD 1,767m vs. USD 1,349m a year earlier (+30.8%, +USD 418m), driven by higher realised energy and capacity prices and a full quarter's contribution from the plants acquired from Lotus.
- Segment split in Q2 2026: retail contributed USD 994m of adjusted EBITDA, generation USD 773m.
- Q1 2026: GAAP net income of USD 1,029m, including a USD 723m unrealised gain on hedges settling in future years, and Ongoing Operations Adjusted EBITDA of USD 1,494m.
- Full-year 2026 guidance reaffirmed: Ongoing Operations Adjusted EBITDA of USD 6.8–7.6bn and Ongoing Operations Adjusted Free Cash Flow of USD 3.925–4.725bn.
- No explicit P/E or P/B multiples were published in the sources reviewed for this period.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- The USD 4bn cash-and-stock Cogentrix Energy acquisition (announced 5 January 2026, ~5,500 MW of gas generation) has cleared FERC and is expected to close in mid-to-late 2026, subject to remaining DOJ and state approvals.
- Management expects Cogentrix to deliver mid-single-digit per-share accretion in 2027 and high-single-digit accretion on average over 2027–2029.
- The October 2025 Lotus Infrastructure transaction (seven gas plants, 2,600 MW, base purchase price USD 1.9bn) is already contributing to earnings and was a main driver of the Q2 EBITDA increase.
- Helix Digital Infrastructure with KKR, KIA and NVIDIA (Vistra commitment up to USD 1.0bn) is the vehicle for integrated data-center power offerings.
- Two 20-year power purchase agreements with Meta and Amazon Web Services underpin long-duration contracted cash flows.
- Key watch items: closing conditions for Cogentrix, the outcome of the ERCOT data-center interconnection audit, and whether retail and generation margins hold as hedges roll off.
