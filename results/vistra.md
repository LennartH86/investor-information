## Aktuelle Meldungen
- Vistra reported Q3 2026 results on November 6, 2026: GAAP net income of $1,140 million (including a $559 million unrealized hedge gain) and Ongoing Operations Adjusted EBITDA of $2,378 million, up more than 50% year over year.
- The company narrowed and raised the midpoint of its 2026 Ongoing Operations Adjusted EBITDA guidance to $7.1–$7.5 billion and initiated 2027 guidance of $7.7–$8.5 billion.
- Shares reportedly rose about 10.6% following the earnings release; earlier in the week (Nov 4) a KeyCorp downgrade to "sector weight" was linked to a roughly 13.9% single-day drop in the stock.
- The board authorized an additional $1.5 billion in share repurchases, expected to be utilized by year-end 2028.
- Vistra's 405 MW Pulaski Solar Facility in Illinois began operating under a power purchase agreement with Microsoft, and a 100 MW solar-plus-storage facility in California also commenced operations.
- A new 20-year, 207 MW power purchase agreement was signed with New Era Energy and Digital at Vistra's Odessa plant in Texas.
- Management (CEO Jim Burke) cited commercial fleet availability of nearly 95% during the quarter.

## Management
- CEO Jim Burke received the Legacy Award in D CEO Magazine's 2026 Energy Awards, profiled for his long-term bets on nuclear and natural gas positioning Vistra for the current electricity-demand boom.
- Burke appeared at CERAWeek 2026, discussing rising data center power demand, grid reliability, and the growing role of nuclear generation.
- Burke was featured on the Texas Power Podcast (Episode 7, with host Doug Lewin), covering ERCOT market dynamics, the energy transition, and Vistra's reliability focus as the largest power generator in Texas.
- On the Q1 2026 earnings call, Burke stated that annual ERCOT load growth of at least 5–6% through 2030 is a reasonable expectation.
- On the Q4 2025 earnings call, Burke projected 2026 adjusted free cash flow before growth (AFCFbG) per share above $12.50, rising to approximately $16 by 2027.

## Finanzielles
- Q3 2026: GAAP net income $1,140 million; Ongoing Operations Adjusted EBITDA $2,378 million (+50%+ YoY).
- Q2 2026: Net income $305 million (down from $327 million YoY), largely due to $488 million in unrealized mark-to-market derivative losses, partly offset by higher realized prices and capacity revenue; Adjusted EBITDA of $1,767 million.
- Q1 2026: GAAP net income $1,029 million (including a large unrealized hedge gain); Adjusted EBITDA $1,494 million; adjusted EPS reported around $2.87, ahead of consensus estimates (~$2.21).
- Full-year 2025 (reported Feb 26, 2026): EPS of $2.34 (narrowly missing estimates by $0.01), revenue of $4.58 billion for the quarter reported.
- 2026 full-year Ongoing Operations Adjusted EBITDA guidance: $7.1–$7.5 billion; 2027 initial guidance: $7.7–$8.5 billion.
- Long-term net leverage target reaffirmed at below 3x.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- Vistra closed its approximately $4.0 billion acquisition of Cogentrix Energy in October 2026, adding roughly 5,500 MW of gas-fired generation capacity across PJM, ISO New England, and ERCOT; the deal is expected to be mid-single-digit accretive to Ongoing Operations AFCFbG per share in 2027.
- This follows an earlier 2025 acquisition of seven gas plants (2,600 MW) from Lotus Infrastructure Partners for $1.9 billion, continuing a pattern of expanding dispatchable generation capacity.
- The company continues to position itself around surging data center and AI-driven electricity demand, highlighted by new long-term PPAs (Microsoft at Pulaski Solar, New Era Energy and Digital at Odessa) and strong anticipated ERCOT load growth (5–6% annually through 2030 per management).
- Capital allocation priorities include continued share buybacks ($1.5 billion newly authorized, through 2028) alongside disciplined leverage management (sub-3x net leverage target).
- Management continues to emphasize nuclear and natural gas as long-term strategic bets to meet rising reliability and demand needs, alongside solar and storage additions.
