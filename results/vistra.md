## Strategie & Ausblick
- The approximately USD 4bn Cogentrix Energy acquisition (announced 05.01.2026, ~5,500 MW / 5,496 MW of gas generation across PJM, ISO New England and ERCOT) remains on track to close in mid-to-late 2026, subject to remaining regulatory approvals (FERC, DOJ/Hart-Scott-Rodino, and state regulators); management expects mid-single-digit per-share accretion in 2027 and high-single-digit accretion on average over 2027–2029.
- Days after announcing Cogentrix, Vistra had unveiled agreements with Meta Platforms supporting 20-year power purchase agreements covering 2,609 MW of nuclear capacity from its Perry, Davis-Besse and Beaver Valley plants — described as the largest corporate-backed nuclear uprate program in U.S. history.
- The new USD 1.5bn subordinated notes offering signals continued active balance-sheet management to support growth capex and upcoming preferred-stock resets without diluting equity.
- Key items to watch: closing and integration progress of Cogentrix, analyst price-target revisions following the recent share-price pullback, and the upcoming USD 0.23/share dividend (ex-date 21.09.2026).
