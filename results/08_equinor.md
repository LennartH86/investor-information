# Equinor (EQNR)
_KW26 / 2026 | Zeitraum: 20.06.2026 – 27.06.2026_

## Aktuelle Meldungen

- **Troll Field Expansion – $412 million investment (June 19):** Equinor and its partners Petoro, Shell, TotalEnergies, and ConocoPhillips agreed to invest just over NOK 4 billion (~USD 412 million) in a new subsea development to boost gas production from Norway's Troll field. The project will increase gas supply to Europe and is directly linked to European energy security goals. ([Equinor.com](https://www.equinor.com/news/20260619-expanding-troll-more-gas-to-europe)) ([TradingView / Reuters](https://www.tradingview.com/news/reuters.com,2026:newsml_L8N42R0BO:0-equinor-to-boost-gas-production-from-norway-s-troll-gas-field/)) ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/energy/articles/equinor-boost-troll-gas-output-153000121.html))
- **Capital Markets Day 2026 – follow-up (June 22):** Equinor formally presented the full details of its 2026 strategy at a Capital Markets Day event, confirming targets of 2.3 MMboe/day production by 2030, doubling the 2026 share buy-back to USD 3 billion, and retiring the 10–12 GW renewable energy capacity goal for 2030. ([Equinor.com](https://www.equinor.com/news/20260616-equinor-capital-markets-day-2026)) ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/16/3312355/0/en/equinor-s-capital-markets-day-2026.html))
- **Equinor drops 2030 renewable energy target:** Equinor officially retired its target to reach 10–12 GW of installed renewable energy capacity by 2030, citing less favorable project economics. CEO Opedal stated the company had "seen for several years we won't reach that target." ([ESG Today](https://www.esgtoday.com/equinor-drops-renewable-energy-goals-seen-for-several-years-we-wont-reach-that-target/)) ([Recharge News](https://www.rechargenews.com/offshore-wind/equinor-ceo-admits-defeat-as-company-drops-offshore-wind-targets/2-1-2004912))
- **Esquina do Vento wind acquisition in Brazil (March 2026, strategic continuation):** Equinor acquired the 230 MW Esquina do Vento onshore wind complex (51 turbines, Rio Grande do Norte) from Vestas, with construction set to begin Q2 2026 and commercial operations targeted for 2028. Expected returns are double-digit. This is part of Equinor's integrated power strategy in Brazil via its Rio Energy subsidiary. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/energy/articles/equinor-expands-brazil-renewables-230-104000902.html)) ([Equinor.com](https://www.equinor.com/news/20260323-strengthens-integrated-power-portfolio-brazil))
- **Share buy-back – second tranche details:** From June 8 to 12, 2026, Equinor purchased 414,792 own shares at an average price of NOK 353.25 per share as part of the second tranche of the 2026 buy-back programme. ([StockTitan/SEC 6-K](https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/EQNR/6-k-equinor-asa-current-report-foreign-issuer-9aff101f0bd6.html))

## Management

- **CEO Anders Opedal on CNBC (June 16):** Anders Opedal appeared on CNBC's "The Exchange" to discuss the impact of the U.S.–Iran deal on oil prices, AI-driven power demand, and Equinor's strategic direction. ([CNBC Video](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/06/16/watch-cnbcs-full-interview-with-equinor-ceo-anders-opedal.html))
- **Opedal admits offshore wind defeat:** In an interview with Norwegian daily Dagens Næringsliv (DN), Opedal acknowledged that Equinor has not actively pursued its renewable energy targets "for years." He stated: "We have seen for several years we won't reach that target." The 10–12 GW 2030 renewable capacity goal was formally abandoned. ([Recharge News](https://www.rechargenews.com/offshore-wind/equinor-ceo-admits-defeat-as-company-drops-offshore-wind-targets/2-1-2004912)) ([ESG Today](https://www.esgtoday.com/equinor-drops-renewable-energy-goals-seen-for-several-years-we-wont-reach-that-target/))
- **Opedal on politicization of energy:** Opedal commented that energy investments are becoming "politicalized and polarized," citing political debates in the U.S. and U.K. as sources of increasing risk for energy project developers. He referenced the Empire Wind project in the U.S. as a case study, where construction was stopped twice by the Trump administration before permits were reinstated. ([E&E News / Politico](https://www.eenews.net/articles/equinor-ceo-energy-investments-becoming-politicalized-and-polarized/))
- **Offshore wind bar raised:** Equinor has quietly raised its internal hurdle rate for offshore wind investments, requiring higher returns before committing capital — a shift that effectively limits new offshore wind project approvals. ([Upstream Online](https://www.upstreamonline.com/energy-transition/how-equinor-has-quietly-raised-its-bar-for-offshore-wind-investment/2-1-2007466))

## Strategie & Ausblick

- **Production target 2030:** Production target of 2.3 MMboe/day by 2030 — an increase of 150,000 boe/day from current levels. Norwegian Continental Shelf (NCS) production target raised by 100,000 boe/day to 1.35 MMboe/day; international oil and gas production to grow 30% to 950,000 boe/day. ([World Oil](https://worldoil.com/news/2026/6/16/equinor-targets-2-3-mmboed-by-2030-boosts-oil-and-gas-investment/))
- **Capital allocation:** Annual capex guidance of approximately USD 13 billion for 2026. Approximately 60% of capex allocated to NCS, ~30% to international oil and gas, and ~10% to power/renewables. USD 11–13 billion per year expected from 2028–2030.
- **Power/renewables scaled back — 20 TWh by 2030:** Despite dropping its GW capacity target, Equinor still aims to reach more than 20 TWh of power output by 2030, a fourfold increase. Around 10% of capex will be allocated to integrated power business development. Focus is on selected markets such as Brazil, where integration with broader energy value chains is achievable. ([Equinor.com – Energy Transition Plan](https://www.equinor.com/sustainability/energy-transition-plan))
- **Troll field as strategic gas supplier:** The Troll expansion decision signals Equinor's continued commitment to Norwegian gas as a cornerstone of European energy security, especially post-2022 geopolitical shifts. More gas from Troll is expected to increase long-term supply flexibility for European buyers.
- **Scatec stake reduction:** Equinor has sold some of its shares in renewable energy firm Scatec, maintaining an 8.05% stake. This signals a selective, rather than expansive, approach to third-party renewables exposure. ([IndexBox](https://www.indexbox.io/blog/equinor-reduces-stake-in-renewable-energy-firm-scatec/))
- **Empire Wind (U.S.):** The U.S. offshore wind project Empire Wind has been permitted to resume construction after two Trump administration stop-work orders. Equinor continues to monitor political risk in the U.S. renewables market closely.
- **Oil and gas growth over energy transition:** The overall strategic message from KW26 is a decisive reaffirmation of oil and gas growth as the core engine of Equinor's value creation, with renewables repositioned as a selective, integrated-power complement rather than a standalone growth pillar.
