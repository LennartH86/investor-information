# Equinor (EQNR)
_KW22 / 2026 | Period: 23.05.2026 – 30.05.2026_

## Recent News

- **Share Buy-Back – Second Tranche Update (27 May 2026):** From 19 May to 22 May 2026, Equinor ASA repurchased a total of 312,060 own shares at an average price of NOK 369.06 per share under the second tranche of its 2026 share buy-back programme. The second tranche has a total volume of up to USD 375 million and runs until 20 July 2026 at the latest. The overall 2026 buy-back programme amounts to up to USD 1.5 billion.
- **Insider Share Allocations (20 May 2026):** Certain primary insiders and their close associates received allocations of Equinor shares through the company's share saving plan and long-term incentive programme at a price of NOK 356.31 per share. This constitutes a notifiable transaction under the EU Market Abuse Regulation.
- **Supplier Agreements for Drilling Services (early May 2026):** Equinor extended key supplier agreements for drilling and well services with a combined value of approximately NOK 17 billion. These agreements are designed to maintain production levels on the Norwegian Continental Shelf (NCS) and contribute to stable energy supplies to Europe.
- **Annual General Meeting – Minutes Published (13 May 2026):** The AGM held on 12 May 2026 approved the annual report and accounts for 2025 and a cash dividend of USD 0.39 per share for Q4 2025. Seven shareholder proposals were submitted; none were adopted.

## Management

- **CEO Anders Opedal** (President & CEO) continues to lead Equinor's dual-mandate strategy of growing oil & gas production while investing in renewables. Key statements from recent interviews and the Q1 2026 earnings call:
  - **Russian Gas:** At the CERAWeek conference in Houston, Opedal stated that a return of Russian gas to the EU market during the ongoing war in Ukraine would be "very, very difficult," citing political resistance from European governments.
  - **Politicisation of Energy Investment:** Opedal warned that energy investments are increasingly "politicalized and polarized," referencing challenges with offshore wind developments in the US and UK.
  - **Production Outlook:** Equinor recorded record production in 2025 and expects approximately 3% production growth in 2026. The company plans 26 exploration and appraisal wells offshore Norway in 2026.
  - **Capital Allocation:** Organic capex guidance for 2026 is approximately USD 13 billion. Long-term, Equinor plans to invest around USD 60 billion per year over the next decade, including 250 exploration wells and 75 subsea field developments.

## Financials

- **Q1 2026 Results (reported 6 May 2026):**
  - Adjusted Operating Income: **USD 9.77 billion**
  - Adjusted Net Income: **USD 3.70 billion** (Adjusted EPS: USD 1.48; +29% YoY)
  - Net Income (IFRS): **USD 3.10 billion** (+18% vs. Q1 2025: USD 2.6 billion)
  - Net Operating Income: **USD 8.78 billion**
  - Operating Cash Flow (after tax): **USD 6.02 billion**
  - Net Debt to Capital Employed (adjusted): **15.3%**
- **Record Production Q1 2026:** Total equity production of **2,313 mboe/d** (+9% vs. Q1 2025: 2,123 mboe/d), driven by growth on the NCS (Johan Castberg, Halten East, Verdande) and international assets. NCS production rose approximately 10% year-on-year.
- **Dividend Q1 2026:** Cash dividend of USD 0.39 per share for Q1 2026, consistent with Q4 2025.
- **Revenue Note:** Despite the EPS increase, total revenue declined slightly year-on-year due to lower European gas prices, partially offset by higher liquids prices and higher US gas prices.

## Strategy & Outlook

- **Production Growth:** Equinor targets ~3% equity production growth for full-year 2026, supported by new field start-ups on the NCS and continued international expansion. 26 exploration and appraisal wells are planned offshore Norway in 2026.
- **Brazil – Raia Project (USD ~9 billion):** Equinor commenced drilling operations in early 2026 for the Raia offshore gas development off Brazil, its largest international investment to date. Equinor operates with a 35% stake; partners are Repsol Sinopec Brasil (35%) and Petrobras (30%). Production is targeted to begin around 2029–2030.
- **Renewables – Esquina do Vento (Brazil):** Via subsidiary Rio Energy, Equinor acquired the 230 MW ready-to-build Esquina do Vento onshore wind complex from Vestas Wind Systems. Construction is expected to begin in 2026.
- **Ørsted Stake:** Equinor completed its acquisition of a 10% shareholding in Ørsted A/S (originally announced October 2024), reinforcing its offshore wind positioning.
- **Halten East – Stake Increase:** Equinor signed an agreement to acquire Sval Energi's 11.8% interest in the Halten East Unit, raising Equinor's ownership to 69.5% and strengthening its NCS production base.
- **Bay du Nord (Canada):** CEO Opedal indicated he hopes to approve the Final Investment Decision for the Bay du Nord offshore oil project (CA$14 billion, 160,000 bbl/d capacity) in 2027, targeting first production by 2032.
