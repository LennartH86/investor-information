# Equinor (EQNR)
_KW24 / 2026 | Zeitraum: 06.06.2026 – 13.06.2026_

## Aktuelle Meldungen

**New Board Chair Elected (June 8, 2026)**
The corporate assembly of Equinor ASA elected Jarle Roth as the new chair of the board of directors on June 8, 2026. Anne Drinkwater was re-elected as deputy chair. Board members Finn Bjørn Ruyter, Haakon Bruun-Hanssen, Mikael Karlsson, Fernanda Lopes Larsen, and Dawn Summers were all re-elected. Outgoing chair Jon Erik Reinhardsen stepped down from the board.

**Share Buy-Back Programme – Second Tranche (June 9, 2026)**
Equinor announced the start of the second tranche of its 2026 share buy-back programme. Between June 1 and June 5, 2026, the company purchased a total of 408,516 own shares at an average price of NOK 350.5696 per share. The total 2026 buyback programme amounts to up to USD 1.5 billion, including shares to be redeemed from the Norwegian State. The company's treasury stake has risen to 2.57%.

**Energy Perspectives 2026 (June 11, 2026)**
Equinor published its 16th edition of "Energy Perspectives 2026", which explores how shifting geopolitical and economic dynamics shape long-term global energy developments toward 2050. The report is set in a context of heightened geopolitical tension, economic fragmentation, and increased uncertainty in energy markets.

**Itaimbezinho Block, Brazil (June 10, 2026)**
Petrobras signed an agreement with Equinor Brasil Energia Ltda. for the acquisition of a 50% interest in the Itaimbezinho exploration block in the offshore Campos Basin, further expanding Equinor's footprint in Brazil.

## Management

Equinor CEO **Anders Opedal** has been publicly active on several strategic fronts in 2026:

- At the **CERAWeek conference in Houston** (March 2026), Opedal stated that the EU is unlikely to increase Russian gas imports despite ongoing supply disruptions, citing the difficulty of reintroducing Russian gas during the continuing war in Ukraine.
- In **February 2026**, following the earnings call for full-year 2025, Opedal indicated he would "think twice" about investing in energy projects after President Donald Trump's attacks on offshore wind. This was in the context of Equinor's Empire Wind project, which had been stopped twice by the Trump administration before being allowed to resume construction.
- Opedal presented Q1 2026 results at a **press conference on May 6, 2026**, highlighting record production and strong financial performance.
- Opedal characterized the energy investment environment as becoming increasingly "politicalized and polarized," signalling heightened caution around politically exposed projects.

On the governance side, incoming board chair **Jarle Roth** replaces long-standing chair Jon Erik Reinhardsen, effective as of the June 8 election.

## Finanzielles

**Q1 2026 Results (reported May 6, 2026)**

Equinor delivered strong first-quarter 2026 results, posting record production:

| Metric | Q1 2026 |
|---|---|
| Adjusted operating income | USD 9.77 billion |
| Adjusted net income after tax | USD 2.86 billion |
| Adjusted net income (total) | USD 3.70 billion (>2x YoY) |
| Net operating income | USD 8.78 billion |
| Net income (IFRS) | USD 3.10 billion |
| Adjusted earnings per share | USD 1.48 |
| Oil & gas production | 2,313 mboe/day (+9% YoY, record high) |
| Renewable power generation growth | +29% YoY |

Adjusted operating income and adjusted net income more than doubled compared to a year earlier, supported by a 9% rise in oil and gas production and higher liquids and US gas prices. Renewable power generation growth of 29% was driven by the Dogger Bank offshore wind farm and new onshore assets.

The first tranche of the 2026 share buy-back programme was completed on March 27, 2026, with a total value of USD 375 million. The second tranche is running through July 20, 2026.

Dividend for Q1 2026 was set at USD 0.39 per share.

## Strategie & Ausblick

**Brazil – Largest International Investment (~USD 9 billion)**
The Raia gas development project offshore Brazil is Equinor's largest international investment to date, with a total estimated investment of approximately USD 9 billion. Drilling commenced in March 2026, underscoring the company's long-term commitment to Brazil as a core growth market. The new Itaimbezinho block deal with Petrobras (June 2026) further broadens Equinor's Brazilian upstream portfolio.

**Renewable Energy Expansion**
Equinor completed the acquisition of a 10% shareholding in Danish offshore wind developer Ørsted A/S (deal initially announced October 7, 2024), signalling continued strategic interest in offshore wind. Additionally, the company acquired the ready-to-build 230 MW Esquina do Vento onshore wind complex in Brazil from Vestas Wind Systems through its Rio Energy subsidiary.

**Norwegian Continental Shelf – Halten East**
Equinor signed an agreement to acquire Sval Energi's 11.8% share in the Halten East Unit, increasing its ownership to 69.5% and reinforcing its dominant position on the Norwegian Continental Shelf.

**Capital Allocation & Shareholder Returns**
The 2026 share buy-back programme of up to USD 1.5 billion reflects management's confidence in cash generation and commitment to returning capital to shareholders, even while pursuing growth investments in both oil & gas and renewables.

**Long-Term Energy Outlook**
Equinor's Energy Perspectives 2026 report signals active monitoring of diverging global energy pathways to 2050. The company is navigating geopolitical fragmentation and energy market uncertainty as it balances its core oil & gas operations with growing renewable energy investments. CEO Opedal's public statements in 2026 point to disciplined capital allocation and sensitivity to politically driven investment risk.
