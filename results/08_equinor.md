# Equinor (EQNR)
_KW24 / 2026 | Zeitraum: 06.06.2026 – 13.06.2026_

## Aktuelle Meldungen

**Board Leadership Change (June 8, 2026)**
The corporate assembly of Equinor ASA elected Jarle Roth as new chair of the board of directors, replacing outgoing chair Jon Erik Reinhardsen (in post since 2017). Anne Drinkwater was re-elected as deputy chair. Finn Bjørn Ruyter, Haakon Bruun-Hanssen, Mikael Karlsson, Fernanda Lopes Larsen, and Dawn Summers were re-elected as board members. The transition was effective as of July 1, 2026.

**Share Buy-Back Program – Second Tranche (June 9, 2026)**
Equinor published details of the ongoing second tranche of its 2026 share buy-back programme. From 1–5 June 2026, the company repurchased 408,516 own shares at an average price of NOK 350.5696, totalling NOK 143,213,293.66. The tranche runs from 19 May to no later than 20 July 2026. The full 2026 programme amounts to up to USD 1.5 billion, and the treasury stake has risen to 2.57%.

**Energy Perspectives 2026 (June 11, 2026)**
Equinor published its annual Energy Perspectives 2026 report, exploring diverging pathways for global energy developments to 2050. The report highlights shifting geopolitical and economic dynamics amid heightened geopolitical tension, economic fragmentation, and increased uncertainty in energy markets.

**Brazil – Itaimbezinho Block Deal (June 10, 2026)**
Petrobras entered an agreement with Equinor Brasil Energia for the acquisition of a 50% interest in the Itaimbezinho exploration block in the offshore Campos Basin, with Equinor becoming operator with a 50% stake. This further deepens Equinor's strategic footprint in Brazil.

## Management

**CEO Anders Opedal – Key Statements in 2026**

At the CERAWeek energy conference in Houston (March 2026), CEO Anders Opedal commented on European energy security, stating the EU was unlikely to increase Russian gas imports even amid Middle East supply disruptions: "When I talk to ministers in several countries, bringing Russian gas back in the middle of the war with Ukraine would be very, very difficult."

During the Q1 2026 earnings press conference (May 6, 2026), Opedal discussed the political risk landscape for energy investments, noting they are becoming increasingly "politicalized and polarized." He cited the Empire Wind offshore project near New York – halted twice by the Trump administration before construction was permitted to resume – as an example of how political intervention is reshaping executive decision-making around energy infrastructure.

**Governance Change**
Incoming board chair Jarle Roth replaced long-standing chair Jon Erik Reinhardsen. Roth's appointment signals continuity of strategic direction, with the transition effective July 1, 2026.

## Strategie & Ausblick

**Brazil – Raia Gas Development (~USD 9 billion)**
Equinor's largest international investment to date, the Raia offshore gas project in Brazil, commenced drilling in March 2026. Equinor operates with a 35% stake; partners are Repsol Sinopec Brasil (35%) and Petrobras (30%). Production is targeted for around 2029–2030. The new Itaimbezinho block deal with Petrobras (June 2026) further broadens Equinor's Brazilian upstream portfolio.

**Renewables – Ørsted and Onshore Wind**
Equinor completed the acquisition of a 10% shareholding in Ørsted A/S (originally announced October 2024), strengthening its offshore wind position. Additionally, the company acquired the ready-to-build 230 MW Esquina do Vento onshore wind complex in Brazil from Vestas Wind Systems through its Rio Energy subsidiary.

**Norwegian Continental Shelf – Halten East**
Equinor signed an agreement to acquire Sval Energi's 11.8% interest in the Halten East Unit, increasing Equinor's ownership to 69.5% and consolidating its NCS production base.

**Long-Term Investment Outlook**
CEO Opedal reiterated that Equinor plans to invest approximately USD 60 billion over the next decade, targeting 250 exploration wells and 75 subsea field developments. The company maintains its ambition to achieve net zero for its own operations by 2030.

**Energy Perspectives 2026**
Equinor's Energy Perspectives 2026 report signals active monitoring of diverging global energy pathways to 2050. The company is navigating a context of geopolitical fragmentation and energy market uncertainty as it balances oil & gas operations with growing renewable investments.
