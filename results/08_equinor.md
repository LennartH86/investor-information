# Equinor (EQNR)
_KW23 / 2026 | Zeitraum: 30.05.2026 – 06.06.2026_

## Aktuelle Meldungen

- **Board Chair Change Recommended (June 1, 2026):** The nomination committee of Equinor ASA recommended that the corporate assembly elect Jarle Roth as the new chair of the board of directors. Jon Erik Reinhardsen, who has served as chair since 2017, requested to resign. The election is scheduled for the corporate assembly meeting on June 8, 2026, with the change taking effect from July 1, 2026, until the ordinary election in June 2027. The committee also recommended re-election of Anne Drinkwater as deputy chair, alongside Finn Bjørn Ruyter, Haakon Bruun-Hanssen, Mikael Karlsson, Fernanda Lopes Larsen, and Dawn Summers as board members.
- **Bacalhau Subsea Contract (June 2026):** Equinor awarded Ocean Installer (a Moreld subsidiary) a 4.5-year contract with extension options for subsea installation work at the Bacalhau field in Brazil. The scope covers installation of rigid well jumpers between flowlines and subsea trees, flying leads installation, and related pre-commissioning activities. The first offshore campaign is scheduled to start in 2027.
- **Capital Markets Day (June 16, 2026):** Equinor held its Capital Markets Day presenting strategy, priorities, and long-term direction. The event coincides with the 25th anniversary of Equinor's listing on the Oslo Stock Exchange and the New York Stock Exchange.

## Management

- **CEO Anders Opedal** (President & CEO) continues to lead Equinor. Key statements from recent interviews and events:
  - **Politicisation of Energy Investment:** Opedal warned that energy investments are increasingly "more and more politicalized and polarized," citing challenges with offshore wind developments in the US and UK.
  - **Long-term Investment Plan:** Equinor plans to invest approximately USD 60 billion per year over the next decade, targeting 250 exploration wells and 75 subsea field developments.
  - **Net-Zero Ambition:** Equinor maintains its ambition to achieve net zero for its own operations by 2030.
- **Governance:** Incoming board chair Jarle Roth replaces long-standing chair Jon Erik Reinhardsen (in post since 2017). Transition takes effect July 1, 2026.

## Strategie & Ausblick

- **Capital Markets Day (June 16, 2026):** Equinor presented its updated strategy and long-term direction, marking 25 years since the company's stock exchange listings.
- **Brazil – Raia Gas Project (~USD 9 billion):** Equinor's largest international investment to date. Drilling commenced in March 2026 for this offshore gas development. Equinor operates with a 35% stake; partners are Repsol Sinopec Brasil (35%) and Petrobras (30%). Production is targeted for around 2029–2030.
- **Renewables – Esquina do Vento (Brazil):** Equinor acquired the ready-to-build 230 MW Esquina do Vento onshore wind complex from Vestas Wind Systems, supporting its multi-technology power portfolio strategy in Brazil.
- **Ørsted Stake:** Equinor completed the acquisition of a 10% shareholding in Ørsted A/S (originally announced October 2024), reinforcing its offshore wind positioning.
- **Norwegian Continental Shelf – Halten East:** Equinor signed an agreement to acquire Sval Energi's 11.8% interest in the Halten East Unit, increasing Equinor's ownership to 69.5%, strengthening its NCS production base.
- **Exploration 2026:** Equinor plans to drill 26 exploration and appraisal wells offshore Norway in 2026, supporting ~3% equity production growth for the full year.
