# Equinor (EQNR)
_KW25 / 2026 | Zeitraum: 13.06.2026 – 20.06.2026_

## Aktuelle Meldungen

- **Capital Markets Day 2026 (June 16):** Equinor held its Capital Markets Day on June 16, 2026, presenting a new strategy focused on oil and gas growth, expanding cash flows, and superior shareholder returns. The event included a live CNBC interview with CEO Anders Opedal. ([Equinor.com](https://www.equinor.com/news/20260616-equinor-capital-markets-day-2026)) ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/16/3312355/0/en/Equinor-s-Capital-Markets-Day-2026.html))
- **Ringvei Vest subsea development (June 18):** Equinor and its partners agreed on the concept for Ringvei Vest, a large subsea development project linked to the Troll B platform in the Norwegian North Sea. ([StockTitan/SEC 6-K](https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/EQNR/6-k-equinor-asa-current-report-foreign-issuer-cacc9f1adf15.html))
- **Johan Sverdrup Phase 4 (June 15):** New volumes in the Johan Sverdrup area were confirmed, forming the basis for Johan Sverdrup phase 4 — a new subsea development intended to maintain production levels at Norway's largest oil field. ([Equinor.com](https://www.equinor.com/news/20260616-equinor-capital-markets-day-2026))
- **Share buy-back for employee programmes (June 18):** Equinor announced a NOK 1.97 billion (approx. 19.6 million shares) buy-back tranche for employee share incentive programmes. ([StockTitan/SEC 6-K](https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/EQNR/6-k-equinor-asa-current-report-foreign-issuer-4c901054c3f0.html))
- **Energy Perspectives 2026 (June 11):** Equinor published its annual Energy Perspectives report, analyzing how shifting geopolitical and economic dynamics may shape global energy markets through 2050. ([Equinor.com](https://www.equinor.com/news-and-media))

## Management

- **CEO Anders Opedal on CNBC (June 16):** Opedal appeared on CNBC's "The Exchange" to discuss the impact of the U.S.-Iran deal on oil markets, AI-driven power demand, and Equinor's broader strategy. ([CNBC Video](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/06/16/watch-cnbcs-full-interview-with-equinor-ceo-anders-opedal.html))
- **Shift away from renewables targets:** Opedal acknowledged that the company has not actively pursued its earlier renewable energy targets "for years," signaling a strategic pivot back toward oil and gas. ([Recharge News](https://www.rechargenews.com/offshore-wind/equinor-ceo-admits-defeat-as-company-drops-offshore-wind-targets/2-1-2004912))
- **Political risk comments:** Opedal commented on the politicization of energy investments, noting that the Trump administration's repeated interventions in the Empire Wind offshore wind project — stopping construction twice before permitting it to resume — have made energy executives more cautious about political risk in the U.S. ([E&E News / Politico](https://www.eenews.net/articles/equinor-ceo-energy-investments-becoming-politicalized-and-polarized/))

## Finanzielles

- **Q1 2026 Results (reported May 6, 2026):**
  - Adjusted operating income: USD 9.77 billion; USD 2.86 billion after tax
  - Reported net income: USD 3.10 billion (+18% year-over-year)
  - Adjusted net income: USD 3.70 billion; adjusted EPS: USD 1.48
  - Production: 2,313 mboe/day (+9% year-over-year) — a record quarterly output
  - Renewable power generation up 29%, driven by Dogger Bank and new onshore assets
  - Seven new oil and gas discoveries on the Norwegian Continental Shelf during Q1
  - First 2026 share buy-back tranche completed March 27, 2026 (total: USD 375 million)
  ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/06/3288464/0/en/Equinor-first-quarter-2026-results.html)) ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/energy/articles/equinor-first-quarter-2026-results-044500681.html))
- **Doubled 2026 share buy-back:** At Capital Markets Day, Equinor announced the doubling of its 2026 buy-back programme to USD 3 billion (previously USD 1.5 billion). ([OilPrice.com](https://oilprice.com/Company-News/Equinor-Doubles-2026-Buyback-and-Bets-on-Higher-Oil-and-Gas-Demand.html))
- **Dividend growth target:** Equinor aims to continue growing its quarterly cash dividend by more than 5% per share annually. ([StockTitan/SEC 6-K](https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/EQNR/6-k-equinor-asa-current-report-foreign-issuer-00297f358ec6.html))

## Strategie & Ausblick

- **Production target 2030:** Equinor targets production of 2.3 MMboe/day by 2030, up from current levels, primarily driven by Norwegian Continental Shelf growth and international oil and gas expansion. ([World Oil](https://worldoil.com/news/2026/6/16/equinor-targets-2-3-mmboed-by-2030-boosts-oil-and-gas-investment/)) ([ChemXplore](https://chemxplore.com/news/equinor-capital-markets-day-strategy))
- **Capital expenditure ramp-up:** Annual capex expected at ~USD 12 billion in 2027, rising to USD 11–13 billion per year from 2028–2030. Approximately 60% will go to the Norwegian Continental Shelf, 30% to international oil and gas, and 10% to power. ([Energy Voice](https://www.energyvoice.com/oilandgas/599359/equinor-investment-ramp-up/))
- **Predictable buy-back framework from 2027:** Equinor introduced a new annual buy-back framework of USD 2–4 billion starting in 2027, providing more visibility for investors. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/energy/articles/equinor-doubles-2026-buyback-bets-035500029.html))
- **Oil & gas over renewables pivot:** The strategy reflects a broader European energy sector shift — renewed emphasis on long-life hydrocarbon assets and flexible gas supply, driven by energy security concerns and AI-linked power demand growth, with renewables ambitions scaled back.
- **Empire Wind (U.S. offshore wind):** The Empire Wind project faced repeated U.S. regulatory interventions under the Trump administration but has been permitted to resume construction. Equinor continues to assess political risk in the U.S. renewables market. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/06/16/watch-cnbcs-full-interview-with-equinor-ceo-anders-opedal.html))
