# Equinor (EQNR)
_KW29 / 2026 | Zeitraum: 11.07.2026 – 18.07.2026_

## Aktuelle Meldungen

- **Johan Sverdrup crude oil assay updated (14 July 2026):** Equinor published an updated crude oil assay for the Johan Sverdrup field, providing refiners and trading counterparts with revised quality specifications for the grade. ([Equinor News](https://www.equinor.com/news/crude-oil-assay-update-14-07-2026))
- **Share buy-back – ongoing tranche progress (disclosed mid-July, covering trading in the period):** Equinor continued executing its 2026 share buy-back programme, repurchasing 507,713 own shares between 6–10 July 2026 at an average price of NOK 327.3386, for a total consideration of roughly NOK 166,194,085.05. This follows an earlier tranche update (439,635 shares repurchased 29 June–3 July) as part of the doubled USD 3 billion 2026 buy-back announced at the June Capital Markets Day. ([StockTitan / SEC 6-K filing](https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/EQNR/6-k-equinor-asa-current-report-foreign-issuer-face5ba73383.html))
- **Stock resilient amid oil-market moves (17 July 2026):** Equinor's share price held its ground in trading on 17 July 2026, with reporting pointing to firmer oil prices and continued project and capital-return momentum supporting the stock. ([ad-hoc-news.de](https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/boerse/news/ueberblick/equinor-stock-holds-its-ground-after-17-july-2026-trading/69784679)) ([Quiver Quantitative](https://www.quiverquant.com/news/EQNR+rises+as+oil+strengthens+and+Equinor+adds+project+and+capital-return+support))
- **Context carried into the period – Bay du Nord acquisition (agreed 6 July 2026):** Just ahead of the reporting week, Equinor reached agreement with bp to acquire bp's interest in the Bay du Nord project offshore Canada, taking Equinor to 100% ownership. The deal reflects bp's portfolio simplification and gives Equinor more flexibility as it matures the project toward a final investment decision (FID) planned for early 2027; it remained a talking point in coverage during the 11–18 July window. ([Equinor News](https://www.equinor.com/news/equinor-bp-bay-du-nord-project))
- **Q2 2026 results preparation – invitation for consensus estimates (published 8 July 2026, ahead of the period):** Equinor invited analysts to submit estimates for second-quarter 2026 financial results, setting the stage for the Q2 report and analyst call scheduled for 22 July 2026, i.e. shortly after the current reporting window closes. ([Equinor News](https://www.equinor.com/news/20260708-invitation-consensus-estimates-2q2026))

## Management

- No new public appearances, personnel changes, or interviews from Equinor's executive management were identified specifically within the 11–18 July 2026 window.

## Strategie & Ausblick

- **Full ownership of Bay du Nord:** Taking 100% of Bay du Nord from bp gives Equinor sole control over pacing the project toward FID, targeted for early 2027, reinforcing its long-term offshore Canada growth option even as near-term strategic focus stays on oil, gas and disciplined capital allocation. ([Equinor News](https://www.equinor.com/news/equinor-bp-bay-du-nord-project))
- **Continued capital-return discipline:** The steady weekly execution of the doubled USD 3 billion 2026 share buy-back programme, confirmed at the June Capital Markets Day, is being visibly delivered through consecutive tranche disclosures (including the one covering 6–10 July within this window), signalling consistent follow-through on shareholder-return commitments ahead of Q2 results. ([StockTitan / SEC 6-K filing](https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/EQNR/6-k-equinor-asa-current-report-foreign-issuer-face5ba73383.html))
- **Next milestone:** The Q2 2026 results and analyst call on 22 July 2026 will be the key near-term event, expected to give further detail on production trends, progress on capital returns, and the scaled-back renewables strategy flagged by CEO Opedal in June.
