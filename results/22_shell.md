# Shell (SHEL.L)
_KW17 / 2026 | Zeitraum: 19.04.2026 – 26.04.2026_

## Aktuelle Meldungen
- Shell conducted multiple share buybacks throughout April 2026 under its ongoing programme announced 05 February 2026, managed by Morgan Stanley & Co. International Plc until 01 May 2026. Weekly repurchases included: ~4.45M shares (08 Apr), ~1.62M shares (16 Apr), ~1.17M shares (20 Apr), ~1.32M shares (23 Apr), and ~1.45M shares (24 Apr).
- Shell published its Q1 2026 Update Note on 08 April 2026, ahead of full Q1 2026 results due on 07 May 2026. The update highlighted increased uncertainty from the Middle East conflict.
- Shell announced changes to its Executive Committee, making significant progress integrating technical divisions into its business lines.
- Scotiabank raised its Shell price target from $91.00 to $122.00 on 22 April 2026.
- Shell shares were up approximately 24% in 2026 as of late April, with a dividend yield of ~3.1%.
- Shell executive dividend share allocations (CEO Wael Sawan, CFO Sinead Gorman and others) were made on 01 April 2026 under existing bonus and employee share plans.

## Management
- CEO Wael Sawan gave a Bloomberg interview on 13 April 2026, discussing Shell's strategy on oil, renewables and the future of energy; he highlighted navigating volatility in the global energy system and shifting company culture from "all-knowing" to "all-learning."
- Sawan also appeared in a CNBC interview in February 2026, discussing M&A strategy and Shell's position in Venezuela.
- CFO Sinead Gorman continues in her role; both Sawan and Gorman received dividend share allocations in April 2026 per incentive plans.

## Finanzielles
- Q1 2026 Update (08 April 2026): Indicative refining margin raised to ~$17/bbl; working capital guidance of $(10)–$(15) billion (a major reversal from the $1.3B inflow in Q4 2025); variable shipping lease components expected to increase non-cash net debt by $3–4 billion.
- Trading & Optimisation results expected to be significantly higher than Q4 2025; Marketing adjusted earnings significantly higher than Q1 2025 – described as a "trading windfall" meeting a billion-dollar cash drain.
- Full Q1 2026 earnings report due 07 May 2026; consensus EPS forecast for Q1 2026: $2.12.
- Full-year 2026 guidance: Cash CapEx $20–22 billion; shareholder distributions of 40–50% of CFFO; 4% dividend increase announced.
- 2025 annual results (reference): $42.9B operating cash flow; $22.4B returned via dividends and buybacks; $20.9B capex.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- Shell is prioritising share buybacks over large-scale M&A, judging buybacks to create more value; a new buyback programme is expected to be announced alongside the Q1 2026 results on 07 May 2026.
- Nigeria deepwater investment: Shell and partners are assessing development of the Bonga South West deepwater oil field – a project that could attract up to $20 billion in investment with peak production of ~220,000 bpd from ~820 million barrels of reserves.
- Shell's overarching strategy remains "more value with less emissions," targeting 2030 emissions and net carbon intensity goals.
- CEO Sawan reiterated a focus on disciplined decision-making and value-over-volume capital allocation, especially amid geopolitical and energy-market volatility.
- Middle East conflict flagged as key risk to operations and planning horizon.
