# Shell (SHEL.L)
_KW27 / 2026 | Zeitraum: 27.06.2026 – 04.07.2026_

## Aktuelle Meldungen
- On 2 July 2026, Shell disclosed Director/PDMR dealings: CEO Wael Sawan, CFO Sinead Gorman and other persons discharging managerial responsibilities acquired dividend shares. Around this period Shell shares rose more than 3%, outperforming the broader European energy sector.
- A further "Director/PDMR Shareholding" notification was published via GlobeNewswire on 6 July 2026, confirming additional share transactions by Shell executives/directors.
- Just after the observation window, on 7 July 2026, Shell published its "Second Quarter 2026 update note" (pre-close trading update ahead of full Q2 results). Key points: Integrated Gas production guidance raised to 610,000–650,000 boe/d (from previously 580,000–640,000 boe/d); LNG liquefaction volumes guidance raised to 7.4–7.8 Mt (from 6.8–7.4 Mt); indicative refining margin guided at around $20/bbl (vs. $17/bbl in Q1 2026); indicative chemicals margin guided at around $240/tonne (vs. $139/tonne in Q1 2026). The update reflects resilient trading performance despite disruption linked to the Middle East crisis (Strait of Hormuz situation).
- On 9 July 2026 (just after the window), Shell plc announced the final results of its exchange offers for outstanding notes, with settlement and issuance of the new Exchange Notes expected on 13 July 2026.
- Shell's full Q2 2026 results are scheduled for publication on 30 July 2026; the company-compiled analyst consensus (managed by Vara Research) is expected around 22 July 2026.

## Management
- CEO Wael Sawan and CFO Sinead Gorman were both named in the Director/PDMR dividend-share acquisition disclosures dated 2 July 2026 and 6 July 2026, indicating continued insider participation in dividend-related share transactions.
- No new CEO interviews were identified specifically within the 27.06.–04.07.2026 window. The most recent prior public commentary by Sawan (April–May 2026, CNBC and Bloomberg) focused on tight global oil/LNG supply due to the Strait of Hormuz blockade, warning that shortages could persist into 2027, and describing Shell as being at an "inflection point" amid the Middle East conflict.

## Finanzielles
- Shell raised its Q2 2026 guidance across several segments (see Aktuelle Meldungen): higher Integrated Gas production and LNG volumes, and notably higher refining and chemicals margins quarter-on-quarter.
- For reference, Q1 2026 results (published 7 May 2026) showed Adjusted Earnings of just under $7 billion, cash flow from operations excluding working capital of over $17 billion, income attributable to shareholders of $5.7 billion, a $3 billion share buyback programme, and a 5% dividend increase.
- Full Q2 2026 financial results are due on 30 July 2026, which will provide concrete figures superseding the 7 July trading update guidance.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- Shell continues to execute on its ~$16.4 billion acquisition of ARC Resources (announced 27 April 2026), which adds roughly 370,000 boe/d of production, 1.5 million net acres in Canada's Montney basin, and about 2 billion boe of reserves. The deal (cash plus equity plus assumed net debt) is expected to close in H2 2026 and to be accretive to free cash flow per share from 2027, with double-digit expected returns. The ARC shareholder vote was scheduled for 14 July 2026.
- In parallel, Shell is streamlining its portfolio: the sale of Jiffy Lube International to Monomoy Capital Partners for $1.3 billion (agreed March 2026) is paired with a long-term lubricants supply agreement with Monomoy, reflecting a focus on core upstream/gas assets and monetization of non-core consumer businesses.
- The completed exchange offers (final results announced 9 July 2026, settlement due 13 July 2026) point to ongoing balance-sheet/debt-management activity.
- Outlook remains shaped by continued Middle East-related energy market disruption; management has flagged persistent tightness in global oil and LNG supply as a key theme heading into Q2 results on 30 July 2026.
