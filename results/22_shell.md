# Shell (SHEL.L)
_KW26 / 2026 | Zeitraum: 20.06.2026 – 27.06.2026_

## Aktuelle Meldungen

**Share Buyback Programme – Suspension and Resumption**
Shell's $3.0 billion share buyback programme, originally announced on May 7, 2026 and executed by Goldman Sachs International, was suspended from June 12, 2026 until market close on July 14, 2026. The pause is required by securities law in connection with the forthcoming ARC Resources Ltd. shareholder vote, scheduled for July 14, 2026. Shell confirmed that any repurchases not completed during the suspension period will be rolled into later 2026 programmes, subject to Board approval. Prior to the suspension, Shell had been buying back approximately 1.0–1.25 million shares per day across the London Stock Exchange (LSE), Chi-X (CXE) and BATS (BXE).

**Shell Prepares $1 Billion Offshore Wind Portfolio Sale (June 12, 2026)**
Bloomberg reported on June 12, 2026 that Shell is preparing a sale of its offshore wind farm portfolio, which could fetch more than $1 billion. Shell has engaged Rothschild & Co. and PJT Partners Inc. as financial advisers to manage the process. The formal sale process could launch by late 2026, with a transaction likely closing in 2027. The divestment is consistent with CEO Wael Sawan's strategy of exiting lower-returning renewable electricity assets. It follows earlier divestments including Shell's European onshore wind business and Indian renewables platform Sprng Energy (acquired in 2022 for $1.55 billion, sold in 2025/2026).

**Shell Recharge and SINEXCEL Open Joint EV Charging Lab in Shenzhen (June 16, 2026)**
Shell Recharge and SINEXCEL officially inaugurated the Shell Recharge–SINEXCEL Joint Innovation Laboratory in Shenzhen, China on June 16, 2026. The MoU was signed by Evan Li, Chief Engineer of Shell E-Mobility China, and Kecheng Guo, General Manager of SINEXCEL EV Charging Solutions. The lab will focus on five technical areas: next-generation ultra-fast charging, cloud-charger synergistic scheduling, solar-storage-charging integration, megawatt charging systems, and smart operations and maintenance technologies. The partnership combines Shell's global operational expertise with SINEXCEL's power electronics capabilities.

**Q1 2026 Dividend Paid (June 29, 2026)**
The Q1 2026 interim dividend of US$0.3906 per ordinary share (US$0.7812 per ADS) was paid to eligible shareholders on June 29, 2026. Sterling and euro equivalents were announced by Shell on June 15, 2026, set at 29.18p and €0.3381 per ordinary share respectively, based on average exchange rates from June 10–12, 2026. The dividend represents a 5% increase year-on-year.

**ARC Resources Acquisition – Shareholder Circular Published**
The ARC Resources shareholder circular was published in mid-June 2026, triggering the regulatory pause on Shell's buyback programme. The ARC shareholder meeting is scheduled for July 14, 2026. Closing of the transaction remains expected in Q3 2026, subject to ARC shareholder approval and customary regulatory clearances.

**Registered Exchange Offers for Notes (June 8, 2026)**
Shell commenced registered exchange offers for outstanding notes on June 8, 2026, a routine capital markets transaction to register previously issued debt securities with the SEC.

## Management

**CEO Wael Sawan – Oil Market Shortage Warning (CNBC, May 7, 2026)**
Shell CEO Wael Sawan appeared on CNBC's 'Money Movers' following the Q1 2026 results to issue a stark warning about global oil supply. Sawan stated that the ongoing Iran conflict has created a structural crude shortage of close to 1 billion barrels: "We have dug ourselves a hole of close to a billion barrels of crude shortage at the moment — that hole is deepening every single day, so the journey back will be a long one." He emphasized that Shell's integrated portfolio and trading operations are well positioned to benefit from prolonged market volatility.

**CEO Wael Sawan – Energy Transition Strategy (Bloomberg, April 13, 2026)**
In a Bloomberg interview with Francine Lacqua, Sawan defended Shell's decision to scale back from renewable electricity generation in favor of LNG as the primary transition bridge fuel. He described a cultural transformation within Shell from an "all-knowing" to an "all-learning" mindset and reinforced the "More Value, Less Emissions" capital allocation framework guiding decisions through 2030. Sawan also outlined Shell's shift within power toward an intermediary role — focused on flexible generation, battery storage, and trading — rather than direct ownership of renewable generation assets.

**CEO Wael Sawan – Policy Engagement (CSIS, January 2026)**
At the Center for Strategic and International Studies in Washington, Sawan called on governments to provide consistent regulatory frameworks for long-term energy infrastructure investment. He also highlighted Shell's commitment to Nigeria, citing potential investment of up to $20 billion alongside partners. Sawan reiterated that "there is no national security without energy security," a theme that has become central to Shell's external communications.

**Executive Committee Changes**
Shell announced changes to its Executive Committee in 2026, reflecting ongoing leadership restructuring as the company realigns its business around its three strategic priorities: Integrated Gas/LNG, deepwater upstream, and Chemicals & Products.

## Strategie & Ausblick

**ARC Resources Acquisition – Defining Strategic Move of 2026**
Shell's acquisition of Canadian upstream producer ARC Resources (announced April 27, 2026) is the largest corporate transaction in Shell's recent history. The deal has an enterprise value of approximately $16.4 billion ($13.6 billion equity value plus $2.8 billion net debt), financed through approximately $3.4 billion in cash and $10.2 billion in newly issued Shell shares. Key strategic rationale:

- Adds approximately 370,000 barrels of oil equivalent per day (kboe/d) to Shell's production.
- Accelerates Shell's production compound annual growth rate from 1% to 4% (base year 2025, through 2030).
- Adds approximately 2 billion barrels of oil equivalent in proved and probable reserves.
- Provides over 1.5 million net acres in the Montney Formation (British Columbia and Alberta), one of North America's most prolific gas and condensate plays.
- Secures long-term feedstock supply for LNG Canada, the Pacific Coast export terminal Shell operates with a 40% stake, positioned to serve Asian markets while avoiding Strait of Hormuz and Panama Canal chokepoints.
- Expected annual synergies of approximately $250 million within one year of closing.

Closing expected Q3 2026 following ARC shareholder vote on July 14, 2026.

**Offshore Wind Exit – Portfolio Reshaping Continues**
Shell's planned $1 billion+ offshore wind sale (reported June 12, 2026) is the latest step in a systematic retreat from renewable electricity assets under CEO Sawan. This follows the sale of Sprng Energy and the European onshore wind business, and confirms that Shell's energy transition role will be concentrated on LNG, power trading, EV charging infrastructure, and downstream flexibility — not direct ownership of large-scale renewables.

**LNG as Core Strategic Pillar**
Shell continues to position LNG as its primary growth vehicle and energy transition contribution. The ARC acquisition strengthens LNG Canada's feedstock supply. Canadian LNG's Pacific routing to Asian markets is increasingly valued given disruption to Middle Eastern supply routes caused by the Iran conflict. Shell operates the world's largest LNG trading portfolio and intends to extend this competitive advantage through 2030.

**EV Charging – Selective Investment in China**
The June 16 Shell Recharge–SINEXCEL joint lab opening in Shenzhen shows that Shell is making targeted investments in EV charging technology, particularly in China, while retreating from renewables generation. This reflects a nuanced approach: Shell is investing in mobility-adjacent infrastructure and power electronics R&D where it can leverage operational scale, rather than in capital-intensive wind or solar farms.

**Geopolitical Context – Iran War**
The closure of the Strait of Hormuz has created a structural global crude deficit of approximately 1 billion barrels according to CEO Sawan. This environment supports higher LNG and oil prices, benefits Shell's integrated trading operations, and reinforces the strategic logic of securing Canadian upstream supply that avoids Middle Eastern routing risk.

**Shareholder Climate Vote**
At Shell's AGM on May 19, 2026, activist Resolution 23 calling for stricter climate commitments was rejected by 86.99% of shareholders, providing a clear institutional mandate for management's current strategic direction. No material change to climate strategy is expected ahead of the next strategy update.
