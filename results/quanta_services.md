## Strategie & Ausblick
- Quanta continues to position itself as the primary execution partner for the North American grid build-out — large-scale electrification, high-voltage transmission upgrades, and power infrastructure for data centers remain the core demand drivers behind the record backlog.
- Acquisition activity remains active in 2026: recent additions include Phalcon, Enerfab, Percheron and PSD, on top of three earlier acquisitions completed in H1 2026, together expected to contribute meaningfully to 2026 revenue and adjusted EBITDA as they are integrated.
- The vertical-integration push into in-house transformer and breaker manufacturing continues, aimed at relieving equipment supply-chain bottlenecks that have constrained the broader utility-contracting industry.
- The new Solar Power World recognition underscores Quanta's growing renewables/solar EPC footprint as a complementary growth vector alongside its traditional transmission, pipeline and communications infrastructure segments.
- Key risk for the position remains valuation: with consensus price targets (~$767.72) already implying continued outperformance, any sign that Q3 results or backlog conversion disappoint relative to the raised guidance could trigger a sharper de-rating than the modest pullback seen this week.
