## Strategie & Ausblick
- Growth strategy remains centered on electric grid modernization, renewable energy interconnection, and data-center/technology load-center demand; management expects the buildout cycle to sustain double-digit growth well past 2030.
- Active M&A program: four acquisitions completed in H1/July 2026 (Phalcon, Enerfab, Percheron, PSD) for approximately $1.24 billion of upfront consideration, net of cash acquired, plus up to $242.3 million of contingent consideration; these deals are expected to contribute $1.2–$1.4 billion of revenue and $120–$140 million of adjusted EBITDA in 2026, deepening self-perform craft-skilled capabilities and expanding exposure in the U.S. and Australia.
- Builds on eight acquisitions completed during full-year 2025, including Tri-City Group and Wilson Construction Company, which expanded craft-skilled labor capacity.
- The record $53.4B backlog as of Q2 2026 underpins management's raised full-year guidance and signals continued demand visibility into 2027.
