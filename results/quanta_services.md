## Strategie & Ausblick
- Growth strategy remains focused on grid modernization, renewable energy interconnection, and rising demand from data centers and technology customers.
- The active M&A program continues: acquisitions such as Tri-City Group, Phalcon, Enerfab, Percheron and PSD are expected to collectively contribute roughly $1.2–$1.4 billion in incremental 2026 revenue and roughly $120–$140 million in incremental adjusted EBITDA; contingent earnout payments of up to approximately $242 million remain tied to the acquired companies hitting performance targets.
- The record backlog of roughly $63.7 billion continues to underpin management's expectation of strong double-digit earnings growth into 2027.
- Supply chain and labor capacity — not demand — remain cited as the key growth constraint, driving continued investment in training, fabrication capacity and bolt-on acquisitions.
- The newly raised, above-trend dividend increase signals continued management confidence in cash flow durability alongside the growth investments.
- Next catalysts: commentary from the remaining investor-conference season (notably UBS in early December), early signals on 2027 guidance, and further acquisition announcements.
