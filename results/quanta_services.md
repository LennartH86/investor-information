- Active M&A program: four acquisitions (Phalcon, Enerfab, Percheron, PSD) were completed in the first half/July 2026 for total consideration of approximately $1.24 billion net of cash acquired (roughly $1.07 billion cash and $173.3 million stock), with up to $242.3 million in additional contingent payments possible; these acquisitions are expected to contribute $1.2–$1.4 billion in revenue and $120–$140 million in adjusted EBITDA in 2026.
