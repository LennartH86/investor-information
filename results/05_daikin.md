# Daikin (6367.T)
_KW26 / 2026 | Zeitraum: 20.06.2026 – 27.06.2026_

## Aktuelle Meldungen

- **$30M Solutions & Technical Institute groundbreaking (June 18, 2026):** [Daikin Applied broke ground](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/daikin-applied-breaks-ground-on-30m-solutions--technical-institute-to-support-future-hvac-workforce-302803960.html) on a 64,000-square-foot training facility in Plymouth, Minnesota. The institute is expected to open in 2027 and is designed to address the looming shortage of HVAC technicians by equipping technicians, engineers, and sales professionals with practical knowledge. [ACHR News also covered the groundbreaking.](https://www.achrnews.com/articles/166342-daikin-applied-breaks-ground-for-new-training-center)
- **$8.5M civil penalty settlement (June 16, 2026):** The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission [announced](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/daikin-agrees-to-pay-8-5-million-civil-penalty-for-failure-to-immediately-report-ptacs-that-posed-a-fire-hazard-302801952.html) that Daikin Comfort Technologies Manufacturing agreed to pay an $8.5 million civil penalty for knowingly failing to immediately report a fire hazard defect in its Packaged Terminal Air Conditioners/Heat Pumps (PTACs) equipped with "DigiAir" modules.
- **Data center cooling push:** Daikin Applied [published details](https://www.daikinapplied.com/news/news/daikin-applied-is-powering-the-next-generation-of-hyperscale-data-centers--with-comprehensive--end-to-end-cooling-solutions) on powering next-generation hyperscale data centers with comprehensive end-to-end cooling solutions, including liquid cooling and advanced thermal management. The company also [shared recent data center division developments](https://www.achrnews.com/articles/165407-daikin-applied-shares-recent-developments-in-data-center-division) via ACHR News.
- **Datacloud Global Congress 2026:** Daikin participated in the leading global data center industry event in Cannes (June 2–4), reinforcing its positioning as an infrastructure partner for AI and cloud operators.

## Management

- **Chairman Emeritus Noriyuki Inoue (Nikkei Asia interview, March 2026):** [Inoue identified](https://asia.nikkei.com/editor-s-picks/interview/daikin-sees-chance-in-india-southern-hemisphere-chairman-emeritus) India and the Southern Hemisphere as Daikin's top growth opportunities, noting persistently low air conditioning penetration in these regions. He also indicated the company is actively exploring a new business line beyond its core HVAC offerings.
- **Daikin U.S. Corporation President Yoshiyuki Uemura (January 2026):** [Uemura](https://www.northamerica-daikin.com/news/daikin-joins-leaders-at-well-2026-flagship-summit-to-advance-the-conversation-on-indoor-air-quality) was a featured panelist at the WELL 2026 Flagship Summit in New York City, discussing indoor air quality and building wellness alongside senior executives from global organizations.
- **Mike Schwartz (CEO, Daikin Applied Americas):** [An interview](https://brookssolutions.net/interview-with-mike-schwartz-ceo-of-daikin-applied-americas/) with Schwartz outlines Daikin Applied's ambition to be the go-to full-stack provider for data center cooling in North America, with solutions-based services and targeted M&A as the primary growth tools.
- **New digital and corporate development leadership:** [Daikin Applied welcomed](https://www.daikinapplied.com/news/news/daikin-applied-welcomes-executives-to-lead-digital-and-corporate-development) new senior executives to drive digital transformation and corporate development strategy.
- **FUSION30 strategic management plan (announced May 12, 2026):** Daikin [published](https://www.daikin.com/press/2026/20260512_6) its "FUSION30" long-range strategic management plan, setting the group's strategic direction from FY2026 through FY2030.

## Finanzielles

- **FY2025 Full-Year Results (announced May 12, 2026):** The [full-year results presentation](https://www.daikin.com/-/media/Project/Daikin/daikin_com/investor/data/kessan/2026/4Q_financial_results_en-pdf.pdf) covers the fiscal year ended March 2026. Full-year net revenue reached JPY 5.015 trillion; both revenue and operating profit reached record highs. The EMEA segment achieved a record EUR 5.25 billion in FY2025.
- **Q3 FY2026 cumulative results (April–December 2025):** [Revenue rose 2.0% YoY](https://finance.biggo.com/news/jpx_tdnet_140120260204546821) to JPY 3.666 trillion (~$23.4B); operating profit fell 3.4% to JPY 307.9 billion (~$2.0B); net profit attributable to parent shareholders rose 4.6% to JPY 195.4 billion (~$1.25B). [Detailed Q3 results presentation available here.](https://www.daikin.com/-/media/Project/Daikin/daikin_com/investor/data/kessan/2026/3Q_financial_results_en-pdf.pdf)
- **Full-year FY2026 guidance revision:** Sales forecast raised to JPY 4.920 trillion (+1.7% vs. prior guidance); operating profit forecast lowered to JPY 413.0 billion (–5.1%), reflecting headwinds from U.S. tariffs, a softer U.S. residential construction market, and slower-than-expected demand recovery in North America and Asia.
- **Dividend:** Interim dividend of JPY 165 per share; full-year dividend forecast of JPY 330 per share.
- **Balance sheet:** Equity ratio maintained above 50%, providing capacity for continued M&A and organic capital deployment.

## Strategie & Ausblick

- **FUSION30 (FY2026–FY2030):** The [FUSION30 plan](https://www.daikin.com/press/2026/20260512_6) sets out three strategic pillars: (1) protecting core HVAC technology and manufacturing excellence; (2) accelerating adaptation to climate and market trends; (3) scaling globally, with a focus on energy efficiency leadership and AI-driven systems.
- **Data center cooling as the primary growth engine:** Daikin is building a full-stack data center cooling ecosystem:
  - Acquisition of [Chilldyne](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/daikin-acquires-liquid-cooling-firm-chilldyne/) — provider of direct-to-chip liquid cooling for HPC and AI data centers.
  - [Acquisition of DDC Solutions](https://www.daikinapplied.com/news/news/daikin-applied-completes-acquisitions-of-ddc-solutions) — San Diego-based developer of ultra-high-density cooling cabinets and management software.
  - [Majority stake in Modular Comfort Systems](https://www.daikinapplied.com/news/news/daikin-applied-continues-growth-acquiring-new-york-based-modular-comfort-systems) (New York) — customized HVAC solutions for commercial applications.
  - $163M investment in an advanced R&D test lab to bolster data center HVAC innovation.
- **North America & Latin America capacity expansion:** Daikin is committing an estimated $230 million to expand manufacturing capacity in North America and Latin America, including two new facilities — one focused on modular data center systems, one dedicated to scaling custom air handler output.
- **U.S. growth strategy (J.P. Morgan Industrials Conference):** Daikin North America [outlined](https://www.northamerica-daikin.com/news/daikin-outlines-us-growth-strategy-at-jp-morgan-industrials-conference) a U.S. roadmap centered on manufacturing investment, data center cooling, and resilience against tariff exposure through localized production.
- **AI-enabled HVAC systems:** Daikin has articulated a product vision of air conditioning systems that "adapt and learn" via AI, targeting improved energy efficiency in the commercial and enterprise sectors.
- **Emerging markets:** Chairman Emeritus Inoue's comments on India and the Southern Hemisphere are embedded in the FUSION30 framework as the key long-term geographic expansion priorities.
- **Workforce development as competitive differentiator:** The $30M Solutions & Technical Institute groundbreaking reflects Daikin's view that investing vertically in technician training is essential to close a looming industry-wide skills gap and sustain service quality at scale.
