# Daikin (6367.T)
_KW22 / 2026 | Zeitraum: 23.05.2026 – 30.05.2026_

## Recent News

- **Share Warrant Issuance & Share Buyback** (May 13, 2026): Daikin Industries announced the terms for share warrant issuances and disclosed the results of the acquisition of own shares through off-auction share repurchase trading, signalling continued capital return to shareholders.
- **U.S. Antitrust Lawsuits**: Daikin Industries and its U.S. subsidiaries continue to be named as defendants in four provisional class action lawsuits filed in the U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Michigan. Plaintiffs allege that the defendants conspired to artificially increase prices for HVAC equipment sold in the United States on or after January 1, 2020. No settlement or resolution was reported during the KW22 period.
- **Share Gap-Down (May 28, 2026)**: Shares of Daikin Industries (OTCMKTS: DKILY) traded lower with a noticeable gap-down on May 28, 2026, reflecting broader market uncertainty and headwinds from U.S. tariff policy concerns.
- **FUSION30 Strategic Plan in Effect**: Following the formal announcement on May 12, 2026, Daikin's five-year strategic management plan "FUSION30" (FY2026–FY2030) continues to guide corporate priorities across all global business units into KW22.
- **Data Centre Cooling MOU (Delta Electronics, May 11, 2026)**: Daikin Holdings Singapore and Delta Electronics, Inc. signed an MOU to collaborate on next-generation Coolant Distribution Unit (CDU) solutions for AI and HPC data centres across the ASEAN-Oceania market — a deal that remained a major strategic topic in the KW22 period.

## Management

In the presentation and Q&A transcript (published May 15, 2026), Togawa outlined management's commitment to protecting core strengths, embracing change, and continuing to win in competitive markets under conditions of elevated global uncertainty.

- **Noriyuki Inoue** (Chairman Emeritus): No new public appearances during KW22. His March 2026 Nikkei Asia interview, in which he highlighted India and the Southern Hemisphere as key growth opportunities, continues to be referenced in market commentary.

## Financials

- **FY2025 Full-Year Results** (fiscal year ended March 31, 2026 — reported May 12, 2026): Net revenue of JPY 5.015 trillion (approx. USD 31.8 bn), up approx. +5.5% year-over-year. Both net revenue and operating profit reached new all-time record highs. Reported EPS of USD 0.17 for the quarter, beating the consensus estimate of USD 0.13 by USD 0.04; revenue of USD 8.56 billion exceeded analyst estimates of USD 7.82 billion.
- **Q3 FY2026 (cumulative April–December 2025)**: Revenue +2.0% YoY to JPY 3.666 trillion; operating profit –3.4% to JPY 307.9 billion; net profit attributable to parent shareholders +4.6% to JPY 195.4 billion.
- **Revised Full-Year FY2026 Forecast**: Net sales guidance raised to JPY 4.920 trillion (+1.7% vs. prior guidance); operating profit forecast cut to JPY 413.0 billion (–5.1%). Key headwinds include U.S. tariff uncertainty, a slowing U.S. labour market, and persistently high interest rates weighing on housing investment.
- **Dividend**: Interim dividend of JPY 165 per share; full-year dividend forecast of JPY 330 per share.
- **Share Buyback**: Active repurchase program announced May 12–13, 2026, reflecting management's confidence in the company's financial position and its commitment to capital returns.
- **EMEA**: Annual turnover for FY2025 reached a record EUR 5.25 billion, exceeding the prior fiscal year, confirming steady performance across the EMEA region.

## Strategy & Outlook

- **FUSION30 (FY2026–FY2030)**: Daikin's five-year strategic management plan focuses on sustainable growth, technological innovation in air conditioning and HVAC, and strengthening the company's global competitive position. The plan emphasises what management calls "what we will protect, what we will change, and what we will continue to win with" in an era of elevated uncertainty. High-growth regions — particularly India and the Southern Hemisphere — are central to the growth agenda.
- **Data Centre Cooling as Strategic Growth Pillar**:
  - MOU with Delta Electronics (May 11, 2026): Collaboration on next-generation CDU solutions for AI and HPC data centres across ASEAN-Oceania.
  - Acquisition of **Chilldyne**: Daikin Applied acquired this specialist in negative-pressure liquid cooling systems for HPC and AI data centres, marking a "pivotal step" in building a comprehensive data-centre cooling ecosystem.
  - Acquisition of **DDC Solutions**: San Diego-based developer of ultra-high-density cooling cabinets and management software for data centres, expanding Daikin Applied's rack-level cooling portfolio.
  - Majority investment in **Modular Comfort Systems** (New York): Provider of customised HVAC solutions for building owners, contractors and consulting engineers in upstate New York.
  - USD 163 million investment in a new Advanced R&D Test Lab (December 2025) to advance HVAC innovation for data centres and commercial applications.
- **Market Challenges**: Global economic uncertainty driven by U.S. tariff policy, a slowing U.S. labour market, and weak housing investment continue to weigh on demand — particularly in the Americas. Management is responding with targeted sales efforts, a focus on high-margin products, selective price adjustments, and cost reduction initiatives.
- **India & Southern Hemisphere**: Long-term demand growth from rising air conditioning penetration in India and southern-hemisphere markets remains a central pillar of the FUSION30 growth strategy.
