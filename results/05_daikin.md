# Daikin (6367.T)
_KW23 / 2026 | Zeitraum: 30.05.2026 – 06.06.2026_

## Aktuelle Meldungen

- **Datacloud Global Congress 2026 (2–4 June, Cannes)**: Daikin participated in one of the most influential global events for the data centre industry, showcasing its end-to-end cooling solutions for hyperscale data centres. The appearance underlines Daikin's continued positioning as a key cooling infrastructure partner for AI and cloud computing operators.
- **FUSION30 Strategic Plan in Effect**: Following its formal announcement on May 12, 2026, Daikin's five-year strategic management plan "FUSION30" (FY2026–FY2030) continues to guide corporate priorities. The plan targets sustainable growth, technological innovation in HVAC, and strengthened global competitiveness under conditions of elevated macroeconomic uncertainty.
- **Data Centre Cooling Focus**: Daikin Applied has been actively expanding its comprehensive data centre cooling portfolio, including the acquisition of Chilldyne (negative-pressure liquid cooling for AI/HPC data centres) and DDC Solutions (ultra-high-density cooling cabinets and management software). Both deals were referenced in ongoing market coverage during the KW23 period.
- **U.S. Antitrust Lawsuits Ongoing**: Daikin Industries and its U.S. subsidiaries remain named defendants in four provisional class action lawsuits filed in the U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Michigan. Plaintiffs allege a conspiracy to artificially inflate HVAC equipment prices in the U.S. since January 1, 2020. No resolution was reported during the KW23 period.
- **New Manufacturing Facility Operational**: Daikin Applied opened a new modular solutions manufacturing facility in January 2026 to enhance production capacity for large-tonnage plants, primarily to meet surging demand from data centre customers.

## Management

- **Masanori Togawa** (Representative Director, Chairman of the Board & CEO): Presented Daikin's FY2025 financial results and the FUSION30 plan on May 12, 2026. Togawa emphasised management's focus on protecting core strengths, embracing change, and continuing to win in competitive markets amid global uncertainty. He remains the primary face of the company in investor communications.
- **Mike Schwartz** (CEO, Daikin Applied Americas): Leads Daikin's U.S. applied cooling business, which is at the forefront of the company's data centre expansion strategy. An interview published via Brooks Solutions highlighted the company's direction under his leadership.
- **Yoshiyuki Uemura** (President, Daikin U.S. Corporation): Participated as a featured panelist at the WELL 2026 Flagship Summit in New York City (January 29, 2026), representing Daikin's commitment to indoor air quality and wellness-focused HVAC solutions in the U.S. market.
- **Noriyuki Inoue** (Chairman Emeritus): His March 2026 Nikkei Asia interview — highlighting India and the Southern Hemisphere as key long-term growth opportunities — continues to be referenced in market commentary as the strategic rationale underpinning FUSION30's geographic ambitions.

## Finanzielles

- **FY2025 Full-Year Results** (fiscal year ended March 31, 2026 — reported May 12, 2026): Full-year net revenue of JPY 5.015 trillion (trailing 12-month), with Q4 revenue of JPY 1,348.7 billion. Basic EPS for FY2025 was JPY 939.92 (USD equivalent: Q4 EPS of USD 0.17, beating consensus of USD 0.13 by USD 0.04). Q4 revenue of USD 8.56 billion exceeded analyst estimates of USD 7.82 billion. Both net revenue and operating profit reached new all-time record highs for the full year.
- **Q3 FY2026 Cumulative Results** (April–December 2025): Revenue +2.0% YoY to JPY 3.666 trillion; operating profit –3.4% to JPY 307.9 billion; net profit attributable to parent shareholders +4.6% to JPY 195.4 billion.
- **Q1 FY2026 Results**: EPS rose to JPY 278.43 from JPY 215.53 year-over-year. U.S. tariffs had a direct negative impact of JPY 7.5 billion on operating profit, partially offset by pricing measures and cost reductions.
- **Revised Full-Year FY2026 Forecast**: Net sales guidance raised to JPY 4.920 trillion (+1.7% vs. prior guidance); operating profit forecast cut to JPY 413.0 billion (–5.1%). Key headwinds include U.S. tariff uncertainty, a softening U.S. labour market, and weak housing investment.
- **Dividend**: Interim dividend of JPY 165 per share; full-year dividend forecast of JPY 330 per share.
- **Share Buyback**: Active repurchase programme announced May 12–13, 2026, reflecting management confidence in the company's financial position.
- **EMEA**: Annual turnover for FY2025 reached a record EUR 5.25 billion, confirming steady performance across the EMEA region.
- **Net Margin**: Net margin stands at approximately 5.5%, which analysts have noted as a point of tension relative to the company's bullish earnings growth narrative.

## Strategie & Ausblick

- **FUSION30 (FY2026–FY2030)**: The five-year strategic plan focuses on three pillars: what Daikin will protect (core HVAC technology and manufacturing excellence), what it will change (faster adaptation to market and climate trends), and what it will continue to win with (global scale, energy efficiency leadership, and brand strength). High-growth regions — particularly India and the Southern Hemisphere — are central to the long-term growth agenda.
- **Data Centre Cooling as Strategic Growth Pillar**:
  - Participation at **Datacloud Global Congress 2026** (Cannes, June 2–4) to present end-to-end hyperscale cooling solutions.
  - Acquisition of **Chilldyne**: Specialist in negative-pressure liquid cooling for HPC and AI data centres — described by Daikin as a "pivotal step" in building a comprehensive data centre cooling ecosystem.
  - Acquisition of **DDC Solutions**: San Diego-based developer of ultra-high-density cooling cabinets and management software, expanding rack-level cooling capabilities.
  - MOU with **Delta Electronics** (May 11, 2026): Collaboration on next-generation Coolant Distribution Unit (CDU) solutions for AI and HPC data centres across ASEAN-Oceania.
  - USD 163 million investment in a new **Advanced R&D Test Lab** (December 2025) to drive HVAC innovation for data centres and commercial buildings.
  - New **modular solutions manufacturing facility** opened January 2026 to scale up large-tonnage cooling production for data centre customers.
- **U.S. Growth Strategy**: Daikin outlined its U.S. growth strategy at the J.P. Morgan Industrials Conference, emphasising manufacturing investment, data centre cooling expansion, and resilience to tariff headwinds through localised production and pricing discipline.
- **India & Southern Hemisphere**: Rising air conditioning penetration in India and southern-hemisphere markets remains a central long-term growth pillar under FUSION30, as highlighted by Chairman Emeritus Inoue.
- **Tariff Headwinds**: U.S. tariff policy remains a material risk, with a direct operating profit impact of JPY 7.5 billion already recorded in Q1 FY2026. Management is mitigating the impact through targeted price increases, cost reduction programmes, and further localisation of U.S. manufacturing.
