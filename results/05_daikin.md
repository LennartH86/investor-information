# Daikin (6367.T)
_KW21 / 2026 | Zeitraum: 16.05.2026 – 23.05.2026_

## Aktuelle Meldungen

- **VRV Emerion Commercial Heat Pump Launch** (22. Mai 2026): Daikin Comfort Technologies North America introduced the Daikin VRV Emerion commercial heat pump and heat recovery systems, featuring low-GWP R-32 refrigerant. The systems are designed for improved energy efficiency and sustainability in commercial applications.
- **WELL 2026 Flagship Summit** (Mai 2026): Daikin U.S. Corporation President Yoshiyuki Uemura joined executives at the WELL 2026 Flagship Summit in New York City as a featured panelist in a fireside chat, focusing on the topic of improving indoor air quality.
- **Kartellklage (USA) – Update**: Daikin Industries and its U.S. subsidiaries continue to be named as defendants in four provisional class action lawsuits filed in the U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Michigan. Plaintiffs allege that defendants conspired to artificially increase prices for HVAC equipment sold in the United States on or after January 1, 2020.
- **Rekordergebnis EMEA bestätigt**: Daikin Europe N.V. confirmed its annual turnover for FY2025 reached a record high of EUR 5.25 billion, exceeding the prior fiscal year — a reflection of steady performance across the EMEA region.
- **FUSION30 Strategieplan in Kraft**: Following its announcement on May 12, 2026, Daikin's five-year strategic management plan "FUSION30" (FY2026–FY2030) is now guiding corporate priorities across all business units.

## Management

- **Masanori Togawa** (Representative Director, Chairman of the Board & CEO) and **Naofumi Takenaka** (President & COO): Both participated in the Financial Results Briefing for FY2025 on May 12, 2026, presenting the full-year results and the new FUSION30 strategic management plan to institutional investors and analysts. A transcript of the Q&A session is publicly available.
- **Yoshiyuki Uemura** (President, Daikin U.S. Corporation): Participated as featured panelist at the WELL 2026 Flagship Summit in New York City, representing Daikin in a fireside chat on indoor air quality.
- **Noriyuki Inoue** (Chairman Emeritus): In an interview published in March 2026 by Nikkei Asia, Inoue emphasized strong opportunities in India and the Southern Hemisphere as demand for air conditioning continues to expand. No new CEO interview or leadership change reported in the KW21 period (16.–23.05.2026).

## Finanzielles

- **Jahresergebnis FY2025** (Geschäftsjahr bis 31. März 2026): Net revenue of JPY 5.015 trillion (ca. USD 31.8 bn), up approx. +5.5% year-over-year; both net revenue and operating profit reached new record highs.
- **EMEA FY2025**: Annual turnover of EUR 5.25 billion — a new record high, surpassing the prior fiscal year's result.
- **Q3 FY2026 (kumuliert, April–Dezember 2025)**: Revenue +2.0% YoY to JPY 3.666 trillion; operating profit –3.4% to JPY 307.9 billion; net profit attributable to parent +4.6% to JPY 195.4 billion.
- **Revised Full-Year Forecast (FY2026)**: Net sales guidance raised to JPY 4.920 trillion (+1.7% vs. prior guidance); operating profit forecast reduced to JPY 413.0 billion (–5.1%). Key headwinds: U.S. tariff uncertainty, sluggish housing investment, persistently high interest rates in the U.S.
- **Dividende**: Interim dividend of JPY 165 per share; full-year forecast of JPY 330 per share.
- **Aktienrückkauf**: Board approved a share buyback program (announced May 12, 2026) — a signal of management confidence in the company's financial position and commitment to capital returns.

## Strategie & Ausblick

- **FUSION30 (FY2026–2030)**: Daikin's newly formulated five-year strategic management plan focuses on sustainable growth, technological innovation in air conditioning and HVAC, and strengthening the global market position — with a particular emphasis on high-growth regions such as India and the Southern Hemisphere.
- **Data Centre Cooling als strategischer Wachstumspfeiler**:
  - MOU with Delta Electronics (signed May 11, 2026): Daikin Holdings Singapore and Delta Electronics will collaborate on next-generation Coolant Distribution Unit (CDU) solutions for AI and HPC data centres in the ASEAN-Oceania market.
  - Acquisition of **Chilldyne**: Daikin Applied acquired this experienced provider of negative-pressure liquid cooling systems for HPC and AI data centres.
  - Acquisition of **DDC Solutions**: San Diego-based developer of ultra-high-density cooling cabinets and management software for data centres.
  - Majority investment in **Modular Comfort Systems** (New York): Provider of customized HVAC solutions for consulting engineers, contractors and building owners.
  - USD 163 million investment in a new Advanced R&D Test Lab to bolster HVAC innovation for data centres and beyond.
- **Markt Herausforderungen**: Global economic uncertainty driven by U.S. tariff policies, a slowing U.S. labor market and weak housing investment continue to weigh on demand, particularly in the Americas. Management is responding with targeted sales and marketing efforts, a focus on high-margin products, selective price increases and cost reduction initiatives.
- **Indien & Südliche Hemisphäre**: Chairman Emeritus Inoue identified India and markets in the Southern Hemisphere as key long-term growth opportunities as air conditioning penetration rises — a theme that aligns with the FUSION30 growth agenda.
