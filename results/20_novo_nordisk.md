# Novo Nordisk (NOVO-B.CO)
_KW27 / 2026 | Zeitraum: 27.06.2026 – 04.07.2026_

## Aktuelle Meldungen
- On July 6, 2026, Novo Nordisk announced an update on its share repurchase programme (part of the overall up-to-DKK-15-billion buyback programme running for a 12-month period that began February 4, 2026). Under the tranche initiated May 6, 2026, Novo Nordisk plans to repurchase B shares for up to DKK 11,200,000,010.45 through February 1, 2027. As of July 3, 2026, the company had repurchased a total of 23,009,179 B shares at an average price of DKK 270.32 per share.
- Novo Nordisk's share price recovered strongly in early July, closing around €43.06, up roughly 20.63% over the preceding 30 days, supported by the ongoing buyback and a fresh analyst upgrade from HSBC.
- Reports from around July 6-7, 2026 indicate Novo Nordisk Korea merged its obesity and diabetes divisions, combining two previously separate units to speed up decision-making and sharpen resource allocation — seen as a defensive move in the Korean market amid intensifying competition from Eli Lilly.
- On July 10, 2026, the Novo Nordisk Foundation awarded nine new Challenge Programme grants (up to €80 million / DKK 600 million) supporting research into fungal diseases, cardiometabolic disease, soil health, and non-equilibrium biological systems.
- As of July 1, 2026, Novo Nordisk's Wegovy injections and tablets are among the medications covered under the new US Medicare GLP-1 Bridge program, lowering costs for eligible seniors.
- At the ADA 2026 conference, data from the REIMAGINE program showed Novo Nordisk's CagriSema (2.4 mg/2.4 mg) achieving significant reductions in HbA1c and body weight across multiple studies.

## Management
- CEO Mike (Maziar) Doustdar, in interviews around mid-2026 (Fierce Pharma, Fortune, Bloomberg, CNBC), described growing confidence after his first year leading the company, calling 2026 "a year of growth in many ways," citing record patient numbers and higher production volumes than in prior years.
- Doustdar outlined three strategic priorities for 2026: accelerating commercial execution (new launches, expanded direct-to-patient sales), advancing the pipeline, and balancing financial discipline with targeted investment.
- He signaled Novo Nordisk is looking beyond weight loss toward longevity and aesthetics applications for future GLP-1 drug generations, suggesting weight reduction could become "just an appendix of a different purpose."
- Doustdar confirmed the company is highly active in business-development talks and acquisitions, stating the ambition to build "not just the best, but the broadest pipeline in the world."
- He highlighted Novo Nordisk's AI partnership with OpenAI as a means to speed up drug discovery, improve accuracy, and reinforce confidence in Wegovy amid rising competitive pressure.

## Finanzielles
- Q1 2026 results (reported May 6, 2026): sales rose 32% at constant exchange rates to DKK 96.8 billion (~$15.2 billion); reported operating profit surged 65% to DKK 59.6 billion; adjusted operating profit reached DKK 32,858 million. On an adjusted basis, sales fell 4% and profit fell 6%.
- Obesity care adjusted sales grew 22% at constant exchange rates; injectable Wegovy sales rose 12% year-on-year to DKK 18.2 billion; the oral Wegovy pill surpassed one million patients since its January 2026 launch. Ozempic sales fell 8% but beat expectations.
- Novo Nordisk raised its full-year 2026 guidance, now expecting adjusted sales and operating profit to contract between 4% and 12% at constant exchange rates (improved from a prior forecast of a 5%-13% decline).
- The company continues executing its DKK 15 billion share buyback programme under EU Safe Harbour rules, reflecting a strong capital-return commitment; treasury shares reportedly represent about 0.8% of share capital.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- Novo Nordisk remains focused on defending its GLP-1/obesity leadership against Eli Lilly, including regional organizational restructuring (e.g., Korea) to speed decision-making.
- The company continues pursuing bolt-on M&A and partnerships to broaden its pipeline: the ~$5.2 billion Akero Therapeutics acquisition (closed late 2025) is expected to weigh on 2026 operating profit growth by roughly 3 percentage points due to increased R&D spend, and a $2.1 billion partnership with Vivtex (announced February 25, 2026) targets next-generation oral medicines for obesity and diabetes.
- Manufacturing capacity expansion remains a priority, including integrating former Catalent sites and a $200 million acquisition of Novavax's Bohumil, Czech Republic facility to boost fill-finish capacity from 2026 onward.
- Management is steering R&D beyond weight loss toward longevity and aesthetics indications, supported by an AI drug-discovery partnership with OpenAI, while emphasizing balanced financial discipline alongside continued investment in the pipeline.
