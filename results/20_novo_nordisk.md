# Novo Nordisk (NOVO-B.CO)
_KW26 / 2026 | Zeitraum: 20.06.2026 – 27.06.2026_

## Aktuelle Meldungen

- **Share Repurchase Programme update (June 22, 2026):** Novo Nordisk published its weekly share buyback disclosure on June 22, 2026. Under the sub-programme initiated May 6, 2026, the company is authorised to repurchase B shares for up to DKK 11,200,000,010.45 through February 1, 2027, as part of an overall 12-month programme of up to DKK 15 billion commencing February 4, 2026. As of June 19, 2026, Novo Nordisk had repurchased a total of 20,959,179 B shares at an average price of DKK 266.09 per share.

- **Hemophilia portfolio data at ISTH Congress 2026 (announced late June 2026):** Novo Nordisk announced plans to present new data across its hemophilia portfolio at the ISTH (International Society on Thrombosis and Haemostasis) Congress 2026, scheduled for July 11–15 in Paris, France. The centrepiece is interim data from the Phase 3 FRONTIER4 study for investigational denecimig (Mim8) — a FVIIIa-mimetic bispecific antibody — covering long-term efficacy and safety across adults, adolescents, and children with haemophilia A, with or without FVIII inhibitors, across once-monthly, biweekly, and weekly prophylaxis dosing. Additional data on concizumab will also be showcased. Earlier in 2026, the NEJM published 26-week FRONTIER2 Phase 3 results showing denecimig once-monthly reduced annualised treated bleeding events by 96.4% versus on-demand therapy.

- **Wegovy pill surpasses 3 million U.S. prescriptions (milestone reached early June 2026):** Novo Nordisk confirmed that Wegovy® (semaglutide) tablets 25 mg exceeded three million U.S. prescriptions in just over five months since the January 2026 launch — roughly one prescription every five seconds. The company is preparing first international launches of the Wegovy pill in H2 2026, including the recently confirmed approval and launch in the United Arab Emirates.

- **CagriSema REIMAGINE Phase 3 data at ADA 2026 (June 5–8, 2026):** At the American Diabetes Association Scientific Sessions in New Orleans, Novo Nordisk presented data from the REIMAGINE 1–3 trials for CagriSema 2.4 mg / 2.4 mg (a fixed-dose co-formulation of cagrilintide and semaglutide). All three trials met their primary HbA1c endpoint and confirmatory weight-reduction endpoint in adults with type 2 diabetes. REIMAGINE 1 and 2 results were simultaneously published in The Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology; REIMAGINE 3 in The Lancet. Novo Nordisk filed an NDA with the FDA for CagriSema for weight management in December 2025.

## Management

- **CEO Mike Doustdar – current strategic priorities:** Mike Doustdar (President & CEO since August 2025, successor to Lars Fruergaard Jørgensen who stepped down in May 2025) has outlined three priorities for 2026: (1) accelerating commercial execution with new product launches and direct-to-patient sales expansion; (2) progressing the clinical pipeline; and (3) maintaining financial discipline while investing selectively. In a Fierce Pharma interview reflecting on his first year, Doustdar stated: "I'm a lot more optimistic today," citing a visible acceleration in R&D and the number of pipeline assets under development.

- **Restructuring and workforce reduction:** In one of his first major decisions as CEO, Doustdar announced plans to lay off approximately 9,000 employees — around 11% of the global workforce of 78,400 — with roughly 5,000 positions in Denmark. The restructuring is intended to generate annual savings of approximately DKK 8 billion (~USD 1.26 billion) by the end of 2026, with the focus on refocusing the company on its diabetes and obesity core. As a result, Novo Nordisk revised its adjusted operating profit growth forecast to +4% to +10% for 2026, down from +10% to +16%.

- **Active M&A posture:** In a CNBC interview (May 2026), Doustdar confirmed that Novo Nordisk is "more active than ever" in seeking deals. He stated: "If our ambition is to help hundreds of millions of patients out there, then we need not just the best, but the broadest pipeline in the world." The company is pursuing a geographically agnostic approach with a focus on potential targets in China, Japan, and Germany.

- **Lars Fruergaard Jørgensen – former CEO:** Jørgensen, who led the company from January 2017 to August 2025, stepped down in May 2025 amid a strategic leadership transition. No new 2026 interviews or public statements from him were identified within the KW26 reporting window.

## Finanzielles

- **Q1 2026 Results (reported May 6, 2026 — most recent quarterly data):**
  - Reported net sales: DKK 96.8 billion (~USD 15.2 billion), +32% year-over-year on a constant exchange rate (CER) basis, significantly inflated by a one-time reversal of provisions related to the U.S. 340B Drug Pricing Program (~USD 4.2 billion).
  - Reported operating profit: DKK 59.6 billion (+65% reported).
  - Adjusted operating profit: DKK 32,858 million (approximately −6% at CER, excluding the 340B effect).
  - **Obesity Care segment:** Adjusted sales +22% at CER. The Wegovy pill contributed DKK 2,256 million in Q1 — well above analyst consensus of ~DKK 1.16 billion — with over 1.3 million prescriptions in the quarter.
  - **Ozempic (injectable semaglutide):** Sales −14% at CER, driven by lower U.S. net pricing, partially offset by volume growth.
  - **U.S. operations:** Adjusted sales −11% at CER due to pricing pressure; partially offset by Wegovy pill volume.
  - **International operations:** Sales +6% at CER, driven by volume growth across markets.

- **Updated 2026 full-year guidance (raised at Q1 2026):** Novo Nordisk now expects adjusted sales and adjusted operating profit to decline between −4% and −12% on a CER basis, an improvement from the previously projected −5% to −13%. Wegovy pill outperformance and international growth underpin the improved outlook.

- **Share buyback programme:** Running at DKK 11.2 billion in the current sub-tranche (May 6, 2026 – February 1, 2027); 20,959,179 B shares repurchased at an average of DKK 266.09 as of June 19, 2026.

- **Q2 2026 Results:** Expected in August 2026. No new financial disclosures occurred in the KW26 reporting window.

## Strategie & Ausblick

- **Pipeline focus — CagriSema and zenagamtide:** CagriSema (cagrilintide + semaglutide 2.4 mg / 2.4 mg) completed its Phase 3 REIMAGINE program with positive results for type 2 diabetes and weight reduction; an NDA for weight management was filed in the U.S. in December 2025. Zenagamtide (GIP/GLP-1/glucagon triple agonist) also featured at ADA 2026; a Phase 3 programme in type 2 diabetes is planned for H2 2026. These assets are being positioned as the next generation succeeding and complementing injectable semaglutide (Ozempic).

- **Hemophilia pipeline — denecimig (Mim8):** A BLA was submitted to the U.S. FDA in September 2025 for denecimig. Phase 3 FRONTIER4 interim data will be presented at ISTH in July 2026. With a 96.4% reduction in annualised bleeding events in Phase 3, denecimig represents a significant non-GLP-1 pipeline asset for Novo Nordisk.

- **Akero Therapeutics acquisition (completed December 2025):** Novo Nordisk paid ~USD 5.2 billion to acquire Akero Therapeutics and its FGF21 analogue efruxifermin (EFX), a Phase 3 MASH (metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis) candidate. The deal is expected to create a ~3 percentage-point headwind to adjusted operating profit growth in 2026 due to increased R&D costs.

- **Vivtex Corporation partnership (February 25, 2026):** A USD 2.1 billion collaboration with Vivtex Corporation to develop next-generation oral biologic medicines for obesity and diabetes, extending Novo Nordisk's oral formulation capabilities beyond the existing Wegovy pill. Vivtex receives upfront payments, research funding, milestones, and tiered royalties; Novo Nordisk assumes full responsibility for development, manufacturing, and commercialisation.

- **International Wegovy pill expansion:** First launches outside the U.S. planned for H2 2026, including the UAE. Further rollouts in additional markets expected to accelerate volume growth and reduce dependency on U.S. pricing dynamics.

- **U.S. manufacturing commitment:** Novo Nordisk has committed USD 10 billion to U.S.-based manufacturing capacity, contingent in part on FDA approval for domestic production of Wegovy tablets. This initiative positions the company within the broader U.S. pharmaceutical supply-chain reshoring trend.

- **Portfolio and cost discipline:** As part of the restructuring under Doustdar, early-stage programmes outside the cardiometabolic core (including certain liver disease, oncology, and stem cell programmes) have been discontinued. The company is returning to its "DNA" in diabetes and obesity while investing in broadening the pipeline through M&A and partnerships.
