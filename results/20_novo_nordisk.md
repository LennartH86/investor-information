# Novo Nordisk (NOVO-B.CO)
_KW21 / 2026 | Zeitraum: 16.05.2026 – 23.05.2026_

## Aktuelle Meldungen

- **Share Repurchase Programme – weekly update (18 May 2026):** Novo Nordisk published its regular repurchase update. Since the overall programme began on 4 February 2026, the company had repurchased a total of 15,999,028 B shares at an average price of DKK 260.62 per share, equivalent to a total transaction value of DKK 4,169,728,617. The current tranche (initiated 6 May 2026) will repurchase shares worth up to DKK 11,200,000,010.45 through 1 February 2027, as part of the overall DKK 15 billion programme.
- **European Congress on Obesity – ECO2026 (12–15 May 2026, Istanbul):** Data presented at the conference showed high-dose Wegovy® caused nearly 28% weight loss in certain patient groups. OASIS-4 data for the Wegovy® pill demonstrated 21.6% weight loss in early responders and improved patient mobility. Novo Nordisk also unveiled semaglutide data addressing MASH (metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis), a liver disease affecting approximately 1 in 3 people living with overweight or obesity worldwide.
- **Wegovy® pill milestone:** Since its US launch on 5 January 2026, total prescriptions exceeded 2 million patients. For the week ending 17 April 2026, total weekly prescriptions surpassed 200,000 – the strongest-ever GLP-1 volume launch in the US.
- **FDA approval – Awiqli® (announced with Q1 results, 6 May 2026):** The FDA approved Awiqli®, the first-ever once-weekly basal insulin for type 2 diabetes, expanding Novo Nordisk's diabetes portfolio.
- **Phase 3 success – etavopivat (announced with Q1 results, 6 May 2026):** The HIBISCUS phase 3 trial of etavopivat in sickle cell disease successfully met both co-primary endpoints, strengthening the rare blood disorders pipeline.

## Management

- **CEO Mike Doustdar** (took over from Lars Fruergaard Jørgensen, who stepped down in 2025): No new public interviews or statements specifically in the week of 16–23 May 2026 were identified. Earlier in May, Doustdar outlined three strategic priorities for 2026: (1) accelerating commercial execution, (2) advancing the pipeline, and (3) maintaining financial discipline.
- **Lars Fruergaard Jørgensen departure (background):** Jørgensen stepped down as CEO of Novo Nordisk in 2025 after serving as President and CEO since January 2017. Mike Doustdar, who joined Novo Nordisk in 1992 and held roles spanning finance, operations, marketing, and international business, succeeded him as President and CEO.
- **Chief Scientific Officer Martin Holst Lange** stated plans to conduct over 200 meetings with potential partners and acquisition targets from China, Japan, and Germany in 2026, reflecting an intensified business development posture.

## Finanzielles

- **Q1 2026 Results (published 6 May 2026):**
  - Reported net sales: +32% to DKK 96.8 billion (~USD 15.2 billion), lifted significantly by a one-time reversal of a provision related to the US 340B Drug Pricing Program (~USD 4.2 billion)
  - Adjusted operating profit: DKK 32,858 million (−6% at constant exchange rates, CER)
  - Reported operating profit: +65% to DKK 59.6 billion
  - Adjusted Obesity Care sales: +22% CER; total volume growth +44% CER, partially offset by lower realised prices (particularly in China)
  - Wegovy® pill revenue in Q1 2026: DKK 2.26 billion – well above analyst consensus of DKK 1.16 billion
  - Ozempic® sales Q1 2026: −14% CER, primarily driven by lower realised prices in the commercial channel
- **Full-year 2026 guidance (raised with Q1 results):** Adjusted sales and operating profit now expected to contract between 4% and 12% CER (improved from previous guidance of −5% to −13% CER).
- **Share repurchase programme:** Total programme of up to DKK 15 billion over 12 months from 4 February 2026. As of 13 May 2026, approximately DKK 4.17 billion (27.8%) had been repurchased.

## Strategie & Ausblick

- **Wegovy® pill as primary growth driver:** With over 2 million patients since the January 2026 US launch, the oral Wegovy® pill is demonstrating 16.6% weight loss in Phase 3 – the highest published figure for any oral GLP-1 competitor, including Eli Lilly's orforglipron.
- **Medicare opportunity:** CEO Doustdar has estimated a potential ~15 million new patients when US Medicare begins reimbursing obesity medications, representing the single largest untapped volume opportunity for the company.
- **International Wegovy® pill rollout:** Global launches are planned for the second half of 2026.
- **Pipeline focus areas:** Core development programmes span Diabetes (including cardiovascular/kidney disease indications), Obesity (including MASH), Rare Endocrine Disorders, and Rare Blood Disorders. Key modalities include small molecules, proteins/peptides/monoclonal antibodies, siRNA, and gene therapy. CagriSema (weekly injection, semaglutide + amylin analogue) and two triple-agonist candidates (GLP-1/GIP/glucagon combinations) are central late-stage assets.
- **M&A and business development:** Novo Nordisk acquired Akero Therapeutics in October 2025 for USD 5.2 billion (metabolic disorder small molecules). In February 2026, the company entered a USD 2.1 billion partnership with Vivtex Corporation to develop next-generation oral biologics for obesity and diabetes. Management has described the company as "more active than ever" in seeking partnerships and acquisitions, with China, Japan, and Germany cited as geographic priorities for targets.
- **Balance sheet impact of Catalent acquisition:** Cash flow remains affected by the USD 11.7 billion acquisition of Catalent manufacturing sites, which was intended to boost production capacity but continues to weigh on the balance sheet.
