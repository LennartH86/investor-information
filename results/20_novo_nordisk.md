# Novo Nordisk (NOVO-B.CO)
_KW23 / 2026 | Zeitraum: 30.05.2026 – 06.06.2026_

## Aktuelle Meldungen

- **ADA 2026 Scientific Sessions (June 5–8, New Orleans):** Novo Nordisk presented extensive new data at the American Diabetes Association's 2026 Scientific Sessions. Post hoc analyses from the SELECT, STEP, ESSENCE, and OASIS trials explored the impact of semaglutide across a range of cardiometabolic conditions including obstructive sleep apnea, asthma-related outcomes, and liver health.
- **Zenagamtide (amycretin) Phase 2 data at ADA 2026:** Novo Nordisk's investigational unimolecular GLP-1 and amylin receptor agonist zenagamtide demonstrated significant A1C reductions with up to 14.6% weight loss in adults with type 2 diabetes. A Phase 3 development program for type 2 diabetes is planned to start in H2 2026.
- **CagriSema data presented at ADA 2026:** New data featuring CagriSema (cagrilintide + semaglutide combination) were also presented, underscoring Novo Nordisk's combination therapy strategy for superior weight loss outcomes.
- **Wegovy® pill international expansion:** Novo Nordisk launched the oral Wegovy® pill in the UAE, making it the first country outside the US to receive the product.
- **R&D Investor Event – June 7, 2026:** Novo Nordisk held a dedicated R&D investor event on June 7, 2026, presenting updates on the cardiometabolic pipeline and portfolio data. Investor sentiment ahead of the event was positive, with shares rising in anticipation.
- **Share Repurchase Programme – June 1, 2026 update:** Novo Nordisk disclosed continued execution of its share buyback programme, repurchasing B shares as part of the programme initiated on May 6, 2026, covering up to DKK 11.2 billion through February 1, 2027.

## Management

- **CEO Mike Doustdar** (President and CEO since mid-2025, succeeding Lars Fruergaard Jørgensen): Doustdar has stated that a key 2026 priority is strengthening the R&D pipeline by bringing new obesity and diabetes assets into the clinic and advancing the late-stage pipeline. He has also signalled openness to acquisitions across multiple geographies, including China, Japan, and Germany.
- **Lars Fruergaard Jørgensen (former CEO):** Stepped down in May 2025 after serving as President and CEO since January 2017. No new interviews or public statements from Jørgensen in 2026 were identified during the reporting period.
- **Combination Strategy as management focus:** Management has placed the company's combination drug approach (CagriSema, zenagamtide) at the center of long-term value creation, with pipeline progress taking center stage in investor communications during KW23.

## Finanzielles

- **Q1 2026 Results (published May 6, 2026):**
  - Reported net sales: DKK 96.8 billion (~USD 15.2 billion), +32% year-on-year in constant currency (CER), boosted by a one-time reversal of a provision relating to the US 340B Drug Pricing Program (~USD 4.2 billion).
  - Reported operating profit: DKK 59.6 billion (+65% on a reported basis).
  - Adjusted operating profit: DKK 32,858 million (approx. −6% at CER, excluding the 340B reversal).
  - **Wegovy® pill (oral):** Q1 2026 was the first quarter covering US sales. Revenue came in at DKK 2.26 billion, significantly above analyst estimates of DKK 1.16 billion. Over 1 million patients have used the pill since the January 2026 US launch.
  - **Adjusted Obesity Care sales:** +22% CER; total volume growth +44% CER, partially offset by lower realised prices.
  - **Ozempic® (injectable semaglutide):** Sales decreased 14% at CER, mainly driven by lower realised prices in the US commercial channel.
- **Full-year 2026 guidance (raised with Q1 results):** Novo Nordisk now expects adjusted sales and adjusted operating profit to contract between 4% and 12% CER — an improvement over the prior guidance of a 5%–13% decline.
- **Q2 2026 results** are expected in August 2026. No interim financial updates were announced during KW23.

## Strategie & Ausblick

- **Focus on metabolic core:** Novo Nordisk has wound down early-stage programs in non-metabolic areas (liver disease, oncology, stem cells), refocusing resources on its core obesity and diabetes franchise.
- **Pipeline advancement:** The Phase 3 initiation for zenagamtide (amycretin) in type 2 diabetes is planned for H2 2026. CagriSema remains a key late-stage asset. The company's agnostic, data-driven pipeline approach is being highlighted in investor communications.
- **Oral GLP-1 as primary growth driver:** The Wegovy® pill is positioned as a major commercial growth driver. Its rapid uptake — surpassing 1 million patients within months of the US launch — has exceeded expectations and is helping to offset headwinds from Ozempic® pricing pressure.
- **M&A and Business Development:** In October 2025, Novo Nordisk completed the acquisition of **Akero Therapeutics** (USD 5.2 billion), gaining Phase 3 MASH asset efruxifermin (EFX). In February 2026, a **USD 2.1 billion partnership with Vivtex Corporation** was announced to develop next-generation oral biologics for obesity and diabetes. Management is actively evaluating further acquisition targets, with a geographic focus on China, Japan, and Germany.
- **Market positioning:** Novo Nordisk continues to position itself as the leading player in the GLP-1 and cardiometabolic space, with ADA 2026 data presentations and the June 7 R&D investor event serving to reaffirm pipeline depth and differentiation ahead of expected competitive pressure from Eli Lilly and others.
