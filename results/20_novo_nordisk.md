# Novo Nordisk (NOVO-B.CO)
_KW22 / 2026 | Zeitraum: 26.05.2026 – 31.05.2026_

## Aktuelle Meldungen

- **Share Repurchase Programme – ongoing (as of 26–31 May 2026):** Novo Nordisk continues its share buyback programme initiated on 6 May 2026. The current tranche covers repurchases of up to DKK 11,200,000,010.45 through 1 February 2027, as part of the overall DKK 15 billion programme running from 4 February 2026. Weekly updates are published in accordance with EU Market Abuse Regulation; as of 18 May, approximately DKK 4.17 billion (~28%) of the overall programme had been completed.
- **Wegovy® HD launched in the US (April 2026, continued rollout):** Wegovy® HD (semaglutide 7.2 mg injectable) received FDA approval in March 2026 and was launched in the US on 7 April 2026. The STEP UP trial demonstrated a mean weight loss of 20.7%, and uptake is being monitored as part of Q2 2026 commercial performance.
- **Wegovy® pill surpasses 2 million patients:** Since its US launch on 5 January 2026, the oral Wegovy® pill has been used by more than 2 million patients. Weekly prescriptions exceeded 200,000 for the week ending 17 April 2026, marking the strongest GLP-1 volume launch in US history.
- **ADA Scientific Sessions (June 2026 – upcoming):** Novo Nordisk announced it will present new data from its cardiometabolic portfolio at the American Diabetes Association's 2026 Scientific Sessions, including results from the CagriSema programme and Wegovy® cardiovascular studies.
- **European Congress on Obesity (ECO2026, 12–15 May 2026):** Data presented showed oral Wegovy® (OASIS-4 trial) achieved 21.6% weight loss in early responders and improved patient mobility; high-dose injectable Wegovy® achieved nearly 28% weight loss in certain patient groups. MASH-related semaglutide data was also presented.

## Strategie & Ausblick

- **Oral GLP-1 as primary growth lever:** Novo Nordisk's Wegovy® pill (oral semaglutide) is positioned as the lead commercial priority for 2026. Phase 3 data showing 16.6% weight loss place it ahead of competing oral GLP-1 programmes, including Eli Lilly's orforglipron, on published efficacy.
- **Medicare coverage opportunity:** CEO Doustdar has estimated a potential ~15 million new patients when US Medicare begins reimbursing obesity medications, representing the single largest untapped volume opportunity.
- **International Wegovy® pill rollout:** Global expansion is planned for the second half of 2026.
- **Pipeline priorities:** Key late-stage assets include CagriSema (semaglutide + amylin analogue for superior weight loss), two triple-agonist candidates (GLP-1/GIP/glucagon), etavopivat (sickle cell disease – Phase 3 success in HIBISCUS trial), and programmes addressing MASH, cardiovascular, and rare endocrine disorders.
- **M&A and partnerships:** Novo Nordisk acquired Akero Therapeutics in October 2025 for USD 5.2 billion (metabolic/MASH focus). In February 2026, a USD 2.1 billion partnership with Vivtex Corporation was announced to develop next-generation oral biologics for obesity and diabetes. Management describes the company as "more active than ever" in business development, with a geographic focus on China, Japan, and Germany. The USD 11.7 billion acquisition of Catalent manufacturing sites continues to weigh on the balance sheet but is intended to secure long-term production capacity.
- **Strategic shift post-Jørgensen:** Under Doustdar, the focus has moved from aggressive volume growth to "defensive diversification," expanding into MASH and heart failure to reduce dependence on semaglutide as a single franchise.
