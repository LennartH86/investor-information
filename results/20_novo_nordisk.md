# Novo Nordisk (NOVO-B.CO)
_KW25 / 2026 | Zeitraum: 13.06.2026 – 20.06.2026_

## Aktuelle Meldungen

- **Wegovy Pill surpasses 3 million prescriptions (June 2026):** Novo Nordisk announced that Wegovy® (semaglutide) tablets 25 mg exceeded three million prescriptions in just over five months since its U.S. launch in January 2026. This corresponds to roughly one prescription filled every five seconds, making the Wegovy pill launch one of the strongest U.S. pharmaceutical launches by volume on record. The milestone was announced in conjunction with new clinical data presented at the American Diabetes Association® (ADA) 86th Scientific Sessions in New Orleans (June 5–8, 2026). The drug is bringing GLP-1 therapy to patients with obesity who were previously untreated or unable to access injectable formulations.

- **ADA 2026 Scientific Sessions – New Pipeline Data (June 5–8, 2026):** Novo Nordisk presented a broad array of clinical data from its cardiometabolic portfolio and pipeline at the ADA 2026 conference. Key datasets included results from the REIMAGINE program (CagriSema 2.4 mg / 2.4 mg), which demonstrated significant HbA1c reduction and weight loss across multiple Phase 3 studies in adults with type 2 diabetes — meeting both the primary endpoint (HbA1c) and confirmatory secondary endpoints (body weight). In addition, new data on zenagamtide were presented, underscoring Novo Nordisk's broadening cardiometabolic pipeline beyond GLP-1 monotherapy.

- **R&D Investor Event (June 7, 2026):** Alongside the ADA conference, Novo Nordisk hosted a dedicated R&D investor event on June 7, 2026, covering the science behind the ADA data and the Wegovy pill commercial milestone. The event was webcast live on the Novo Nordisk investor website.

- **International Wegovy Pill Expansion:** Novo Nordisk is preparing for the first launches of the Wegovy pill outside the United States in the second half of 2026, including a recently announced approval and market launch in the United Arab Emirates. Further international rollouts are expected throughout H2 2026.

- **Share Repurchase Programme ongoing:** Novo Nordisk's share repurchase programme (up to DKK 15 billion over 12 months, started February 4, 2026) continues. The sub-programme launched on May 6, 2026 covers up to DKK 11.2 billion in B-shares through February 1, 2027.

## Management

- **CEO Mike Doustdar – Longevity and Aesthetics Vision (June 7, 2026):** In a Bloomberg interview at the ADA 2026 conference, CEO Mike Doustdar (President & CEO since August 2025) outlined a vision extending beyond weight loss into longevity research and aesthetic medicine, signaling the company's intention to expand the therapeutic relevance of its GLP-1 and related platforms.

- **Doustdar on Accelerated R&D (Fierce Pharma, 2026):** In an interview with Fierce Pharma reflecting on his first year as CEO, Doustdar expressed increased optimism about Novo Nordisk's trajectory: "I'm a lot more optimistic today." He highlighted a visible acceleration in the R&D organization and the number of assets in development, stating the company has "seen a serious acceleration of our R&D organization and number of assets that are being developed." He teased further pipeline disclosures at an upcoming capital markets day.

- **Strategic Priorities for 2026:** Doustdar has outlined three key priorities: (1) accelerating commercial execution — including new product launches and direct-to-patient sales expansion; (2) progressing the clinical pipeline; and (3) maintaining financial discipline while investing selectively. He also reiterated the company's active posture on business development and M&A, stating Novo Nordisk needs "not just the best, but the broadest pipeline in the world."

- **Medicare Opportunity:** Doustdar has emphasized the potential of Medicare coverage for obesity drugs, targeting approximately 15 million new patients — a major long-term commercial opportunity that is expected to materially expand the addressable market for Wegovy.

## Finanzielles

- **Q1 2026 Results (reported May 6, 2026 — most recent quarterly data available):**
  - Reported net sales: DKK 96.8 billion (~USD 15.2 billion), +32% year-over-year on a constant exchange rate (CER) basis — significantly impacted by a one-time reversal of provisions related to the U.S. 340B Drug Pricing Program (~USD 4.2 billion).
  - Reported operating profit: DKK 59.6 billion (+65% reported).
  - Adjusted operating profit: DKK 32,858 million (~−6% on a CER basis, excluding the 340B effect).
  - **Obesity Care segment:** Adjusted sales +22% at CER. Wegovy pill contributed DKK 2,256 million in Q1 2026 — well above analyst expectations of ~DKK 1.16 billion — with over 1.3 million prescriptions in the quarter.
  - **Ozempic (injectable semaglutide):** Sales −14% at CER, driven by lower realized prices in the U.S. commercial channel, partially offset by volume growth.
  - **U.S. Operations:** Adjusted sales −11% at CER due to lower net pricing; partially offset by Wegovy volume growth.
  - **International Operations:** Sales +6% at CER, driven by higher volumes across markets.

- **Updated 2026 Full-Year Guidance (raised at Q1 2026):** Novo Nordisk now expects adjusted sales and adjusted operating profit to decline between −4% and −12% on a CER basis, an improvement from the previously projected range of −5% to −13%. The guidance improvement reflects increased expectations for GLP-1 product sales, led by strong Wegovy pill uptake.

- **Q2 2026 Results:** Expected to be published in August 2026. No additional financial disclosures occurred in the KW25 reporting window.

## Strategie & Ausblick

- **Active M&A Strategy:** CEO Doustdar confirmed in a CNBC interview (May 2026) that Novo Nordisk is "more active than ever" in seeking out deals, emphasizing that its ambition to serve hundreds of millions of patients requires the broadest pipeline in the world. The company is pursuing a geographically agnostic approach with a specific focus on China, Japan, and Germany. Business development is considered a core pillar alongside organic R&D investment.

- **Akero Therapeutics Acquisition:** Novo Nordisk completed the acquisition of Akero Therapeutics, gaining access to efruxifermin (EFX), a Phase 3 MASH (metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis) asset. This acquisition is expected to create an approximately 3-percentage-point headwind to adjusted operating profit growth in 2026.

- **Vivtex Corporation Partnership (February 25, 2026):** A USD 2.1 billion partnership with Vivtex Corporation was announced to co-develop next-generation oral biologics targeting obesity and diabetes — extending Novo Nordisk's oral formulation capabilities beyond the existing Wegovy pill.

- **Pipeline Focus — CagriSema and Zenagamtide:** At ADA 2026, CagriSema demonstrated significant reductions in HbA1c and body weight across the REIMAGINE Phase 3 program. Zenagamtide, a GIP/GLP-1/glucagon triple agonist, also featured prominently. A Phase 3 program for zenagamtide in type 2 diabetes is planned for H2 2026. These assets are positioned as the next-generation platform to succeed and complement semaglutide.

- **Portfolio Refocus on Cardiometabolic Core:** Novo Nordisk has discontinued early-stage programs outside its metabolic core (including areas of liver disease, oncology, and stem cells), concentrating fully on its obesity and diabetes franchise. This strategic focus is intended to accelerate execution and improve capital allocation.

- **Oral GLP-1 as Primary Growth Driver:** The Wegovy pill is viewed as the most important near-term commercial growth lever, providing a pathway to address pricing pressure on injectable Ozempic and expanding the patient population. International launches in H2 2026 are expected to further accelerate volume growth.

- **Capital Markets Day:** CEO Doustdar indicated that further pipeline disclosures and strategic updates are planned for an upcoming capital markets day, where earlier-stage assets not yet publicly detailed are expected to be revealed.
