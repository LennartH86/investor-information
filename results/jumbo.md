## Latest News
- Jumbo reported H1 FY2026 results on 24.09.2026: group sales of €519.26m (+4.42% YoY) and net profit of €120.60m (+2.92% YoY).
- Gross margin narrowed to 53.5% from a year earlier, mainly due to the company absorbing a VAT increase in Romania and facing currency pressure there.
- The Board of Directors approved an extraordinary cash distribution of €1.00 per share, signaling confidence in the company's cash flow and capital position.
- Regionally, Greece, Cyprus and Bulgaria all posted growth, while Romania sales declined 6.5% in H1.
- The share price eased from about 25.42 EUR (21.09.2026) to 24.62 EUR by 25.09.2026, a modest pullback following the earnings release.
- The share buyback program (min €1 / max €27.20 per share) that had been running through 25.09.2026 has now concluded alongside the earnings period.

## Management
- Konstantina Demiri remains CEO; Apostolos-Evangelos Vakakis, Executive Chairman, and CFO Polys Polycarpou presented the H1 2026 results on the 24.09.2026 earnings call.
- No management or board changes were reported in the period; commentary from the earnings call emphasized continued regional growth alongside near-term margin pressure from Romania.

## Financials
- H1 FY2026: sales €519.26m (+4.42% YoY), net profit €120.60m (+2.92% YoY); gross margin compressed to 53.5%.
- Full-year 2025 (for reference): revenue €1.233bn (+7%), net profit €320m (+4%), EBITDA €436m (+5%).
- FY2026 guidance reaffirmed: ~5% sales growth, net income of €310–320m, CapEx near €60m.
- Net cash position of approximately €473m continues to support investment, shareholder distributions, and the recently completed buyback.

## Strategy & Outlook
- A new Baia Mare hyper store in Romania is expected to open in October 2026, with further Romania and Cyprus openings planned for 2027.
- Four additional stores are in preparation in Greece, expected to open in 2028; Bulgaria remains earmarked for one more hyper store within the next two years.
- Long-term plan to grow the store network to up to 116 locations over the next decade across Greece, Cyprus, Bulgaria, Romania, Israel, and Canada.
- Reported plans (via Fox Group) to introduce the Jumbo brand in Canada via a former Toys "R" Us site.
- The extraordinary dividend and reaffirmed guidance signal management confidence despite Romania-driven margin softness, which remains the key trend to monitor going into H2.
