## Finanzielles
- H1 2026 (to 30 June): sales ~€519m (some sources cite ~€590m; figure unverified against the company's own release), net profit ~€120.6–121m, with gross margin down slightly year-on-year mainly due to Romanian VAT increases and currency effects.
- Q1 2026: group sales rose approximately 7.3% year-on-year.
- Store footprint as of 30 June 2026: 89 stores group-wide — 53 in Greece, 6 in Cyprus, 10 in Bulgaria, 20 in Romania.
- Shareholder distributions: total 2026 cash distributions of approximately €161.2 million (~€1.20/share) already paid; an additional extraordinary distribution of €1.00/share is scheduled with ex-date 16 November 2026.
- Romania remains the weak spot regionally, with sales down roughly 6–7% year-on-year for H1 2026, while Greece, Cyprus and Bulgaria continue to post positive growth.
- No Q3/nine-month 2026 results were found as published yet in this window; one source references a possible next earnings date around 20 November 2026, unconfirmed elsewhere.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- Capital allocation remains shareholder-friendly: regular dividend plus the upcoming extraordinary €1.00/share distribution (record date 17 November 2026), while a capital increase continues to be ruled out.
- Logistics investment continues, including a planned ~60,000 sqm distribution centre in Romania and planned distribution centres in Thessaloniki (completion 2027) and Oinofyta, Greece, to support Northern Greece and Bulgaria.
- Property strategy: the company plans to continue acquiring properties it currently leases, alongside new store openings.
- Turkey entry remains paused for physical stores, with online entry still targeted for late 2026 and physical investment acceleration expected from 2027 onward.
- Overall outlook: steady, cash-generative growth driven by Greece, Cyprus and Bulgaria, offsetting continued Romanian softness; FY2026 guidance of ~5% sales growth and €310–320m net profit remains the latest confirmed target, though not independently reconfirmed this week.
