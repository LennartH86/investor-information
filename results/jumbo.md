## Strategy & Outlook
- New Romania hyperstore opening planned for October 2026, alongside a new distribution center and further logistics investment in Romania.
- Further store openings planned in Romania, Cyprus and Greece; a Hungary online store is targeted by year-end 2026, with pop-up stores planned for 2027–28.
- A Giga Distribution Center (60,000 sqm) in Romania was acquired via an asset purchase in April 2026, and the company is reportedly eyeing further Romanian asset acquisitions amid the local market downturn.
- Long-term ambition remains growing the store network toward up to 116 locations over the next decade across Greece, Cyprus, Bulgaria, Romania, Israel and Canada.
- Romania's margin and sales pressure remains the key trend to monitor into H2 2026, against an otherwise resilient, cash-generative, debt-free core business.
