- CEO/Chairman Apostolos Vakakis confirmed at the July 2026 AGM that the planned physical entry into Turkey is paused, citing an unfavorable, high-risk trade environment; an online-only entry into Turkey is still targeted for late 2026, with physical investment acceleration pushed out to 2027 and a horizon into 2028. He described 2026 as a year for strengthening cash reserves, with any capex step-up conditional on no broader geopolitical crisis.

## Financials
- H1 2026 (to 30 June): sales ~€519.26m (+4.42% y-o-y), net profit ~€120.6m (+2.92% y-o-y), operating profit ~€149m (+3.2% y-o-y).
- Gross margin 53.53%, down ~33 bps y-o-y, mainly due to a Romanian VAT increase and currency weakness.

- Shareholder distributions: ~€161.2m (€1.20/share) already distributed year-to-date in 2026; a further proposed €1.00/share extraordinary distribution would bring the 2026 total to €2.20/share, with an ex-date reported around 16 November 2026 (not independently confirmed).
- FY2026 guidance maintained: ~5% sales growth, net profit €310–320m.

## Strategy & Outlook
- Turkey expansion plans for physical stores remain frozen; online/e-commerce entry still targeted for late 2026, with store investment expected to accelerate from 2027, horizon to 2028.
- Continued logistics investment in Romania (new ~60,000 sqm "Giga" distribution centre) despite the segment's weak sales, alongside a new Cyprus store planned for 2027 and smaller pop-up store formats targeted for 2027/2028.
- Franchise network expansion via BALFIN into six new markets in H1 2026, plus a planned Hungary online store in late 2026 and a reported (unverified) Canada franchise move by Fox Group.
- Capital allocation remains shareholder-friendly: regular dividend plus a proposed extraordinary €1.00/share distribution, while M&A/capex stays focused on logistics and store/leasehold assets rather than large external deals.
- Overall outlook: steady, cash-rich growth profile with Greece/Cyprus/Bulgaria offsetting continued Romanian softness; FY2026 guidance (~5% sales growth, €310–320m net profit) unchanged.
