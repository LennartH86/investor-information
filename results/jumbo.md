## Management
- CEO/Chairman Apostolos Vakakis confirmed at the July 2026 AGM that the planned entry into Turkey is being paused, citing an unfavorable and high-risk business/trade environment; an online-only (e-commerce) entry into Turkey remains targeted for late 2026, with physical investment acceleration now pushed out to 2027 and a horizon into 2028.
- Strategic focus remains on existing Balkan markets (Greece, Cyprus, Bulgaria, Romania) and EU growth opportunities; a capital increase/share placement continues to be ruled out given current valuation levels.
- No new CEO interview specific to Jumbo S.A. (BELA) was found in this period. Note: search results also surfaced a CEO change (Jesper Højer) at the unrelated Dutch retailer "Jumbo Supermarkten" — not connected to Jumbo S.A. Greece.

## Financials
- H1 2026 (to 30 June): sales ~€519.26m (+4.42% y-o-y), net profit ~€120.6m (+2.92% y-o-y), operating profit ~€149m (+3.2% y-o-y).
- Gross margin 53.53%, down ~33 bps y-o-y, mainly due to a Romanian VAT increase and currency weakness.
- Balance sheet: ~€546.14m cash and equivalents as of 30 June 2026, no debt.
- Regional H1 performance: Greece (62% of sales) +7.08%, Cyprus +3.92%, Bulgaria +10.78%, Romania -6.5% (continued weak spot).
- FY2026 guidance maintained: ~5% sales growth, net profit €310–320m.
- Next scheduled results: H2/FY2026 report expected around 20 November 2026 (date not independently confirmed; treat as indicative).

## Strategy & Outlook
- Turkey expansion plans frozen for physical stores; online/e-commerce entry still targeted for late 2026, with store investment now expected to accelerate from 2027 with a horizon to 2028.
- Continued investment in store network expansion in Romania despite the segment's weak sales (including conversion of a former factory/distribution site, ~60,000 sqm, with a goal to double the Romanian store count within 10 years) and in Cyprus (new store planned for 2027).
- Store network: approx. 53 stores Greece, 20 Romania, 10 Bulgaria, 6 Cyprus.
- Capital allocation remains shareholder-friendly: regular dividend plus a proposed €1.00/share extraordinary distribution, while M&A/capex stays focused on store/leasehold acquisitions rather than large external deals.
- Overall outlook: steady, cash-rich growth profile with Greece/Cyprus/Bulgaria offsetting Romanian softness; FY2026 guidance (~5% sales growth, €310–320m net profit) unchanged.
