## Recent News
- Group sales for the eight months to August 2026 were up ~5.8% YoY, with August alone up ~9% YoY — running ahead of management's ~5% full-year sales growth target.
- Regional August performance: Greece (parent company) +11% YoY, Cyprus +9% YoY, Bulgaria +17% YoY; eight-month figures: Greece +8%, Cyprus +6%, Bulgaria +14%.
- Cyprus Mail (25.09.2026) confirmed H1 2026 net profit of €120.6m (+2.9% YoY) on sales of €519.3m (+4.4% YoY), and reported Jumbo is lining up a new Cyprus store.
- Analyst house Axia reiterated a constructive view on Jumbo's 2026 start, naming Greece and Bulgaria as the key growth levers.
- Fox Group, a Jumbo franchise partner, is reportedly working to bring the Jumbo brand to Canada via conversion of a former Toys "R" Us store location.

## Management
- Konstantina Demiri continues as CEO; Evaggelos-Apostolos Vakakis remains Chairman. No management or board changes reported this period.

## Financials
- FY2026 guidance reaffirmed: ~5% sales growth, net profit of EUR 310–320m; management still expects energy-price and transport-cost volatility from ongoing Middle East tensions.
- 2026 year-to-date shareholder distributions total EUR 161.2m (EUR 1.20/share), on top of the previously resolved additional EUR 1.00/share — EUR 2.20/share (~EUR 296m) in total for 2026.
- Cash position remains ~EUR 546m with zero debt (last reported at H1 2026; no updated balance-sheet figures this period).

## Strategy & Outlook
- Continued investment in Romania (Giga Distribution Center, new hyperstore) despite ongoing VAT-driven margin and sales pressure there.
- New Cyprus store in the pipeline; potential Canada market entry via Fox Group's Toys "R" Us conversion.
- Company continues to eye further Romanian asset acquisitions amid the local market downturn.
- Smaller-format pop-up stores for tourist/high-footfall locations still targeted for 2027/2028.
- Romania's margin trajectory remains the key variable to watch into H2 2026 results, against an otherwise resilient, cash-generative, debt-free core business.
