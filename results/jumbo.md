- Group-wide October 2026 sales rose approximately 13% y/y (the best month of 2026), with 10-month cumulative group sales growth of about 6.7% y/y (consistent with last week's figures, no material revision found).
- Trading briefs (Stockopedia) reported Q3/9-month 2026 EBIT up roughly 10% y/y and EBITDA up roughly 16.6% y/y — the first indication of actual income-statement profitability metrics for this period, beyond pure sales trading updates.
- The extraordinary cash distribution of €1.00/share (gross) was reconfirmed: ex-dividend date 16 November 2026, record date 17 November, payment from 20 November 2026, bringing total 2026 shareholder cash distributions to about €2.20/share (~€295.6m aggregate, figures from press commentary, not independently audited).
- Jumbo extended its e-commerce footprint into Hungary in early November 2026, currently served from its existing Romanian online operation.
- Asset manager Fidelity (via parent FMR LLC) raised its indirect voting rights in Jumbo above 5% in mid/late October 2026, a holding disclosure that continued to be referenced in coverage this week.
- Press commentary referenced plans for the first Jumbo-branded stores in Moldova and Canada before the end of 2026; this timeline appears to conflict with an earlier guidance of an early-2027 Toronto opening, so it should be treated as unconfirmed pending an official company statement.

- FY2026 guidance (last confirmed): sales growth of ~5%, net profit of €310–320m, capital expenditure of roughly €60m.
- New this week: Q3/9-month 2026 trading briefs indicate EBIT +10% y/y and EBITDA +16.6% y/y, the first operating-profitability signals for the period; these are press-brief figures, not yet a full audited income statement.
- 10-month 2026 sales growth of ~6.7% y/y continues to run ahead of full-year sales guidance of ~5%.
- The group's net cash position remains strong at roughly €473m, underpinning both the dividend/distribution policy and continued investment capacity.
- H1 2026 recap (confirmed): sales up ~4% to €590m, net profit up ~3% to €121m; gross margin eased ~33bps to 53.5%, pressured by cost dynamics in Romania.
- FY2025 context (confirmed): record sales of ~€1.23bn and net profit of ~€320m, with a €0.70/share ordinary dividend approved at the July 2026 AGM.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- Capital allocation remains shareholder-friendly: ordinary dividend plus the extraordinary €1.00/share distribution due later this month; management continues to rule out a capital increase or new share placement.
- Logistics/supply-chain investment continues, anchored by the pre-agreed acquisition of a ~60,000 sqm "Giga" distribution center in Romania, alongside previously announced distribution centers in Thessaloniki (completion 2027) and Oinofyta, Greece.
- International footprint expansion continues on two fronts: incremental e-commerce rollout (new Hungary online presence served from Romania) and physical store expansion, with Moldova and Canada now mentioned as near-term targets before end-2026 (unconfirmed timeline, pending official company statement).
- Turkey entry remains paused for physical stores, with an online-only launch still expected and physical investment acceleration targeted from 2027 with a 2028 horizon.
- An unconfirmed item referencing a share sale and purchase agreement with Eurobank involving subsidiary "Herald 2" remains unresolved; no new development was found this week.
- Overall outlook: broad-based sales growth across Greece, Cyprus, Bulgaria and Romania, improving Q3/9-month profitability signals (EBIT/EBITDA growth), and a strong net cash position support the constructive medium-term picture; FY2026 guidance of ~5% sales growth and €310–320m net profit remains the latest confirmed target.
