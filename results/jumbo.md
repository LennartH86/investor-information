## Strategy & Outlook
- Turkey: physical store expansion remains frozen; online/e-commerce entry still targeted for late 2026, with physical investment expected to accelerate from 2027, horizon to 2028.
- Romania: continued logistics investment (new ~60,000 sqm "Giga" distribution centre) despite the segment's ongoing sales weakness; long-term ambition to roughly double the Romanian store count over the coming decade.
- Franchise network: BALFIN-led expansion into six new international markets (with a planned China logistics hub to support them), plus Fox Group's continued Israel and Canada rollout under the Jumbo brand.
- New store pipeline: a new Cyprus store planned for 2027, an online store in Hungary and online entry into Turkey both targeted for late 2026.
- Capital allocation: shareholder-friendly, combining a regular dividend with a proposed extraordinary distribution, while capex/M&A stays focused on logistics and store/leasehold assets rather than large external deals; a capital increase remains ruled out.
- Overall outlook: steady, cash-generative growth with Greece, Cyprus and Bulgaria offsetting persistent Romanian softness; FY2026 guidance (~5% sales growth, €310–320m net profit) unchanged as of the latest confirmed update.
