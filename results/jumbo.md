## Aktuelle Meldungen
- H1 FY2026 results (released 24.09.2026): group sales of €519.26m (+4.42% YoY) and net profit of €120.60m (+2.92% YoY).
- Regional performance: Greece +7%, Bulgaria +11%, Cyprus +4%, while Romania sales declined 6.5%, pressured by inflation, currency effects and a VAT hike.
- Gross margin narrowed to 53.5% (down 33 basis points YoY), mainly due to the Romanian VAT increase and currency pressure.
- The Board approved an extraordinary cash distribution of €1.00 per share, bringing the total planned 2026 cash distribution to €2.20 per share (~€296m).
- Jumbo lines up a new store opening in Cyprus for 2027, as market conditions there normalize after earlier Middle East-related disruption near the island.
- Share price recovered somewhat over the period, moving from the post-earnings dip (24.62 EUR on 25.09.2026) to 24.75 EUR by 28.09.2026.

## Management
- Apostolos-Evangelos Vakakis, Executive Chairman, and CFO Polys Polycarpou, together with Head of Investor Relations Amalia Karamitsoli, presented the H1 2026 results on the earnings call.
- Vakakis reiterated that the company's strategic direction favors growth, stating that directing capital toward further investment and growth is the first priority given the strong cash position.
- No changes to the CEO/management structure were reported during the period.

## Finanzielles
- H1 FY2026: sales €519.26m (+4.42% YoY), net profit €120.60m (+2.92% YoY); gross margin 53.5% (-33bps YoY).
- Cash position of approximately €546m at end of June, with a debt-free balance sheet.
- Franchise partner sales reached approximately €43m in H1, up from €38m a year earlier.
- FY2026 guidance reaffirmed: ~5% sales growth, net income of €310–320m.
- Total planned 2026 shareholder distribution of €2.20 per share (~€296m), including €1.20/share already paid year-to-date plus the newly approved €1.00/share extraordinary distribution.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- A new hyperstore in Romania is set to open in October 2026, following the completed acquisition of a 60,000 sqm distribution center in Romania (closed April 2026) to support logistics growth.
- Further expansion planned: a new store in Cyprus for 2027, additional Greece store openings, a Hungary online store launch by year-end 2026, and pop-up stores planned for 2027-28.
- Long-term ambition to grow the network to up to 116 stores over the next decade across Greece, Cyprus, Bulgaria, Romania, Israel, and Canada, including a reported plan (via Fox Group) to bring the Jumbo brand to Canada via a former Toys "R" Us site.
- Management continues to prioritize reinvestment for growth alongside shareholder returns, with Romania's margin pressure remaining the key risk to watch into H2 2026.
