- **Bewertung:** With NAV per share at 53.3 EUR as of 30.06.2026 and the stock now at 25.60 EUR, the holding discount has widened further to roughly 52% – still the dominant valuation driver for this position, alongside low trading liquidity. ## Strategie & Ausblick
- Italmobiliare continues operating as a diversified investment holding with a medium- to long-term horizon; portfolio companies include Caffè Borbone, Officina Profumo-Farmaceutica di Santa Maria Novella, CDS-Casa della Salute, Italgen, SIDI Sport, Capitelli, Tecnica Group, Iseo, Bene Assicurazioni and Clessidra Holding.
- CDS-Casa della Salute keeps expanding via M&A (recent entries in Valle d'Aosta, Liguria and Sardinia, plus a new Turin clinic, bringing the network to 43 clinics across four regions), underscoring active portfolio management alongside partial divestments such as the Tecnica/Zanatta buyback.
- Strategic priorities remain internationalisation of consumer brands (Borbone, Santa Maria Novella) and continued build-out of the healthcare and insurance businesses (CDS, Bene Assicurazioni).
- The persistently wide NAV discount (~52% at current prices) remains the central watch item: the market is still not rewarding the holding's operating growth, and no catalyst for a re-rating has emerged in the current period; raw material cost exposure at Caffè Borbone remains the main earnings risk.
