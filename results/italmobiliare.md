With the 9-month 2026 results due 11.11.2026 (6 days away) as the next concrete catalyst, a cautious Hold still looks more appropriate than adding further until that print and buyback/M&A progress confirm the turnaround.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- Continued portfolio rotation ("M&A e cessioni" — M&A and disposals): exits from mature/non-core holdings (Capitelli, partial Tecnica Group exit) at attractive terms (approx. 3x cash-on-cash on Capitelli) to free up capital for new acquisitions.
- 2026 investment activity spans healthcare, lifestyle and food: Servizi Medici Hesperia via Casa della Salute (May 2026, targeting 100 million EUR revenue and expansion into Valle d'Aosta), CF Life Compagnia di Assicurazioni Vita (April 2026), Technos Medica (March 2026), and the approx. 60% stake (roughly 140 million EUR) in L'Aromatika, the holding company behind Caffè Borbone, alongside founder Massimo Renda retaining 40%.
- Confirmed strategic focus on "eccellenze italiane" (Italian champions) in resilient industrial, services and consumer sectors (e.g. Caffè Borbone, CDS, Officina Profumo-Farmaceutica di Santa Maria Novella), with continued appetite for food & beverage and services deals.
- The buyback programme continues to signal management's own view that the shares trade below intrinsic value, consistent with the persistently wide NAV discount (~53%) that analysts (Kepler Cheuvreux) still view with caution.
- Next concrete catalyst: 9-month 2026 figures on 11.11.2026, plus possible closing confirmations for Capitelli (Q4 2026, pending Golden Power clearance) and the Tecnica Group stake sale.
