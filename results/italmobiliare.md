## Strategy & Outlook
- Continued portfolio rotation ("M&A e cessioni" — M&A and disposals): exits from more mature/non-core holdings (Capitelli, partial Tecnica Group exit) at attractive terms (approx. 3x cash-on-cash on Capitelli) to free up capital for new acquisitions.
- 2026 investment activity spans healthcare and lifestyle/food sectors: Servizi Medici Hesperia via Casa della Salute (May 2026, targeting 100 million EUR in revenue and expanding into Valle d'Aosta), CF Life Compagnia di Assicurazioni Vita (April 2026), Technos Medica (March 2026), and a reported (unconfirmed closing) 60% investment of roughly 140 million EUR in L'Aromatika, the holding company behind Caffè Borbone, alongside founder Massimo Renda retaining 40%.
- Confirmed strategic focus on "eccellenze italiane" (Italian champions) in resilient industrial, services and consumer sectors (e.g. Caffè Borbone, CDS, Officina Profumo-Farmaceutica di Santa Maria Novella), with company commentary pointing to continued interest in food & beverage and services deals.
- The ongoing buyback programme continues to signal management's own view that the shares trade below intrinsic value, consistent with the persistently wide NAV discount.
- Next concrete catalyst: 9-month 2026 figures on 11 November 2026, plus possible closing confirmations for Capitelli (Q4 2026) and the Tecnica Group stake sale.
