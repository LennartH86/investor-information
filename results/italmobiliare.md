## Strategie & Ausblick
- Italmobiliare continues operating as a diversified investment holding with a medium- to long-term horizon; portfolio companies include Caffè Borbone, Officina Profumo-Farmaceutica di Santa Maria Novella, CDS-Casa della Salute, Italgen, SIDI Sport, Capitelli, Tecnica Group, Iseo, Bene Assicurazioni and Clessidra Holding.
- CDS-Casa della Salute keeps expanding via M&A (recent entries in Valle d'Aosta, Liguria and Sardinia, plus a new Turin clinic, bringing the network to 43 clinics across four regions), and Bene Assicurazioni grew its perimeter via the Tecnocasa/CF Assicurazioni deal in March 2026, underscoring active portfolio management alongside partial divestments such as the Tecnica/Zanatta buyback.
- Strategic priorities remain internationalisation of consumer brands (Caffè Borbone, Santa Maria Novella) and continued build-out of the healthcare and insurance businesses (CDS, Bene Assicurazioni).
- The persistently wide NAV discount (~52% at current prices) remains the central watch item: the market continues to not fully reward the holding's operating growth, and no catalyst for a re-rating has emerged in the current period.
