## Management
- Carlo Pesenti remains Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Italmobiliare, a combined role formalized in April 2026 after shareholders approved a shift to a one-tier board and control system.
- Pesenti also chairs Clessidra Private Equity SGR and Officina Profumo-Farmaceutica di Santa Maria Novella, and holds board seats at Tecnica Group and Caffè Borbone, giving him wide oversight of the group's portfolio companies.
- No new 2026 media interview with Carlo Pesenti was found in this search cycle; available material remains limited to corporate presentations, governance announcements and biographical profiles (Morningstar, MarketScreener, company IR site).

## Financials
- H1 2026: aggregate revenue and income from Industrial Portfolio Companies reached €807 million (+9.2% y/y, up from €739.3 million), with aggregate EBITDA of €49.9 million (+14.9% y/y, up from €43.4 million); standout performers were CDS-Casa della Salute (+34.4%), Bene Assicurazioni (+24.2%), Iseo (+7.2%), Santa Maria Novella (+6.7%) and Caffè Borbone (+2.7%).
- Q1 2026: aggregate Portfolio Companies revenue of €395.7 million (+5.4% y/y) and aggregate EBITDA of €28.1 million (+16.5% y/y).
- Group NAV stood at approximately €2,240.5 million as of 30 June 2026, down 0.7% versus December 2025, mainly due to holding costs and dividend payments, partially offset by fair-value gains at portfolio companies (versus ~€2,299.7 million / ~€54.7 per share reported for March 2026).
- Net financial position was positive at €169.4 million as of 30 June 2026, indicating continued balance-sheet flexibility for further investment or buybacks.

## Strategy & Outlook
- Italmobiliare's stated strategy remains to support the development of its portfolio companies while staying opportunistic on further investment and divestment moves, illustrated by the completed exit of the Tecnica Group minority stake to the Zanatta family (September 2026).
- CDS-Casa della Salute continues an active M&A and clinic-opening strategy (Liguria, Sardinia, Turin, and entry into Valle d'Aosta), reaching 43 clinics across 4 regions.
- Bene Assicurazioni expanded its insurance business in March 2026 by acquiring CF Assicurazioni's home P&C business and a 51% stake in CF Vita from Tecnocasa.
- The group continues to run a share buyback program, and management has flagged international retail expansion (e.g., Santa Maria Novella's first directly operated store in China, opened September 2026) as part of the growth path for portfolio companies.
- Next milestone: Q3 2026 financial disclosure scheduled for 11 November 2026.
