# Italmobiliare (ITM)
_Updated: 22.10.2026 | Period: 15.10.2026 – 22.10.2026_

## Portfolio Analysis
- **Position:** 130 shares
- **Average purchase price:** 26.99 EUR
- **Current price:** 24.80 EUR
- **Market value:** 3224.00 EUR
- **Unrealized gain/loss:** -300.71 EUR (-8.53%)
- **Portfolio allocation:** 2.02%
- **Dividends received:** 90.74 EUR
- **Assessment:** At H1 2026 Italmobiliare reported a NAV per share of 53.3 EUR, so the current price of 24.80 EUR implies a discount to NAV ("sconto sul NAV") of roughly 53% — an unusually wide gap for a holding company, which CEO Carlo Pesenti has repeatedly called unjustified by fundamentals. Combined with growing industrial-portfolio revenue (+9.2% H1) and EBITDA (+14.9% H1), a positive net financial position, an ongoing buyback, and profitable exits (Capitelli, partial Tecnica Group) funding new acquisitions, the fundamentals look stronger than the share price suggests. Despite the -8.53% unrealized loss since purchase, the wide NAV discount and continued portfolio progress argue for Hold/Add rather than Reduce, though the discount is structural and may persist.

## Recent News
- Early October 2026: Italmobiliare signed a binding agreement to sell its full 80% stake in Capitelli Fratelli (Italian charcuterie/salumi producer) to French agri-food group CA Animation. Enterprise value (100% basis) of 36 million EUR, with a price adjustment tied to FY2026 results. Closing expected in Q4 2026, subject to "Golden Power" clearance from the Italian government. Expected return for Italmobiliare: approx. 3x cash-on-cash and ~20% IRR on the 2019 investment.
- The previously announced partial exit from Tecnica Group (sale of 9.09% to Prime Holding/the Zanatta family, valuation approx. 550 million EUR) remains in progress; Italmobiliare retains a residual stake of approx. 30.91% plus options for a possible future full exit.
- Ongoing share buyback programme (started 16 March 2026 under the April 2025 AGM authorization) continues.
- No new standalone company "comunicato stampa" (press release) found specifically dated October 2026; the Capitelli news is primarily covered by Italian financial media (Soldionline, MilanoFinanza) and international trade outlets (Leaders League).
- Next key date: Board meeting on 11 November 2026 to review the interim report for the period ended 30 September 2026 (9-month figures).

## Management

- Pesenti has repeatedly argued (at the October 2025 Capital Market Day and in a 2026 "Grandi Imprese" podcast episode) that the market does not fully price in the holding's NAV, pointing to a 64% increase in shareholder distributions over six years.

## Strategy & Outlook
- Continued portfolio rotation: exits from more mature/non-core holdings (Capitelli, partial Tecnica Group exit) at attractive terms (3x cash-on-cash, ~20% IRR on Capitelli) to free up capital for new acquisitions.
- Per PitchBook data, several new, not yet fully disclosed investments in healthcare/lifestyle during 2026: Servizi Medici (May 2026, outpatient healthcare services), CF Life Compagnia di Assicurazioni Vita (April 2026) and Technos Medica (March 2026) — deal sizes undisclosed.
- The company's July 2026 investor presentation confirms a continued focus on "eccellenze italiane" (Italian champions) in resilient industrial, services and consumer sectors.
- The ongoing buyback programme continues to signal management's own view that the shares trade below intrinsic value.
- Next concrete catalyst: 9-month 2026 figures on 11 November 2026, plus possible closing confirmations for Capitelli (Q4 2026) and Tecnica Group.
