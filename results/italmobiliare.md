## Strategy & Outlook
- Management continues to pursue active portfolio rotation: completed the sale of the remaining ~9% Tecnica Group stake (deal value ~€550 million, finalized 24.09.2026 with the Zanatta family/Prime Holding) at a sizeable capital gain, while continuing to deploy capital into add-on acquisitions (e.g., Servizi Medici Hesperia into the CDS healthcare platform in May 2026, CF Life Compagnia di Assicurazioni Vita in April 2026, Technos Medica in March 2026).
- CDS-Casa della Salute keeps expanding its clinic network (new region entries, e.g. Valle d'Aosta in H1 2026), consistent with the group's buy-and-build approach in healthcare services.
- With €169 million in net cash, the group has stated it remains focused on further M&A and selective divestments ("cessioni") to optimize the portfolio, alongside ongoing shareholder distributions (dividends and buybacks).
- Next milestone: Q3 2026 financial disclosure, Board meeting scheduled for 11.11.2026.
