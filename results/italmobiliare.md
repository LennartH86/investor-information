## Portfolio Analysis
- **Position:** 130 shares
- **Avg. purchase price:** 26.99 EUR
- **Current price:** 24.65 EUR
- **Market value:** 3204.50 EUR
- **Unrealized gain/loss:** -320.21 EUR (-9.08%)
- **Portfolio allocation:** 2.02%
- **Dividends received:** 90.74 EUR
- **Assessment:** At H1 2026, Italmobiliare reported NAV per share of 53.3 EUR, meaning the current 24.65 EUR price implies a discount to NAV of roughly 54% — a very wide gap even for a holding company, and management itself (CEO Carlo Pesenti) has publicly argued the market is not reflecting the group's full NAV. Combined with continued portfolio growth (H1 revenue +9.2%, EBITDA +14.9%), a positive net financial position, an active buyback program, and profitable divestments (Capitelli, Tecnica Group) generating cash for reinvestment, the fundamentals look stronger than the price action suggests; Hold/Add appears more sensible than Reduce, though the persistent NAV discount and the -9% unrealized loss warrant patience rather than urgency.

## Latest News
- Binding agreement (announced ~1-2 October 2026) to sell Italmobiliare's entire 80% stake in Capitelli (Italian cured-meat producer) to French agrifood group CA Animation, at an Enterprise Value of 36 million EUR (100% basis), with a price adjustment tied to 2026 results; closing expected in Q4 2026, subject to Golden Power clearance. Expected cash-on-cash return of ~3x and IRR of ~20% on the 2019 investment.
- On 31 July 2026, Italmobiliare agreed to sell a 9.09% stake in Tecnica Group to Prime Holding (Zanatta family) for roughly 50-60 million EUR, implying a company valuation of ~550 million EUR; closing originally expected by end of Q3 2026. Italmobiliare retains a ~30.91% stake and purchase options that could lead to a full future exit.
- Next scheduled corporate event: Board meeting on 11 November 2026 to review the additional periodic financial report at 30 September 2026 (9-month figures).
- Ongoing treasury share buyback program, launched 16 March 2026 under the April 2025 shareholder authorization.

## Management
- CEO Carlo Pesenti has been Chief Executive Officer since 27 May 2014 and became Chairman as of 28 April 2026.
- Pesenti has repeatedly argued (including at the October 2025 Capital Market Day) that the market does not fully reflect the holding's NAV, citing a 64% increase in shareholder distributions over six years.
- Management has signaled an active portfolio rotation strategy: continuing to grow core industrial holdings (Caffè Borbone, Officina Profumo-Farmaceutica di Santa Maria Novella, Clessidra) while selectively exiting mature positions (Capitelli, Tecnica Group) and redeploying capital into new M&A.

## Financials

- Q1 2026 (reported 13 May 2026): aggregate industrial portfolio revenue 395.7 million EUR (+5.4%), EBITDA 28.1 million EUR (+16.5%); NAV 2,299.7 million EUR; net financial position 225.2 million EUR.

## Strategy & Outlook
- Continued focus on portfolio rotation: exiting mature/non-core holdings (Capitelli, partial Tecnica Group exit) at favorable returns (3x cash-on-cash, ~20% IRR on Capitelli) to fund new acquisitions.
- Management is actively evaluating new M&A opportunities, supported by a positive net financial position and divestment proceeds.
- Ongoing share buyback program signals management's own view that shares are undervalued relative to NAV.
- Next concrete catalyst: 9-month 2026 results due 11 November 2026, plus potential closing confirmations for the Capitelli (Q4 2026) and Tecnica Group transactions.
