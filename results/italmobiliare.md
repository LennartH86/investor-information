## Strategie & Ausblick
- Portfolio rotation at Tecnica Group continues: following the 24.09.2026 closing, Italmobiliare holds 30.91% (Zanatta family via Prime Holding at 69.09%), with call options retained on the residual stake.
- Capitelli Fratelli disposal: binding agreement (signed early October 2026) to sell the full stake to the CA Animation group at an enterprise value of 36 mln EUR (cash-free/debt-free), implying an expected cash-on-cash multiple of ~3x and IRR of ~20%; closing still targeted for Q4 2026, subject to Golden Power clearance.
- Continued bolt-on growth at the portfolio-company level rather than new large holding-level acquisitions: Casa della Salute's acquisition of Servizi Medici Hesperia (May 2026, CDS targeting >100 mln EUR revenue in 2026), plus the previously reported Caffè Borbone/L'Aromatika (60%) and Sidi Sport deals; exact 2026 dating of the latter two remains unverified in this round of research.
- Buyback programme (active since 16.03.2026 under the April 2025 AGM mandate) continues; strategic focus stays on portfolio clean-up/exits at attractive multiples, with management likely to point to the wide holding discount as a buyback rationale if the current share-price weakness persists.
