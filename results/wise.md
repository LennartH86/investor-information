## Aktuelle Meldungen
- The dominant news flow in August 2026 is a wave of US securities class action filings against Wise Group plc (Nasdaq: WSE). Law firms including Levi & Korsinsky, Glancy Prongay Wolke & Rotter, Portnoy Law Firm, Robbins LLP and SBS Law issued investor alerts and lead-plaintiff notices between 10.08. and 21.08.2026.
- The complaints cover a class period of 11.05.2026 to 23.07.2026 and allege materially false or misleading statements and failure to disclose adverse facts about the company's business, operations and prospects. Note: the portfolio's first purchase date (11.05.2026) falls on the first day of that class period.
- Underlying the litigation are two disclosures: an investigation of Wise's European entity by the Brussels Public Prosecutor's Office involving reportedly more than half a billion euros of suspicious transactions (alleged money laundering linked to fraud, corruption and drug trafficking), and the OCC's denial on 24.07.2026 of Wise's application for a US national trust bank licence.
- The OCC cited significant supervisory and compliance concerns, including longstanding deficiencies in Wise U.S.'s anti-money laundering and counter-terrorist financing programme.
- On 04.08.2026 Wise published a "Transaction in Own Shares" announcement: 523,584 Class A ordinary shares were repurchased on Nasdaq and/or other applicable US exchanges over 27.–31.07.2026, under the buyback authorised in June 2026.
- Effective 14.08.2026, Wise Group plc acceded as guarantor to Wise Financing plc's GBP 2,000,000,000 Euro Medium Term Note Programme — a step that broadens the group's debt funding capacity after the corporate restructuring.
- No dedicated company announcement was identified within the narrow 24.08.–31.08.2026 window itself; the most recent item in that period is the SBS Law lead-plaintiff notice of 21.08.2026, whose news cycle continued into the reporting week.

## Finanzielles
- Q1 FY27 (quarter ended 30.06.2026): net revenue of USD 714.0m versus USD 573.3m in the prior-year quarter, an increase of roughly 24.5% y/y.
- FY26 (year ended 31.03.2026, reported 25.06.2026): net revenue USD 2.5bn, up 19% y/y.
- FY26 income before tax USD 660.4m, equal to a 26% margin — above the upper end of the 20–25% medium-term margin target range.
- Active customers rose 21% to 19 million; cross-border volume rose 31% to USD 243bn; customers were saved over USD 3bn in fees.
- Customer holdings increased 40% to USD 39bn and card spend increased 37% to USD 44bn — both indicators that Wise is deepening balance and spending relationships beyond pure remittance flows.
- No dividend is paid; shareholder returns are delivered exclusively through the share repurchase programme of more than USD 500m announced with the FY26 results.
- Shareholder register: Baillie Gifford established a new holding of 108,011,990 shares at USD 12.77 on 29.05.2026, representing about 1.39% of the manager's portfolio — a substantial post-listing institutional endorsement, though established before the July regulatory disclosures.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- FY27 guidance and medium-term targets were reiterated at the FY26 results: net revenue CAGR of 15–20% and an income before tax margin of 20–25%, with the buyback of over USD 500m as the capital return vehicle.
- The strategic centrepiece of 2026 is the shift of the primary listing to Nasdaq, intended to raise the US investor profile and support expansion in the American market.
- That US strategy has now suffered a direct setback: without the OCC national trust bank charter, Wise must continue to rely on state licences and partner banks in the US, which limits direct access to payment rails and slows the intended infrastructure build-out.
- The GBP 2bn EMTN programme guarantee (14.08.2026) signals a move toward institutional debt funding alongside equity, giving flexibility for balance-sheet growth as customer holdings scale toward USD 39bn and beyond.
- No acquisitions by Wise were identified in 2026; growth remains organic. The only "acquisition" in the search results is the internal reorganisation in which Wise Group plc acquired Wise plc as part of the holding-company restructuring on 11.05.2026.
- Key risks going forward: outcome and cost of the Brussels prosecutor's investigation, remediation expense for the AML/CFT programme, US securities litigation exposure, and the possibility that regulatory constraints compress the growth runway that the current valuation multiple assumes.
