## Aktuelle Meldungen

- Rhode Island State Energy Center: Constellation agreed in September 2026 to acquire 100% of Risec, owner of the plant, from a Shell Energy North America subsidiary for 715 million USD; closing is expected in Q1 2027.
- Divestiture of the Brazos Valley Energy Center (606 MW, ERCOT market) to LS Power was agreed for approximately 860 million USD before closing adjustments.
- FERC approved a waiver to transfer interconnection capacity from the Eddystone units to the Crane Clean Energy Center, and the NRC approved a related fuel license amendment; the 2027 restart timeline for Crane remains confirmed.
- New long-term nuclear power agreements totaling 920 MW (15–20 year terms, deliveries beginning 2029–2032) were signed with corporate customers.

## Management
- CEO Joseph Dominguez described the long-term outlook as compelling, pointing to a base earnings growth rate exceeding 20% through 2029 on the Q1 earnings call; free cash flow for 2028–2029 is projected at roughly 10,5–13 billion USD depending on the source, well above prior-period levels.
- Dominguez characterized the company's ongoing share buybacks as "an intentional statement from management and our board" reflecting confidence in the business.
- On data-center/AI demand, he noted hyperscaler spending projections for 2026 are "nearly 75% higher than last year and continue to be revised upward," while cautioning that reported interconnection queues (over 400,000 MW) overstate the load that will actually materialize.
- He called for greater regulatory clarity to bring new large loads online in the Mid-Atlantic and Midwest, citing Pennsylvania as supportive of competitive market solutions.
- Earlier in 2026 (Davos in January, CERAWeek in March), Dominguez discussed the Calpine transaction, the partnership with Meta, and the currently exceptional level of power demand.

## Finanzielles
- Q2 2026 (reported 06.08.2026): Revenue of roughly 7,5 billion USD (approx. +23% YoY); adjusted operating EPS rose to 2,55 USD (vs. 1,91 USD prior year), while GAAP EPS fell to 1,42 USD (vs. 2,67 USD prior year) — beating the analyst consensus of 2,36 USD.
- Following Q2, full-year adjusted operating EPS guidance was raised to 11,50–12,50 USD (from 11,00–12,00 USD set after Q1).
- Q1 2026 (reported 11.05.2026): Adjusted operating EPS of 2,74 USD (vs. 2,14 USD prior year), above the 2,61 USD consensus; revenue of roughly 11,1 billion USD, driven largely by the Calpine acquisition completed in January 2026.
- The Calpine acquisition (closed 07.01.2026) was structured as a cash-and-stock deal valued at approximately 16,4 billion USD; including assumed net debt, total transaction value is roughly 26,6 billion USD. The combined company has nearly 60 GW of generating capacity.
- Visible Alpha consensus estimates cited by S&P Global put full-year 2026 revenue at approximately 34,5 billion USD, up from 25,5 billion USD in 2025 (approx. +35%).
- Next scheduled report: Q3 2026 results on 05.11.2026 — not yet released as of this update.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- Through the Calpine combination, Constellation pairs carbon-free nuclear generation with gas and renewable capacity, positioning itself as one of the largest independent power producers in the US.
- The restart of the Crane Clean Energy Center (formerly TMI-1) remains on track for 2027, with the required FERC and NRC approvals already granted.
- The pending Rhode Island State Energy Center acquisition (expected close Q1 2027) and the Brazos Valley Energy Center divestiture reflect active portfolio reshaping toward low/zero-emission generation near growing demand centers.
- Long-term nuclear supply contracts (920 MW, deliveries 2029–2032) alongside earlier hyperscaler deals (including Meta) are intended to lock in revenue against power-price volatility and capture rising data-center/AI electricity demand.
- Management views earnings growth exceeding 20% per year through 2029 as achievable, while cautioning that current interconnection queue figures likely overstate the load that will actually come online.
- Watch items: Q3 2026 results on 05.11.2026, progress on the Crane restart, evolution of data-center/AI demand, and completion of the Rhode Island and Brazos Valley transactions.
