## Management
- CEO Joseph Dominguez continued an active public presence through 2026 (Davos/World Economic Forum in January, CERAWeek in March, and ongoing earnings-call commentary), consistently emphasizing the Calpine integration, hyperscaler/data-center demand, and nuclear economics.
- Dominguez has reiterated a "compelling" long-term outlook, citing base earnings growth above 20% through 2029 and projected free cash flow of $10.5–$12 billion in 2028–2029.

## Strategy & Outlook
- Core strategy: combining Constellation's nuclear-heavy fleet with Calpine's natural gas, geothermal, battery storage and solar assets (now roughly 23 GW of Calpine generation capacity post-divestitures) and a competitive retail electricity business to form one of the largest clean energy producers in the US.
- Growth continues to be anchored in long-term power purchase agreements with hyperscalers and large corporate customers to meet rising data-center electricity demand.
- The newly agreed RISEC Holdings acquisition (Rhode Island State Energy Center) from Shell Energy North America signals continued bolt-on expansion of the generation fleet even as the company works through Calpine-related divestitures.
- Portfolio optimization continues via divestitures (LS Power's ~4.4 GW Delaware/Pennsylvania acquisition and the previously reported Brazos Valley gas plant sale) to satisfy DOJ/FERC conditions and concentrate capital on core nuclear and clean energy growth.
- Market attention is now sharply focused on the Q3 2026 earnings report (Nov 5, 2026) for updated guidance, further Calpine integration detail, and any commentary on recent capital raises and acquisitions.
