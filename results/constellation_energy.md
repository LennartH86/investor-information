## Financials
- Q2 2026 (reported Aug. 6, 2026) remains the latest confirmed quarter: adjusted operating EPS of 2.55 USD (vs. 1.91 USD a year earlier), while GAAP EPS fell to 1.42 USD (vs. 2.67 USD a year earlier); results were driven largely by the Calpine acquisition and favorable market conditions, partly offset by nuclear outages.
- Full-year adjusted operating EPS guidance was raised to 11.50–12.50 USD after Q2 (up from 11.00–12.00 USD set after Q1 2026).
- Q1 2026: GAAP net income of 4.49 USD per share; adjusted operating EPS of 2.74 USD; revenue of roughly 11.1 billion USD, up about 64% year over year, largely reflecting the Calpine acquisition.
- Q3 2026 results had not been published as of this update; the exact release date remained unconfirmed across sources, making it the key near-term catalyst to watch.
- Also agreed to sell the Brazos Valley Energy Center (approx. 860 million USD) and announced the planned acquisition of the Rhode Island State Energy Center from a Shell subsidiary for approx. 715 million USD, both part of ongoing portfolio reshaping.

## Strategy & Outlook
- Through the completed Calpine acquisition (closed Jan. 7, 2026; ~16.4 billion USD equity value / ~26.6 billion USD total transaction value including assumed debt), Constellation has become one of the largest independent power producers in the US, pairing carbon-free nuclear generation with gas and renewable capacity.
- Continued expansion of long-term nuclear supply agreements with hyperscalers (Google, Amazon, Meta, Microsoft) underpins management's framing of data-center/AI demand as a structural growth driver, even as Dominguez has cautioned that headline interconnection-queue figures likely overstate load that will actually materialize.
- The new conditional DOE loan commitment of up to 5 billion USD supports further nuclear uprates, adding to capacity growth alongside the previously announced Crane Clean Energy Center restart (targeted for 2027).
- Watch items: confirmation and outcome of Q3 2026 earnings, progress on the DOE-backed uprates and Crane restart, and completion of the Rhode Island State Energy Center acquisition and Brazos Valley divestiture.
