## Strategy & Outlook
- Strategic focus on combining Constellation's nuclear-heavy generation fleet with Calpine's natural gas and geothermal assets plus its competitive retail electricity business, creating one of the largest clean energy producers in the US.
- Growth strategy is increasingly centered on long-term power purchase agreements with hyperscalers and large corporate customers to meet surging data-center electricity demand.
- Continued emphasis on nuclear asset life extensions (e.g., New York assets, Crane Clean Energy Center restart) as a core growth and reliability lever.
- Portfolio optimization continues via divestitures (e.g., sale of the Brazos Valley gas plant) to focus capital on core nuclear and clean energy growth areas.
