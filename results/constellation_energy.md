## Management
- CEO Joseph Dominguez continued an active public presence in 2026 (Davos/World Economic Forum in January, CERAWeek in March, and subsequent earnings-call commentary), discussing the Calpine integration, hyperscaler/data-center demand, and nuclear economics.
- Dominguez reiterated a "compelling" long-term outlook, citing base earnings growth above 20% through 2029 and projected free cash flow of $10.5–$12 billion in 2028–2029.

## Strategy & Outlook
- Core strategy: combining Constellation's nuclear-heavy fleet with Calpine's natural gas and geothermal assets and competitive retail electricity business to form one of the largest clean energy producers in the US.
- Growth is increasingly anchored in long-term power purchase agreements with hyperscalers and large corporate customers to meet rising data-center electricity demand (e.g., the 920 MW of new nuclear PPAs with 15–20 year terms, starting 2029–2032).
- Continued emphasis on nuclear life extensions and restarts (e.g., Crane Clean Energy Center, New York nuclear assets) as a core growth and reliability lever.
- Portfolio optimization continues via divestitures (e.g., the pending Brazos Valley gas plant sale) to concentrate capital on core nuclear and clean energy growth.
- Market attention is now shifting toward the Q3 2026 earnings report (Nov 5, 2026) for updated guidance and further detail on Calpine integration progress.
