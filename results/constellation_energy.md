## Strategie & Ausblick
- Through the Calpine combination, Constellation continues to pair carbon-free nuclear generation with gas and renewable capacity, positioning itself as one of the largest independent power producers in the US (nearly 60 GW of generating capacity).
- The pending Rhode Island State Energy Center acquisition and Brazos Valley Energy Center divestiture continue to reflect active portfolio reshaping toward low/zero-emission generation near growing demand centers; no material change to these timelines was found this week.
- The Crane Clean Energy Center restart remains targeted for 2027, with required regulatory approvals already in place.
- Long-term nuclear supply contracts and hyperscaler/data-center agreements remain intended to lock in revenue against power-price volatility and capture rising AI-driven electricity demand.
- Watch items: confirmation of the Q3 2026 earnings date and results (05.11. or 09.11.2026), progress on the Crane restart, continued evolution of data-center/AI demand, and completion of the Rhode Island and Brazos Valley transactions.
