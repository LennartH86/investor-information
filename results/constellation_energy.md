## Strategie & Ausblick / Strategy & Outlook
- Core strategy remains combining Constellation's nuclear-heavy fleet with Calpine's natural gas, geothermal, battery storage and solar assets, plus a competitive retail electricity business, to form one of the largest clean energy producers in the US.
- Portfolio optimization continues via divestitures required under DOJ/FERC conditions tied to the Calpine deal, including the agreed $860 million sale of the Brazos Valley Energy Center to LS Power (expected to close by year-end 2026).
- Growth continues to be anchored in long-term power purchase agreements with hyperscalers and large corporate customers to meet rising data-center electricity demand, alongside nuclear fuel-license and interconnection progress at facilities such as the Crane Clean Energy Center.
- The newly agreed Rhode Island State Energy Center acquisition from Shell (expected to close Q1 2027) signals continued bolt-on expansion of the generation fleet even as Calpine-related divestitures proceed.
- Market attention is now sharply focused on the Q3 2026 earnings report (November 5, 2026) for updated guidance, further Calpine integration detail, and confirmation of pending acquisition/divestiture timelines.
