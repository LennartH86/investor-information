## Strategie & Ausblick
- Constellation remains the largest independent power producer in the US following the Calpine acquisition (closed 07.01.2026, purchase price of roughly $16,4 billion in stock plus $4,5 billion in cash, for a total of about $26,6 billion including ~$12,7 billion of assumed net debt), combining nuclear, gas and renewable generation across a combined ~55 GW of capacity to serve rising AI/data-center electricity demand.
- The ~$4,3 billion nuclear expansion plan (adding roughly 1.165–1.200 MW across 11 nuclear units) and the DOE-backed $5 billion financing commitment point to continued heavy multi-year capital deployment into existing nuclear capacity.
- Long-term supply agreements with Google, Amazon, Meta and Microsoft continue to reinforce the hyperscaler demand thesis, though management has previously cautioned that headline interconnection-queue figures can overstate load that ultimately materializes.
- Watch items: whether this week's rebound continues or last week's pullback resumes; confirmation of the dividend posting to the portfolio; closing of the Rhode Island State Energy Center acquisition and clarification on the Brazos Valley divestiture status; progress on DOE-backed nuclear uprates; and whether the raised FY2026 guidance holds through Q4 reporting.
