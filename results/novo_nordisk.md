- In an interview near the ADA 2026 Scientific Sessions, Doustdar told Fierce Pharma he is "a lot more optimistic today" reflecting on his pivotal first year as CEO.

## Finanzielles
- Q2 2026 adjusted sales reached DKK 78.5 billion, up 7% at constant exchange rates (CER); adjusted operating profit was DKK 33.4 billion, up 11% at CER.
- Q2 2026 net profit decreased 21% year-on-year to DKK 20,989 million; diluted EPS decreased 20% to DKK 4.75. Free cash flow was DKK 42.5 billion.
- Q1 2026 sales had jumped 32% on a reported basis to DKK 96.8 billion (adjusted sales -4%); reported operating profit rose 65% to DKK 59.6 billion (adjusted operating profit -6%).
- Following Q2 2026 results, Novo Nordisk raised its full-year 2026 guidance, though on an adjusted basis the company still expects sales to be down 6% to flat at constant exchange rates — an improvement versus the previous, lower range rather than a return to growth.
- The Akero Therapeutics acquisition (agreed October 2025, USD 5.2 billion) is expected to weigh on 2026 operating profit growth by roughly 3 percentage points due to higher R&D costs.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- The Wegovy franchise remains the central growth driver for 2026, led by the US pill launch and now expanding into Europe with a launch expected in September 2026.
- Doustdar has outlined three 2026 priorities: accelerating commercial execution (new launches, direct-to-patient sales expansion), advancing the pipeline, and balancing financial discipline with targeted investment.
- M&A strategy continues to favor bolt-on acquisitions; management has said large-scale, transformative deals are not currently being pursued, even as the CEO separately describes the company as highly active in deal-sourcing talks — reporting does not clearly reconcile the timing of these two positions.
- Manufacturing capacity is being expanded through the acquisition of three sites from Catalent and a Czech Republic site from Novavax (~USD 200 million), aimed at increasing filling capacity from 2026 onward.
- The Capital Markets Day on 21 September 2026 is a key upcoming event where updated strategic direction, pipeline progress, and financial targets are expected to be communicated.
