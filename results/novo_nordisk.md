## Management
- CEO is Maziar "Mike" Doustdar (took over in 2025 from Lars Fruergaard Jørgensen).
- At the September 2026 Capital Markets Day, Doustdar said Novo is weighing acquisitions to fill gaps in its weight-loss pipeline amid intensifying competition, and is also looking at cardiovascular treatments as potential acquisition targets.
- In earlier 2026 remarks (JPM healthcare conference, February 2026), Doustdar acknowledged a "difficult 2025," forecast 2026 sales growth of roughly -5% to -13% at constant exchange rates, and said volumes would help offset price decreases.
- In August 2026, following the Q2 outlook hike, Doustdar faced investor pressure over pipeline concerns and said the company would speed up research and pursue smaller bolt-on acquisitions after recent trial setbacks.

## Financials

- Reported net profit fell 21% year-on-year to DKK 20,989 million; diluted EPS fell 20% to DKK 4.75.
- The company raised its full-year 2026 guidance to roughly -6% to 0% growth (constant exchange rates) for both adjusted sales and adjusted operating profit.
- DKK 6.3 billion in non-recurring, non-cash impairment charges were excluded from operating profit, including DKK 4.0 billion related to monlunabant.

## Strategy & Outlook
- Management has flagged 2026 as a potential "pullback" or transition year due to loss of market exclusivity in key markets and intensifying GLP-1 competition, particularly from Eli Lilly.
- Novo is signaling appetite for further M&A, with earlier commentary pointing to deal sizes in the $20–40 billion range, later tempered toward smaller, more targeted bolt-on acquisitions in obesity-adjacent and cardiovascular areas.
- The oral Wegovy pill is positioned as a key growth driver to widen the addressable obesity market.
- Investor sentiment soured after the September 2026 Capital Markets Day, with concerns centered on patent expirations in the early 2030s and the durability of the GLP-1 growth franchise.
