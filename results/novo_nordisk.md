# Novo Nordisk 'B' (NOVO-B.CO)
_Aktualisiert: 14.09.2026 | Zeitraum: 07.09.2026 – 14.09.2026_

## Portfolio-Analyse
- **Position:** 120 Aktien
- **Kaufkurs (Ø):** 48,27 EUR
- **Aktueller Kurs:** 36,76 EUR
- **Marktwert:** 4.411,20 EUR
- **Unrealisierter G/V:** -1.414,68 EUR (-24,28%)
- **Portfolioanteil:** 2,78%
- **Dividenden erhalten:** 158,63 EUR
- **Bewertung:** The stock remains well below the average purchase price, reflecting the sharp de-rating of GLP-1 stocks over the past year, even as underlying obesity and diabetes care sales keep growing double digits and management has raised full-year guidance. With a Capital Markets Day on September 21, 2026, a large ongoing DKK 15bn buyback, and dividends still flowing, a Hold appears reasonable for existing holders; a meaningful Aufstockung (top-up) could be considered only after clarity from the Capital Markets Day and further evidence that the ziltivekimab trial setbacks are contained.

## Aktuelle Meldungen
- On September 7, 2026, Novo Nordisk announced an update to the DKK 15 billion share repurchase programme (running since February 2026), with B shares being bought back through February 1, 2027; as of September 4, 2026 the company had repurchased 32,034,179 B shares.
- The stock fell around 3% on September 8, 2026 despite the ongoing buyback, as the market weighed mixed clinical trial news against continued obesity-care growth.
- Two additional clinical trials of the experimental heart drug ziltivekimab were halted, while the STEP Young obesity trial (in adolescents) delivered positive Phase 3 data on September 7, 2026.
- Novo Nordisk invited employees to a townhall meeting on September 15, 2026 to discuss the company's "next chapter," ahead of a Capital Markets Day in London on September 21, 2026 (09:00–15:30 BST / 10:00–16:30 CET).

## Management
- CEO Mike Doustdar (in office just over a year) has repeatedly said Novo is "more active than ever" in seeking business-development deals and bolt-on acquisitions, describing the goal of building "the broadest pipeline in the world" in cardiometabolic disease.
- Doustdar has signaled ambitions to extend Novo's GLP-1 franchise beyond weight loss into longevity research and aesthetic medicine, while stressing the core strategic focus remains diabetes, obesity, cardiometabolic disease and adjacent areas.
- Management has reiterated that large-scale, transformative M&A is not currently on the table; the focus is on smaller, complementary bolt-on deals and partnerships.
- At the JPMorgan Healthcare Conference (January 2026) and in subsequent interviews, Doustdar addressed investor questions on GLP-1 pricing and expectations for the oral Wegovy pill, expressing growing optimism about the company's trajectory under his leadership.

## Finanzielles
- Q1 2026: reported sales rose 32% at constant currency to DKK 96.8 billion (~USD 15.2bn); reported operating profit rose 65% to DKK 59.6 billion. On an adjusted basis, sales and profit were down (comparisons affected by one-off items), while adjusted Obesity Care sales grew 22% at constant exchange rates and the oral Wegovy pill passed one million patients since its January 2026 launch.
- Q2 2026: net profit decreased 21% to DKK 20,989 million, and diluted EPS fell 20% to DKK 4.75.
- Full-year 2026 guidance was raised: adjusted sales and operating profit are now expected to decline between 4% and 12% at constant exchange rates, an improvement versus the previously guided decline of 5–13%.
- The company expects roughly USD 6.4 billion in free cash flow for 2026, supporting both increased capital expenditure and continued shareholder returns (dividends and buybacks).

## Strategie & Ausblick
- Capital allocation remains centered on the DKK 15 billion share buyback programme (through February 2027) alongside continued dividend payments, even as profitability has come under pressure.
- Strategic focus stays on cardiometabolic disease (diabetes, obesity, and adjacencies), with management exploring extensions into longevity and aesthetics as long-term growth avenues.
- M&A strategy is centered on bolt-on deals and partnerships (e.g., the 2025 Akero Therapeutics acquisition for MASH, and the February 2026 partnership with Vivtex on next-generation oral obesity/diabetes medicines) rather than transformative large-scale acquisitions.
- Manufacturing capacity is being expanded through the integration of Catalent sites (via Novo Holdings) and the acquired Novavax Czech Republic site, with filling capacity expected to increase gradually from 2026 onward.
- The upcoming Capital Markets Day (September 21, 2026) and the "next chapter" employee townhall (September 15, 2026) are likely to provide updated strategic direction and should be watched closely for signals on pipeline priorities and long-term guidance.
