## Management
- CEO Mike Doustdar reiterated in a June 2026 Fierce Pharma interview an "optimistic" outlook, saying he has long believed Big Pharma needs to become more competitive — a stance consistent with his push through 2026 to pursue bolt-on licensing and acquisition deals (Hengrui, the Chinese oral weight-loss asset) rather than large transformative M&A.
- In February 2026 Bloomberg interviews, Doustdar said he was confident that volume growth would offset lower realized prices on Novo's main GLP-1 drugs and explicitly ruled out mass layoffs, even as FY2026 guidance was initially cut to -5% to -13% at constant exchange rates (since revised upward during the year).

## Strategie & Ausblick / Strategy & Outlook
- Business development remains focused on sourcing external obesity/metabolic assets (Chinese oral weight-loss pill, Hengrui GLP-1/GIP license, the earlier Akero Therapeutics acquisition) to diversify beyond semaglutide ahead of the early-2030s patent cliff on Ozempic/Wegovy's active ingredient, which continues to weigh on investor sentiment despite long-term 2030/2035 ambitions set out at the September Capital Markets Day.
- The active share buyback programme (now ~1.2% of share capital held in treasury) signals management's view that the stock is undervalued and provides an ongoing source of demand for the shares.
- The oral Wegovy pill remains the main near-term commercial growth driver in the US and now the EU.
- Capital returns (buyback plus dividend) stay central to rebuilding investor confidence through a lower-growth 2026, with management pointing to pipeline-driven reacceleration from 2027 onward as new launches and licensed/acquired assets mature.
