## Latest News
- No major company-specific news was found dated specifically within 21.11.–28.11.2026; the most recent confirmed items remain those from the preceding weeks, carried forward below as the latest confirmed company information available.
- Q3 2026 results (reported Nov 4): adjusted operating profit up 27% (CER) to DKK 31,720 million; reported operating profit fell 9% (CER) due to DKK 8.7 billion in non-cash impairment charges, mainly tied to the discontinuation of the heart-drug candidate ziltivekimab after it missed its main trial goals.
- Wegovy pill: DKK 5,394 million in Q3 sales, with cumulative US prescriptions surpassing 8 million; the oral formulation has now reached eight launched markets.
- CagriSema Phase 3 data (REDEFINE 9, presented Nov 14 at ObesityWeek 2026, published in The Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology): average weight loss of approximately 20–21% at week 68 for the two lower-dose combinations versus about 2% for placebo.
- STEP Young trial (Nov 16): primary results showed a BMI reduction of 10% or more in almost 80% of children with obesity aged 6 to under 12, published in the New England Journal of Medicine.
- World Diabetes Foundation commitment (Nov 11): USD 200 million over 10 years for diabetes prevention and care programs.

## Strategy & Outlook
- No new strategic announcements or acquisitions were confirmed within the 21–28 November window specifically. The main pipeline narrative continues to be the CagriSema and STEP Young data from ObesityWeek 2026, reinforcing management's framing of pipeline breadth as the key differentiator versus Eli Lilly.
- Previously reported M&A threads remain the main strategic context: the Vivtex oral-biologics partnership (up to USD 2.1 billion, licensing for next-generation oral obesity and diabetes drugs, agreed February 2026) remains active; CEO Doustdar has said Novo is considering further acquisitions to strengthen its weight-loss pipeline ahead of patent expiries for the Wegovy/Ozempic active ingredient in the early 2030s, while also noting fewer attractive targets purely within obesity treatments; a reported Akero Therapeutics deal (efruxifermin for MASH, cited value around USD 4.7 billion plus a USD 0.5 billion contingent value right) could not be verified against a primary source and its status remains unclear.
