## Aktuelle Meldungen
- Novo Nordisk launched its oral Wegovy weight-loss pill in Germany on 1 September 2026, making Germany the first EU member state to offer prescription semaglutide in pill form following EU approval in July 2026; expected pharmacy retail prices range from EUR 170 to EUR 280 per package depending on dosage.
- Novo Nordisk B shares traded around DKK 291.85 on 1 September 2026, down about 1.24% on the day, before recovering roughly 3.17% by 2 September according to market-mover commentary.
- Commentary frames September 2026 as a "test" month for Novo Nordisk, with pipeline milestone payments and profit-guidance credibility in focus amid ongoing structural doubts about US GLP-1 competition.
- Novo Nordisk achieved the required initial dosing levels in a Phase 1 study for LX9851, a licensed oral inhibitor for obesity and related metabolic conditions, triggering an expected third milestone payment before year-end.
- Novo Nordisk won a Dutch court injunction protecting patients from unapproved, unlawful compounded semaglutide nasal spray products.
- The Capital Markets Day is confirmed for 21 September 2026 in London (09:00–15:30 BST / 10:00–16:30 CET), where Executive Management will update investors on strategy, R&D pipeline, and business performance.

## Management
- CEO Mike Doustdar continues to lead his first full year at the helm, with recent public appearances focused on defending the Wegovy growth story amid US pricing and competitive pressure.
- Near the ADA 2026 Scientific Sessions, Doustdar told Fierce Pharma he is "a lot more optimistic today" reflecting on his pivotal first year as CEO, citing acceleration in the R&D organization and pipeline.
- In an August 2026 CNBC "Mad Money" appearance with Jim Cramer, Doustdar discussed quarterly results, the GLP-1 pill launch, and competitive dynamics, stating "competition has to be fierce, but competition also has to be fair."
- In a May 2026 CNN interview, Doustdar addressed manufacturing the Wegovy pill in the US, direct-to-consumer weight-loss drug sales, competition with Eli Lilly, and potential supply-chain effects from the Iran conflict.
- In April 2026, Doustdar discussed an AI partnership with OpenAI intended to speed drug discovery and delivery and strengthen confidence in Wegovy amid rising competition.
- In May 2026, Doustdar said Novo Nordisk is looking for deals "more than ever," seeking "the broadest pipeline in the world" through active business-development talks.

## Finanzielles
- Q1 2026: reported sales up 32% at constant exchange rates to DKK 96.8 billion (adjusted sales down 4% CER, excluding a 340B provision reversal); reported operating profit up 65% to DKK 59.6 billion. Obesity care adjusted sales grew 22% CER.
- Q2 2026: adjusted sales reached DKK 78.5 billion (+7% CER); adjusted operating profit DKK 33.4 billion (+11% CER); net profit fell 21% year-on-year to DKK 20,989 million; diluted EPS fell 20% to DKK 4.75. Gross margin was 78.2%, operating margin 42.5%; free cash flow was DKK 42.5 billion.
- Following Q2 2026 results, full-year 2026 guidance was raised, though on an adjusted basis the company still expects sales/operating profit growth in a range of 0% to -6% at constant exchange rates — an improved but still negative outlook versus the prior, lower range.
- Novo Nordisk expects USD 6.4 billion in free cash flow for 2026, intended to support both capex ramp-up and shareholder returns.
- The Akero Therapeutics acquisition (agreed October 2025, USD 5.2 billion) is expected to reduce 2026 operating profit growth by roughly 3 percentage points due to higher R&D costs.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- The Wegovy franchise remains the central 2026 growth driver, with the US pill launch (from January 2026) now extended into the EU, starting with Germany on 1 September 2026.
- Novo Nordisk is licensing early-stage pipeline assets such as LX9851, an oral obesity/metabolic inhibitor, alongside a broader push to expand and diversify its R&D pipeline.
- The company entered a USD 2.1 billion partnership with Vivtex to develop next-generation oral biologics for obesity and diabetes, complementing the AI-focused collaboration with OpenAI announced earlier in 2026.
- M&A strategy continues to favor bolt-on deals and licensing/partnership structures (Akero, Vivtex, LX9851) over large-scale transformative acquisitions, even as the CEO describes the company as highly active in deal-sourcing talks — reporting does not clearly reconcile the timing of these two positions.
- Manufacturing capacity continues to expand via the Catalent site acquisitions and the ~USD 200 million purchase of Novavax's Czech Republic site, aimed at increasing filling capacity from 2026 onward.
- The Capital Markets Day on 21 September 2026 is the key near-term event, expected to update strategic direction, pipeline progress, and financial targets; September is otherwise viewed as a test month for pipeline milestone payments and the credibility of raised profit guidance.
