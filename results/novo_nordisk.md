## Recent News
- CagriSema Phase 3 data published (Nov 14): Results from the REDEFINE 9 trial were presented at ObesityWeek 2026 and published in The Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology. CagriSema 1.0 mg/1.0 mg and 1.7 mg/1.7 mg produced estimated average weight loss of 21.0% and 20.7%, respectively, at week 68.
- Food noise data (Nov 14): Novo presented real-world findings from the SIGNAL and OCTANE studies at ObesityWeek 2026, reporting that Wegovy (semaglutide 2.4 mg) was associated with reduced "food noise" scores; detailed figures were not accessible from available search results.
- REDEFINE 1 post hoc analyses (Nov 15): Novo announced results from two post hoc analyses of the Phase 3 REDEFINE 1 trial of investigational CagriSema 2.4 mg/2.4 mg; specific findings were not available in the search snippets reviewed.
- STEP Young trial (Nov 16): Novo shared primary results from the Phase 3 STEP Young trial evaluating the safety and efficacy of investigational once-weekly semaglutide injection; detailed results were not accessible from available search results.
- Share buyback update (Nov 16): As of November 13, 2026, Novo had repurchased a cumulative 43,734,179 B shares since the program started on February 4, 2026, for roughly DKK 12.1 billion (program ceiling up to DKK 15 billion, running to February 2027).
- No new quarterly results or major M&A announcements were found dated specifically within this 12–19 November period; Q3 2026 results (reported Nov 4) and the World Diabetes Foundation commitment (Nov 11) fall just before this window and were covered in the prior update.

## Management

## Strategy & Outlook
- Clinical pipeline activity was heavy this week, with CagriSema (REDEFINE 9 and REDEFINE 1 post hoc data) and the STEP Young trial for semaglutide all reported at or around ObesityWeek 2026, reinforcing management's framing of pipeline breadth as the key differentiator versus Eli Lilly.
- No new acquisitions were confirmed within this specific period. Previously reported M&A threads remain unconfirmed as to status: the Akero Therapeutics acquisition (~USD 4.7 billion, announced October 2025) was expected to close in early 2026 but no confirmed closing has been found; the Vivtex oral-biologics partnership (up to USD 2.1 billion) and a possible rare-disease / cardiovascular push (per CEO Doustdar, USD 10–40 billion acquisition capacity) continue to be the main strategic threads to diversify beyond the ~75% of revenue tied to semaglutide.
- An unconfirmed single-source report of a possible ~EUR 2.5 billion deal for a Chinese weight-loss pill developer (Jiangsu Hengrui referenced) remains uncorroborated and should still be treated with caution.
- Watch items going forward: continued readout of CagriSema and STEP Young data, Wegovy pill prescription growth, denecimig's expected H1 2027 US launch, and any confirmation of pending M&A (Akero closing status in particular).
