## Strategy & Outlook
- Business development remains focused on external bolt-on deals in obesity/metabolic disease (possible Chinese oral asset, Hengrui license, potentially Nanexa and Kallyope deals, earlier Akero Therapeutics acquisition) to diversify away from semaglutide dependence ahead of the early-2030s patent cliff.
- The ongoing buyback (~1.2% of share capital already held in treasury) continues to signal management's view that shares are undervalued and provides technical support for the price.
- Oral Wegovy remains the key near-term growth driver in the US and EU, in direct competition with Eli Lilly's Zepbound/orforglipron.
- Capital returns (buyback plus dividend) remain central to rebuilding investor confidence during a weaker growth year in 2026; the Q3 report around 4 November will be the next concrete signal on the company's trajectory.
