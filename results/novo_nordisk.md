- **Bewertung:** The share price slipped again this week (37.20 EUR vs. 37.61 EUR a week earlier), widening the unrealized loss to roughly -23.4% versus the average entry price. No reliable P/E or P/B figures were available from this period's research, but the underlying operating trend remains constructive following the Q3 beat and raised guidance, strong CagriSema (REDEFINE 9) weight-loss data presented at ObesityWeek, and continued Wegovy pill momentum. Reported profit is still being dragged down by non-cash impairments tied to ziltivekimab and manufacturing restructuring, and the CEO's stated USD 10–40bn M&A appetite adds integration/execution risk. With operating momentum improving but the share price continuing to drift lower against persistently intense Lilly competition, Hold remains the reasonable stance; a near-term Add would be better justified on clearer confirmation that recent pipeline readouts (CagriSema, denecimig) translate into sales and margin improvement.

## Aktuelle Meldungen
- CagriSema REDEFINE 9 data (Nov 14): Presented at ObesityWeek 2026 and published in The Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology; two lower-dose CagriSema combinations achieved estimated average weight loss of 20.7% and 21.0% at 68 weeks.
- Real-world food-noise data (Nov 14): Novo presented findings from the SIGNAL and OCTANE studies on "food noise" scores at ObesityWeek 2026.
- REDEFINE 1 post hoc analyses (Nov 15): Novo announced results from two post hoc analyses of the Phase 3 REDEFINE 1 trial of investigational CagriSema 2.4 mg/2.4 mg; detailed findings were not accessible from available search results.
- Share buyback update (Nov 9): As of November 6, Novo had repurchased roughly 42.6 million B shares under the program running from May 2026 to February 2027 (up to DKK 11.2–15 billion, reported ranges vary by source).
- World Diabetes Foundation commitment (Nov 11): Novo announced a USD 200 million commitment over ten years (2027–2036) to the World Diabetes Foundation, timed ahead of World Diabetes Day.
- Q3 2026 results and raised outlook (Nov 4, carried over from prior period): Adjusted operating profit rose 27% at CER to DKK 31,720 million; Wegovy pill sales reached DKK 5,394 million in the quarter and have surpassed 8 million US prescriptions since launch. Reported operating profit fell 9% (CER) due to DKK 8.7 billion in non-cash impairments (ziltivekimab discontinuation and manufacturing-network restructuring).

## Management
- CEO is Mike (Maziar) Doustdar, in office since August 2025, succeeding Lars Fruergaard Jørgensen.
- In a Fierce Pharma interview reflecting on his first year, Doustdar said he is "a lot more optimistic today" and emphasized rebuilding investor trust and sharpening competitiveness after a slower 2025.
- In an August 2026 Reuters interview, Doustdar rejected the "winner-take-all" framing of the obesity-drug contest with Eli Lilly, pointing to pill optionality as a key differentiator.
- Doustdar has signaled Novo is looking beyond weight-loss-only deals toward broader metabolic disease M&A, citing fewer attractive obesity-specific targets as of late September 2026.
- He has pushed an internal culture initiative ("The Novo Way") built on customer obsession, competitiveness, clarity, and care/integrity, including a September town hall near Copenhagen.

## Finanzielles
- Q3 2026: Adjusted operating profit +27% (CER) to DKK 31,720 million; Wegovy pill sales of DKK 5,394 million in the quarter; one secondary source also cites adjusted sales growth of 4% to DKK 79.2 billion, with US Operations +1% and International Operations +8% (figure not independently corroborated).
- Reported operating profit -9% (CER) in Q3, driven by DKK 8.7 billion in non-cash impairments (ziltivekimab and manufacturing-network restructuring).
- FY2026 guidance was raised at Q3 for the third time this year; for context, Q1 2026 reported sales were +32% (CER), heavily influenced by a US 340B provision reversal, while adjusted sales excluding that one-off were -4% (CER).
- Ongoing share buyback program: approximately 42.6 million B shares repurchased as of November 6, 2026, with the program running into February 2027.
- No new standalone financial release was identified for the 11–18 Nov period; this week's financial news was dominated by clinical/pipeline readouts rather than new company guidance.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- Novo continues to build its obesity/metabolic pipeline beyond semaglutide: CagriSema's REDEFINE 9 and REDEFINE 1 post hoc data (mid-November) are central to the company's case for differentiation against Eli Lilly's tirzepatide franchise.
- CEO Doustdar has reiterated a large (reported USD 10–40 billion) acquisition capacity focused on metabolic disease, aiming to reduce GLP-1 concentration risk following the ziltivekimab Phase 3 setback.
- The Akero Therapeutics acquisition (announced October 2025, ~USD 4.7 billion, MASH drug efruxifermin) and the Vivtex oral-biologics partnership (February 2026, up to USD 2.1 billion) remain part of the diversification strategy; no new closing confirmation was found this period.
- Denecimig (Frehemgo) remains under US regulatory review, with launch still expected in H1 2027.
- The World Diabetes Foundation commitment (USD 200m, 2027–2036) signals continued investment in diabetes care alongside the obesity growth story.
- Pipeline catalysts to watch: further CagriSema trial readouts, denecimig's regulatory progress, and continued Wegovy/Ozempic pill uptake, all positioned by management as key to defending market share against Lilly.
