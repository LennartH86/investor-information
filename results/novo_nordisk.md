## Latest News
- Late September 2026: Novo Nordisk agreed to acquire a Chinese oral weight-loss pill (small-molecule obesity asset) for approximately EUR 2.5 billion, as part of CEO Mike Doustdar's push to broaden the obesity/GLP-1 pipeline beyond Wegovy/Ozempic.
- Novo separately struck a licensing deal with Hengrui Pharma for an oral GLP-1/GIP dual agonist, potentially worth up to USD 2.6 billion in milestones.
- Shares fell nearly 8% (their largest single-day drop since February 2026) in the days following the 21.09.2026 Capital Markets Day, as investors focused on the early-2030s patent expiries for the active ingredient in Ozempic/Wegovy (semaglutide) rather than the long-term 2030/2035 ambitions presented.

- Carried-over context: the oral Wegovy pill continues its US/EU rollout (available at 70,000+ US pharmacies, ~90% of the US oral obesity-drug market) and real-world data continue to favor Ozempic over Eli Lilly's Mounjaro on cardiovascular risk.

## Management
- CEO Mike Doustdar (in office since 07.08.2025) said in September 2026 that Novo is actively considering further acquisitions to close pipeline gaps and strengthen its position in weight-loss/metabolic disease, including buying in medicines that could complement or outperform in-house candidates.
- Doustdar continues to frame 2026 as a transitional year, maintaining that volume growth will offset lower realized prices and that the company is "more active than ever in seeking out deals."
- No new C-suite changes were identified in this period; prior changes (Jamey Millar heading the US business, Hong Chow heading product & portfolio strategy) remain in place.

## Financials
- Q2 2026 (last reported, 04.08.2026): adjusted sales +7% at CER, adjusted operating profit DKK 33,389 million (+11% at CER); net profit down 21% YoY to DKK 20,989 million; diluted EPS DKK 4.75 (-20%).
- FY2026 guidance (raised with Q2): adjusted sales and adjusted operating profit now expected at 0% growth to -6% decline at CER (previously -4% to -12%).
- The new Chinese obesity-asset deal (~EUR 2.5bn) and the Hengrui licensing agreement (up to ~USD 2.6bn in milestones) add to 2026 capital deployment alongside the existing $2.1bn share buyback and the proposed higher dividend.

## Strategy & Outlook
- Business development has shifted toward actively sourcing external obesity/metabolic assets (Chinese oral weight-loss pill, Hengrui GLP-1/GIP license) to diversify beyond semaglutide ahead of the early-2030s patent cliff on Ozempic/Wegovy's active ingredient — a key driver of investor caution despite the long-term 2030/2035 targets set out at the September Capital Markets Day (5+ multi-blockbuster launches by 2030; >DKK 150bn pipeline sales by 2035).
- Management continues to frame bolt-on deals (Akero Therapeutics, and now the Chinese and Hengrui agreements) as the preferred route to pipeline expansion over large transformative M&A.
- The oral Wegovy pill remains the main near-term commercial growth driver in the US and now the EU.
- Capital returns (buyback plus a proposed higher dividend) remain central to rebuilding investor confidence through a lower-growth 2026, with management pointing to a 2027+ reacceleration.
