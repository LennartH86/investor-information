## Strategie & Ausblick
- Pipeline diversification beyond semaglutide remains the central strategic theme: CagriSema (potential FDA decision Q4 2026), oral Wegovy, and reported bolt-on licensing/acquisition activity (Chinese oral asset, Hengrui GLP-1/GIP license, Nanexa drug-delivery license, Kallyope early-stage obesity programs — none independently confirmed this period) aim to close pipeline gaps ahead of the early-2030s patent cliff.
- The ongoing buyback (~1.2% of share capital held in treasury) continues to signal management's view that shares are undervalued and provides technical support for the price.
- Competitive pressure from Eli Lilly (Zepbound/orforglipron) remains the key external risk to Novo's obesity/diabetes franchise.
- Capital returns (buyback plus dividend) remain central to rebuilding investor confidence during a weaker growth year in 2026; the Q3 report around 4 November will be the next concrete signal on the company's trajectory, including any update on CagriSema's regulatory path.
