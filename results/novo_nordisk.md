## Aktuelle Meldungen
- Novo Nordisk halted two phase 3 cardiovascular trials (HERMES and ATHENA) of ziltivekimab for heart failure with inflammation on 7 September 2026, after an independent data monitoring committee concluded there was a low likelihood of success; the stock fell about 1.9-2% on the news.
- The same week, the STEP Young trial delivered positive data: among 165 children aged 6 to under 12 treated with semaglutide over 68 weeks, 40.4% dropped below the obesity threshold versus 0% on placebo, reaffirming strength in the core GLP-1 franchise even as the cardiovascular pipeline setback weighed on sentiment.
- Novo Nordisk continued its DKK 11.2 billion (up to ~DKK 15 billion cited in market commentary) B-share buyback programme running from 6 May 2026 to 1 February 2027; as of 4 September 2026 the company had repurchased 32,034,179 B shares at an average price of DKK 281.63, worth DKK ~9.02 billion, with the stock dipping around the 7-8 September buyback update.
- Commentary continues to frame the stock as caught between a weakened cardiovascular diversification narrative (post ziltivekimab halt) and continued obesity-care strength, reinforcing the company's reliance on the GLP-1 franchise for growth.
- The Capital Markets Day is confirmed for 21 September 2026 in London (09:00-15:30 BST / 10:00-16:30 CET), where Executive Management will update investors on strategy, R&D pipeline, and business performance.

## Management
- CEO Mike Doustdar continues to lead his first full year at the helm; no new dedicated interviews were identified for the 04.09.-11.09.2026 window itself, but recent (June-August 2026) appearances remain the latest public record of his strategic thinking.
- In a June 2026 Bloomberg/Fortune interview, Doustdar said Novo Nordisk is looking beyond weight loss toward longevity research and aesthetic medicine, suggesting weight reduction could eventually become "just an appendix of a different purpose."
- In an August 2026 CNBC appearance, Doustdar addressed investors' high expectations for the oral Wegovy pill launch.
- Near the ADA 2026 Scientific Sessions, Doustdar told Fierce Pharma he is "a lot more optimistic today" reflecting on his pivotal first year as CEO, citing acceleration in the R&D organization and pipeline.
- In May 2026, Doustdar said Novo Nordisk is looking for deals "more than ever," seeking "the broadest pipeline in the world" through active business-development talks, even as Eli Lilly has overtaken Novo in weekly GLP-1 injectable market share.

## Finanzielles
- Q1 2026: reported sales up 32% at constant exchange rates to DKK 96.8 billion (adjusted sales down 4% CER); reported operating profit up 65% to DKK 59.6 billion; operating margin 46.9%. Obesity care adjusted sales grew 22% CER; Wegovy sales up 12% to DKK 18.2 billion, Ozempic sales down 8%.
- Q2 2026: adjusted sales DKK 78.5 billion (+7% CER); adjusted operating profit DKK 33.4 billion (+11% CER); net profit fell 21% year-on-year to DKK 20,989 million; diluted EPS fell 20% to DKK 4.75. Gross margin 78.2%, operating margin 42.5%.
- Following Q1/Q2 2026 results, full-year 2026 adjusted guidance was raised to a range of roughly -4% to -12% (sales/operating profit at CER), an improvement on the prior, lower range.
- Novo Nordisk expects USD 6.4 billion in free cash flow for 2026, supporting both capex ramp-up and continued shareholder returns via the ongoing buyback.
- Share buyback: DKK 11.2 billion programme (6 May 2026 - 1 February 2027); DKK ~9.02 billion executed as of 4 September 2026 at an average price of DKK 281.63 per B share.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- The Wegovy franchise (injectable and oral pill) remains the central 2026 growth driver, with obesity care adjusted sales growth of 22% CER in Q1 2026 and the German oral pill launch extending EU rollout.
- The halt of the ziltivekimab HERMES/ATHENA cardiovascular trials is a setback for the diversification narrative beyond obesity/diabetes, reinforcing near-term dependence on the GLP-1 franchise, even as Doustdar signals ambitions toward longevity and aesthetics research.
- M&A/licensing strategy continues to favor bolt-on deals and partnerships (Akero Therapeutics, USD 5.2 billion, agreed October 2025; Vivtex, USD 2.1 billion oral biologics partnership) over large-scale transformative acquisitions, even as the CEO describes the company as highly active in deal-sourcing talks.
- Manufacturing capacity continues to expand via Catalent site acquisitions and the ~USD 200 million purchase of Novavax's Bohumil, Czech Republic site, aimed at increasing filling capacity from 2026 onward.
- The ongoing DKK 11.2 billion share buyback signals management confidence in valuation even amid pipeline setbacks and competitive pressure from Eli Lilly.
- The Capital Markets Day on 21 September 2026 in London is the key near-term event, expected to update strategic direction, pipeline progress (including how the company addresses the ziltivekimab setback), and financial targets.
