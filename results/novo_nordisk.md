## Recent News
- The Capital Markets Day on 21.09.2026 in London (just after this reporting period ends) is the central upcoming event, where management plans to lay out the updated corporate strategy, pipeline progress and financial targets under the new "Novo" identity.
- On 14.09.2026, Novo Nordisk formally unveiled its rebrand to "Novo" under the tagline "Lasting Health Starts Now," reviving the historical Apis-bull logo and introducing a new corporate culture, "The Novo Way," while the legal entity name remains Novo Nordisk A/S.
- On 16.09.2026, Novo Nordisk announced a collaboration with Anthropic aimed at accelerating drug discovery and advancing AI-driven software development within the company.
- The ongoing share buyback programme (up to DKK 11.2bn, 06.05.2026–01.02.2027, part of a broader annual repurchase framework of up to DKK 15bn started 04.02.2026) continued: by 11.09.2026 a cumulative 33,084,179 B-shares had been repurchased at an average price of DKK 281.89, with a further update published on 14.09.2026.
- Shares saw a volatile week, including a session with an intraday gain of as much as 3.5%, as sentiment shifted ahead of the Capital Markets Day.

## Management
- CEO Mike Doustdar continued to frame the rebrand and culture shift ("The Novo Way") as "part of the same package" needed to sharpen decision-making speed and regain GLP-1 market share from Eli Lilly (CNBC interview, 14.09.2026).
- Doustdar was also quoted saying Novo Nordisk is not currently in a position to pursue large-scale, transformative M&A, reiterating that bolt-on acquisitions and partnerships remain the company's approach — a more cautious tone than his May 2026 comment that the company was looking for deals "more than ever."
- Earlier 2026 remarks remain relevant background: in August he discussed investor expectations for the oral Wegovy pill (Jim Cramer/CNBC "Mad Money"); in June he spoke about extending research toward longevity and aesthetic medicine; in May he emphasized an active search for deals and partnerships.
- No further changes to the Executive Management or Board of Directors were reported during the period 12.–19.09.2026.

## Strategy & Outlook
- The Capital Markets Day on 21.09.2026 is the pivotal near-term catalyst: management is expected to detail how the "Novo" rebrand, cultural reset and pipeline strategy translate into concrete financial targets amid the FY2026 guidance cut and intensifying obesity-market competition.
- The rebrand and "The Novo Way" culture initiative aim to make the company faster and more consumer-centric as the obesity-drug market becomes increasingly direct-to-consumer and contested by Eli Lilly.
- The new collaboration with Anthropic signals a push to use AI both to speed up drug discovery and to modernize internal software development — an emerging strategic theme alongside the core GLP-1 franchise.
- M&A strategy stays focused on bolt-on deals and licensing partnerships (Akero Therapeutics, USD 5.2bn, Oct. 2025; Vivtex, USD 2.1bn; Catalent/Novavax manufacturing-site acquisitions), with the CEO confirming that large, transformative acquisitions remain out of reach for now.
- The Wegovy franchise (injectable and oral pill) remains the central growth driver, and continued buyback activity signals management confidence despite the recent FY2026 guidance downgrade and analyst caution (e.g., Morgan Stanley's "Underweight" rating from the prior week).
