## Management
- CEO/Chairman Bernard Arnault again deflected succession questions at the April 2026 AGM, saying a succession discussion was still "sept ou huit ans" (seven or eight years) away; a bylaw change allows him to remain at the helm until age 85.
- All five of Bernard Arnault's children now hold senior group positions; Jean Arnault is reported to be taking over as CEO of LVMH Watches from Jean-Christophe Babin during 2026 (not yet officially confirmed in this reporting period).
- The Arnault family holding companies control roughly 49–50% of LVMH's share capital and around 66% of voting rights, after private share purchases (~407m EUR / ~440m USD) aimed at pushing the family stake above the 50% threshold.
- Other personnel notes remain valid from prior weeks: Matthieu Soudan as Managing Director of Repossi; Alexandre Arnault sits on Nike's board of directors.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- Capital allocation continues to favor targeted bolt-on deals and portfolio pruning over large acquisitions: sale of Marc Jacobs to a WHP Global/G-III Apparel joint venture (~850m USD, closing date reported inconsistently as either September 2026 or G-III's fiscal Q3 2027 — unresolved); acquisition of French Bloom (alcohol-free sparkling wine) completed April 2026; stake in Loro Piana raised to 94% via a ~1bn EUR call option (January 2026); full acquisition of Les Editions Croque Futur (Challenges, Sciences & Avenir, La Recherche) completed end of December 2025.
- Management confirmed at the January 2026 results presentation that Louis Vuitton will not expand into branded hospitality/hotels.
- Management continues to describe 2026 growth as "resilient but fragile" ("résiliente mais fragile"); Middle East geopolitical tensions and Chinese consumer demand remain the key swing factors for the remainder of the year.
- The diverging trajectories between Watches & Jewelry / Wines & Spirits (recovering) and Fashion & Leather Goods (persistent weakness) remain the key thing to watch; the next real data point is the FY2026 / Q4 results release, provisionally scheduled for 28.01.2027.
