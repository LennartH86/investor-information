## Management
- Olivier Blum has been CEO since November 2024, after the board replaced his predecessor Peter Herweck over disagreements on strategy execution.
- No new 2026 interview with Blum was found beyond the Semafor piece from March 2026 ("moving at the pace of Nvidia"), in which he frames his CEO agenda around three forces: AI, the energy transition, and geopolitics ("the world needs roughly 10,000 more terawatts of energy by 2035").
- Blum continues to link the PTC and Cognite/AVEVA acquisitions to building an industrial AI/software platform; his October 2025 Fortune interview described his approach to "rebuilding" the 189-year-old energy group for the AI era, stressing that "speed matters more than perfection."
- CFO Nathan Fast and IR head Antoine Sage, alongside Blum, have reaffirmed the goal of maintaining an A-category credit rating despite rising PTC-related debt, consistent with the early-October rating affirmations from S&P and Moody's.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- Schneider is pushing ahead with building an industrial AI/software platform: the ~$22.6bn PTC acquisition (announced 05.10.2026) complements the already-completed ~$3.1bn Cognite acquisition (June 2026) and AVEVA — the third major software acquisition in under four years.
- The ~€6 billion capital increase launched on 19.10.2026 is the concrete financing step for the PTC deal, whose risks the market had already begun pricing in since the early-October announcement.
- Data center electrification remains the key structural growth driver, with triple-digit percentage demand growth in Q3 and a record order backlog at quarter-end.
- The PTC deal is expected to close by Q3 2027, subject to PTC shareholder approval and regulatory clearances; share buybacks are set to pause in 2027/2028 to help fund the transaction, and the current €600m buyback programme concludes end of November 2026.
- Risks: dilution now realized through the capital increase, integration risk from several parallel large acquisitions (PTC, Cognite, AVEVA), maintaining the A-category rating amid rising leverage, and anticipated Q4 2026 headwinds from supply-chain disruption and cost inflation linked to Middle East tensions.
- No new information surfaced this week specific to the 26.10.–02.11.2026 window beyond continued share-price weakness; the next scheduled catalyst is full-year 2026 results, reportedly expected 18 February 2027.
