## Recent News
- On 19.10.2026, Schneider Electric launched a capital increase of approximately EUR 6 billion (a share offering excluding existing shareholders' preferential subscription rights) to help finance the PTC acquisition.
- The Q3 2026 trading update (published 16.10.2026) remains the dominant news driver of the period: record quarterly revenue of €11.46bn, up 16.9% organically, beating the ~€11.13-11.14bn consensus.
- This was the second full-year 2026 guidance raise of the year: organic adjusted EBITA growth now targeted at +18% to +21%, organic revenue growth +12% to +14%.
- China & East Asia grew ~26% organically in Q3; North America grew ~20%; data-center demand was described by CEO Olivier Blum as growing at a "triple-digit rate."
- S&P Global Ratings and Moody's confirmed Schneider's credit ratings with a stable outlook on 06.10.2026, despite the PTC-related rise in debt.
- Management continues to flag that Middle East-related disruptions could weigh on Q4 2026 results via supply-chain strain and higher input-cost inflation.
- A €600m share buyback tranche had been executed to the amount of ~€497m as of 12.10.2026; sources indicate buybacks are expected to pause in 2027-2028 while the PTC deal is financed and integrated.

## Management
- Olivier Blum has been CEO since November 2024, after the board removed his predecessor, Peter Herweck, over disagreements on strategy execution.
- Blum continues to frame strategy around the convergence of energy and AI/software, linking the PTC and Cognite/AVEVA deals to building an industrial-AI/software platform; a Fortune interview (October 2025) detailed his approach to "rebuilding the 189-year-old energy giant for the AI era," emphasizing corporate agility amid geopolitical volatility. No new 2026 CEO interview was found this period.
- Management reaffirmed its commitment to retaining an A-category credit rating even as leverage rises to fund the PTC transaction, consistent with the early-October rating confirmations from S&P and Moody's.
- Management (CFO Nathan Fast, IR head Antoine Sage alongside Blum) has been actively engaging investors through the Q3 call and the newly launched capital increase process.

## Strategy & Outlook
- Schneider is executing on its industrial-software/AI push, combining the pending ~€22.6bn PTC acquisition (announced 05.10.2026) with the already-closed ~$3.1bn Cognite deal (June 2026) to build an AVEVA-centered industrial-AI platform; this marks the company's third major software acquisition in under four years.
- The ~€6bn capital increase launched 19.10.2026 is the concrete financing step for the PTC deal that markets had been pricing in risk for since the early-October announcement.
- Data-center electrification remains the single biggest structural growth driver, with triple-digit demand growth cited for Q3 2026 and management pointing to strengthened visibility into 2027-2028.
- The PTC deal is expected to close by Q3 2027, subject to PTC shareholder approval and regulatory clearances; share buybacks are expected to pause in 2027-2028 to help fund the deal, though this should be confirmed against the company's own investor communication as sources vary.
- Key risks: equity dilution now materializing via the capital increase, integration risk from stacking large acquisitions (PTC, Cognite, AVEVA), maintaining the A-category credit rating amid rising debt, and Middle East-related supply-chain/inflation headwinds flagged for Q4 2026.
