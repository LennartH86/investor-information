## Aktuelle Meldungen
- Shares held broadly steady around EUR 287-289 in the week to 07.09.2026, with ad-hoc-news reporting the stock "holds firm as analysts stick to buy ratings" and separately "holds steady on smart water deal" news.
- Schneider Electric launched **EcoStruxure™ Water Advisor**, a new digital solution to improve visibility and performance of water infrastructure — the "smart water deal" referenced in recent market commentary.
- The company was ranked **#1 in the 2026 Gartner® Top 25 Supply Chain** for the fourth consecutive year, and named **"World's Most Sustainable Company"** by TIME Magazine and Statista for the third consecutive year.
- Schneider Electric and **Hon Hai Technology Group (Foxconn)** announced a strategic collaboration to accelerate next-generation AI data centers.
- On 07.09.2026 the company published new research (London-based release) on **condition-based maintenance for AI-era data centers**, addressing rising reliability and uptime demands from AI workloads.
- At **Semafor World Economy 2026**, Schneider Electric spotlighted its "Time to Power" theme, framing power availability as the key bottleneck for AI and industrial growth.
- At **Automate 2026**, the company advanced its open, software-defined automation portfolio, including agentic manufacturing capabilities built on Microsoft Azure AI.

## Management
- **Olivier Blum** remains CEO; no leadership changes were identified in the 31.08.-07.09.2026 window.
- Blum's public messaging through 2026 has consistently linked AI growth to broader electrification and grid demand (CNBC, July 2026: "AI-driven growth extends beyond data centres") and to Europe's need for "AI sovereignty" (Davos, January 2026).
- Earlier 2026 profiles (Semafor, March; CNBC "Executive Decisions," February) continue to frame Blum's tenure as running the company "at the pace of a tech company," with no new interviews surfacing in the current period.

## Finanzielles
- **H1 2026 (published 30 July 2026):** record results — net income of EUR 2.5bn (+30%); adjusted net income of EUR 2.7bn (+21%); revenue of EUR 21.2bn with 14% organic growth; operating cash flow of EUR 3.8bn (+28%).
- **Full-year 2026 guidance raised**: organic revenue growth now expected at **10-13%** (up from 7-10%), and adjusted EBITA margin improvement target raised to **70-100 basis points** (from 50-80 bps), reflecting strong data-center, grid and industrial-automation demand.
- **Share buyback program**: EUR 249m repurchased as of 30 June 2026 under a newly initiated systematic buyback.
- **Financing costs** are expected to rise by roughly EUR 150m in 2026, linked to the acquisition of the remaining 35% stake in **SEIPL (Schneider Electric India Private Limited)**.
- **Q1 2026:** record revenues of EUR 9.77bn (+11.2% organic); Energy Management EUR 8bn (+12.8% organic).
- No dividend received on this position, consistent with the May 2026 first purchase falling after the annual ex-dividend date; trailing yield at the current price remains roughly 1.4-1.5%.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- **Cognite acquisition** (announced 30 June 2026, ~USD 3.1bn all-cash) continues to be positioned alongside Aveva as the core of Schneider Electric's push into industrial AI and data infrastructure.
- **AiDASH acquisition** (announced 30 July 2026) extends the group's reach into AI-powered grid resilience and utility asset management.
- New **Foxconn/Hon Hai collaboration** on next-generation AI data centers reinforces the "Time to Power" strategic narrative — positioning power delivery and reliability as the critical constraint on AI infrastructure buildout.
- Continued capacity investment in the US (over USD 700m through 2027) and ongoing product innovation (EcoStruxure Water Advisor, agentic manufacturing on Azure AI) underline a broad platform strategy spanning electrification, automation, water and AI infrastructure — not just data centers.
- Key risks to monitor: currency translation headwinds, a still-premium valuation, integration execution on Cognite/AiDASH, and the added financing cost from the SEIPL stake buy-in.
