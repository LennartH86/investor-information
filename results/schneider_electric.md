## Strategie & Ausblick
- Largest acquisition to date: agreement announced 05.10.2026 to acquire US software company PTC for $205/share (~42% premium), implying an enterprise value of roughly $23.7 billion; expected to close by Q3 2027, with projected ~EUR 800 million in revenue synergies and ~EUR 250 million in run-rate cost savings by year three post-closing. *Il s'agit de la plus grande acquisition de l'histoire de Schneider Electric, visant à renforcer son offre logicielle industrielle.*
- Voluntary public takeover offer for Bulgarian smart-home firm Shelly Group announced 24.09.2026, at EUR 70/share (reported deal value ~EUR 1.2–1.37 billion), conditional on reaching 95% of share capital; founders holding ~57% support the deal; expected close by end of Q1 2027.
- Acquisition of Norwegian/US industrial AI firm Cognite announced 30.06.2026 for ~$3.1 billion, to be integrated with Schneider's AVEVA industrial software unit to build a unified industrial AI platform.
- Overall strategic direction: continued bet on electrification, data center demand, and industrial/AI software, funded through an active, large-scale M&A program (PTC, Shelly, Cognite) alongside organic growth in Energy Management and Industrial Automation. *La stratégie reste centrée sur l'électrification, les data centers et le logiciel industriel/IA, financée par une politique de fusions-acquisitions soutenue.*
- Watch item: integration execution and balance-sheet leverage from three simultaneous acquisitions (PTC, Shelly, Cognite) in under six months, alongside Schneider's stated commitment to keeping an "A" category credit rating.
