## Management
- **Olivier Blum** remains CEO; no personnel changes identified in the reporting period.
- No new CEO interviews were identified in this specific week (12.09.–19.09.2026); Blum's most recent public commentary (CNBC, 30.07.2026) emphasized that AI-driven growth extends well beyond data centers into the entire energy infrastructure ("no AI at scale without energy intelligence").
- Earlier 2026 statements remain the guiding strategic framework: at Davos (January 2026) Blum argued "Europe needs its own AI sovereignty," and in a Semafor interview (March 2026) he stressed the need to move "at the pace of Nvidia" for the company's tech transformation.

## Financials
- **H1 2026 (published 30.07.2026):** Record results — net income 2.5bn EUR (+30%), adjusted net income 2.7bn EUR (+21%), revenue 21.2bn EUR (+14% organic), operating cash flow 3.8bn EUR (+28%).
- Energy Management delivered 9.6bn EUR revenue in Q2 (+18% organic); Industrial Automation delivered 1.9bn EUR (+11% organic).
- Regional strength: China & East Asia +19.7% organic (semiconductor and data-center demand), Europe +7.9% organic, South Asia & International +12.3% (India posting strong double-digit growth).
- Q1 2026 revenue reached a record 9.77bn EUR with 11.2% organic growth, though shares dipped on FX headwinds projected to weigh on FY margins by roughly 750-850 million EUR.
- Following H1, full-year 2026 organic revenue growth guidance was raised to 10-13% (from a prior 7-10%).
- Share buyback: 249 million EUR year-to-date as of 30.06.2026, plus a further 28.7 million EUR tranche in September.
- No dividend received on this position (purchased May 2026, after the ex-dividend date); running yield at the current price is approximately 1.4-1.5%.
- Financing costs are expected to rise by roughly 150 million EUR in 2026 due to the acquisition of the remaining 35% stake in SEIPL (Schneider Electric India Private Limited).

## Strategy & Outlook
- The **Cognite acquisition** (~3.1bn USD, announced 30.06.2026) and the **AiDASH acquisition** (30.07.2026, AI-powered grid resilience/utility asset management) remain central to the industrial-AI and grid-infrastructure strategy, with integration alongside Aveva planned.
- Continued investment in US capacity (over 700 million USD through 2027) and product innovation (EvoPact switchgear, new EV charging solutions, Altivar drives, TeSys Deca contactors) underline a broad platform strategy spanning electrification, automation and grid resilience.
- New product launches at Global Industrie 2026 and the Innovation Summit Middle East and Africa reinforce the company's positioning around industrial AI and connected, cyber-secure equipment.
- CEO Blum continues to tie growth closely to global AI infrastructure build-out ("no AI at scale without energy intelligence") and to European AI/energy sovereignty themes.
- Risks to monitor: continued short-term share-price volatility despite strong fundamentals, valuation levels, integration risk from Cognite/AiDASH, FX headwinds, and increased financing costs from the SEIPL stake acquisition.
