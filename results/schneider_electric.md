## Aktuelle Meldungen
- The share price eased from its recent high near EUR 288-302 to around EUR 287 in the week to 03.09.2026, described in French/German press as a mild dip even as the stock's H1 sustainability score advanced.
- Analysts continue to maintain upside price targets on the stock despite the pullback, citing the strong H1 2026 results and raised guidance as underlying support.
- Schneider Electric confirmed 2nd place in the LinkedIn Top Companies France 2026 ranking, its third consecutive year in that position.
- At Global Industrie 2026 the company presented new connected and cyber-secured products, including the MCSeT medium-voltage panel with EvoPact vacuum circuit breaker, the Altivar ATH230/ATH600 variable frequency drives, and the next-generation TeSys Deca contactors and motor circuit breakers — part of its ongoing electrification and industrial-automation push.
- The Impact 2030 sustainability roadmap update (H1 2026) reported an Impact Score of 3.69 out of 10, on track for the full-year target of 4.20, alongside an 82% cut in Scope 1+2 emissions versus 2017 and a 12% cut in Scope 3 emissions versus 2021.

## Management
- **Olivier Blum** remains CEO; in a late-July 2026 CNBC interview tied to the H1 results he argued that **AI-driven growth extends beyond data centres**, pointing to broader electrification and grid demand over the next three years.
- Blum has been a recurring voice on AI and energy policy through 2026, including at Davos in January ("no AI at scale without energy intelligence") and in a March 2026 Semafor profile on running Schneider Electric "at the pace of a tech company."
- A CNBC "Executive Decisions" segment (February 2026) profiled Blum's path to CEO, underscoring continuity in leadership since succeeding Jean-Pascal Tricoire.
- No management changes or new appointments were identified in the 27.08.-03.09.2026 window.

## Finanzielles
- **H1 2026 (published 30 July 2026):** record results — net income of EUR 2.5bn (+30%); adjusted net income of EUR 2.7bn (+21%); revenue of EUR 21.2bn with 14% organic growth.
- **Operating cash flow of EUR 3.8bn (+28%)**, indicating strong cash conversion.
- **Q2 2026:** Energy Management revenue of EUR 9.6bn (+18% organic); Industrial Automation EUR 1.9bn (+11% organic); Europe grew 7.9% organically, China & East Asia 19.7%, South Asia & International 12.3%.
- **Q1 2026 (30 April 2026):** record revenues of EUR 9.77bn, +11.2% organic, ahead of expectations, though shares initially fell on concerns over FX headwinds of roughly EUR 750-850m projected on full-year margins.
- Full-year 2026 guidance was raised following the H1 print, reflecting confidence in continued organic momentum despite currency pressure.
- No dividend has been received on this position, consistent with the May 2026 first purchase falling after the annual ex-dividend date; the trailing yield at the current price is roughly 1.4-1.5%.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- **Cognite acquisition** (announced 30 June 2026): all-cash deal valued at about USD 3.1bn, to be combined with the group's Aveva industrial software business — a major push into industrial AI and data infrastructure.
- **AiDASH acquisition** (announced 30 July 2026): targets AI-powered solutions for the utility and grid infrastructure market, extending the group's reach into grid resilience and asset management software.
- Capital investment continues on manufacturing, including the previously announced commitment of over USD 700m in US operations through 2027 to support energy infrastructure and domestic capacity for AI-related demand.
- Strategic thesis remains the convergence of electrification, automation and AI: data centres remain the most visible driver, but management continues to emphasize broader demand across grid, industry and buildings.
- Key risks to monitor: currency translation headwinds, a still-premium valuation that leaves limited room for disappointment, and integration execution on the Cognite and AiDASH transactions.
