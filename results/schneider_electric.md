## Aktuelle Meldungen
- Shares eased from around EUR 288.7 to EUR 286.9 over the week, with ad-hoc-news.de reporting the stock "falls as France restructuring plan and analyst split weigh" — the most notable negative newsflow of the period.
- Schneider Electric confirmed it will **close its Chasseneuil-du-Poitou plant (France) by March 2028**, affecting around 145 employees, while investing **more than EUR 150 million** to consolidate activities from three Normandy sites into a new facility in **Évreux**.
- On 07.09.2026, the company disclosed a further **share buyback tranche**: 98,564 shares repurchased for EUR 28.7 million between 31 August and 4 September 2026, under the systematic buyback program launched earlier in 2026.
- Schneider Electric's **September 2026 Patch Tuesday** advisories included a **critical authentication vulnerability (CVE-2026-3869, CVSS 9.2)** affecting Modicon M580 and Modicon M580 Safety controllers, plus high-severity issues in PowerLogic T300 and EcoStruxure IT Data Center Expert — four new advisories and four updates in total.
- At **Global Industrie 2026**, Schneider Electric unveiled **MCSeT with the EvoPact vacuum circuit breaker**, a new connected, air-insulated primary switchgear line aimed at sustainable grid performance.
- The company placed **2nd in LinkedIn's "Top Companies France 2026"** ranking for the third consecutive year.
- Continued product cadence: new **Altivar ATH230** (connected VFD for HVAC) and **Altivar ATH600** (VFD for fluid/chiller control up to 250kW), plus next-generation **TeSys Deca** contactors and motor circuit breakers with Snap-in technology.

## Management
- **Olivier Blum** remains CEO; no leadership changes were identified in the 04.09.-11.09.2026 window.
- In a Bloomberg interview (30.07.2026, still the most recent substantive CEO commentary), Blum reiterated a strategy of prioritizing organic growth while remaining open to further tech acquisitions to accelerate expansion, and pointed to fast-growing data-center demand in China.
- Blum's messaging through 2026 continues to link AI-driven growth to broader electrification and grid demand (CNBC, July: "AI-driven growth extends beyond data centres") and to Europe's need for "AI sovereignty" (Davos, January).
- No new CEO interviews or management changes surfaced specifically in the current reporting period; the France restructuring decision (Chasseneuil-du-Poitou closure / Évreux investment) was communicated via corporate press release rather than a personal interview.

## Finanzielles
- **Q2 2026 revenue:** EUR 11.5 billion, +17% organic growth; Energy Management Q2 revenue EUR 9.6 billion (+18% organic), Industrial Automation EUR 1.9 billion (+11% organic).
- CEO Olivier Blum **lifted full-year adjusted EBITA growth guidance to 14-19%**, alongside the previously raised organic revenue growth guidance of 10-13% (from 7-10%) and adjusted EBITA margin improvement target of 70-100 basis points.
- **H1 2026 (published 30 July 2026):** record results — net income EUR 2.5bn (+30%); adjusted net income EUR 2.7bn (+21%); revenue EUR 21.2bn (+14% organic); operating cash flow EUR 3.8bn (+28%).
- Regional strength: **China & East Asia +19.7%** organic (semiconductor and data-center momentum), **South Asia & International +12.3%** with India delivering strong double-digit growth.
- **Share buyback:** EUR 28.7 million repurchased in the latest tranche (98,564 shares, 31.08.-04.09.2026), part of a systematic program that had already reached EUR 249 million by 30 June 2026.
- **Financing costs** expected to rise by roughly EUR 150 million in 2026, linked to the acquisition of the remaining 35% stake in **SEIPL (Schneider Electric India Private Limited)**.
- No dividend received on this position (first purchase in May 2026 fell after the annual ex-dividend date); trailing yield at the current price remains roughly 1.4-1.5%.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- **French industrial restructuring**: closure of the Chasseneuil-du-Poitou site by March 2028 alongside a EUR 150m+ investment to consolidate Normandy operations in Évreux — a cost-and-efficiency move that weighed on sentiment this week despite being framed as part of a broader footprint modernization.
- **Cognite acquisition** (~USD 3.1bn, announced 30.06.2026) and **AiDASH acquisition** (announced 30.07.2026, AI-powered grid resilience/utility asset management) remain the core of the group's industrial-AI and grid-infrastructure push, to be combined with Aveva.
- Continued US capacity investment (over USD 700m through 2027) and ongoing product innovation (EvoPact switchgear, Altivar VFDs, TeSys Deca) underline a platform strategy spanning electrification, automation and grid resilience — not just data centers.
- Cybersecurity remains an operational risk theme given the critical Modicon M580 vulnerability disclosed this period; no indication of exploitation, but patching urgency is high for industrial customers.
- Key risks to monitor: near-term French restructuring/severance costs, a still-premium valuation, integration execution on Cognite/AiDASH, added financing cost from the SEIPL stake buy-in, and currency translation headwinds.
