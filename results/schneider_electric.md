## Strategy & Outlook
- New: the **Shelly Group takeover offer** (~1.2bn EUR, announced 24.09.2026) adds a smart-home/IoT device portfolio to Schneider's electrification and connected-building strategy, alongside the ongoing **Cognite** (~3.1bn USD, industrial AI/data software) and **AiDASH** (AI-powered grid resilience) acquisitions, with integration planned alongside Aveva.
- New 2.5MW AI power modules and the Lenovo 360 Circle partnership reinforce Schneider's positioning at the center of AI-driven data-center and electrification demand.
- Continued investment in US capacity (over 700 million USD through 2027) underlines a broad platform strategy spanning electrification, automation and grid resilience.
- CEO Blum continues to tie growth closely to global AI infrastructure build-out ("no AI at scale without energy intelligence") and to European AI/energy sovereignty themes.
- Risks to monitor: valuation levels after the strong run-up, integration risk from running three parallel acquisitions (Cognite, AiDASH, Shelly), regulatory/FSC approval risk on the Shelly deal, FX headwinds, and increased financing costs from recent M&A activity.
