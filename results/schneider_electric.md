## Strategy & Outlook
- The **Cognite acquisition** (~3.1bn USD, announced 30.06.2026) and the **AiDASH acquisition** (30.07.2026, AI-powered grid resilience/utility asset management) remain central to the industrial-AI and grid-infrastructure strategy, with integration alongside Aveva planned.
- New 2.5MW AI power modules and the Lenovo 360 Circle partnership reinforce Schneider's positioning at the center of AI-driven data-center and electrification demand.
- Continued investment in US capacity (over 700 million USD through 2027) underlines a broad platform strategy spanning electrification, automation and grid resilience.
- CEO Blum continues to tie growth closely to global AI infrastructure build-out ("no AI at scale without energy intelligence") and to European AI/energy sovereignty themes.
