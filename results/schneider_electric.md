## Financials

## Strategy & Outlook
- The planned acquisition of PTC (announced early October 2026, approximately $22.6 billion / roughly €20 billion equity value) remains the largest deal in the company's history and is intended to substantially expand its industrial software and AI business. The announcement triggered a share price decline of roughly 9%, and financing/integration risk remains a key overhang on the stock.
- The new StarCharge joint venture for the European EV charging market complements the electrification/e-mobility growth strategy.
- Other 2026 transactions reinforce the software/AI/energy management build-out: Cognite ($3.1 billion, industrial AI, to be integrated into AVEVA), Shelly Group (approximately €1.2 billion investment agreement, smart home/IoT, tender offer at €70/share), AiDASH (approximately $350 million, climate adaptation software), and a joint acquisition of Uplight with Octopus Energy (cleared by the EU Commission, with Schneider as minority partner).
- Data centers and AI infrastructure remain the central growth engine; management continues to point to record order backlog and several multi-billion-dollar US data center deals.
- Sustainability: the 2021–2025 Sustainability Impact (SSI) program has been completed, with Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions down 83% versus 2017; continued COP31 participation underscores an ongoing strategic focus on decarbonization and grid modernization.
