## Aktuelle Meldungen
- Schneider Electric unveiled the world's first software-defined medium-voltage switchgear, aimed at AI-ready data centers, enabling up to 3x faster ordering/manufacturing and 2x faster commissioning; broader availability expected 2028.
- Presented new "Optimized Motor Control Center" and software-defined automation advances at Automate 2026 and SPS 2026 (Nuremberg, late September 2026).
- Stock dipped ~2.3% following confirmation of the Shelly Group acquisition, reflecting short-term investor caution on deal pricing/integration.
- Q3 2026 revenue release scheduled for October 29, 2026.

## Management
- CEO Olivier Blum (in post since November 2024) continues to frame strategy around "energy intelligence" as a prerequisite for AI-scale data center growth, reiterated in Bloomberg/CNBC interviews (July 2026) and at Davos.
- Management signaled willingness to pursue further bolt-on acquisitions in fast-growing technology segments while prioritizing organic growth.

## Finanzielles
- H1 2026 revenue: €21.2bn (record for a first half).
- Q2 2026 revenue: €11.5bn, +17% organic growth, highest quarterly revenue in company history; Energy Management €9.6bn (+18% organic), Industrial Automation €1.9bn (+11% organic).
- Adjusted EBITA: €4.1bn, +22% organically; margin expanded 120bps to 19.3%.
- Q1 2026 revenue: €9.8bn, +11% organic growth.
- Full-year guidance raised following record H1 results.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- Active portfolio management via three major 2026 acquisitions: Cognite (industrial AI, ~$3.1bn, announced June 30), AiDASH (grid/wildfire-risk platform, ~$350m enterprise value, ~90% stake), and Shelly Group (~€1.37bn, announced September 24).
- Strategic focus remains on AI-driven data center electrification, industrial automation, and energy transition technology; investment-grade credit ratings (S&P A/A-1, Moody's A2) maintained despite M&A spend.
- Next catalyst: Q3 2026 results on October 29, 2026, which should clarify integration costs and updated full-year outlook.
