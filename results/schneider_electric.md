## Strategy & Outlook
- Largest acquisition to date: agreement announced 05.10.2026 to acquire US software company PTC for about $205/share (~42% premium), implying an enterprise value of roughly $23.7 billion including debt. Expected to close in H2 2027, with projected ~€800 million in revenue synergies and ~€250 million in run-rate cost savings by year three post-closing.
- Voluntary public takeover offer for Bulgarian smart-home firm Shelly Group announced 24.09.2026, at €70/share (reported deal value ~€1.2–1.37 billion depending on source), conditional on reaching 95% of share capital; founders holding ~57% support the deal; expected close by end of Q1 2027.
- Acquisition of Norwegian industrial AI firm Cognite announced 30.06.2026 for ~$3.1 billion, to be integrated into Schneider's AVEVA industrial software unit as part of building a unified industrial AI platform.
- Overall strategic direction: continued bet on electrification, data center demand, and industrial/AI software, funded through an active, large-scale M&A program (PTC, Shelly, Cognite) alongside organic growth in Energy Management and Industrial Automation.
