## Management
- CEO Olivier Blum (in office since November 2024) continues to emphasize the need to innovate at the pace of AI infrastructure providers such as Nvidia ("Schneider Electric's Olivier Blum on moving at the pace of Nvidia", Semafor). He frames the company's strategy around three forces: AI, the changing energy landscape, and geopolitics, and argues energy must become "more intelligent" to meet projected demand growth.
- Blum positions data centers and AI-driven energy infrastructure as the company's central growth driver, pointing to record order backlog and multi-billion-dollar US data-center deals.
- The company's Chairman has given further 2026 interviews (e.g., Bloomberg TV, 15 May 2026, Africa CEO Forum in Kigali) on geopolitics, AI, and growth in Africa; exact speaker attribution in some listings is unclear.

## Finanzielles
- Q3 2026: Record revenue of €11.46–11.5 billion, up 17.9% reported / ~16.9% organically year-over-year ("Un chiffre d'affaires trimestriel en hausse de 17,9 %"). Nine-month 2026 revenue reached approximately €32.69 billion, organic growth of ~15%.
- Segment detail (Q3): Energy Management €9.62 billion (+18.8% organic); Industrial Automation €1.85 billion (+8.1% organic). By region: North America +19.9% organic, China & East Asia +26.1% organic, Europe +8.6% organic.
- Full-year 2026 guidance raised again after Q3: organic adjusted EBITA growth now targeted at +18% to +21% (up from the previous range).
- H1 2026 (reported 30.07.2026): record revenue of €21.2 billion, organic adjusted EBITA margin improvement of 120 basis points, free cash flow of €1.6 billion; FX translation effects reduced H1 revenue by nearly €750 million.
- Some secondary sources (e.g., TipRanks) show implausible Q2 revenue figures; these were discarded as unreliable.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- The planned acquisition of PTC (announced early October 2026, roughly $22.6 billion / ~€20 billion in equity value) remains the company's largest-ever deal and is intended to significantly expand its industrial software and AI business. It triggered a roughly 9% share-price drop on announcement, and integration and leverage risk remain a key overhang on the stock.
- Other 2026 deals reinforce the software/AI/energy-management build-out: Cognite ($3.1 billion, industrial AI, to be folded into AVEVA), Shelly Group (~€1.2 billion investment agreement, smart-home/IoT, tender offer at €70/share), AiDASH (~$350 million, climate-adaptation software), and a joint acquisition of Uplight with Octopus Energy (cleared by the European Commission, Schneider as minority partner).
- Data centers and AI infrastructure remain the central growth engine, with management citing record order backlog and multiple multi-billion-dollar US data-center deals, and visibility extending into 2027–2028.
- Sustainability: the SSI 2021–2025 program has been completed, with Scope 1 and 2 emissions down 83% versus 2017 levels; the company's COP31 presence underscores continued strategic focus on decarbonization and grid modernization.
