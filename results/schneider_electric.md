## Aktuelle Meldungen
- Shares held near a one-year/52-week high through late August 2026, with year-to-date gains of roughly 24–26% and analyst sentiment described as consistently constructive.
- Mid-August 2026: announcement of a new **Helios AI platform reference design with AMD**, reinforcing data-centre and AI infrastructure as a core growth driver for the group.
- 10–14 August 2026: the company repurchased **23,000 own shares for EUR 7,063,440** under its ongoing weekly buyback, executed to cover obligations from long-term incentive plans for employees and corporate officers.
- 5 August 2026: press release on the **Impact 2030 sustainability roadmap**, reporting an Impact Score of 3.69 out of 10 after six months, on track for the 2026 annual target of 4.20 points.
- Sustainability release also noted 67 million people given access to sustainable electricity since 2009, with more than 2.2 million added in Q2 2026 alone, largely in Africa and India.
- Investors continue to weigh the stock's rich valuation against its exposure to electrification and AI-driven infrastructure spending.

## Management
- **Olivier Blum** remains CEO and has been highly visible in 2026, giving interviews to Bloomberg and CNBC on 30 July 2026 alongside the half-year results.
- In the Bloomberg interview Blum said the strategy is to grow **organically as much as possible**, while remaining open to acquiring new technology companies to accelerate expansion, and highlighted data-centre growth including in China.
- On CNBC he argued that **AI-driven growth extends beyond data centres**, sketching an outlook for AI infrastructure demand over the next three years.
- Earlier in the year Blum framed his tech-speed agenda ("if you want to be a tech company, you have to move at the speed of a tech company", Semafor, March 2026) and at Davos in January 2026 stated there is "no AI at scale without energy intelligence".
- A CNBC "Executive Decisions" profile in February 2026 covered his path to the CEO role, underlining continuity in leadership after the transition from Jean-Pascal Tricoire.

## Finanzielles
- **H1 2026 (published 30 July 2026):** record results. Net income of EUR 2.5bn, up 30%; adjusted net income of EUR 2.7bn, up 21%.
- **Operating cash flow of EUR 3.8bn, +28%**, indicating strong earnings quality and cash conversion.
- **Q2 2026** revenue of roughly USD 13.4bn with about 16.5% organic growth; Energy Management contributed EUR 9.6bn (+18% organic) and Industrial Automation EUR 1.9bn (+11% organic).
- **Q1 2026 (30 April 2026):** record revenues of EUR 9.77bn, +11.2% organic, ahead of expectations; Energy Management EUR 8bn (+12.8% organic), Industrial Automation about EUR 2bn.
- Management flagged material **FX headwinds of roughly EUR 750–850m** on full-year margins, the main offset to otherwise strong operating momentum.
- Share buyback programme running systematically through 2026: EUR 249m repurchased as of 30 June 2026, with weekly tranches continuing into August.
- No dividend has been received on this position, consistent with the May 2026 entry falling after the annual ex-dividend date; the yield at the current price is around 1.4%.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- **Full-year 2026 guidance was raised** alongside the H1 results, driven by the strength of the electrification and automation franchises.
- **Cognite acquisition:** announced 30 June 2026, an all-cash deal valued at about **USD 3.1bn**, with Cognite to be combined with the group's Aveva industrial software business — a decisive push into industrial AI and data.
- **AiDASH** transaction announced 30 July 2026, targeting AI-powered solutions for the utility and grid infrastructure market.
- Together these deals signal active portfolio management aimed at fast-growing technology segments and geographies, complementing rather than replacing the organic growth priority.
- Capital investment continues on the manufacturing side, including a commitment of over USD 700m in US operations through 2027 to strengthen energy infrastructure and domestic capacity for AI-related demand.
- Strategic thesis remains the convergence of electrification, automation and AI: data centres are the visible driver, but management insists demand broadens into grid, industry and buildings.
- Key risks to monitor: currency translation, the premium valuation leaving little room for disappointment, and integration execution on Cognite and Aveva.
