# Iberdrola (IBE.MC)
_KW25 / 2026 | Zeitraum: 13.06.2026 – 20.06.2026_

## Aktuelle Meldungen

**EN**
- **Green Bond – €1.5 Billion (June 16, 2026):** Iberdrola successfully closed a European senior green bond for a total amount of **€1.5 billion**, structured in two tranches with 4-year and 10-year maturities. This continues the company's position as a leading private issuer of green bonds globally. ([Iberdrola Press Room](https://www.iberdrola.com/press-room/news/2026))
- **Labour Strike – Spain (June 19, 2026):** More than **9,000 Iberdrola workers** across Spain were set to strike on 19 June 2026 after collective bargaining talks that began in January 2025 remained deadlocked after sixteen months. Unions UGT and CCOO led the action over pay demands. ([IndustriALL Union](https://www.industriall-union.org/iberdrola-strike-spain-2026))
- **125th Anniversary Celebrations (June 2026):** Iberdrola is celebrating its founding anniversary with a broad programme of events. In June, concerts at Torre Iberdrola in Bilbao and events in Madrid on 27 June featured artists including Lenny Kravitz, Manuel Carrasco, and Ana Mena. Shareholders massively supported management and strategy at the anniversary General Meeting (29 May 2026). ([Iberdrola](https://www.iberdrola.com/press-room/news/detail/iberdrola-celebrate-125th-anniversary-2026-with-extensive-programme-activities))
- **Stock Performance:** Iberdrola's share price (IBE.MC) has risen approximately **29% year-to-date** as of mid-June 2026, outperforming its sector by ~5 percentage points. Market capitalisation exceeded **€135 billion**, making it the **largest electricity company in Europe** and second-largest in the world. ([NAGA](https://naga.com/en/instruments/IBE.re))
- **Brazil Investment Announcement (June 8, 2026):** Iberdrola announced plans to invest nearly **€4.5 billion in electricity networks in Bahia, Brazil, by 2030**, reinforcing its commitment to its subsidiary Neoenergia. ([Iberdrola Press Room](https://www.iberdrola.com/press-room/news/2026))

**ES**
- **Bono Verde – 1.500 millones de euros (16 de junio de 2026):** Iberdrola cerró con éxito una emisión de bonos verdes sénior europeos por **1.500 millones de euros**, estructurada en dos tramos (4 y 10 años), consolidando su posición como principal emisora privada de bonos verdes a nivel mundial. ([Sala de Prensa Iberdrola](https://www.iberdrola.com/press-room/news/2026))
- **Huelga laboral en España (19 de junio de 2026):** Más de **9.000 trabajadores de Iberdrola** en España convocaron una huelga para el 19 de junio de 2026 tras 16 meses de negociación colectiva bloqueada con los sindicatos UGT y CCOO en materia salarial. ([IndustriALL Union](https://www.industriall-union.org/iberdrola-strike-spain-2026))
- **125º Aniversario (junio 2026):** Iberdrola celebra su aniversario con un amplio programa de actos; en junio, conciertos en la Torre Iberdrola de Bilbao y actos en Madrid el 27 de junio con Lenny Kravitz, Manuel Carrasco y Ana Mena. La Junta General de Accionistas (29 de mayo de 2026) respaldó masivamente la gestión y la estrategia. ([Iberdrola](https://www.iberdrola.com/press-room/news/detail/iberdrola-celebrate-125th-anniversary-2026-with-extensive-programme-activities))
- **Evolución bursátil:** La acción IBE.MC acumula una revalorización de aproximadamente el **29% en lo que va de año** (mediados de junio de 2026), superando a su sector en ~5 puntos porcentuales. La capitalización bursátil supera los **135.000 millones de euros**, lo que la convierte en la **mayor eléctrica de Europa** y la segunda del mundo. ([NAGA](https://naga.com/en/instruments/IBE.re))
- **Inversión en Brasil (8 de junio de 2026):** Iberdrola anunció planes para invertir cerca de **4.500 millones de euros en redes eléctricas en Bahía, Brasil, hasta 2030**, reforzando su compromiso con su filial Neoenergia. ([Sala de Prensa Iberdrola](https://www.iberdrola.com/press-room/news/2026))

## Finanzielles

**EN**
- **Q1 2026 Results (reported 29 April 2026):** Adjusted net profit rose **11% year-on-year to €1,865 million** (+17% excluding FX effects), driven by the Networks segment in A-rated countries despite significant currency headwinds. ([Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/iberdrola-q1-2026-slides-profit-jumps-11-guidance-raised-to-8-93CH-4644123))
- **Segment highlights:**
  - UK Networks: EBITDA +32% (in sterling), driven by Electricity North West consolidation
  - US Networks: EBITDA +21.5% (local currency), boosted by higher distribution rates and NECEC
  - Spain Networks: EBITDA +6.2%, benefiting from the new regulated return of 6.58%
  - Networks total EBITDA growth: +9% to ~€2,048 million
- **Renewable capacity** expanded by **3,300 MW** over the past twelve months.
- **Full-year 2026 guidance raised** to **"double-digit" net income growth** year-on-year (previously "mid to high single-digit"), with a 2026 net profit target of **€6.6 billion**. ([Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-iberdrolas-q1-2026-profit-grows-stock-edges-up-93CH-4643991))
- **Total investments (last 12 months):** **€14.5 billion**, majority directed to regulated networks in the UK and US. ([SolarQuarter](https://solarquarter.com/2026/05/02/iberdrola-invests-e14-5-billion-in-twelve-months-with-majority-directed-to-networks-in-the-uk-and-united-states/))
- **Sustainable financing:** €2.4 billion in new sustainability-linked transactions in Q1 2026; total sustainable debt portfolio reached **€68.9 billion**. ([Iberdrola Q1 2026 Results](https://www.iberdrola.com/documents/20125/5846486/results-26Q1.pdf))
- **Upcoming results:** H1 2026 on **22 July 2026**; 9-month results on **21 October 2026**.

**ES**
- **Resultados 1T 2026 (presentados el 29 de abril de 2026):** El beneficio neto ajustado creció un **11% interanual hasta los 1.865 millones de euros** (+17% excluyendo el efecto divisa), impulsado por el segmento de Redes en países con calificación A, pese a vientos en contra cambiarios. ([Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/iberdrola-q1-2026-slides-profit-jumps-11-guidance-raised-to-8-93CH-4644123))

([SolarQuarter](https://solarquarter.com/2026/05/02/iberdrola-invests-e14-5-billion-in-twelve-months-with-majority-directed-to-networks-in-the-uk-and-united-states/))
- **Financiación sostenible:** 2.400 millones de euros en nuevas operaciones vinculadas a la sostenibilidad en el 1T 2026; cartera total de deuda sostenible: **68.900 millones de euros**. ([Resultados 1T 2026 Iberdrola](https://www.iberdrola.com/documents/20125/5846486/results-26Q1.pdf))

## Strategie & Ausblick

**EN**
- **Strategic Plan 2025–2028 – €58 Billion Programme:** Iberdrola's plan foresees total gross investments of ~€58 billion, focused on regulated electricity networks: UK (€20bn), US (€16bn), Iberian Peninsula (€9bn), Brazil (€7bn), other EU countries & Australia (€5bn). ([Iberdrola Strategic Plan](https://www.iberdrola.com/about-us/iberdrola-strategic-plan))
- **UK Market Expansion:** Iberdrola acquired an **88% stake in North West Electricity Networks** (owner of Electricity North West Limited) for approximately **£2.134 billion**, significantly expanding its UK regulated asset base. This drove a 52% surge in distributed energy volumes in the UK in 2025. ([TipRanks](https://www.tipranks.com/news/company-announcements/iberdrola-expands-uk-market-presence-with-major-acquisition))
- **Brazil – Neoenergia (100% ownership):** Following the completion of the public tender offer (OPA) earlier in 2026, Iberdrola holds 100% of Neoenergia. Concession renewals extend more than 30 years, providing long-term earnings visibility. Plans to invest €4.5 billion in Bahia networks by 2030 reinforce this commitment. ([Iberdrola Press Room](https://www.iberdrola.com/press-room/news/2026))
- **Australia – New Market Entry:** In March 2026, Iberdrola completed the acquisition of its **first wind farm in Victoria**, marking its entry into the Asia-Pacific market. ([Iberdrola Corporate Operations](https://www.iberdrola.com/shareholders-investors/operational-financial-information/corporate-operations))
- **France – Divestment:** Iberdrola sold its French onshore assets — 118 MW of operational wind capacity and a 639 MW development pipeline (wind + solar PV) — to Technique Solaire, as part of ongoing portfolio optimisation toward core regulated markets. ([Iberdrola Corporate Operations](https://www.iberdrola.com/shareholders-investors/operational-financial-information/corporate-operations))
- **Mexico – Divestment Completed:** Cox received regulatory authorisation to finalise its acquisition of Iberdrola México assets (~12 GW renewable pipeline), announced July 2025 for ~USD 4.2 billion, removing non-core assets and improving portfolio risk profile.
- **AI & Digital Transformation:** With nearly 400 active AI projects (>70% generative AI), Iberdrola is embedding artificial intelligence across grid operations, asset management, and customer services.
- **Structural tailwinds:** Growing electricity demand from electrification, data centres, and AI supports the long-term investment case. Iberdrola remains the only European utility present in every edition of the Dow Jones Best-in-Class World Index.

**ES**
- **Plan Estratégico 2025–2028 – 58.000 millones de euros:** El plan contempla inversiones brutas de ~58.000 millones de euros en redes eléctricas reguladas: Reino Unido (20.000 M€), EE.UU. (16.000 M€), Península Ibérica (9.000 M€), Brasil (7.000 M€), otros países UE y Australia (5.000 M€). ([Plan Estratégico Iberdrola](https://www.iberdrola.com/about-us/iberdrola-strategic-plan))
- **Expansión en el Reino Unido:** Iberdrola adquirió el **88% de North West Electricity Networks** (propietaria de Electricity North West Limited) por aproximadamente **2.134 millones de libras esterlinas**, ampliando significativamente su base de activos regulados en el Reino Unido, lo que impulsó un aumento del 52% en energía distribuida en el país en 2025. ([TipRanks](https://www.tipranks.com/news/company-announcements/iberdrola-expands-uk-market-presence-with-major-acquisition))
- **Brasil – Neoenergia (100% propiedad):** Tras completar la OPA en 2026, Iberdrola posee el 100% de Neoenergia. Las renovaciones de concesiones superan los 30 años. La inversión prevista de 4.500 M€ en redes de Bahía hasta 2030 refuerza este compromiso. ([Sala de Prensa Iberdrola](https://www.iberdrola.com/press-room/news/2026))
- **Australia – Entrada en un nuevo mercado:** En marzo de 2026, Iberdrola completó la adquisición de su **primer parque eólico en Victoria**, marcando su entrada en el mercado de Asia-Pacífico. ([Operaciones Corporativas Iberdrola](https://www.iberdrola.com/shareholders-investors/operational-financial-information/corporate-operations))
- **Francia – Desinversión:** Iberdrola vendió sus activos terrestres en Francia — 118 MW de capacidad eólica operativa y una cartera de desarrollo de 639 MW (eólica y solar FV) — a Technique Solaire, como parte de la optimización de cartera hacia mercados regulados clave. ([Operaciones Corporativas Iberdrola](https://www.iberdrola.com/shareholders-investors/operational-financial-information/corporate-operations))
- **México – Desinversión completada:** Cox recibió autorización regulatoria para finalizar la adquisición de los activos mexicanos de Iberdrola (~12 GW de cartera renovable), anunciada en julio de 2025 por ~4.200 millones de USD, eliminando activos no estratégicos y mejorando el perfil de riesgo.
- **IA y transformación digital:** Con casi 400 proyectos de IA activos (>70% de IA generativa), Iberdrola integra la inteligencia artificial en la gestión de redes, activos y clientes.
- **Vientos de cola estructurales:** El crecimiento de la demanda eléctrica derivado de la electrificación, los centros de datos y la IA respalda la tesis de inversión a largo plazo. Iberdrola es la única empresa europea de servicios públicos presente en todas las ediciones del Índice Dow Jones Best-in-Class World.
