# Iberdrola (IBE.MC)
_KW24 / 2026 | Zeitraum: 06.06.2026 – 13.06.2026_

## Aktuelle Meldungen

**Share Buyback Programme – Termination (June 11, 2026)**
Iberdrola's share buyback programme (up to 5.5 million shares, maximum value €132 million), launched on June 1, 2026, was terminated on June 11, 2026. Transactions under the programme were reported for the period June 1–10, 2026.

**Brazil Network Investment – €4.5 Billion for Bahia (June 8, 2026)**
Iberdrola announced plans to invest nearly €4.5 billion in electricity networks in Bahia, Brazil, by 2030. This reinforces the group's commitment to its Brazilian subsidiary Neoenergia, where Iberdrola now holds 100% ownership following the completion of the OPA earlier in 2026.

**Flexible Dividend – Board Resolutions (June 2026)**
The Board of Directors approved resolutions regarding the first capital increase, the payment of the Supplementary Dividend, and, if applicable, the Adjustment Dividend under the "Iberdrola Retribución Flexible" optional dividend scheme. Trading days of June 25, 26, 29, and 30 and July 1, 2026 were used to determine the number of free allocation rights required to receive one new share and the gross amount of the Interim Dividend per share.

**Stock Performance**
Iberdrola's share price rose approximately 29% year-to-date as of early June 2026, outperforming its sector by roughly 5 percentage points.

**UK: Major Network Acquisition**
Iberdrola continued to expand its UK market presence with a significant distribution network acquisition, further consolidating its foothold in Britain following the earlier integration of SP Electricity North West (which drove a 52% surge in distributed energy in the UK in 2025).

## Management

**CEO Pedro Azagra (The CEO Magazine, May 2026)**
Pedro Azagra, who became Group CEO in 2025 after leading Iberdrola's U.S. subsidiary Avangrid, gave an extensive interview to The CEO Magazine. He underlined surging energy demand as the primary force behind the global energy transition: *"Increasing energy demand is leading the global energy transition, reshaping industries, economies and geopolitics."* He noted that the company invested nearly €15 billion in 2025, generating approximately €6.3 billion in net income. Azagra also highlighted that Iberdrola is pursuing nearly 400 AI-related projects and initiatives, with over 70% being generative AI applications.

**Q1 2026 Earnings Call (April 29, 2026)**
Azagra reported Q1 2026 investments of over €2.7 billion, with significant allocations in the UK, US, and Brazil, and emphasised the company's ongoing digital and AI transformation agenda.

**Executive Chairman Ignacio Sánchez Galán – Davos (January 2026)**
At the World Economic Forum in Davos, Galán told CNBC that heightened geopolitical tensions have returned energy autonomy to the top of policymakers' agendas: *"Energy security and national security are absolutely linked."*

**Galán – ElEconomista Interview (March 2026)**
Galán stated that geopolitical and energy-market volatility has had limited impact on Iberdrola's operations, and confirmed that between 2025 and 2028 the company plans to invest €40 billion in the United States and United Kingdom markets.

## Strategie & Ausblick

**Strategic Plan 2025–2028: €58 Billion Investment Programme**
Iberdrola's Strategic Plan 2025–2028 foresees total gross investments of approximately €58 billion, focused on regulated electricity networks:
- United Kingdom: €20 billion
- United States: €16 billion
- Iberian Peninsula: €9 billion
- Brazil: €7 billion
- Other EU countries & Australia: €5 billion

**Portfolio Rotation**
- **Mexico – Divestment:** Cox received regulatory authorisation to finalise its acquisition of Iberdrola México's assets (12 GW renewable pipeline), a transaction first announced in July 2025 valued at approximately USD 4.2 billion. Proceeds are being redeployed into core regulated markets.
- **Australia – Entry:** In March 2026, Iberdrola completed the acquisition of its first wind farm in the state of Victoria, marking an important step in the group's Asia-Pacific expansion.
- **Brazil – Neoenergia:** Iberdrola now holds 100% of Neoenergia following the completion of the OPA. Concession renewals extend more than 30 years, providing long-term earnings visibility.
- **UK – Network Expansion:** A major UK distribution network acquisition (reported June 2026) further consolidates Iberdrola's presence in Britain.

**AI & Digital Transformation**
With nearly 400 active AI projects (>70% generative AI), Iberdrola is systematically embedding artificial intelligence across grid operations, asset management, and customer services as part of its efficiency and innovation agenda.

**Structural Tailwinds**
Growing electricity demand driven by electrification, data centres, and AI continues to support the long-term investment case. Iberdrola's increasing share of regulated earnings in A-rated markets (US, UK, Spain, Brazil) provides high earnings visibility. The company remains the only European utility present in every edition of the Dow Jones Best-in-Class World Index.
