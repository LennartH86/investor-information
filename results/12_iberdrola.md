## Aktuelle Meldungen

**EN**
- **125th Anniversary Concert – Madrid (27 June 2026):** Iberdrola headlined its "125 Light Years" anniversary programme with a major public concert in Madrid on 27 June 2026, featuring Lenny Kravitz, Manuel Carrasco, Ana Mena and Álvaro de Luna. The event is part of a year-long programme of activities across Spain for employees, customers, shareholders and the wider public. ([Iberdrola Press Room](https://www.iberdrola.com/press-room/news/detail/iberdrola-celebrate-125th-anniversary-2026-with-extensive-programme-activities))
- **Green Bond – €1.5 Billion (June 16, 2026):** Iberdrola successfully closed a European senior green bond for a total of **€1.5 billion**, structured in two tranches with 4-year and 10-year maturities. The issue attracted more than **€4.5 billion in demand** (3× oversubscribed). Proceeds will finance electricity network investments in core markets and refinance selected renewable energy projects, reinforcing Iberdrola's position as a leading private issuer of green bonds globally. ([ESG News](https://esgnews.com/iberdrola-raises-1-7-billion-green-bond-to-fund-grid-expansion-and-renewable-energy-investments/))

([Iberdrola Press Room](https://www.iberdrola.com/press-room/news/2026))
- **Market Capitalisation Exceeds €140 Billion:** As of late June 2026, Iberdrola's market cap surpassed **€140 billion**, confirming its status as the **world's second-largest electricity company and the largest in Europe**. The share (IBE.MC) has risen approximately **29% year-to-date**, outperforming its sector by ~5 percentage points. ([NAGA](https://naga.com/en/instruments/IBE.re))

## Finanzielles

**EN**
- **Q1 2026 Results (reported 29 April 2026):** Adjusted net profit rose **11.4% year-on-year to €1,865 million** (+17% excluding FX effects), driven by the Networks segment in A-rated countries despite significant currency headwinds. Adjusted EBITDA increased **2.4% to €4.1 billion**. ([Renewables Now](https://renewablesnow.com/news/iberdrolas-q1-adj-net-profit-up-11-4-percent-fy-2026-guidance-raised-1293896/))
- **Segment highlights:**
  - UK Networks: EBITDA +32% (in sterling), driven by Electricity North West consolidation
  - US Networks: EBITDA +21.5% (local currency), boosted by higher distribution rates and NECEC
  - Spain Networks: EBITDA +6.2%, benefiting from the new regulated return of 6.58%
  - Networks total EBITDA: +9% to ~€2,048 million
  - Renewables: offshore wind production surged **+42%** vs. Q1 2025; 3.3 GW added in 12 months
- **Q1 2026 Investments:** **€2.7 billion** deployed during Q1, with more than half directed to the UK and US. ([Iberdrola Q1 2026 Results](https://www.iberdrola.com/shareholders-investors/operational-financial-information/results))

([Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/iberdrola-q1-2026-slides-profit-jumps-11-guidance-raised-to-8-93CH-4644123))
- **Raised Interim Dividend:** Iberdrola raised its interim dividend to **€0.253 per share**. ([Iberdrola](https://www.iberdrola.com/press-room/news/detail/iberdrola-raises-its-interim-dividend-to-0-253-per-share))
- **Sustainable financing:** €2.4 billion in new sustainability-linked transactions in Q1 2026; total sustainable debt portfolio reached **€68.9 billion**. The June green bond (€1.5bn) adds to this figure. ([Iberdrola Q1 2026 Report PDF](https://www.iberdrola.com/documents/20125/5846486/results-26Q1.pdf))

([SolarQuarter](https://solarquarter.com/2026/02/11/iberdrola-hits-record-255976-gwh-in-distributed-energy-for-2025-driven-by-a-52-surge-in-the-uk-after-integrating-sp-electricity-north-west/))

## Strategie & Ausblick

**EN**
- **Strategic Plan 2025–2028 – €58 Billion Programme:** Iberdrola's plan foresees total gross investments of ~€58 billion, focused on regulated electricity networks: UK (€20bn), US (€16bn), Iberian Peninsula (€9bn), Brazil (€7bn), other EU countries & Australia (€5bn). Renewables receive €21 billion, mainly for projects already under construction. ([Iberdrola Strategic Plan](https://www.iberdrola.com/about-us/iberdrola-strategic-plan))
- **UK Market Expansion:** Following the acquisition of an **88% stake in North West Electricity Networks** (owner of Electricity North West Limited) for approximately **£2.134 billion**, Iberdrola holds a significantly expanded regulated asset base in the UK. This drove a 72.6% surge in distributed energy volumes in Q1 2026. ([TipRanks](https://www.tipranks.com/news/company-announcements/iberdrola-expands-uk-market-presence-with-major-acquisition))
- **Brazil – Neoenergia & €4.5 Billion Bahia Investment:** Following the public tender offer (OPA) completed in 2026, Iberdrola holds **100% of Neoenergia**. Concession renewals extend more than 30 years. The announced €4.5 billion investment in Bahia electricity networks by 2030 (June 8, 2026) provides long-term earnings visibility. ([Iberdrola Press Room](https://www.iberdrola.com/press-room/news/2026))
- **Australia – New Market Entry:** In March 2026, Iberdrola completed the acquisition of its **first wind farm in Victoria**, marking its entry into the Asia-Pacific market. ([Iberdrola Corporate Operations](https://www.iberdrola.com/shareholders-investors/operational-financial-information/corporate-operations))
- **Norges Bank Alliance – €2 Billion Co-investment in Iberian Renewables:** Iberdrola expanded its co-investment alliance with Norges Bank Investment Management (NBIM) to co-invest more than **€2 billion in renewables in the Iberian Peninsula**, targeting a 2,500 MW portfolio; strengthened in September 2025 with an additional 708 MW of renewable capacity in Spain. ([Iberdrola Corporate Operations](https://www.iberdrola.com/shareholders-investors/operational-financial-information/corporate-operations))
- **Mexico – Divestment Completed (April 2026):** Iberdrola completed the sale of its business in Mexico in April 2026 (originally announced July 2025, ~USD 4.2 billion). This removes a non-core, politically exposed asset and improves the overall portfolio risk profile.
- **France – Divestment:** Iberdrola sold its French onshore assets (118 MW operational wind + 639 MW development pipeline) to Technique Solaire, continuing portfolio optimisation toward core regulated markets.
- **AI & Digital Transformation:** With nearly **400 active AI projects** (>70% generative AI), Iberdrola is embedding artificial intelligence across grid operations, asset management, and customer services.
- **Structural tailwinds:** Growing electricity demand from electrification, data centres, and AI infrastructure supports Iberdrola's long-term investment case. The company remains the only European utility present in every edition of the Dow Jones Best-in-Class World Index.

**ES**
- **Plan Estratégico 2025–2028 – 58.000 millones de euros:** El plan contempla inversiones brutas de ~58.000 M€ en redes eléctricas reguladas: Reino Unido (20.000 M€), EE.UU. (16.000 M€), Península Ibérica (9.000 M€), Brasil (7.000 M€), otros países UE y Australia (5.000 M€). Las energías renovables reciben 21.000 M€, principalmente para proyectos en construcción. ([Plan Estratégico Iberdrola](https://www.iberdrola.com/about-us/iberdrola-strategic-plan))
- **Expansión en el Reino Unido:** Tras la adquisición del **88% de North West Electricity Networks** por ~**2.134 millones de libras**, Iberdrola dispone de una base de activos regulados ampliada en el Reino Unido, que impulsó un crecimiento del 72,6% en energía distribuida en el 1T 2026.
- **Brasil – Neoenergia e inversión de 4.500 M€ en Bahía:** Tras la OPA completada en 2026, Iberdrola posee el **100% de Neoenergia**. Las renovaciones de concesiones superan los 30 años. La inversión de 4.500 M€ en redes de Bahía hasta 2030 (anunciada el 8 de junio de 2026) aporta visibilidad de ingresos a largo plazo. ([Sala de Prensa Iberdrola](https://www.iberdrola.com/press-room/news/2026))
- **Australia – Entrada en un nuevo mercado:** En marzo de 2026, Iberdrola completó la adquisición de su **primer parque eólico en Victoria**, marcando su entrada en el mercado de Asia-Pacífico.
- **Alianza con Norges Bank – más de 2.000 M€ en renovables ibéricas:** Iberdrola amplió la alianza de coinversión con Norges Bank Investment Management (NBIM) para coinvertir más de **2.000 millones de euros en renovables en la Península Ibérica** con un objetivo de cartera de 2.500 MW.
- **México – Desinversión completada (abril 2026):** Iberdrola completó en abril de 2026 la venta de su negocio en México (anunciada en julio de 2025 por ~4.200 millones de USD), eliminando activos no estratégicos y mejorando el perfil de riesgo.
- **Francia – Desinversión:** Iberdrola vendió sus activos terrestres en Francia (118 MW eólicos operativos + 639 MW en desarrollo) a Technique Solaire.
- **IA y transformación digital:** Con casi **400 proyectos de IA activos** (>70% de IA generativa), Iberdrola integra la inteligencia artificial en la gestión de redes, activos y clientes.
- **Vientos de cola estructurales:** El crecimiento de la demanda eléctrica derivado de la electrificación, centros de datos e infraestructura de IA respalda la tesis de inversión a largo plazo. Iberdrola es la única empresa europea de servicios públicos presente en todas las ediciones del Índice Dow Jones Best-in-Class World.
