# Iberdrola (IBE.MC)
_KW23 / 2026 | Zeitraum: 30.05.2026 – 06.06.2026_

## Aktuelle Meldungen

**Share Buyback Program (June 1–15, 2026)**
Iberdrola launched a new share buyback program for up to 5.5 million shares with a maximum value of €132 million, running from June 1 to June 15, 2026.

**Spain's Largest Battery Storage System Inaugurated (June 3, 2026)**
Iberdrola started up Spain's biggest battery energy storage system, integrated into the Campo Arañuelo solar complex in Cáceres (Extremadura). The system has 58 MW of power and 120 MWh of storage capacity, comprising two LFP lithium-ion battery modules of ~60 MWh each, connected to the Campo Arañuelo I and II solar plants. The batteries store surplus renewable energy and release it during peak demand hours, supporting grid stability and accelerating renewable integration.

**Eight EU Industrial Heat Auction Projects Secured (June 2, 2026)**
Iberdrola secured funding for eight industrial decarbonisation projects under the first European Commission Innovation Fund auction dedicated to reducing industrial heat emissions. The projects focus on the direct electrification of industrial heat through electric boilers powered by renewable electricity, requiring approximately 240 GWh of renewable electricity annually. This marks a significant step in Iberdrola's push to electrify industrial processes and replace fossil fuels in hard-to-abate sectors.

**Flexible Dividend – Board Resolutions Approved (June 2026)**
The Board of Directors approved resolutions regarding the first capital increase, payment of the Supplementary Dividend, and the Adjustment Dividend under the "Iberdrola Retribución Flexible" optional dividend system.

## Management

**Pedro Azagra – Group CEO**
Pedro Azagra (CEO since June 2025, formerly head of US subsidiary Avangrid) has underlined that "increasing energy demand is leading the global energy transition, reshaping industries, economies and geopolitics." In a recent interview with The CEO Magazine, he highlighted that the company invested nearly €15 billion in the prior year, generating approximately €6.3 billion in net income.

**Ignacio Sánchez Galán – Executive Chairman**
In a March 2026 interview with ElEconomista, Galán stated that the geopolitical and energy crisis has had limited impact on Iberdrola, citing the company's ability to offer long-term fixed-price contracts. He confirmed priority investments of €40 billion in the US and UK between 2025 and 2028. At the January 2026 World Economic Forum (CNBC interview), Galán linked energy security directly to national security, noting that heightened geopolitical tensions have returned energy autonomy to the top of policymakers' agendas.

**José Sáinz Armada – CFO**
Co-presented the Q1 2026 results alongside Galán and Azagra.

## Strategie & Ausblick

**Strategic Investment Plan 2025–2028**
Iberdrola targets gross investments of ~€58 billion through 2028, with the bulk allocated to regulated electricity networks in the US (€16 B) and UK (€20 B), plus Iberia (€9 B) and Brazil (€7 B).

**Industrial Electrification Push**
The eight EU Innovation Fund projects secured in June 2026 extend Iberdrola's strategy beyond power generation into industrial decarbonisation. By supplying 240 GWh/year of renewable electricity for industrial heat boilers, Iberdrola positions itself as a key enabler of hard-to-abate sector decarbonisation in Europe.

**Portfolio Rotation**
- **US – Avangrid Full Consolidation:** Iberdrola acquired the remaining 18.4% of Avangrid for ~€2.8 billion, bringing it to 100% ownership and simplifying the US corporate structure.
- **Mexico – Divestment Closed:** Cox Energy acquired Iberdrola México — including a 12 GW renewable pipeline — for approximately USD 4.2 billion. Proceeds will be redeployed into core regulated markets.
- **France – Onshore Exit:** 118 MW of operational onshore wind and a 639 MW project portfolio (wind + solar PV) sold to Technique Solaire, sharpening focus on regulated networks and offshore wind.
- **Australia – Expansion:** First wind farm in Victoria (315 MW, ~€1.28 B) acquired in March 2026; commissioning expected Q4 2026.
- **Brazil – Neoenergia Deep Dive:** Following the successful OPA (98% acceptance, April 2026), Neoenergia announced a BRL 50 billion (~USD 10.2 B) investment plan through 2030, 82% above the previous investment cycle.

**Structural Tailwinds**
Growing electricity demand driven by electrification, data centres, and AI continues to reinforce the long-term investment case. Iberdrola's increasing share of regulated earnings in A-rated markets (US, UK, Spain, Brazil) provides high earnings visibility. The company remains the only European utility present in every edition of the Dow Jones Best-in-Class World Index.
