# Italmobiliare (ITM.MI)

## Aktuelle Meldungen

**Morgan Stanley increases stake / Morgan Stanley aumenta la quota (June 3, 2026)**
Morgan Stanley has disclosed a potential stake (quota potenziale) of **5.509%** in Italmobiliare S.p.A. (ITM.MI), listed on Euronext STAR Milan. The transaction was dated May 27, 2026, with minor variations in the preceding days. This positions Morgan Stanley as a significant financial investor (investitore finanziario significativo) in the company.

**Share Buy-Back Programme updates (aggiornamento programma di acquisto azioni proprie)**
Multiple updates on the share buy-back programme were published during and just before the reporting period. The programme, launched on March 16, 2026, covered a maximum of 300,000 shares (azioni proprie) for a maximum consideration of €10,000,000. The buy-back is explicitly designed to create a treasury stock (riserva di azioni proprie) usable in extraordinary transactions such as equity exchanges (scambi azionari) or possible acquisitions (possibili acquisizioni).

**H1 2026 results scheduled (risultati del primo semestre 2026)**
The half-year financial results (risultati semestrali) at June 30, 2026 are scheduled for publication on **July 29, 2026**, according to Italmobiliare's financial calendar (calendario finanziario 2026).

## Management

**Carlo Pesenti – Chairman & CEO (Presidente e Amministratore Delegato)**
At its first meeting following the Annual General Meeting (Assemblea degli Azionisti), the Board of Directors (Consiglio di Amministrazione) on **April 28, 2026** confirmed **Carlo Pesenti** as Chairman and Chief Executive Officer. Pesenti has been a member of the Board since 1999, was appointed Chief Operating Officer (COO) in 2001, and assumed the role of CEO (Amministratore Delegato) in May 2014. He previously served for more than a decade as CEO of Italcementi, and has held board positions at major listed companies including UniCredit S.p.A., RCS S.p.A. and Mediobanca S.p.A.

**Laura Zanetti – Chair of the Board (Presidente del Consiglio di Amministrazione)**
Laura Zanetti chairs Italmobiliare's Board of Directors (Consiglio di Amministrazione). Together with CEO Pesenti, she presents the group's main results and strategic direction at shareholder meetings (assemblee degli azionisti) and investor events.

**Governance reform (riforma della governance)**
The new single-tier board model (modello monistico) with 12 members and a mandate covering 2026–2028 has been active since the AGM of April 22, 2026. A Phantom Stock compensation plan (piano di compensazione phantom stock) 2026–2028, linking management remuneration to NAV growth, was also approved.

No specific CEO interview (intervista al CEO) for the period KW23/2026 was identified in publicly available sources.

## Strategie & Ausblick

**CDS-Casa della Salute – M&A and expansion (acquisizioni ed espansione)**
During Q1 2026, CDS-Casa della Salute — the healthcare clinic network (rete di cliniche sanitarie) — completed **three M&A transactions (tre operazioni di M&A)**:
- Entry into the **Valle d'Aosta** region through a strategic acquisition (acquisizione strategica)
- Two further deals in **Liguria** and **Sardegna**
- Opening of the first clinic in **Turin (Torino)**
- Total: **43 clinics** now operating across **4 Italian regions (4 regioni italiane)**

This accelerated roll-out strategy (strategia di crescita accelerata) targets further regional expansion throughout 2026 and H1 2027.

**Caffè Borbone – Internationalisation (internazionalizzazione)**
The strong growth in foreign markets (~40% increase in Q1 2026) signals a continuing internationalisation push (spinta all'internazionalizzazione) for the coffee brand, particularly in the single-serve segment (segmento monoporzione).

**NAV discount as structural investment theme (sconto al NAV come tema di investimento strutturale)**
At ~50% discount, ITM.MI is regularly cited in independent financial analysis as a value opportunity (opportunità di valore). The governance reforms (riforme della governance) — single-tier board model and professional management — are the stated levers to close the discount over time.

**Share buy-back as strategic tool (buy-back come strumento strategico)**
The buy-back programme is structured to create a treasury stock (riserva di azioni proprie) usable in extraordinary financial transactions, including equity exchanges (scambi azionari) or potential acquisitions (possibili acquisizioni), signalling readiness for further deal activity.

**Outlook 2026 (prospettive 2026)**
No formal guidance (guidance formale) has been issued. Growth continues across all major portfolio companies; further M&A by CDS and potentially new platform investments (investimenti in nuove piattaforme) are expected for H2 2026, supported by the strong cash position of over €225 million. The next key financial disclosure will be the **H1 2026 results (risultati del primo semestre) scheduled for July 29, 2026**.
