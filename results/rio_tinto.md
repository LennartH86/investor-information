- **Bewertung:** No explicit current P/E or P/B figure was disclosed in the sources reviewed, but H1 2026 underlying EBITDA rose 28% to 14.8 bn USD with free cash flow up 75%, underpinning Rio Tinto's traditionally high dividend yield — the 1.021,64 EUR of net dividends received to date equal roughly 22% of the 4.582,40 EUR invested. With portfolio diversification progressing (copper, aluminium and lithium now over 50% of underlying EBITDA), full-year guidance maintained, and new bolt-on M&A activity in aluminium and lithium, **holding** the position remains appropriate; the 4,41% portfolio weight and cyclical iron-ore exposure argue against adding on strength.

## Aktuelle Meldungen
- Rio Tinto and **Aluminum Corporation of China (Chalco)** signed a definitive agreement to jointly acquire Votorantim's 68.596% controlling stake in **Companhia Brasileira de Alumínio (CBA)** via a JV owned 33% by Rio Tinto and 67% by Chalco; Rio Tinto's pro-rata contribution is approximately 297.8 million USD, strengthening its global low-carbon aluminium footprint.
- Rio Tinto appointed **Trudi Charles** as Chief Legal Officer, Governance & Corporate Affairs, joining from BP plc and taking up the role on 1 August 2026, succeeding Isabelle Deschamps.
- The **Tomago Aluminium** long-term future agreement (with the Australian and New South Wales Governments, including a 10-year power purchase agreement to 2038 and 100% renewable supply from 2033) continues to be highlighted as a key stabilising move for Rio Tinto's Australian aluminium operations.
- Shares have traded a touch softer over the past week amid ongoing **Chinese iron ore demand jitters**, even as the underlying H1 2026 earnings and cash flow trends remain solid.
- Rio Tinto continues to **maintain its full-year 2026 guidance**, with iron ore sales and lithium output tracking higher year to date.

## Management
- **Simon Trott** remains Chief Executive; his cost-discipline programme has identified 870 million USD of efficiencies, targeting a 1.8 billion USD annualised run-rate by year-end 2026.
- **CFO Peter Cunningham** continues alongside Trott in investor communications following the H1 2026 results.
- The appointment of **Trudi Charles** as Chief Legal Officer, Governance & Corporate Affairs (effective 1 August 2026) is the latest step in reshaping Rio Tinto's executive leadership team under Trott.
- Management continues to emphasise productivity gains, cash generation and non-core asset divestment as strategic priorities.

## Finanzielles
- **H1 2026 underlying EBITDA: 14.8 bn USD, up 28%** year on year, driven by higher copper and aluminium prices plus productivity gains.
- **Free cash flow up 75%** in H1 2026, described by management as a step-change supporting higher shareholder returns.
- **Copper equivalent production +3%** in H1 2026, following +9% year-on-year growth in Q1 2026 as Oyu Tolgoi continued ramping up.
- **Copper, Aluminium and Lithium together contributed more than 50% of underlying EBITDA**, underscoring continued diversification away from iron ore.
- **870 million USD of productivity benefits already banked**, on track for the 1.8 bn USD annualised run-rate by end of 2026.
- New M&A commitments this period: approximately **297.8 million USD** pro-rata for the CBA stake acquisition (aluminium) alongside the previously announced 300+ million USD Nemaska Lithium investment in Québec.
- No explicit current P/E, P/B or dividend yield figures were disclosed in the sources reviewed for this period.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- **Full-year 2026 guidance maintained**, with iron ore sales and lithium volumes trending higher.
- **Aluminium expansion:** the joint CBA acquisition with Chalco strengthens Rio Tinto's global low-carbon aluminium footprint and Brazilian market presence.
- **Lithium build-out:** continued investment in the Québec lithium business (Nemaska Lithium) alongside Rincon, Sal de Vida and Fénix 1B in Argentina, targeting 200,000 t/y LCE by 2028, building on the 6.7 bn USD Arcadium Lithium acquisition completed in October 2024.
- Continued focus on **cost reduction, productivity and non-core asset disposals** to fund growth while protecting shareholder returns.
- Key watch items: **Chinese iron ore demand**, integration progress on the CBA and Nemaska investments, the pace of the Oyu Tolgoi copper ramp-up, and delivery on the 1.8 bn USD productivity run-rate.
