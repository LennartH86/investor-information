## Management
- **Simon Trott** remains Chief Executive, having succeeded Jakob Stausholm in 2026; his tenure continues to center on cost discipline, with USD 870 million in efficiencies already banked by mid-2026 and an annualised run-rate target of USD 1.8 billion by year-end ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-28/rio-tinto-s-half-year-profit-rises-on-strong-commodity-prices)).
- Trott has outlined a **restructuring of operations into three global product groups** — iron ore, aluminium, and energy transition minerals (lithium and copper) — intended to strengthen supply capacity, competitiveness and growth ([e-mj.com](https://www.e-mj.com/departments/people/rio-tinto-matthew-holcz-jerome-pecresse-katie-jackson-kellie-parker/)).
- **Trudi Charles** succeeded Isabelle Deschamps as Chief Legal Officer, Governance & Corporate Affairs, effective 1 August 2026.
- At the 2026 AGM, Trott addressed safety concerns following fatalities at company sites and reiterated plans to unlock **more than USD 10 billion** through portfolio optimisation and asset divestments.

## Strategy & Outlook
- **Portfolio optimisation continues:** the CBA joint acquisition with Chalco, the earlier Arcadium Lithium acquisition, and the agreed purchase of the Aurukun bauxite project (from Glencore/Mitsubishi Development) all expand Rio Tinto's energy-transition and aluminium footprint.
- **Growth capex:** a USD 180 million investment was approved for the Norman Creek access project at the Amrun bauxite mine, targeting first production in 2027.
- **Iron ore capacity expansion:** mid-term guidance targets 425–440 million tonnes of sales capacity (up from 346 million tonnes in 2025), anchored by Simandou (Guinea, rail-to-port infrastructure fully commissioned by February 2026) and Rhodes Ridge (Western Australia).
- **M&A stance:** Rio Tinto is seen as unlikely to revive merger talks with Glencore, while management says further M&A could be needed to secure long-term copper growth beyond 2030.
- **Cost discipline remains central**, with the group continuing toward its USD 1.8 billion year-end productivity target and a broader ambition to unlock over USD 10 billion from the asset base via divestments and efficiencies.
- Key watch item: **Q3 2026 production results on 13 October 2026**.
