## Financials
- Q3 2026 production results (14 October 2026) remain the latest operational disclosure: Oyu Tolgoi copper output up ~27-29% year-to-date, group copper production roughly flat for the quarter, lithium carbonate equivalent output up 33% YTD, and global iron ore sales up ~3% YTD (90.8 Mt in Q3). Simandou reached an annualised run-rate of about 10 Mtpa during the quarter.
- The company reiterated it remains on track for about $5 billion of capital releases (part of a $5-10bn program, with over $2bn achieved YTD) and a $1.8 billion annualised productivity benefit run-rate, both targeted by end-2026.
- No new quarterly or annual financial statements were released this week; the next scheduled major disclosure is expected to be full-year/annual results in early 2027.

## Strategy & Outlook
- Rio Tinto has formally ended merger talks with Glencore; focus remains on organic growth, cost reduction, and copper expansion.
- Copper continues to be the primary long-term growth pillar via Resolution Copper (Arizona) and the Oyu Tolgoi ramp-up.
- The pending Chalco/CBA aluminium joint acquisition (Rio's pro-rata share ~$297.8m, subject to regulatory approval) points to continued portfolio moves within the low-carbon aluminium value chain.
- Simandou's ramp-up toward (and now at) its ~10 Mtpa annualised run-rate and continued integration of Arcadium Lithium remain additional medium-term growth drivers.
