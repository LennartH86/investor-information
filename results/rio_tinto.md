## Recent News
- Q3 2026 production update (14 Oct 2026): Oyu Tolgoi copper output up 27% YoY in the quarter (+29% YTD); Q3 group copper output flat YoY at 205kt, 9-month copper production 646kt (+1% YoY).
- Global iron ore sales reached 90.8 Mt in Q3 (+3% YoY), with Pilbara sales up 2% YoY; full-year Pilbara shipment guidance maintained at 323-338 Mt.
- Simandou (Guinea) iron ore project reached an annualised production run-rate of roughly 10 Mtpa (100% basis) during the quarter.
- Alumina output rose about 10% YoY while bauxite production dipped roughly 4% YoY (~15.75 Mt in Q3); primary aluminium output held steady.
- Lithium carbonate equivalent production rose 33% year-to-date to 40,300 tonnes, reflecting the integration of the Arcadium Lithium business.
- Rio Tinto confirmed it is on track to complete around $5bn of capital-release initiatives by the end of 2026, having already achieved more than $2bn year-to-date under its broader $5-10bn program.
- Tomago Aluminium smelter received a reported A$2.5bn support package from the Australian government (mid-August 2026).

## Management
- CEO: **Simon Trott** has led Rio Tinto since 25 August 2025, succeeding Jakob Stausholm (who announced his departure in May 2025). Trott previously headed the company's iron-ore division.
- Trott addressed the 2026 Annual General Meetings on 6 May 2026, reiterating focus on cost discipline, portfolio optimisation and growing the copper business.
- Trudi Charles was appointed Chief Legal Officer, Governance and Corporate Affairs in August 2026, succeeding Isabelle Deschamps, who is leaving the company during 2026.

## Financials
- Half-year 2026 results were published on 29 July 2026 (detailed P&L figures were not covered in this research round — see Sources for the official release).
- Full-year 2026 production and unit-cost guidance across key commodities (iron ore, copper, aluminium, lithium) was reaffirmed alongside the Q3 production update.
- Capital allocation: more than $2bn of capital returned to shareholders year-to-date 2026 as part of the $5-10bn multi-year capital-release program; company reiterated it remains on track for roughly $5bn of releases by end-2026.
- In June 2026, Rio Tinto reached an agreement with the Government of Mongolia on the Oyu Tolgoi shareholder loan interest rate, a positive step for the economics of that key copper growth asset.

## Strategy & Outlook
- Rio Tinto formally ended merger talks with Glencore; as of the August 2026 standstill expiry, the company is considered unlikely to revive the discussions, with management concluding the deal did not offer sufficient value. Focus instead remains on organic growth, cost reduction and copper expansion.
- Rio Tinto and Chalco agreed to jointly acquire Votorantim's controlling stake in Brazil's Companhia Brasileira de Alumínio (CBA) for roughly $902.6m (Rio's share approx. $297.8m), reinforcing its low-carbon aluminium footprint in Brazil.
- Rio Tinto reached a deal with the Queensland and Commonwealth governments to secure the long-term future of the Boyne aluminium smelter in Gladstone ahead of its power contract expiry in 2029 (announced March 2026).
- Rio Tinto and BHP are exploring a Pilbara iron-ore collaboration that could see joint mining of up to 200 Mt at the Yandicoogina and Yandi operations, with first ore not expected before the early 2030s and subject to a final investment decision.
- Continued ramp-up of Simandou (iron ore, Guinea) and integration of the Arcadium Lithium business are the main medium-term growth drivers, alongside copper expansion via Oyu Tolgoi.
