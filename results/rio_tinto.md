## Aktuelle Meldungen
- Rio Tinto set the **local currency exchange rates for its 2026 interim dividend** of 211.00 US cents per share, payable to shareholders on **24 September 2026**.
- The **Nyangumarta Warrarn Aboriginal Corporation (NWAC) and Rio Tinto signed a Project Agreement** on 10 September 2026 for the proposed **Winu copper-gold mine** on Nyangumarta Country, a further milestone in securing social licence for the project after the Traditional Owner consent reported the prior week.
- Rio Tinto and the **Perth Wildcats renewed their partnership** with a new three-year agreement (announced 15 September 2026), continuing community and youth-development programmes across Western Australia.
- **Bernstein raised its price target** on Rio Tinto from USD 88.50 to USD 89.50 (9 September 2026) amid mixed iron ore price signals; Rio Tinto's share price has returned 30.42% year-to-date as of 14 September 2026, well ahead of the FTSE 100's 7.67%.
- The **CMRG-led pause in Chinese steel mills' contract negotiations** with Rio Tinto (first reported 8-10 September 2026) remains an open, unresolved item carried over from last week's update.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- **Social licence build-out continues in the Pilbara/Kimberley region**, with the new NWAC Project Agreement for Winu following the Traditional Owner consent and the Ngarlawangga Interim Modernised Agreement reported the previous week — reducing permitting risk on two significant Western Australian growth assets.
- **Capital discipline and cost-cutting remain the core narrative**, with management continuing to target a 1.8 bn USD annualised productivity run-rate by year-end 2026 and group capex guided at up to 10 bn USD across 2025–2026, including ongoing lithium investment (Nemaska, Québec: over 300 million USD in 2026) and the CBA aluminium joint acquisition with Chalco (Rio Tinto's pro-rata share: 297.8 million USD).
- **China relationship remains a swing factor**: the CMRG-driven pause in iron ore contract negotiations with Chinese steel mills, first reported in early September, had not been reported as resolved as of 16 September 2026.
- **Shareholder returns in focus**: the confirmed 211 US cents interim dividend and strong year-to-date share price outperformance versus the FTSE 100 underline the "step-change in performance" management described at the H1 2026 results.
- Key watch items: **resolution of the CMRG/China iron ore dispute**, delivery of the 1.8 bn USD productivity target, and progress on the Aurukun bauxite and Winu copper-gold approvals reported previously.
