- **Bewertung:** Rio Tinto trades on a low double-digit P/E typical for a large diversified miner (no explicit P/E or P/B figure was disclosed in the sources reviewed), while H1 2026 delivered a 28% jump in underlying EBITDA and a 75% rise in free cash flow, supporting the historically high single-digit dividend yield the group is known for — the 1.021,64 EUR of net dividends already received equal roughly 22% of the 4.582,40 EUR invested. With the position up +55,07% versus the average purchase price, a diversifying shift toward copper, aluminium and lithium (now more than 50% of underlying EBITDA) and a 1,8 bn USD productivity programme on track, **holding** looks appropriate; adding should be considered only on weakness, as Chinese iron ore demand jitters at the end of August 2026 and the 4,42% portfolio weight already argue against increasing cyclical exposure.

## Aktuelle Meldungen
- On 22 August 2026 Rio Tinto marked **60 years in Western Australia**, the anniversary of the first iron ore shipment from Dampier to Japan aboard the *Houn Maru* on 22 August 1966, which followed a landmark agreement with Japanese steel mills to buy 65.5 million tonnes of iron ore over 15 years.
- Also on 22 August 2026, Rio Tinto and the Perth Wildcats announced the **expansion of the Rio Tinto Perth Wildcats Academy into the Pilbara**, opening elite development pathways for young players and coaches in the region; the announcement was made at the first official NBL game played in Karratha.
- On 13 August 2026 Rio Tinto welcomed an agreement between **Tomago Aluminium**, the Australian Government and the New South Wales Government securing the long-term future of Australia's largest aluminium smelter, including a 10-year power purchase agreement running to 2038 with 100% renewable supply from 2033.
- Towards the end of August 2026 the shares eased back from recent highs as **iron ore market jitters in China** offset the solid H1 2026 EBITDA, cash flow and dividend growth.
- Rio Tinto **maintained its full-year 2026 guidance**, with iron ore sales and lithium output rising across the year to date.

## Management
- **Simon Trott** continues as Chief Executive, hosting the 2026 half-year results investor webcast alongside CFO **Peter Cunningham**.
- Trott has made cost discipline his signature theme: he has identified **870 million USD of efficiencies and savings** and raised the target to **1.8 billion USD annualised by year-end 2026**.
- In interviews around the half-year results, Trott stated that **iron ore demand remains resilient and stable** and positioned Rio Tinto as a good bet on the AI boom via copper and aluminium demand.
- Management is doubling down on boosting productivity, building cash and **divesting non-core assets**.
- Rio Tinto issued a separate **executive team change** announcement during 2026, indicating continued reshaping of the leadership team under Trott.

## Finanzielles
- **H1 2026 underlying EBITDA: 14.8 bn USD, up 28%** year on year, driven by higher copper and aluminium prices plus productivity gains.
- **Free cash flow up 75%** in H1 2026 — a step-change in performance that management explicitly linked to higher shareholder returns.
- **Copper equivalent production +3%** in H1 2026; Q1 2026 (released 21 April) had shown **+9% year-on-year** growth as Oyu Tolgoi continued ramping up to plan.
- **Copper, Aluminium and Lithium together contributed more than 50% of underlying EBITDA**, a milestone in portfolio diversification away from iron ore.
- **870 million USD of productivity benefits already banked**, on track for a 1.8 bn USD annualised run-rate by the end of 2026.
- No explicit P/E, P/B or current dividend yield figures were disclosed in the sources reviewed for this period.

## Strategie & Ausblick
- **Full-year 2026 guidance maintained** after the Q2/H1 production update, with iron ore sales and lithium volumes trending higher.
- **Lithium build-out:** more than 300 million USD invested in 2026 to develop the Québec lithium business, with the Government of Quebec subscribing up to 200 million USD of equity in Nemaska Lithium.
- Rio Tinto has committed **over 4.8 bn USD of announced capital to lithium and copper expansion between late 2024 and 2026**, combining direct project investment with strategic joint-venture buy-ins (including the ENAMI JV in Chile) to accelerate entry into key production regions.
- **Target of 200,000 t/y LCE by 2028** from Rincon and Sal de Vida / Fenix 1B in Argentina and Bécancour / Nemaska in Canada, building on the 6.7 bn USD Arcadium Lithium acquisition completed in October 2024.
- Continued focus on **cost reduction, productivity and non-core asset disposals** to fund growth while protecting shareholder returns.
- Key watch items: **Chinese iron ore demand**, the pace of the Oyu Tolgoi copper ramp-up, and delivery on the 1.8 bn USD productivity run-rate.
