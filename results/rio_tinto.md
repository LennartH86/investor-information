- Rio Tinto has formally ended merger talks with Glencore; focus remains on organic growth, cost reduction, and copper expansion.

- Simandou's ramp-up toward its ~10 Mtpa annualised run-rate and continued integration of Arcadium Lithium remain additional medium-term growth drivers.
