## Strategy & Outlook
- M&A-driven portfolio reshaping continues: Eaton closed the $1.53 billion Ultra PCS acquisition (aerospace electronics/controls) on January 23, 2026, and the roughly $9.55 billion Boyd Thermal acquisition (data center liquid cooling) in March 2026; these are expected to add about $250 million and $1.5 billion to 2026 sales, respectively.
- On January 26, 2026, Eaton announced its intent to spin off its Mobility business (Vehicle and eMobility segments) into a separate, independent publicly traded company via a Reverse Morris Trust transaction, expected to close in Q1 2027.
- An unconfirmed report referenced a possible $5.1 billion deal involving Dana Incorporated to combine mobility businesses (dated June 14, 2026); this could not be verified against a primary source and may simply be another description of the Mobility spin-off process.
- Overall strategic direction: continued focus on higher-growth, higher-margin electrical, aerospace, and data-center-related end markets, funded partly through portfolio simplification (Mobility separation) and bolt-on/larger M&A (Ultra PCS, Boyd Thermal, 2025's Fibrebond and Resilient Power Systems).
