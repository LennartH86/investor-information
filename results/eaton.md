## Strategy & Outlook
- M&A-driven portfolio reshaping continues: completed acquisitions include Ultra PCS ($1.53 billion, aerospace electronics/controls, closed January 2026) and Boyd Thermal ($9.5 billion, data-center liquid cooling, closed in the first half of 2026); the pending COL Group acquisition (~€810 million, European medium-voltage power distribution) is expected to close in Q1 2027.
- Eaton's planned Reverse Morris Trust spin-off of its Mobility business (Vehicle and eMobility segments) into an independent, publicly traded company remains on track for a Q1 2027 close.
- Overall strategic direction: continued focus on higher-growth, higher-margin electrical, aerospace, and data-center-related end markets, funded partly through portfolio simplification (Mobility separation) and ongoing bolt-on/larger M&A.
- Record order backlog and accelerating organic growth across all three segments support management's raised guidance and continued analyst confidence (reflected in RBC's raised price target), though some caution persists (Wells Fargo's reduced target) given the stock's elevated valuation after the Q3 rally.
