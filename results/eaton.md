## Strategie & Ausblick / Strategy & Outlook
- Eaton is pursuing an aggressive, multi-billion-dollar acquisition strategy centered on AI/data-center power and cooling, European power distribution and aerospace: Boyd Thermal (liquid-cooling technology, reported deal value of ~$9.55B, closed around March 2026), Ultra PCS Limited (aerospace electronic controls/sensing, ~$1.53B, closed 23.01.2026) and the pending COL Group acquisition from Oaktree (European medium-voltage power distribution, ~€810M/$923M).
- Eaton announced on 26.01.2026 its intention to separate its Mobility business via a Reverse Morris Trust transaction, reportedly combining it with Dana Incorporated in a deal described in secondary sources as valued at roughly $5.1 billion; the transaction is expected to close in Q1 2027. (This valuation figure comes from a secondary aggregator and could not be verified against a primary Eaton filing in this search.)
- Management continues to frame data-center/AI infrastructure, electrification and aerospace demand as the key secular growth drivers underpinning the raised FY2026 guidance; Q3 2026 results (expected late October/early November) will be the next major checkpoint for confirming this trajectory.
