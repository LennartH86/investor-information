## Strategie & Ausblick
- Continued build-out in electrical infrastructure for data centers and utilities, plus expansion in aerospace, via both organic investment and M&A.
- Active acquisition pipeline: completed acquisition of Boyd Thermal ($9.5B deal, closed March 12, 2026) adding liquid-cooling/thermal management for data centers and aerospace (forecasted ~$1.7B 2026 sales, expected accretive to adjusted EPS in year two); earlier acquired Ultra PCS (~$1.53B, January 2026) adding mission-critical aerospace electronics; agreed to acquire COL Group (European medium-voltage electrical distribution, enterprise value ~€810M) from Oaktree's Power Opportunities strategy, expected to close Q1 2027.
- Previously announced planned separation of the Mobility business via a Reverse Morris Trust transaction continues to signal a portfolio shift toward higher-growth electrical and aerospace segments.
- Management has repeatedly raised full-year guidance through 2026, reflecting confidence in sustained demand from data-center buildouts and broader electrification trends; no change to this narrative was found in the current period.
