## Strategy & Outlook
- Eaton continues an aggressive, multi-billion-dollar acquisition strategy to expand exposure to AI data centers, European power distribution and aerospace: Boyd Thermal (~$9.5B, liquid-cooling/"grid-to-chip" capabilities), Ultra PCS (closed H1 2026, ~$1.5B, aerospace electronics), and COL Group (~€810M, European medium-voltage power distribution).
- In parallel, Eaton plans to separate its Mobility business via a Reverse Morris Trust transaction, expected to close in Q1 2027, with Eaton shareholders retaining majority ownership of the combined entity and Eaton receiving a cash distribution of roughly $1.1 billion.
- Management continues to point to data center/AI infrastructure, electrification and aerospace as the key secular growth drivers underpinning guidance for the remainder of 2026.
