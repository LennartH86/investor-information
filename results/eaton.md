- CEO Paulo Ruiz (in role since June 2025, previously President & COO of Eaton's Industrial Sector, and formerly with Siemens) stated at the Laguna Conference: "We see that the best years for this business are still ahead of us."
- Ruiz indicated Eaton is expected to have added roughly 10 billion USD in incremental revenue between 2024 and 2026 — about ten times the top-line growth pace of the prior decade.
- Management reiterated that electrical revenue expectations through 2030 could exceed the current 31 billion USD target, reflecting confidence in the long-term electrification and AI power-demand thesis.

## Financials
- Q1 2026: record sales of 7.5 billion USD (+17% YoY, +10% organic); adjusted EPS of 2.81 USD (Q1 record); 2026 organic growth guidance raised to 10% at the midpoint.
- Q2 2026: record sales of 8.53 billion USD (+21% YoY, +14% organic, +7% from acquisitions); adjusted EPS of 3.15 USD (Q2 record); full-year guidance raised again.
- Full-year 2026 guidance: adjusted EPS of 13.40–13.60 USD, representing roughly +12% YoY at the midpoint; GAAP EPS guidance of 10.36–10.56 USD.
- Cash deployed for acquisitions reached approximately 11.1 billion USD in the first half of 2026 (primarily Boyd Thermal and Ultra PCS), alongside 85 million USD invested in nonmarketable securities, underscoring an aggressive but disciplined capital deployment strategy.

## Strategy & Outlook
- The 9.55 billion USD acquisition of Boyd Thermal (closed March 2026) adds liquid-cooling technology, enabling an integrated "grid-to-chip" offering as AI data-center workloads drive sharply higher power and cooling demand.
- The 1.53–1.55 billion USD acquisition of Ultra PCS Limited (completed 23.01.2026) strengthens Eaton's aerospace and defense safety/mission-critical systems capabilities.
- Eaton is advancing its "2030 Growth Strategy," including a planned Reverse Morris Trust separation combining its Mobility Group with Dana Incorporated, targeted for completion around Q1 2027.
- Management continues to position Eaton as a primary beneficiary of AI data-center growth, electrification, and decarbonization megatrends, with accelerating orders and backlog across the Electrical and Aerospace segments supporting continued guidance raises through 2026.
