## Strategie & Ausblick
- Eaton continues an aggressive, multi-billion-dollar acquisition strategy to expand exposure to AI data centers, European power distribution and aerospace: Boyd Thermal (closed 12.03.2026, ~$9.55B, liquid-cooling/"grid-to-chip" capabilities), Ultra PCS (closed January 2026, ~$1.53B, aerospace electronics) and the pending COL Group deal (~€810M, European medium-voltage power distribution).
- Eaton plans to separate its Mobility business via a Reverse Morris Trust transaction combined with Dana Incorporated, expected to close in Q1 2027, with Eaton shareholders retaining majority ownership of the combined entity and Eaton receiving a cash distribution of roughly $1.1 billion.
- Management continues to point to data-center/AI infrastructure (including a collaboration with NVIDIA on data-center power platforms), electrification and aerospace as the key secular growth drivers underpinning FY2026 guidance.
