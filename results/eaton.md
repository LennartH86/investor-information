## Recent News
- Eaton reported record Q3 2026 results on November 3, 2026: net sales rose 28% YoY to a record $8.95 billion, with organic growth of 19% (above the high end of guidance); adjusted EPS was $3.68 vs. a $3.54 consensus estimate, and GAAP EPS was $2.77 (+7% YoY).
- Record segment operating margins of 25.3%, above the high end of prior guidance.
- Orders and backlog hit record levels across all three segments; backlog grew 46% YoY in Electrical and 33% YoY in Aerospace.
- Eaton raised full-year 2026 guidance: organic growth now expected at 13–15% (up from 11–13%), and adjusted EPS now guided to $13.70–$13.80 (up from $13.40–$13.60); Q4 adjusted EPS guidance is $4.05–$4.15.
- Shares reacted positively to the print, with reported intraday moves ranging from roughly +1.6% (premarket) to +5.9% depending on the source/timing cited.
- Alongside Q3 results, Eaton announced an agreement to acquire Italy-based COL Group (medium-voltage power distribution) from Oaktree for an enterprise value of about €810 million (~$921 million); closing is expected in Q1 2027.

## Strategy & Outlook
- M&A-driven portfolio reshaping continues: completed acquisitions include Ultra PCS ($1.53 billion, aerospace electronics/controls, closed January 2026) and Boyd Thermal ($9.5 billion, data-center liquid cooling, closed March 2026); a new agreement to acquire COL Group (~€810 million, European medium-voltage power distribution) was announced alongside Q3 2026 results, expected to close Q1 2027.
- Eaton's planned Reverse Morris Trust spin-off of its Mobility business (Vehicle and eMobility segments) into an independent, publicly traded company remains on track for a Q1 2027 close.
- Overall strategic direction: continued focus on higher-growth, higher-margin electrical, aerospace, and data-center-related end markets, funded partly through portfolio simplification (Mobility separation) and ongoing bolt-on/larger M&A.
- Record order backlog and accelerating organic growth across all three segments support management's raised guidance and suggest continued momentum heading into Q4 2026 and FY2027.
